# Send a voice message in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can send a voice message to a person or group in Google Chat and transcribe these voice messages into text.

### Send a voice message

**Important:**

- If this is the first time you send a message to a particular person, they must accept your message request before you can chat with them.
- This feature isn't available on Chrome OS.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.

&nbsp;

2.  Click **New chat**.
3.  Enter a name or an email address.
4.  Click **Start chat**.
    - When you enter a name or email address, you can get suggestions.
    - To start a group DM, add more names or email addresses.

&nbsp;

5.  In the reply area, click the mic .
6.  In the popup, to record and stop the recording, select the red circle .

**Tip:** After recording, you can find options to either restart or send the voice message.

### View or hide voice message transcription

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Click a conversation, direct message, or space.
3.  To view the voice message transcript:
    - **Sender:** Click below the voice message and then **Show more**.
    - **Receiver:** Click below the voice message and then **View transcript**.
4.  To hide the transcript, click **Hide transcript**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can send a voice message to a person or group in Google Chat and transcribe these voice messages into text.

### Send a voice message

**Important:** If this is the first time you send a voice message to a particular person, they must accept your message request before you can chat with them.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap New chat .
3.  Enter a name or email address, then tap **Done**.
    - When you enter a name or email address, you can get suggestions.
    - To start a group DM, add more names or email addresses.

&nbsp;

4.  In the reply area, tap the mic .
5.  Your voice message begins to record.
6.  Tap the stop button and then Send .
7.  To delete your recording, tap Delete .

### View or hide voice message transcription

1.  On your Android device, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Tap a conversation, direct message, or space.
3.  To view the voice message transcript, below the voice message, tap **View transcript**.
    - **Sender:** Tap below the voice message and then **Show more**.
    - **Receiver:** Tap below the voice message and then **View transcript**.
4.  To hide the transcript, tap **Hide transcript**.
