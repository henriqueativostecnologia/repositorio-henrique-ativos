# Use the Asana app in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Use the Asana app in Chat to get notifications about updates to Asana tasks.

### Before you begin

- You need permission to install apps from your Google Workspace administrator.
- Add the app to Chat. [Learn how to find apps and add them to Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655820).
- You need an Asana account.

### Set up the Asana app in Chat

1.  Open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open a direct message with the app or go to a space with the app.
3.  To sign in to your Asana account, tap **Sign Into Asana**.
    - The configuration window opens.
4.  Under **Choose Workspace**, to use the workspace, tap the Down arrow .
    - Select the Asana workspace.
5.  Under **Choose Project**, to get notifications, tap the Down arrow .
    - Select the Asana project.
6.  Under **Thread messages by** menu, choose an option:
    - **No threads**
    - **Threaded by project**
    - **Threaded by task**
7.  To get the notifications of the items in Chat, check the boxes next to the items.
8.  Click **Save**.
9.  Optional: To add another project, click **Add**.

**Tip:** You need to set up notifications in each space where you add the app. The app sends notifications to all spaces where it's a member.

### Change app settings

In spaces, only the person who added the app can change the settings.

1.  Open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open a direct message with the app or go to a space with the app.
3.  Enter **settings**.
    - In spaces, enter **@Asana settings** or **/Asana settings**.
    - The current settings for the app opens.
4.  Click **Edit Settings**.
    - The settings page opens.
5.  Click Edit .
6.  Change the settings as needed.
7.  Click **Save**.

Once the app is removed from a space, the app settings get deleted.
