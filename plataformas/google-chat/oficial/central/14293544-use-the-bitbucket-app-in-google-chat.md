# Use the Bitbucket app in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Use the Bitbucket app in Chat to:

- Receive messages when events occur in Bitbucket.
- Perform quick actions, such as to view and approve pull requests.
- View and reply to comments on your pull requests.

### Learn what you need before you begin

- Permission from your Google Workspace administrator to install apps.
- Add the app to Chat. [Learn how to find apps and add them to Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655820).
- Use your Bitbucket administrator privileges to install the Bitbucket for Google Chat plugin in the Bitbucket workspace from the [Bitbucket Marketplace](https://bitbucket.org/account/addon-directory?addon=googlechat-bitbucket-bot).
  - Once an administrator installs the app, all users with access to the workspace can add the Bitbucket for Google Chat and work with that workspace.

### Set up the Bitbucket app in Chat

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [your Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select an option:
    - Open a direct message with the app.
    - Go to a space with the app.
3.  Click **Connect**.
    - The configuration window opens.
4.  Enter the Bitbucket workspace URL.
    - Example URL: https://bitbucket.org/*workspace-name*/workspace/overview
5.  Click **Next Step.**
    - If the required plugin is already installed, go to step 6.
    - Otherwise, follow the instructions on the page to install the plugin, then click **Run App**.
6.  Click **Connect** and then **Configure Access**.
7.  ** **To provide the app access to your Google and Bitbucket accounts, follow the steps during setup.
    - If successful, you should receive a message that the app is now connected to your chosen Bitbucket account.

### Manage repository notifications

#### Change workspace

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [your Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select an option:
    - Open a direct message with the app.
    - Go to a space with the app.
3.  Click **Settings**.
    - Alternatively, enter `/bitbucket_settings`.
4.  At the top right, click **Change**.
5.  Select a workspace.
6.  Click **Change workspace**.

#### Add notification for a repository

Add the repositories that you want to receive notifications for.

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [your Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select an option:
    - Open a direct message with the app.
    - Go to a space with the app.
3.  Click **Settings**.
    - Alternatively, enter `/bitbucket_settings`.
4.  At the bottom right, click **Add repository**.
5.  Choose a repository from the list.
6.  Click **Select**.
7.  Select an option:
    - Check the boxes that you want to receive notifications for.
    - Check **Select all**.

### Change app settings

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [your Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select an option:
    - Open a direct message with the app.
    - Go to a space with the app.
3.  Enter `/bitbucket_settings`.
4.  Update your settings.
5.  Click **Save**.

### Uninstall the Bitbucket app

I**mportant:** If you uninstall the app from a space, the Bitbucket workspace is removed from the space and all the notifications you set up are lost. However, your login information remains.

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [your Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select an option:
    - Open a direct message with the app.
    - Go to a space with the app.
3.  At the top, click the Down arrow .
4.  Select an option:
    - For Chat: Click **Uninstall**.
    - For Spaces: Click **Apps & integrations** and then More and then **Remove from space**.
