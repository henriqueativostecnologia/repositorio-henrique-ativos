# Personalize your Google Chat display settings

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: iPhone & iPad, Android phone & tablet, Computer. All versions are copied below.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

To personalize your experience in Google Chat, you can select your color, mode, and density settings.

### Turn on Dark Mode

**Important:**

- Dark Mode is available on iOS 13 and up.
- When you update your device settings, all apps on your iPhone or iPad change to match the background theme.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, [turn on Dark Mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT210332).
2.  Open Google Chat .

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To personalize your experience in Google Chat, you can select your color, mode, and density settings.

### Turn on Dark theme

**Important:** Dark theme for Chat is available on Android 10 and up.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open Google Chat .
2.  Tap Menu Menu and then **Settings** and then **Theme**.
3.  Tap **Dark**.

[Go to Chat settings](https://chat.google.com/settings)

## Version: Computer

To personalize your experience in Google Chat, you can select your color, mode, and density settings.

Change the appearance of chat

### Change the appearance of Chat

**Important:** These appearance settings apply for [chat.google.com](http://chat.google.com) and the [Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386). These settings don’t apply to Chat in Gmail. [Learn more about Chat in Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9341104).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).

2.  At the top right, click Settings .

3.  In the side panel, click Appearance .

4.  Select an option:
    - Under “Color,” select the color palette you want.
    - Under “Mode,” select: **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**.
      - **Tip:** The “System” option is based on your OS or browser setting.
    - Under “Density,” select **Comfortable** or **Compact**. [Learn more about density settings for Chat in Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9951283#density_setting_gmail).
      - **Tip:** Use Compact mode to find more information at a glance in Chat.

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Comfortable (Default)                                                         | Compact                                                                       |
+===============================================================================+===============================================================================+
|                                                                               |                                                                               |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| -   Sender and recipient message bubbles are on opposite sides.               | -   Sender and recipient message bubbles are on the same side.                |
| -   Narrower message bubbles.                                                 | -   Wider message bubbles.                                                    |
| -   More space in the left bar, conversation list, and Home.                  | -   Less space in the left bar, conversation list, and Home.                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

5.  Click Close .

**Tip:** Appearance settings in Chat comply with accessibility guidelines. [Learn more about Accessibility in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/16264638).

### Adjust density settings for Chat in Gmail

When you adjust the density settings in Gmail, your changes apply to both Gmail and Chat in Gmail.

**Important:** These changes don’t apply to Chat when you access it through:

- [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com/)
- [Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386)

To change the density settings:

1.  On your computer, open [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings .
3.  In the settings side panel, under “Density,” select:
    - **Default**
    - **Comfortable**
    - **Compact**

**Tip:** In Chat in Gmail, the “Default” and “Comfortable” options use the same Chat density layout.
