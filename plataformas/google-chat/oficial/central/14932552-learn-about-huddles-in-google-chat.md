# Learn about huddles in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** Only users with a work account can access huddles in Chat, which aren't available for personal or school accounts.

- You can start a huddle instantly as a voice chat from a 1:1 conversation, group conversation, or space in Google Chat without ringing.
- You can share your screen or turn on your camera in a huddle.
- You can also choose to start a video meeting or call someone directly from Chat or Gmail. [Learn more about how to start a video meeting or call someone](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653283).

### Start a huddle in Chat

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Open a direct message or space.
3.  At the top right, click the dropdown and then click Huddles .
4.  To end the call, click End call Call end icon .

**Tips:**

- If participants are in a huddle, it remains active.
- Someone can join a huddle anytime through a huddle invitation chip as long as the person who started the huddle is in it.

### Join a huddle

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  You can join a huddle from your list of conversations or from a direct message or space:
    - **To join from your list of conversations:** click the huddle icon next to the name or space name.
    - **To join from within a conversation:**
      1.  Open a conversation.
      2.  At the top right, click Huddles .

##### Turn cameras on or share your screen in a huddle

By default, the huddle opens as a voice chat.

- To change the voice chat to a video chat, click Turn on camera .
  - **Tip:** If someone in the huddle turns on their camera, a video badge Start a video meeting displays next to their profile picture.
- To share your screen, click Present now .

##### Resize your huddle window

To resize a huddle, at the bottom left, drag the handle .

**Tip:** The huddle window automatically resizes if someone turns on their camera or you click the presentation tile .

##### Switch from a Chat huddle to Google Meet

To switch from a Chat huddle to Google Meet, at the top right, click Open Meet in tab .

##### Change background effects

To use a background effect in a huddle, hover over your video, then click Apply visual effects .

##### Send an emoji reaction

To send an emoji reaction in a huddle, click More and then Send a reaction .

## Version: Android phone & tablet

**Important:** Only users with a work account can access huddles in Chat, which aren't available for personal or school accounts.

- You can start a huddle instantly as a voice chat from a 1:1 conversation, group conversation, or space in Google Chat without ringing.
- You can share your screen or turn on your camera in a huddle.
- You can also choose to start a video meeting or call someone directly from Chat or Gmail. [Learn more about how to start a video meeting or call someone](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653283).

**Important:** To start or join a huddle on an Android phone or tablet, you must install the Gmail or Meet app. To download the app, go to the Google Play Store.

### Start a huddle in Chat

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Open a direct message or space.
3.  To start a huddle with the members of the chat or space:
    - **In a 1:1 conversation:** At the top right, tap Call and then Start a huddle .
    - **In a group conversation or a space:** At the top right, tap Send a Meet link and then Start a huddle .
4.  To end the call, tap End call Call end icon .

**Tips:**

- By default, the huddle opens as a voice chat. To change the huddle to a video chat, tap Turn on camera .
- If someone in the huddle turns on their camera, a video badge Start a video meeting displays next to their profile picture.

#### Join a huddle

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  You can join a huddle from your list of conversations or from a direct message or space:
    - To join from your list of conversations: tap the huddle icon next to the name or space name.
    - To join from within a conversation: select a direct message or space with an active huddle, which has the huddle icon next to the name or space name. In the huddle invitation banner, tap **Join**.

**Tip:** If a huddle starts while you’re in a conversation, a banner pops up. Tap **Answer**.
