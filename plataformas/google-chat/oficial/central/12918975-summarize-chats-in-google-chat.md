# Summarize chats in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you're a Google Workspace user, conversation summaries are now automatically available by default in spaces with many read and unread messages. Some users can [use Gemini to request summaries for read and unread conversations in Home](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/12918975#use-Gemini-summaries).

### Use Gemini summaries for read & unread conversations

**Important:**

- This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace subscription. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).
- This feature is available in select supported languages. [Learn about supported languages for summaries in home](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14925782#zippy=%2Csummaries-in-home).

You can use Gemini to show summaries for read and unread conversations that include 1:1 direct messages, group direct messages, spaces, and threads. Summaries aren't available for:

- Messages with apps
- Conversations with history turned off

**Tip:** To enable conversation and space summaries in Home, [turn on smart features in Gmail](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/10079371).

#### Summarize read & unread conversations in Home

This image shows how to summarize unread conversation or space in Home

You can choose to summarize a specific read and unread conversation in Home.

1.  On your computer, [open Google Chat](http://chat.google.com) or Chat in [Gmail](http://gmail.google.com/).
2.  Hover over the conversation you want to summarize in Home.
3.  Click **Summarize**.

#### Summarize unread messages in a conversation

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://gmail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click Chat .
2.  Choose a conversation with unread messages to summarize.
3.  Click **Summarize unread**.
    - Under the "Recent discussions" section, you can find a summary list of your recent unread messages.
4.  Click **Close summary**.

### Give feedback about Gemini conversation summaries

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request. Because feedback may be human readable, please do not submit data that contains personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can submit feedback. Your feedback can help improve AI-assisted Workspace features and broader Google efforts in AI.

1.  At the bottom of the generated output, click Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .
2.  In the pop-up window, you can select the data you want to share along with your feedback, including additional context and outputs. If you don’t want to share certain data, you can uncheck it.
    - If you select Bad suggestion , select **Next** to select the issue you found and enter additional feedback.
3.  Click **Submit**.

To provide general feedback on this feature, at the top, go to Help Help & Feedback and then **Send feedback to Google**. [Learn more about sending feedback in Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13985546).

To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).

### Learn about Gemini feature suggestions

- Gemini feature suggestions don’t represent Google’s views, and should not be attributed to Google.
- Don’t rely on Gemini features as medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
- Gemini features may suggest inaccurate or inappropriate information. Your feedback makes Gemini more helpful and safe.
- Enterprise end users can submit feedback about their experience using this feature. End users are informed before they submit feedback that the feedback data shouldn’t contain personal, sensitive, or confidential information.
