# Link to a specific message

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In a chat conversation, you can copy a link to a message and share it with other members in the conversation.

### Copy a specific message link

**Important:** Only people already in the conversation can access and share links to specific messages. When you share a message link with someone who isn’t part of the conversation, they receive a notification that says they don't have access to it.

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or your [Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select a chat conversation you want to get a message link from.
3.  Hover over a message.
4.  Click More options ​ and then **Copy link**.

**Tip:** This feature isn’t available for spaces grouped by topic.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In a chat conversation, you can copy a link to a message and share it with other members in the conversation.

### Copy a specific message link

**Important:** Only people already in the conversation can access and share links to specific messages. When you share a message link with someone who isn’t part of the conversation, they receive a notification that says they don't have access to it.

1.  Open the Chat app or Gmail app .
2.  Select a chat conversation you want to get a message link from.
3.  Tap and hold a message.
4.  Tap **Copy link**.

**Tip:** This feature isn’t available for spaces grouped by topic.
