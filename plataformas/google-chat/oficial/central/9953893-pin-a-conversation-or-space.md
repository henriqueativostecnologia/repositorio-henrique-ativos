# Pin a conversation or space

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To check an important conversation or space in Google Chat, you can pin it to the top of your conversation or space list. A dot shows up next to pinned conversations and spaces with unread messages.

### Pin or unpin a conversation

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QHxOwyVNtgI

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.

2.  In Chat, next to a conversation, click More options .
3.  Click **Pin** or **Unpin**.

### Pin or unpin a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  In Chat, next to a space, click More options .
3.  Click **Pin** or **Unpin**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To check an important conversation or space in Google Chat, you can pin it to the top of your conversation or space list. A dot shows up next to pinned conversations and spaces with unread messages.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .

    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .

2.  Choose an action:
    - In your conversation list, tap and hold the conversation.
    - In your space list, tap and hold the space.
3.  Tap **Pin** or **Unpin**.
