# Join a space in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

When you’re invited to join a space, you can preview the space and view its message history.

When you leave a space, you can’t:

- Rejoin unless another member adds or invites you.
- Get updates on active conversation threads.

If you rejoin a space, you can view all message history. If someone removes you from a space, you’ll need to be invited again.

**Tip:** If the space is open, you can rejoin at any time. [Learn about open spaces](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11971020).

### Join a space

**Important:** If the space owner or manager turns off the request option, you can’t request to join a space.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, click New chat and then Browse spaces .
3.  Search for a space.
    - The spaces that you're invited to appear at the top of the list.
4.  Click the space name or **View**.
    - If the space is open, click **Join**. You can also preview the messages.
    - If the space is private or discoverable and the space allows it, you can submit a request to join.

**Tip:** If you request to join a space, your username, email address, and avatar are shared with the space owner and manager once they receive your request. Some spaces may not allow requests to join.

### Leave a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Next to a space name, click More options **Leave** and then **Leave space**.

### Rejoin a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, click New chat and then Browse spaces .
3.  Search for a space.
4.  Click the space name or **View**.
    - If the space is open, click **Join**. You can also preview the messages.
    - If the space is private or discoverable and the space allows it, you can submit a request to join.

**Tip:** You can delete a space you created. [Learn how to delete a space](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653861#delete_a_space).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

When you’re invited to join a space, you can preview the space and view its message history.

- Rejoin unless another member adds or invites you.
- Get updates on active conversation threads.

If you rejoin a space, you can view all message history. If someone removes you from a space, you’ll need to be invited again.

**Tip:** If the space is open, you can rejoin at any time. [Learn about open spaces](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11971020).

### Join a space

**Important:** If the space owner or manager turns off the request option, you can’t request to join a space.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap New chat and then Browse spaces .
3.  Search for a space.
    - The spaces that you're invited to appear at the top of the list.
4.  Tap the space name or **View**.
    - If the space is open, tap Join . You can also preview the messages.
    - If the space is private or discoverable and the space allows it, you can submit a request to join.

**Tips:**

- Touch and hold a conversation for more options.
- If you request to join a space, your username, email address, and avatar are shared with the space owner or manager once they receive your request. Some spaces may not allow requests to join.

### Leave a space

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections .
3.  Open the space that you want to leave.
4.  At the top, tap the name of the space.
5.  Tap **Leave** and then **Leave space**.

**Tip:** Touch and hold a conversation for more options.

### Rejoin a space

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap New chat and then Browse spaces .
3.  Search for a space.
4.  Tap the space name or **View**.
    - If the space is open, tap Join . You can also preview the messages.
    - If the space is private or discoverable and the space allows it, you can submit a request to join.

**Tip:** You can delete a space you created. [Learn how to delete a space](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653861?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&oco=1#delete_a_space_android).
