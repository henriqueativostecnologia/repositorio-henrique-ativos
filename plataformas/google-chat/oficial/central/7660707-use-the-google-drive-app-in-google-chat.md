# Use the Google Drive app in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can use the Google Drive app to get notifications in Google Chat about activity in Drive.

### Learn about Google Drive notifications in Chat

If you have the Google Drive app for Google Chat installed, you get direct messages about Google Drive events, such as:

- A new file or folder that’s shared with you.
- A new comment in a file you own, a comment, or an action item that mentions you or is assigned to you.
  - New mentions also show up in Chat’s Mentions shortcut.
  - You can reply to comments directly from the Google Drive app for Google Chat. For example, to mention a colleague, type “@” followed by their name or email address.
- An access request to a file you own.
  - To share the file, select the access level you want to provide, such as:
    - **Editor**
    - **Viewer**
    - **Commenter**
  - Select an option:
    - Click **Share**.
    - Decline access.

The notifications from the Google Drive app for Chat have a few limitations compared to Drive notifications that you get through email, browser, or mobile apps. These limitations include:

- To reduce notification volume, email notifications are delayed and grouped together. Notifications in Google Chat are sent immediately and not grouped together.
- If a user edits a comment after they post it, the notification doesn’t update. To find the updated comment, you need to access the original document through the link in the notification.
- You don’t receive notifications when tasks are assigned to you in documents.
- You don’t receive notifications when there are edits or suggestions in documents you own.
- You don’t receive notifications for emoji reactions to comments. You can’t provide emoji reactions from the Google Drive app.

### Before you begin

If the app isn't installed, add the app to Chat. [Learn how to use apps in Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655820).

### Manage Google Drive notifications in Chat or Gmail

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under “Direct messages,” click **Google Drive**.
3.  Click More options and then **Notifications**.
4.  Select **All** or **None**.
5.  Optional: To prevent Google Drive from being bolded and moved to the top of your conversation list, select the box next to "Mute conversation."

### Uninstall the Google Drive app

**Important:** For work or school accounts, you can’t uninstall apps your admin installs.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under “Direct messages,” click **Google Drive**.
3.  Click the app name and then **Uninstall**.

### Block a sender or report a specific file

**Important:** This option is only available on a shared file owned by a user outside your organization.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under “Direct messages,” click **Google Drive**.
3.  At the bottom of the relevant notification card, click **Block sender** or **report**.
