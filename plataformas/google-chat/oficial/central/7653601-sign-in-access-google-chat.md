# Sign in & access Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

On your computer, you can sign in to use Chat in Gmail or access Chat directly at [chat.google.com](http://chat.google.com). You can also sign in to the [Google Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386).

On your phone or tablet, you can sign in to the Chat app for Android or iPhone or iPad , or use Chat in Gmail or in a browser.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uwIE5OHZZfc

### Sign in to Google Chat

**Important:** To use Chat, you need a Google Account. [Learn how to create a Google Account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441).

1.  In your browser, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).

    **Tip:** You can also:

    - [Use Chat in Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9341104)
    - [Install the Google Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386)

2.  If prompted, sign in.

**Tip:** Chat displays the name and profile photo from your Google Account. [Learn how to change personal info in your Google Account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27442).

### Use Chat in Gmail

You can access Chat in Gmail on your computer. [Learn how to use Chat in Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9341104).

If you can't find Chat in Gmail, you need to turn it on in Gmail settings.

[Go to Gmail settings](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/chat)

### Access Chat from the web

To message people on the web, open Chat in your browser.

[Open Chat](https://chat.google.com/)

**Tip:** You can also install the standalone app for Google Chrome. [Learn how to install the Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386).

### Use Chat at work or school

If you have a work or school account that’s part of Google Workspace, you can use Chat to communicate in large spaces, small groups, or with another person.

Before you use Chat, your organization needs to set up Chat. If you don’t see Chat, reach out to your administrator. [Learn how to find your administrator.](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/6208960)
