# Forward a message from Chat to Gmail

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can forward a message from Google Chat to your Gmail inbox. The email includes the last 4 messages in the conversation thread. Later, if you turn chat history off, the messages stay in your inbox.

The email may take up to 2 minutes to appear in your inbox.

**Tips:**

- If you forward messages that are more than 25 MB to your inbox, the attachments aren't included.
- Chat app messages in cards won't forward to Gmail inbox.

1.  Point to the message and click Forward to inbox .
2.  Go to your Gmail inbox to find the forwarded messages.
