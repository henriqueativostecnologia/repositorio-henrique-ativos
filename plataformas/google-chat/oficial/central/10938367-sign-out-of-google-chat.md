# Sign out of Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can sign out of, remove, or switch between different Google Chat accounts from any device.

### Sign out on your computer

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/?pli=1) or your [Gmail account](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#inbox).
2.  At the top right, click your profile picture.
3.  Click **Sign out** or **Sign out of all accounts**.

### Sign out from another computer

If you forgot to sign out of Google Chat on another computer, you can remotely sign out of your account. [Sign out on a device you no longer use](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3067630#remove).

### Switch accounts & stay signed in

If you have more than one Google Account, you can sign in to multiple accounts at the same time. You can also switch between your accounts and stay signed in to both.

[Learn how to sign in to multiple accounts](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can sign out of, remove, or switch between different Google Chat accounts from any device.

### Remove your Google Account from your device

**Important:** 

- To sign out of the Google Chat app, you must remove your account from your phone or tablet. 
- When you remove an account, it’s deleted from all apps on your device. When your account is removed, you won’t be signed in to Google products, like Maps or YouTube.

1.  Open the Chat app or Gmail app .
2.  At the top right, tap your profile picture.
3.  Tap **Manage accounts on this device.**
4.  Tap the account you want to remove and then **Remove account.**

**Tip:** If you remove the account from your phone or tablet, you can still use Google Chat on a computer.

### Switch accounts & stay signed in

**Important:** If you add multiple accounts to the Chat app, you can switch between them. Messages on the Chat app stay separate for each account.

1.  Open the Chat app or Gmail app .
2.  At the top right, tap your profile picture.
3.  Tap the account you want to use with Google Chat.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can sign out of, remove, or switch between different Google Chat accounts from any device.

### Remove your Google Account from your device

**Important:** 

- To sign out of the Google Chat app, you must remove your account from your phone or tablet. 
- When you remove an account, it’s deleted from all apps on your device. When your account is removed, you won’t be signed in to Google products, like Maps or YouTube.

1.  Open the Chat app or Gmail app .
2.  At the top right, tap your profile picture.
3.  Tap **Manage accounts on this device.**
4.  Choose an option:
    - To disable an account on Google Chat temporarily, next to the account, tap Toggle . 
    - To remove an account from your device completely, tap **Remove from this device ** and then **Remove**.

**Tip:** If you remove the account from your phone or tablet, you can still use Google Chat on a computer.

 

### Switch accounts & stay signed in

**Important:** If you add multiple accounts to the Chat app, you can switch between them. Messages on the Chat app stay separate for each account.

1.  Open the Chat app or Gmail app .
2.  At the top right, tap your profile picture.
3.  Tap the account you want to use with Google Chat.
