# Manage space settings

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

As a space owner or manager, you can:

- Control who can join a space.
- Control who can manage members and groups.
- Change whether the space is accessible to anyone in your organization or only to people you invite.
- Control permissions for space members.
- Control whether non-members can request to join a space.

**Important:**

- To manage space settings, you need a Google Workspace account.
- When you create a space, you can invite people outside of your organization into a space. However, they won’t have access to some features, such as updating space details or adding people. [Learn more about chatting with people outside your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/9296706).

### Update space access settings

If you're a space owner or manager, you can make a space accessible to anyone in your organization or only to people you invite.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select a space.
3.  At the top, click the space name and then Space settings .
4.  Under the "Access" section, select a space access level:
    - **Private:** Users can't find or join this space unless you add them.
    - **Discoverable:** Anyone in your organization can find the space.
    - **Open:** Anyone in your organization can find and join the space and preview messages.
5.  If your space is private, discoverable, or open to a group of users that is smaller than your entire organization, you can choose whether users are allowed to request to join the space.
6.  Click **Save**.

If you have multiple target audiences:

1.  Next to "Advanced access settings," click Advanced .
2.  Select who can join and who can find your space.
    - You can add only one audience in the "Who can join" field and multiple audiences in the "Who can find" field.
3.  Turn **Request to join** on or off.
4.  Click **Done**.

**Tip:** If you already allow people outside your organization to join the space, you can't make the space discoverable or open to others.

### Update permissions for a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select a space.
3.  At the top, click the space name and then Space settings .
4.  Under the "Permissions" section, select whether all members, managers and owners, or owners only, can:
    - **Modify space board and details**
    - **Turn history on or off**
    - **Use @all**
    - **Manage apps**
    - **Manage webhooks**
5.  Click **Save**.
