# Use GIFs & stickers in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To express yourself with fun visuals, you can use the multimedia picker in Chat to send GIFs and stickers in a direct message or space.

### Send a GIF or sticker

**Important:** You can use GIFs across all devices. Stickers are only available on your computer.

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  Open a direct message or space.
3.  At the bottom, in the message field, click Add GIF or Sticker .
4.  To select a:
    - **GIF:** Click GIF and then a GIF and then Send .
    - **Sticker:** Click Sticker and then a sticker and then Send .

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To express yourself with fun visuals, you can use the multimedia picker in Chat to send GIFs and stickers in a direct message or space.

### Send a GIF

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - In the Gmail app, at the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Open a direct message or space.
3.  At the bottom, tap Action menu and then GIF .
4.  Tap a GIF and then Send .
