# Change the space access level

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

For work or school accounts, you can restrict access to a space to specific members or make the space discoverable or open to everyone in your organization.

### Learn how private, discoverable & open spaces work

#### Learn about private spaces

- Ideal for discussion amongst teams, projects, and groups.
- To join a space, a space member must invite or add you to the space.
- Unless you're a member or invited to the space, you can’t browse for it in Chat.

#### Learn about discoverable spaces

- Ideal for topics that aren’t limited to specific individuals or teams.
- When a space is discoverable, you can:
  - Find the space in "Browse spaces."
  - See the name and description.
  - If the space owners and managers allow it, request to join.

#### Learn about open spaces

- Ideal for topics that aren’t limited to specific individuals or teams.
- When a space is open, you can:
  - Find the space in "Browse spaces."
  - See the name and description.
  - Preview messages.
  - Join the space.
- If you have a link to the space, you can join it with the link.

### Update the space access level

**Important:** To change the access level for a space, you need to be a space owner or manager. [Learn about your role as a space owner or manager](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11833441).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select a space.
3.  At the top, click the space name and then Space settings .
4.  Under the "Access" section, select a space access level:
    - **Private:** Users can't find or join this space unless you add them.
    - **Discoverable:** Anyone in your organization can find the space.
    - **Open:** Anyone in your organization can find and join the space and preview messages.
5.  If your space is private, discoverable, or open to a group of users that is smaller than your entire organization, you can choose whether users are allowed to request to join the space.
6.  Click **Save**.

If you have multiple target audiences:

1.  Next to "Advanced access settings," click Advanced .
2.  Select who can join and who can find your space.
    - You can add only one audience in the "Who can join" field and multiple audiences in the "Who can find" field.
3.  Turn **Request to join** on or off.
4.  Click **Done**.

**Tips:**

- If a space is open to everyone in your organization, you can’t invite people outside your organization to join.
- You can find additional access options your administrator creates. If you have questions about available access options, [contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).
- If your administrator deletes the target audience you select for your space, " Deleted Audience" displays in the space history and space access is private by default.

### Add new members to an open space

You can add new members to an open space by either:

- Copying a link to the space and sharing with others.
- Directly adding or inviting them.

##### Add members with an open space link

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the top, click the space name and then **Copy link to this space**.
3.  Share the link to anyone you’d like to invite or reference the space with.

##### Add members to your space directly

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the top, click the space name and then **Manage members**.
3.  Click **Add**.
4.  Enter the name, email, or Google Group, or select suggested contacts to invite.
5.  Click **Add**.
