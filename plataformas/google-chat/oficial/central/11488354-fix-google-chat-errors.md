# Fix Google Chat errors

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** If you can’t sign in to Gmail or Google Chat, go to [Can't sign in to your Google Account](https://support.google.com/accounts/troubleshooter/2402620).

To resolve Google Chat errors, including those listed below, follow the troubleshooting steps in this page.

- "Unable to load chat."
- "Trying to connect to chat."
- "Can't connect to chat."
- "Can’t load the list of spaces. Try again in a minute."

If you can’t access or sign in to Google Chat, you can:

- [Check your network](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11488354?co=GENIE.Platform%3DComputer#check_your_network)
- [Refresh Google Chat or Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11488354?co=GENIE.Platform%3DComputer#refresh_google_chat_gmail)
- [Check your browser](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11488354?co=GENIE.Platform%3DComputer#check_your_browser)
- [Uninstall browser extensions & apps](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11488354?co=GENIE.Platform%3DComputer#uninstall_browser)
- [Check blocked domains & site permissions](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/11488354?co=GENIE.Platform%3DComputer#check_blocked_domains)

### Check your network

Make sure you have an active internet connection.

### Refresh Google Chat

To refresh a tab or window, reload [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com/).

### Check your browser

Make sure you [use a supported browser](https://support.google.com/a/answer/33864). Chat in Gmail isn’t supported in mobile browsers or in Internet Explorer. You can download apps for:

- [Google Chat on iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-chat/id1163852619)
- [Google Chat on Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.dynamite)

### Uninstall browser extensions & apps

Some extensions and apps might block Google Chat from loading. To fix this issue, you can:

- [Uninstall active extensions](https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/2664769#uninstall-extension).
- [Uninstall active apps](https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/1053346#zippy=%2Cuninstall-an-app).
- [Browse privately to avoid the use of extensions](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464).

**Tips:**

- If you use a browser other than Chrome, check its support page for help.
- Anti-virus software may prevent Google Chat from loading.

### Check blocked domains & site permissions

**Tips:**

- For work or school accounts, make sure your network administrator doesn’t block the browsers above. [Learn how to change site settings permissions on Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/114662?&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop#zippy=%2Cpermissions-that-can-be-changed).
- If you use a browser other than Chrome, check its support page for help.

### Related resources

- [Learn how to manage space settings](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13340792)
- [Learn why you're not getting Google Chat notifications](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9169357)
- [Learn why you can't chat with someone](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9301096)
