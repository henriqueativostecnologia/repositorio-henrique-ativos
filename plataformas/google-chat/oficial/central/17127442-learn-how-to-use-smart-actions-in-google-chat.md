# Learn how to use Smart Actions in Google Chat

**Feature availability**

- These features are available as part of the Gemini Beta.
- Your administrator controls access to the Gemini Beta. If you can’t find these features, your administrator may not have [Beta features enabled for your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14170809).
- This feature is also available as part of our early access testing program, [Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).

**Important:** To use Gemini's Smart Actions, you must have “Google Workspace smart features” turned on. [Learn about smart features & controls for Google Workspace & other Google products](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/15604322).

## Use inline meeting intent & schedule meetings

If someone in a conversation asks for a time to meet, Gemini suggests a possible meeting time and can schedule on your behalf.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](http://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](http://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Hover over the underlined section of the message.
3.  To confirm your suggested meeting:
    - To schedule the proposed meeting time and send an invite, click **Send**.
    - To edit the name, participants, or time in the Calendar Companion app, click Edit .
    - To view more suggested meeting times, click **Help me schedule**.

## Turn off Smart Actions in Chat

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](http://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](http://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then **See all settings**.
3.  In the “Message & Media” tab, turn off **Smart Actions**.
4.  Click **Save Changes**.

## Give feedback about Gemini in Chat for meetings

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can let us know by submitting feedback. Your feedback can help improve AI-assisted Workspace features and broader Google efforts in AI.

Because feedback may be human readable, do not submit data that contains personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

1.  At the top right of the generated output, select Good summary or Bad summary .
2.  In the pop-up window, you can select the data you want to share along with your feedback, with additional context and output. If you don’t want to share certain data, you can uncheck it.
    - If you select Bad summary : To select the issue and enter additional feedback, select Next.
3.  Select **Submit**.

To provide general feedback on this feature, select Help Help & Feedback and then **Send feedback to Google**. [Learn how to send feedback in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13985546).

To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).
