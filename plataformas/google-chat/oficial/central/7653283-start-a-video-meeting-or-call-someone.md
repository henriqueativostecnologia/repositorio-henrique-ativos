# Start a video meeting or call someone

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can start a video meeting or call someone from Google Chat or Chat in Gmail.

If you have a work account, you can start a huddle in Chat. [Learn about huddles in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/14932552).

**Important**: If your organization allows external users to join rooms or group conversations, anyone in the room or group can join a video meeting without permission.

**Tip:** To make sure you don't join a meeting with an expired code and to better plan for the future meetings you create, check when meeting codes expire. [Learn about Google Meet meeting codes](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/10710509).

### Start a video meeting from Chat or Gmail

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oVktFBTPPZA

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/)or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Open a conversation.
3.  In the reply area, click Add video meeting and then Send .
    - You can also type /calendar in the reply area to create an invite to a meeting.
4.  To join a video meeting, click **Join video meeting** and then **Join now**.

**Tips**

- If you have smart features turned on, you can schedule meetings using inline meeting intent. When someone asks to meet, Gemini suggests a possible meeting time and can schedule the meeting for you. [Learn how to use Smart Actions in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17127442).
- To learn more about Meet video meetings, go to the [Meet Help Center](https://support.google.com/meet/).

### Call someone directly from Chat or Gmail

**Important:** You can call someone directly from Chat or Gmail in Chrome. If you make a call to a non-Chrome user, they can receive and join the call but they won't hear a ring.

You can start a voice or video meeting from Chat or Gmail. When the other person receives the call, they’ll hear a ring.

1.  In Chat, open a direct message.
2.  At the top, click Start video call . The call opens in a small window.
3.  To end the call, click End call Call end icon .

**Tip:** If you want to adjust the call window:

- To move the call to a new tab, click Move to a tab New window .
- To move, click and hold the window.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can start a video meeting or call someone from Google Chat or Chat in Gmail.

If you have a work account, you can start a huddle in Chat. [Learn about huddles in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/14932552).

**Important**: If your organization allows external users to join rooms or group conversations, anyone in the room or group can join a video meeting without permission.

**Tip:** To make sure you don't join a meeting with an expired code and to better plan for the future meetings you create, check when meeting codes expire. [Learn about Google Meet meeting codes](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/10710509).

**Important:** For call notifications to work as intended, please make sure you have the latest version of the app installed.

### Start a video meeting from Chat or Gmail

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app ;.
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In Chat, open a conversation.
3.  In the reply area, tap Action menu Add and then Meet link and then Send .
    - You can also type /calendar in the reply area to create an invite to a meeting.
4.  To join a video meeting, tap **Join video meeting** and then **Join now**.

**Tips**

- If you have smart features turned on, you can schedule meetings using inline meeting intent. When someone asks to meet, Gemini suggests a possible meeting time and can schedule the meeting for you. [Learn how to use Smart Actions in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17127442).
- To learn more about Meet video meetings, go to the [Meet Help Center](https://support.google.com/meet/).

### Call someone directly from Chat or Gmail

**Important:** To make direct Meet calls from the Google Chat app, you must have the latest version of the Gmail app.

You can start a voice or video meeting from Chat or Gmail. When the other person receives the call, they’ll hear a ring.

1.  In Chat, in the bottom navigation menu, tap Direct messages .
2.  Open a direct message.
3.  At the top, click Start voice call or Start video call .
4.  To end the call, click End call Call end icon .

**Tip:** If you call someone who uses the Chat app but doesn’t have the Gmail app:

- Their phone doesn’t ring.
- They receive a Chat notification that you started a voice or video call.
