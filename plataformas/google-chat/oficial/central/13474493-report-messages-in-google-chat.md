# Report messages in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can report messages that are inappropriate or that violate your organization’s guidelines in Google Chat. This feature is only available in:

- Google Workspace for Education Standard
- Google Workspace for Education Plus
- Google Workspace Enterprise Plus

**Important:**

- Before you can report messages, your admin must turn on Chat content reporting for your organization.
- Your admin sets up the types of messages that you can report in Chat. If you don’t see the option to report a message, you won’t be able to send the message to your admin for review.
- You can only report messages sent in your organization. [Chat history](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7664687) must be turned on in the conversation.

### Report a specific message

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Open a conversation.
3.  Hover over the message.
4.  From the available message actions, click More options and then **Report**.
5.  Enter your feedback.
6.  Click **Submit report**.
    - The report is sent to your admin to review.
