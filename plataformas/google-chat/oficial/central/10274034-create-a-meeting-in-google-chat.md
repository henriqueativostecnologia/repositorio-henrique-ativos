# Create a meeting in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can schedule a Google Calendar meeting and share a link to the meeting in Google Chat.

When you share the meeting, an event card appears in the message with the meeting title, date, and time.

### Schedule & share a Calendar meeting

**Important:**

- Before you can schedule a meeting in Chat, [download and install the Calendar app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.calendar&hl=en_US&gl=US).
- You can only share and schedule a meeting in Chat with the Calendar app.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail**: At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In Chat, open a message.
3.  In the reply area, tap Action menu and then **Calendar invite**.
    - You can also type /calendar in the reply area to create a meeting invite.
4.  In Calendar, enter your meeting details.
5.  Tap **Save & share** and then **Send**.

**Tips**:

- If you have smart features turned on, you can schedule meetings using inline meeting intent. When someone asks to meet, Gemini suggests a possible meeting time and can schedule the meeting for you. [Learn how to use Smart Actions in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17127442).
- If you remove someone from a meeting invitation, but they’re still in the chat, they can view the event card.
- You can also share a link to a video meeting in Chat. [Learn how to create a video meeting](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653283).

## Version: Computer

You can schedule a Google Calendar meeting and share a link to the meeting in Google Chat.

When you share the meeting, an event card appears in the message with the meeting title, date, and time.

### Schedule & share a Calendar meeting

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/?pli=1) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#inbox).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  In Chat, open a message.
3.  In the reply area, click Google Workspace tools and then **Calendar invite**.
    - You can also type /calendar in the reply area to create a meeting invite.
4.  On the right, in the side panel, enter your meeting details.
5.  Click **Save & share** and then **Send**.

**Tips**:

- If you have smart features turned on, you can schedule meetings using inline meeting intent. When someone asks to meet, Gemini suggests a possible meeting time and can schedule the meeting for you. [Learn how to use Smart Actions in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17127442).
- If you remove someone from a meeting invitation, but they’re still in the chat, they can view the event card.
- You can also share a link to a video meeting in Chat. [Learn how to create a video meeting](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653283).

### Related resources

- [Add or remove a video conference from your Calendar event](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/9896550)
- [Start a video meeting](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653283)
