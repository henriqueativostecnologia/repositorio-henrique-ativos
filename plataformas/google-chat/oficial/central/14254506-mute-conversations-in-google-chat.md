# Mute conversations in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

If you don’t want to get notifications for a specific conversation in Chat, you can mute it. When you mute a conversation, the conversation isn’t:

- Highlighted in your list of conversations
- Moved to the top of your list of conversations
- Shown in Home
  - **Tip**: When you mute a conversation or space, the threads you follow still appear in Home. If someone mentions you directly in a thread, you follow that thread automatically.

A badge still shows next to conversations where someone mentions you. [Learn more about mentions in Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/14170425).

### Mute or unmute a conversation

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app .
2.  In your conversation list, tap and hold a conversation.
3.  Tap **Mute** or **Unmute**.

### Mute or unmute a conversation

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  In your conversation list, on the right of a conversation, click More options and then **Mute** or **Unmute**.
