# Learn about Google Chat app permissions

Google Chat apps can perform actions that are similar to user actions in Google Chat. You can find more information about the permissions that a specific app can request in the app’s listing in the Google Workspace Marketplace.

**Tip:** If you have a work or school account and you want to know what permissions an app is granted, contact your admin.

## Understand permissions for apps added to spaces

Apps added to spaces can:

- Post messages
- Manage space members membership
- Update space metadata
- Read:
  - Messages that invoke the app
  - [Membership information](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655820)
  - Space metadata, such as a space name, description, and on or off history

## Understand permissions for app-created spaces

- App-created spaces may not have human space managers.
- The app can perform management actions typically limited to space managers in app-created spaces, like:
  - Manage space managers membership
  - Update space permissions
- For work or school accounts, you can:
  - Remove the app, if the space permission settings allow.
  - Leave the space.
  - Contact your admin.
    - If needed, admins can revoke app permissions and appoint a human space manager.

## Understand advanced app permissions

- [Learn about how to set up app authorization for Chat](https://support.google.com/a/answer/15137461).
- You can find permissions the app can request in the app’s listing in the Google Workspace Marketplace.

## Learn the differences between app permissions & space permissions

Generally, space permissions don’t apply to apps. Instead, they’re based on the app's membership in your space. They may also require your admin's consent. Even if permissions only allow space managers to perform an action, an application may still have permission to perform that action if it’s a member of the space and has the appropriate consent. [Learn more about space permissions here](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13340792).

Actions that only require the app to be a space member include:

- Use @all.
- Post in announcement spaces.
- Reply in announcement spaces.

Actions that require admin consent include:

- Change space details.
- Turn history on or off.
- Manage members and groups.

Actions normally limited to space managers can generally be performed by applications in spaces the app created with admin consent. With permission, an app can:

- Manage space managers, which includes promotions, demotions, and deleting membership.
- Manage space permissions.
- Delete the space.
