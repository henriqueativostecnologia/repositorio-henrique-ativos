# Block & report a space

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you experience abusive behavior in a Google Chat space, you can block and report the space. You can also preview spaces marked as spam, then choose to block or join the space.

### Learn what happens when you block a space

When you block a space:

- Posts you made in the space stay visible to others.
- You no longer get updates from the space.
- The space won’t appear in your “Spaces” list or space searches.
- You won’t get any future invitation notifications for the space, but your name appears as an invited member.

If you get an invitation to a space you blocked:

- You appear as an invited member, but you won’t get any notifications or be added to the space.
- The space organizer or other members won't know you blocked the space.

**Tips:**

- Blocked users can't add you to a space, nor can you add blocked users to a space.
- When you unblock a space, you can only rejoin the space if add  Add  appears in "Browse spaces."

### Block a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](http://gmail.google.com).
2.  Choose one:
    - On the left, under “Spaces,” next to the space name, click More options  and then **Block space**.
    - Select the space you want to block and then Click the space details drop-down and then **Block space**.
3.  Click **Block**.

**Tips:**

- To report the space as spam or abuse, check the box next to **Report this space as spam or abuse**.
- If you report a space for abuse, a copy of the last 50 messages in the space is sent to Google for review.
- To rejoin a space that you blocked, another member of that space must invite or add you again.

### Unblock a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](http://gmail.google.com/).
2.  Choose an option:
    - In Chat, at the top, click Settings and then **Manage blocked spaces**.
    - In Gmail, at the top, click Settings and then **See all settings** and then **Chat and Meet** and then **Manage chat settings**   and then **Manage blocked spaces**.
3.  Next to the space, click **Unblock**.

### Block a space marked as spam

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](http://gmail.google.com/).
2.  In Chat, click **New Chat** and then **Browse spaces**.
3.  Click Browse spaces menu items and then **Spam invites**.
4.  Next to the space, click **Preview**.
    - For your protection, you can't download links or find images when you preview the space.
5.  To block the space and future invitations, click **Block**.

Optional: To report the space, click the box next to "Report this space as spam or abuse" and then **Block**.

### Join a space marked as spam

1.  In Chat, click **New Chat** and then **Browse spaces**.
2.  Click Browse spaces menu items and then **Spam invites**.
3.  Next to the space, click **Preview**.
    - For your protection, you can't download links or find images when you preview the space.
4.  Click **Join**.

**Tip**: When you join a space marked as spam, the spam label is removed and the space is added to your list of joined spaces.

### Manage spam space invites

Space invites that appear to be spam are filtered into a separate section. You can choose to join or block the space.

You can prevent future invites from spaces marked as spam if you block the space. If you think Google wrongly filtered a space as spam, you can still join the space.

### Related resources

- [Block & report someone](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9277792)
- [Leave or rejoin a space](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653963)

### Block a space

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail**: At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Spaces .
3.  Press and hold a space.
4.  Tap **Block**.

**Tips:**

- To report the space as spam or abuse, check the box next to “Report this space as spam or abuse.” If you use a Google Workspace account, you can only report spaces outside your organization.
- To rejoin a space that you blocked, another member of that space must invite or add you again.

### Unblock a space

1.  In chat, tap Menu and then Settings .
2.  Tap **Manage blocked spaces and chats**.
3.  Tap Unblock .

[Go to Chat settings](https://chat.google.com/settings)

### Block a space marked as spam

1.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Spaces and then **New space** and then **Browse spaces** and then More options and then **Spam invites**.
2.  To preview the space marked as spam, tap on the space.
    - For your protection, you can't download links or find images when you preview the space.
3.  Tap **Block**.

### Join a space marked as spam

1.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Spaces and then **New space** and then **Browse spaces** and then More options and then **Spam invites**.
2.  To preview the space marked as spam, tap on the space.
    - For your protection, you can't download links or find images when you preview the space.
3.  Tap **Accept**.

**Tip:** When you join a space marked as spam, the spam label is removed and the space is added to your list of joined spaces.

### Manage spam space invites

Space invites that appear to be spam are filtered into a separate section. You can choose to join or block the space.

You can prevent future invites from spaces marked as spam if you block the space. If you think Google wrongly filtered a space as spam, you can still join the space.
