# Where you can use Google Workspace Experiments

For personal Google Accounts, Google Workspace Experiments is currently available in some languages and over 170 countries and territories. We’ll gradually expand to more countries and territories in a way that’s consistent with local regulations and our [AI principles](https://ai.google/principles/).
