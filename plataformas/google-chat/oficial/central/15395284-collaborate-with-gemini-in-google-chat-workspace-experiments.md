# Collaborate with Gemini in Google Chat (Workspace Experiments)

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

With Gemini in Chat, when you have a space or conversation open, you can:

- Summarize that space or conversation.
- Generate a list of action items from that space or conversation.
- Answer specific questions about that space or conversation.

**Feature availability:**

- This feature is available as part of our early access testing program, [Learn more about Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).
- To turn off Google Workspace Experiments features, exit the Workspace Experiments program.
- This feature is also available in the Gemini Business and Gemini Enterprise Beta.

### Get started with Gemini in Chat

1.  On your computer, go to [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com).
    - Open the [Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386) or [Chat in Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9341104).
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  In the side panel, select a suggested prompt or write your own prompt. For example:
    - You can ask Gemini to find information from your past chats:
      - When are we launching \[feature name\]?
      - What did \[colleague\] announce to the team yesterday?
    - Or ask Gemini to summarize multiple conversations:
      - Summarize my conversations with \[colleague\] from this week.
      - Summarize my unread messages.

##### Select a suggestion

1.  Select from the list of suggestions.
    - To get more options, select **More suggestions**.
2.  At the bottom, in the prompt box, replace the example text with the information that you want.
3.  Click **Enter**.

##### Enter a prompt in Gemini

1.  At the bottom, in the prompt box, enter a prompt.
2.  Click **Enter**.

**Tips:**

- You can lose your conversation history when:
  - You refresh your browser.
  - You close and reopen Chat.
  - Your computer goes offline.
- To remove your conversation history, click More options and then **Clear history**.

### Things to ask Gemini in Chat

While using Gemini in Chat, you can use Gemini to summarize, list action items, or answer specific questions about a conversation you have open. In the side panel, you can use a prompt to collaborate with Gemini in Chat.

##### Summarize a conversation

While using Gemini in Chat, you can use Gemini to summarize a conversation you have open. The conversation should be open while using Gemini in Chat.

- In the side panel, you can prompt to ask for a summary or to summarize action items from a conversation that you currently have open.
  - Examples:
    - "Summarize this conversation."
    - "What are the key takeaways in this conversation?”
    - After an initial summary is generated, you can ask for a longer summary, "Give me a detailed summary of \[topic\] discussed in this space."

##### Summarize a file

You can use Gemini to summarize a file within a conversation if:

- A message isn’t blocked.
- File types shared as Drive links in Google Chat are:
  - Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides
  - .pdf
  - .csv
  - .txt
  - Zip files
  - Microsoft Office formats, like:
    - .docx (Word)
    - .xlsx (Excel)
    - .pptx (PowerPoint)
- Download restrictions are off for the file.

To summarize a file:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://gmail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Select a direct message or space with a file in the conversation.
3.  Under the file, click Summarize this file.

##### List action items

While using Gemini in Chat, you can use Gemini to list action items from a conversation you have open.

- In the side panel, you can prompt to ask for a list of action items from a conversation that you currently have open.
  - Examples:
    - "What are the action items in this conversation?”
    - “Are there any action items for me?"
    - “Summarize \[person's\] action items”

##### Answer specific questions about a conversation

While using Gemini in Chat, you can ask Gemini to answer questions about a conversation you have open.

- In the side panel, you can prompt to ask specific questions about the conversation that you currently have open.
  - Examples:
    - “What did \[person\] say about the timeline extension?”
    - “What’s the decision on the project discussed in this conversation?"

##### Use a skill

**Important:** Before you can use a skill in Chat, you must create and turn it on in [Workspace Studio](https://studio.workspace.google.com).

To analyze chat conversations, use skills in Ask Gemini in Google Chat.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  On the left, under “Shortcuts,” click Ask Gemini .
3.  In the message field, type `@` followed by the skill name. For example, “@Project Summary.”
4.  Enter your prompt.
5.  Click Send .

**Key use case:** To analyze recent chat conversations within a particular chat space and generate a structured status update, use a “Project Summary” skill.

### Turn off Gemini in Chat

To turn off any of the features on Google Workspace Experiments, you must exit Workspace Experiments. If you exit, you’ll permanently lose access to [all Workspace Experiments features](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/13447104#labs_features), and you won’t be able to rejoin Workspace Experiments. [Learn how to exit Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/13447104#labs-opt-out).

### Download your conversation history

You can access and export your side panel chat history using [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com), even if you don’t have access to side panel in Google Chat.

1.  Go to [takeout.google.com](https://takeout.google.com).
2.  Sign in to your Google Account.
3.  On the Google Takeout page, under “Select data to include,” select **Gemini in Workspace**.
    1.  **Note:** Chat side panel data is included in “Gemini in Workspace” data, not under “Chat.”
4.  Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your export.
    1.  If you are unable to export your data, you may need to contact your administrator. [Learn more about how to export your data](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190).

**Important:**

- Your data from most Google products and services is included in the export by default. This includes your Gemini in Workspace data. [Learn how to download your Workspace data using Takeout](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190).
- Downloading your Gemini in Workspace conversation history doesn’t delete it from Google’s servers.
