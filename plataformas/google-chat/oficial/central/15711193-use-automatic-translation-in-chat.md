# Use Automatic Translation in Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).

You can easily understand messages sent to you in Google Chat through Automatic Translation, which automatically converts messages to your preferred language set in your Google account settings. [Learn how to change your language settings](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32047).

**Tip:** If you [change your preferred language](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32047), it can take up to 24 hours for the feature to update.

### Learn more about supported languages

You can use Automatic Translation in all languages available for the [Neural Machine Translation model](https://cloud.google.com/translate/docs/languages#neural_machine_translation_model).

### Turn on Automatic Translation in Chat or Chat in Gmail

After you turn on Automatic Translation, messages are automatically translated. To find the original untranslated content, click the toggle below the message. Translated messages are only visible to you.

**In Google Chat:**

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then Message & Media .
3.  Under "Automatic Translation," check the box.
4.  Click **Done**.

**In Gmail:**

1.  On your computer, open [Gmail](https://gmail.google.com/).

2.  At the top right, click Settings and then **See all settings**.

3.  At the top, click the **Chat and Meet** tab.

    [Go to Gmail settings](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/chat)

4.  Next to "Chat settings," click **Manage chat settings**.

5.  Under "Automatic Translation," check the box.

6.  Click **Done**.

**Tips:**

- **On mobile:** Unless you reinstall the app, the setting affects new messages but not the ones that already exist.
- **On the Web:** Upon refresh, the setting affects messages that already exist.
- If the message is short or the source language is unclear, Automatic Translation might not work.

### Give feedback about a translation

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request.

If the generated translation is inaccurate or looks unsafe, you can let us know by submitting feedback:

- At the top right, click Support Help & Feedback and then **Send feedback to Google**. [Learn how to send feedback in Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13985546).
- To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
