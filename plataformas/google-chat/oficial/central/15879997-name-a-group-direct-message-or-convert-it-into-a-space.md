# Name a group direct message or convert it into a space

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In a group direct message, you can:

- Name it without the need to convert it into a space.
- Convert it into a space.

### Name a group direct message

To personalize or find a group direct message easily, name the chat.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click Chat .
2.  Select a group direct message.
3.  At the top, click the chat name and then Name this chat .
4.  Enter a chat name.
5.  Click **Save**.

### Convert a group direct message into a space

To share files, assign tasks, and connect with others easily, convert a group direct message to a space.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click Chat .
2.  Select a group direct message.
3.  At the top, click the chat name and then Turn this chat into a space .
4.  Enter a space name.
5.  Click **Done**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In a group direct message, you can:

- Name it without the need to convert it into a space.
- Convert it into a space.

### Name a group direct message

To personalize or find a group direct message easily, name the chat.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Select a group direct message.
3.  At the top, tap the chat name and then Name this chat .
4.  Enter a chat name.
5.  Tap **Save**.

### Convert a group direct message into a space

To share files, assign tasks, and connect with others easily, convert a group direct message to a space.

**Note:** Group direct messages created via continuous meeting chat cannot be converted to spaces.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Select a group direct message.
3.  At the top, tap the chat name and then Turn this chat into a space .
4.  Enter a space name.
5.  Tap **Create.**

- [Send a direct message](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7652736)
- [Create a space](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/12176488)
