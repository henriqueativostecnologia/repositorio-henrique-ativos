# Create & organize sections in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To easily track your conversations, you can organize your direct messages and spaces into sections in Google Chat.

### Create new sections

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Click and hold a conversation in the Chat navigation panel.
3.  Drag and drop to the top of the conversation list .
4.  Type the section name.
5.  Click confirm .

**Tip:** You can also hover over any section header, such as Direct messages or any custom section you created, click More and then **Create new section**.

### Move a direct message or space to a section

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  In the navigation panel, click and hold a direct message or space and then drag to a new section.

**Tip:** You can also drag and drop spaces or direct messages into sections.

### Change display preferences

In sections, you can customize your display to:

- Show all messages or unread messages only.
- Adjust the number of spaces you can find in each section.
- Resize the chat navigation panel horizontally.

#### Show all messages or unread messages only

For each custom section, you can turn sections on or off to Show all messages or Show unread messages .

#### Adjust section heights & placement

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  To adjust sizing for:
    - **Section height:** Drag a section.
      - Hover over the bottom of the section until a resize cursor appears.
      - Drag the resize cursor up or down.
    - **Section placement:** Click and hold a section name, then drag to its new location.

#### Sort alphabetically or by most recent

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Next to a section header, click More .
3.  Select **Sort by most recent** or **Sort alphabetically**.

#### Resize the chat navigation panel

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
    - If the navigation panel is hidden, at the top left, click Menu .
2.  To adjust the chat roster horizontally, drag the bar left and right.

**Tip:** This feature is only available in Google Chat on the web.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To easily track your conversations, you can organize your direct messages and spaces into sections in Google Chat.

### Change section display preferences

In sections, you can customize your display to:

- Show all messages or unread messages only.
- Adjust the number of spaces you can find in each section.

#### Show all messages or unread messages only

In sections, for each custom section, you can turn sections on or off to Show all messages or Show unread messages .

#### Adjust custom section heights

1.  To adjust the section height, touch and hold the section name.
2.  Under "Section height," tap one of the following:
    - **Show all**
    - **Show 5 more**
    - **Show 5 less**
    - **Show active conversations only**

#### Sort alphabetically or by most recent

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app .
2.  Touch and hold a section header.
3.  In the menu that opens, under "Sort this section," tap **Recent** or **Alphabetically**.
