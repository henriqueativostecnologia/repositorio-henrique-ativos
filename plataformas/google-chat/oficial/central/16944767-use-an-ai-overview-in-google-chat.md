# Use an AI Overview in Google Chat

**Feature availability:**

- This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace subscription. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).
- This feature is only available in English. [Learn about supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14925782).

To quickly find info from your Chat conversations, you can ask questions or enter keywords in the Chat search bar. Based on this, Chat generates an AI Overview that can:

- Locate specific information or messages across your full Workspace profile.
- Understand past discussions or outcomes mentioned.
- Find attachments without extensive search.

## Search your Google Chat conversations

**Important:**

- If you use search operators, like `is:unread` or `from:`, you won’t get an AI Overview.
- Search within any conversation or space doesn’t provide an AI Overview.

To get an AI Overview in Google Chat:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  In the search bar, enter keywords or a query. For example:
    - "What was the deadline for project X?"
    - "Find messages from Alice about the Q3 review."
    - "Show me attachments from the design sprint space."
3.  Click Search .
    - Or, you can press **Enter**.
4.  At the top of your search results, review the AI Overview.

**Tip:** For the best AI Overview, provide as much detail as you can in your search description.

## Expand or collapse an AI Overview

When you get a summary, you can expand or collapse the AI Overview.

- To get the full summary, click **Show more**.
- To collapse the overview, click Collapse .

## Give feedback about an AI Overview in Google Chat

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can let us know by submitting feedback. Your feedback can help improve AI-assisted Workspace features and broader Google efforts in AI.

Because feedback may be human readable, do not submit data that contains personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

1.  At the top right of the generated output, select Good summary or Bad summary .
2.  In the pop-up window, you can select the data you want to share along with your feedback, with additional context and output. If you don’t want to share certain data, you can uncheck it.
    - If you select Bad summary : To select the issue and enter additional feedback, select Next.
3.  Select **Submit**.

To provide general feedback on this feature, select Help Help & Feedback and then **Send feedback to Google**. [Learn how to send feedback in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13985546).

To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).
