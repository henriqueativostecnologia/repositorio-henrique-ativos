# Use Smart Compose in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

With Smart Compose, powered by machine learning, you can write messages faster. Smart Compose offers suggestions as you enter text.

**Important:**

- Smart Compose is available in English, Spanish, French, Italian, and Portuguese. Smart Compose is not designed to provide answers and may not always predict factually correct information.
- To use Smart Compose, you must turn on smart features and controls. Learn more about [Smart features & controls in Google products](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/10079371).

### Use Smart Compose in Chat

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click Settings Settings .
3.  Under “Smart Compose,” check the box next to "Enable predictive writing suggestions as you compose a message on web and desktop.”

### Use Smart Compose in Chat for Gmail

1.  On your computer, go to [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click Settings Settings and then **See all settings**.
3.  At the top, click **Chat and Meet** and then **Manage chat settings**.
4.  Under “Smart Compose,” check the box next to "Enable predictive writing suggestions as you compose a message on web and desktop.”

**Tip:** To accept a suggestion, on your keyboard, press **Tab**.

### Learn about machine learning

As language understanding models use billions of common phrases and sentences to automatically learn about the world, they can also reflect human cognitive biases.

Being aware of this is a good start, and the conversation around how to handle it is ongoing.

Google is committed to making products that work well for everyone, and are actively researching unintended bias and mitigation strategies.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

With Smart Compose, powered by machine learning, you can write messages faster. Smart Compose offers suggestions as you enter text.

**Important:**

- To use Smart Compose, you must turn on smart features and controls. Learn more about [Smart features & controls in Google products](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/10079371).

### Use Smart Compose in Chat

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Under "General," turn on **Smart Compose**.

[Go to Chat settings](https://chat.google.com/settings)

### Use Smart Compose in Chat for Gmail

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gmail app .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Select your email account.
4.  Under "General," check the box next to "Smart Compose in Chat."

**Tip:** To accept a suggestion, when you enter a text, swipe right.
