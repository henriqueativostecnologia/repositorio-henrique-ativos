# Add or remove someone in a direct message

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To add someone to a direct message (DM) with two or more people in Google Chat, add them to the member list or mention them in the message.

### Add people to a DM

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Open a DM.
3.  At the top, click the conversation name and then **Start a new chat**.
4.  Enter a name or email address.
5.  Click **Create**.

**Tips:**

- To quickly add a new person to a DM with 2 or more people, in the reply area, enter @ followed by the person’s name.
- Or, next to conversation name, click the dropdown and then **Add people**.

### Remove people from a DM

**Important**: When you remove someone from a DM, they can't:

- Participate in the conversation.
- Check message history.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Open a DM.
3.  At the top, next to conversation name, click the dropdown and then **View members**.
4.  Next to the person’s name, click More options and then **Remove from conversation**.

### Manage members in a DM created from Calendar

With continuous meeting chat, you can send messages to meeting participants before, during and after a meeting in Google Chat from a computer or mobile device. Continuous meeting chat is available to all business and enterprise users. [Learn how to use Chat with Google Meet](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308979).

You can use the dedicated conversations from your meetings to:

- Remind or prepare attendees for the meeting.
- Chat during the meeting.
- Access the conversation through Chat after the meeting has ended. For users in the same organization as the meeting host, after the meeting has ended, you can access the conversation through Chat.

When you create a DM from a Google Calendar invite, it links to the event's invite list. This happens when you:

- Select “Chat with Guests” from the Calendar invite.
- Access the calendar reminder in the Chat roster.
- Send the first message in Meet when continuous meeting chat is enabled.

You can add or remove members during or after a meeting.

- **Before or during a meeting:** To add or remove someone from a DM created from a Calendar event, update the Calendar invite. You can’t update the members through the DM in Chat.
- **After a meeting:** You can add or remove members directly within the Chat DM.

When you use the “Chat with Guests” option to create a DM from Calendar, third-party (3P) or external users aren’t included. When they join the meeting, they can find the messages sent after the time they joined. They have access to these messages only from within the meeting chat, and have no access once the meeting ends.

**Tip:** The host can turn off continuous meeting chat in the settings under “Chat Moderation.” [Learn how to use Chat with Google Meet](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308979).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To add someone to a direct message (DM) with two or more people in Google Chat, add them to the member list or mention them in the message.

### Add people to a DM

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail**: At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Direct messages .
3.  Open a DM.
4.  At the top, tap the conversation name.
5.  Tap **Manage members**.
6.  Tap Add people .
7.  Enter a name or email address and then tap **Done**.

**Tips**:

- To convert a DM with one person into a group DM, at the top, tap the conversation name and then **Start a new chat**.
- To quickly add a new person to a DM with two or more people, in the reply area, enter **@** followed by the person’s name.
