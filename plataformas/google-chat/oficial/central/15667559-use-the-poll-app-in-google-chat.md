# Use the Poll app in Google Chat

You can quickly gather team input and make decisions with Google Chat's Poll app.

**Important:**

- If you use a work or school account, your admin manages the apps you can access in Chat, such as the Poll app.
- If you’re an admin, [learn how to allow users to install Chat apps](https://support.google.com/a/answer/7651360).

## Create a poll

You can create a poll in direct messages, group messages, or spaces and people can vote on it.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click Chat .
2.  **Add the Poll app:**
    1.  Select a direct message, group message, or space.
    2.  In the reply field, enter `/poll` or `@poll`.
    3.  Select the Poll app.
        - If you use the app for the first time, in the dialogue box, click **Add app & send**.
3.  **Create a poll:** In the dialogue box, enter a question and up to 4 choices:
    - **Optional:**
      - To add up to 6 more choices, click **Add more choices**.
      - Under “Advanced options”:
        1.  Check the box next to "Allow multiple votes."
        2.  Check the box next to "Hide name of participants."
        3.  To set a voting deadline, select **Set voting deadline**. Then, choose a date and time.
      - To translate your poll:
        1.  Click **Change poll language**.
        2.  Select the language.
4.  Click **Create**.

The set closing date and time appears in the poll message. A private message notifies you when your poll closes.

## Edit a poll

After creating a new poll, you receive a private message. This message includes an **"Edit poll"** button. You can use this button to modify your poll.

## Vote in a poll

When someone creates a poll, you receive a chat notification.

- To vote in a poll, next to your preferred choice, click **Vote**.
- To find more details about the poll results, click **Show results**.
