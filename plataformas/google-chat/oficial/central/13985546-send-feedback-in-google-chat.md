# Send feedback in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can send feedback in Google Chat to:

- Give feature requests and suggestions.
- Report something that doesn’t work, such as errors or product bugs.

Your comments can help us improve your experience.

### Give feedback in Google Chat

**Important:** To help us understand your feedback, include details and a screenshot. The more info you include in your feedback, the more helpful it is for us.

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](http://chat.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Help Help & Feedback   and then ** Send feedback to Google**.
3.  Click **Report an issue** or **Suggest an idea**.
4.  Describe your issue or suggestion.
5.  Optional: To add a screenshot, click **Capture screenshot**. 
    - To highlight or hide parts of the screenshot, click **Highlight or Hide Info**.
    - If you don't want to include the screenshot, click Remove screenshot .
6.  Click **Send**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can send feedback in Google Chat to:

- Give feature requests and suggestions.
- Report something that doesn’t work, such as errors or product bugs.

Your comments can help us improve your experience.

### Give feedback in Google Chat

**Important**: 

- To help us understand your feedback, include details and a screenshot. The more info you include in your feedback, the more helpful it is for us.
- To include device logs, when prompted, tap **Allow one-time access**. These logs help Google when you report a technical issue. [Learn how to manage device logs on Android](https://support.google.com/android/answer/12986432).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Chat app .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu   and then **Send feedback ** and then ** Get started**.
3.  Tap **Report an issue** or **Make a suggestion**.
4.  Describe your issue or suggestion.
    - A screenshot of the last used screen is taken automatically.
5.  Optional: Choose if you want to include a screenshot. 
    - To highlight or hide parts of the screenshot, tap **Highlight or hide info**.
    - If you don’t want to include the screenshot, tap Remove the attached screenshot .
6.  Confirm if you want us to email you for more information or updates.
7.  Tap **Continue**.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can send feedback in Google Chat to:

- Give feature requests and suggestions.
- Report something that doesn’t work, such as errors or product bugs.

Your comments can help us improve your experience.

### Give feedback in Google Chat

**Important**: To help us understand your feedback, include details and a screenshot. The more info you include in your feedback, the more helpful it is for us.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Chat app .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu   and then **Send feedback**.
3.  Describe your issue or suggestion.
4.  Optional: Choose if you want to include a screenshot. 
    - To highlight or hide parts of the screenshot, tap **Screenshot**.
    - If you don’t want to include the screenshot, tap the checkmark next to “Screenshot.”
5.  Optional: Choose if you want to include iOS system logs.
6.  Confirm if you want us to email you for more information or updates.
7.  Tap Send .
