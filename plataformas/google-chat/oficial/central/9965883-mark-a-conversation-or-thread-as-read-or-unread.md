# Mark a conversation or thread as read or unread

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In Google Chat, you can mark conversations as unread to return to them later. You can also mark a conversation as read without opening it.

When you mark a conversation as unread, a dot shows up next to it in your conversation list.

### Change a conversation to read or unread

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.

2.  In the navigation panel or "Home," next to the conversation or thread, click More options .
3.  Click Mark as read or Mark as unread .

**Tips:** Other ways to mark a conversation as read or unread include:

- At the top of a DM or space, click the conversation name and then **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**.
- For individual messages in a conversation, point to the message and then click More actions and then **Mark as unread**. This adds an “Unread” line above the message in the conversation.

### Locate your unread conversations

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.

2.  On the left, click **Home**.
3.  At the top right, click **Unread**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Chat, you can mark conversations as unread to return to them later. You can also mark a conversation as read without opening it.

When you mark a conversation as unread, a dot shows up next to it in your conversation list.

### Change a conversation to read or unread

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .

    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .

2.  In the navigation panel or "Home," next to the conversation or thread, tap More options .
3.  Tap **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**.

**Tips:** Other ways to mark a conversation as read or unread include:

- At the top of a DM or space, tap the conversation name and then **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**.
- For individual messages in a conversation, tap and hold the message and then **Mark as unread**. This adds an “Unread” line above the message in the conversation.

### Locate your unread conversations

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .

    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .

2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Home .
3.  At the top right, tap **Unread**.
