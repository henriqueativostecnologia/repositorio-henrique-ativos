# I’m not getting Google Chat notifications

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you don’t get notifications, you may need to update your device settings or check your Google Chat notification settings.

### Understand app notifications

Some Google Chat applications can adjust notification settings, to send updates, including::

- **@notify** messages: These messages are for critical or time-sensitive information. They work like @all messages to get immediate attention and ignore your “Do Not Disturb” status, muted conversations, or notification settings.
- **@silent** messages: These messages are for low-priority updates. They minimize disruption by working like system messages.

**Tip:** Only applications or bots can send @silent or @notify messages. You can't send these message types.

### I don't get any notifications

If you don’t get notifications, turn on notifications in your computer’s operating system and browser.

#### Allow notifications in your computer’s operating system

- Each computer operating system has different steps to turn on notifications. For more info, check your operating system’s online help.
  - [Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-qa/guide/mac-help/mh40583/mac)
  - [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/3220216)
  - [Windows](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/notifications-and-do-not-disturb-in-windows-feeca47f-0baf-5680-16f0-8801db1a8466)
- Some computer operating systems have settings that mute notifications. If you don’t get Google Chat notifications, check your operating system’s online help and make sure notifications aren’t muted.

**Tip:** Older Windows versions may have different notification settings.

#### Turn on Chat notifications from the web

To make sure you receive Chat notifications, you may need to enable them from your Google Chat or Gmail settings on the web. This process involves granting both browser notification and idle detection permissions through a single prompt.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or Gmail .
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then Notifications .
    - In Gmail, click Settings and then See all settings and then Chat and Meet and then Manage chat settings.
3.  Under "Desktop notifications," you will find the "Allow chat notifications" toggle.
    - If this toggle is off, turn it on.
    - If this toggle is already on, but you are not receiving notifications, you may see a **Turn on** button. Click this button.
4.  In the browser permission dialog that appears, click **Allow** to grant both browser-level notifications and idle detection permissions for Google Chat.

#### Understand and manage idle detection for notifications

Idle detection permission helps Google Chat ensure notifications are sent to your correct active device, reducing duplicate notifications across multiple devices.

To grant idle detection permission when browser notifications are already active:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or Gmail .
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then **Manage Chat settings**.
3.  Under "Desktop notifications," if prompted to turn on idle detection separately, click **Turn on**.
4.  Follow the prompts from your browser to grant idle detection permission.

#### Allow Google Chat notifications in Google Chrome

1.  On your computer, open Chrome Chrome .
2.  At the top right, click More and then Settings .
3.  On the left, click Privacy and security and then Site Settings .
4.  Scroll to **Permissions** and then select Notifications .
5.  Find "https://chat.google.com:443" and "https://mail.google.com:443."
6.  Follow the steps listed in the table below under "Steps to turn on notification permissions for both of these URLs."

### I get notifications sometimes

If you occasionally miss Google Chat notifications on your computer, follow these steps.

#### Close the mobile app

Close the Google Chat or Gmail mobile app. When an app is open, notifications show up there and don’t show up when you’re:

- Online at [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com/).
- In the Chat desktop app.
- In Chat in Gmail.

#### Find out where you get notifications

Check where you receive notifications.

If you’re using chat.google.com:

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then Notifications .
3.  Under "Desktop notifications," check if the "Show an example" option shows.
    - If you can find “Show an example,” you get Google Chat web notifications at chat.google.com.
    - If you can’t find “Show an example,” you get Google Chat notifications in another browser tab or in the Chat app itself.

If you’re using Gmail:

1.  On your computer, open [Gmail.](https://gmail.google.com/)
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then **See all settings**.
3.  At the top, click the **Chat and Meet** tab.

[Go to Chat and Meet settings](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/chat)

4.  Next to "Chat settings," click **Manage chat settings**.
5.  Under "Desktop notifications," check if the "Show an example" option shows.
    - If you can find “Show an example,” you get Google Chat web notifications in Gmail.
    - If you can’t find “Show an example,” you get Google Chat notifications in another browser tab or in the Chat app itself.

### I don't get notifications for a specific space

Make sure notifications are turned on for spaces. [Learn how to manage thread notifications](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/12176589).

**Tip:** Notification settings for spaces and group direct messages are the same on the web and your mobile device. If you change notification settings for a space or group direct message on the web, the same settings apply on your mobile app.

### I don't get notifications for a specific 1:1 or group direct message

Make sure notifications are turned on for the direct message. [Learn how to Turn Google Chat notifications on or off](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655718?co=GENIE.Platform%3DComputer#notifications_direct_message).

**Tip:** Notification settings for direct messages are the same in the web and mobile versions of Google Chat. If you change notification settings for a direct message on the web, the same settings apply on your mobile app.

### I don't get any notifications

If you don't get notifications, check the notification settings on your mobile device.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open "Settings" on your device.
2.  Tap **Apps** and then **Chat** and then **Notifications**.
3.  Make sure app notifications are turned on.
4.  Check if [Google Chat app notifications are also turned on](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655718).

**Tip:** The settings menu on your device might have a different name for “Apps & notifications.” For more info on how to access app notification settings, check your device’s online help.

### I get notifications sometimes

On your computer, close active Gmail and Google Chat browser tabs. When you’re in a browser, notifications show up there instead of the mobile app. If you’re not active in Google Chat, notifications show up on all devices signed in to your Google Account.
