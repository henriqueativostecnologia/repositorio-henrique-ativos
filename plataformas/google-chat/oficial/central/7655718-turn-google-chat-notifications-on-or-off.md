# Turn Google Chat notifications on or off

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Notifications appear on the devices you used most recently. If you’re not active in Chat, notifications appear on all devices signed in to your Google Account.

By default, you receive notifications when:

- Someone @ mentions you
- Someone reacts or responds to your message

If you mute a specific conversation or set your Chat status to “[Do not disturb](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9093489),” you won’t receive notifications.

In this article, learn how to:

- [Turn notifications on or off](#on_or_off).
- [Customize notifications for specific conversations & spaces](#direct_messages).
- [Customize notification sounds](#notification_sounds).
- [Get notifications in the Chat mobile app](#mobile-app).
- [Update device or browser notification settings for Chat on a computer](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655718#update-notification-settings).

**Tips:**

- If email notifications are on, you get email reminders for Chat messages you haven’t read for more than 12 hours.
- If you have multiple Google Accounts, you can set different notification settings for each account.
- Some Google Chat apps can ignore your “Do Not Disturb” status or notification settings, to send updates, including:
  - **@notify** messages: These messages are for critical or time-sensitive information. They work like @all messages to get immediate attention and ignore your “Do Not Disturb” status, muted conversations, or notification settings.
  - **@silent** messages: These messages are for low-priority updates. They minimize disruption by working like system messages.

### Turn Google Chat notifications on or off 

##### Turn on notifications

##### Turn on notifications in Google Chat

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then Notifications .
3.  To turn on notifications for Google Chat messages, under "Desktop notifications," check the box next to "Allow chat notifications."

**Tips:**

- If you don’t get Google Chat notifications after you turn it on, [allow Google Chat notifications in your operating system and browser](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9169357#zippy=%2Ci-dont-get-any-notifications).
- To turn email notifications for unread messages on or off, under "Email notifications," select **Only @mentions & direct messages** or **Off**.

##### Manage combined notification permissions

Web users can grant browser notifications and idle detection permissions through a single flow. This occurs in two ways:

- **Enabled but blocked:** If "Allow chat notifications" is enabled but the browser blocks notifications, a "Turn on" button appears. Click it to trigger permission dialogs.
- **Disabled toggle:** If "Allow chat notifications" is disabled, turning on the toggle triggers permission dialogs.

### Customize Google Chat notifications for spaces & direct messages

##### Customize notifications for a space with in-line threading

By default, only threads you follow are bolded and moved to the top of your conversation list. To control how thread updates show in Chat, customize your notification settings.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Next to the space name, click More options Down Arrow and then **Notifications**.
3.  Select an option for notifications:
    - **All:** Notifications for all new messages and replies to threads.
      - **Tip:** If you select "All," it automatically follows all new threads for that space. To unfollow a thread, click Active Threads and then **Following**.
    - **Main conversations:** Notifications for all new main conversations, @mentions, and replies to threads you follow.
    - **For you:** Notifications for @mentions and replies to threads you follow.
    - **None:** No notifications, just an indicator for @mentions.
4.  Optional: To prevent the conversation from being bolded and moved to the top of your conversation list, select the box next to "Mute conversation."
5.  Click **Save**.

##### Turn notifications on or off for a direct message

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Next to the direct message or group DM, click More options and then **Notifications**.
    - For group DMs, you can select to get notifications for:
      - **All:** All new messages
      - **For you:** Only @mentions
      - **None**
3.  Select **All** or **None**.
4.  Optional: To prevent the conversation from being bolded and moved to the top of your conversation list, select the box next to "Mute conversation."

### Grant idle detection permissions

Idle detection delivers notifications to your active device. Granting this permission helps reliably send notifications and pauses mobile alerts when you are active on desktop.

If you are idle on Gmail or Google Chat on your computer, notifications go to your mobile device. You are considered idle if you’re inactive for over 30 seconds, even if you still have a web browser open.

For Chrome users, you can adjust settings to receive chat notifications on mobile only when you’re inactive on Chrome.

#### Respond to the permission prompt

When you enable Chat notifications, a dialog may appear. This dialog asks you to "Pause mobile notifications when you are active on this device." Click **Continue** to grant idle detection permission.

#### Adjust Chrome settings manually

If the permission dialog doesn’t appear, you can manually enable idle detection in your Chrome settings.

1.  Go to `chrome://settings/content/idleDetection.`
2.  Check the box for "Sites can ask to know when you're actively using your device."
3.  Under "Customized behaviors," add `mail.google.com` or `chat.google.com` to the allowed sites list.
4.  To add a site, click **Add**.

### Update device or browser notification settings

##### Update device notifications

Each computer operating system has different steps to turn on notifications. For more info, go to your operating system’s online help.

Some computer operating systems have settings that mute notifications. If you don’t get the notifications, make sure notifications aren’t muted.

##### Update browser notifications

To turn notifications on or off, you might need to update your browser notification settings. [Learn how to allow Google Chat notifications](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9169357).

### Troubleshoot issues with Google Chat notifications

##### Prevent multiple notifications

To avoid duplicate mobile notifications from the Gmail and Chat apps, in your device’s settings, turn off Google Chat app notifications.

1.  On your device, open the Settings app.
2.  Tap **Notifications** and then **App settings** and then **Chat**.
    - If you don't find "Chat," update the list to show all apps:
      - At the top, tap the **Down arrow** and then **All apps**.
3.  Turn “All Chat notifications” off.

**Tip:** For more info, visit [stop extra chat notifications](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9058318).

##### Not getting notifications

If you don’t get notifications, visit [I’m not getting Google Chat notifications](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9169357).

### Get notifications in the Google Chat mobile app

When the Google Chat mobile app is open, you get notifications in the app.

You won’t get notifications:

- Online at [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com/)
- In the Chat desktop app
- In Chat in Gmail

When the Chat app is open, you get the message "You're receiving Chat notifications here.” [Learn more about the Chat standalone app](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9455386).

**Important:** When iPhone or iPad users get messages from you, your profile picture could also appear on the notifications. [Learn how to change your profile picture](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/35529).
