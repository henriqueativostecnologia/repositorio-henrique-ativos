# Get started with Ask Gemini in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Feature availability**

- This feature is currently only available in English and requires an eligible Google Workspace subscription. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).
- [Learn about supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14925782)**.**
- You may not have access to some or all of the Ask Gemini features.
  - **Note:** Ask Gemini requires Gemini in Workspace in Chat and Workspace Intelligence to be turned on at the admin level for all apps.

You can manage your day and find information more efficiently with Ask Gemini in Google Chat. This AI-powered assistant:

- Finds quick answers from scattered information across your Google Workspace data
- Summarizes updates on key topics across Chat spaces
- Identifies daily priorities

**Important:** To use these features, you must enroll in Workspace Smart Features. [Learn about smart features & controls for Google Workspace & other Google products](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/15604322).

### Find Ask Gemini

You can connect with Gemini in Chat in a few different ways.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.

2.  Find Ask Gemini .

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Where to find Ask Gemini in Chat**                                          | **Steps**                                                                     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Shortcuts or Conversation list                                                | On the left, under “Shortcuts,” click Ask Gemini .                            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Keyboard shortcut                                                             | -   **ChromeOS and Windows:** **Ctrl** + **g**                                |
|                                                                               | -   **macOS:** **Command** + **g**                                            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Search bar                                                                    | To the right of the search bar, click the Ask Gemini button.                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

### Use Ask Gemini to start and use Sessions

You can manage your conversations with Ask Gemini by starting new sessions to keep discussions focused on single topics. Sessions help organize your interactions and improve the clarity of Gemini's responses.

To start or open a session:

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  **To start a new session**:
    - In the message field, enter a new question or prompt.
    - Select one of the suggested topics displayed at the top of the chat interface.

3.  **To open a past session**: Select the desired session from your recent conversations.

When a session opens, all chats from that conversation will be displayed and stored together.

### Use Ask Gemini to get daily briefs

You can get summaries of your day's to-do items and things to follow up on from Ask Gemini that are sent to you automatically. This feature is on by default.

**Tip:** This feature is available through Gemini Alpha ([for work accounts only, and enabled by your organization](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/workspace-with-gemini/turn-access-to-google-workspace-with-gemini-alpha-on-or-off?visit_id=639173179550847503-2702113336&rd=1)), and Google Workspace Experiments. The Google Workspace Experiments is a trusted tester program that users with personal accounts can use to try new AI features. [Learn more about Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).

To access your daily brief:

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  Locate the most recent session called "Daily brief" from Ask Gemini. Your daily brief will appear with the date, a summary of your daily tasks and events, and links to related resources.
    1.  **Note:** You can access your daily brief from the Chat home screen, the Ask Gemini conversation thread, or the Ask Gemini home screen within Chat.

3.  **Optional:** To unsubscribe from daily briefs, follow the prompt at the bottom of a brief.

### Use Ask Gemini to answer specific questions

You can find information and get a unified knowledge base across the entire workspace or search the web with Ask Gemini. Since Gemini keeps your context across conversations, you don’t need to provide specific details every time you ask questions.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  In the message field, enter a prompt. For example:
    - “Find me the deck about \[Project Name\] that \[Person’s Name\] shared with me last week.”
    - “Give me the latest update on \[Example Topic\].”
    - “What did \[Person's Name\] ask me last week about \[Example Topic\] and when am I meeting them next?”

3.  Click Send .

**Tip:** We include citations to note the source of the Ask Gemini’s information.

### Use Ask Gemini to prepare for your day

You can ask Gemini for your action items at any time, and it’s based on the most recent information.

To ask for your action items:

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  In the message field, enter a prompt. For example, “What are my action items?”

3.  Click Send .

### Use Ask Gemini to draft communications

You can use Ask Gemini to draft email or chat responses.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  In the message field, enter a prompt. For example:
    - “Draft an email to \[Person’s Name\] summarizing our progress on \[Topic Name\].”
    - “Refine the following chat message I am sending to \[Person’s Name\]: \[Paste your draft here\]. Make sure it’s professional and concise.”

3.  Click Send .

### Use Ask Gemini to generate a Google Docs document

You can use Ask Gemini to create a Google Docs document.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  In the message field, enter a prompt. For example:
    - “Create a meeting notes agenda.”
    - “Generate a draft with a response to this email.”
    - “Create a quick project coordination doc with a 30/60/90 plan section.”

3.  Click Send .

### Use Ask Gemini to generate a Google Slides presentation

You can use Ask Gemini to create a fully editable presentation in Google Slides with a single prompt.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  In the message field, describe the presentation you want to create. For example: “Create a slide on quantum computing for a general audience with a modern design.”

3.  Optional: To give your presentation the same style as an existing slide deck, you can use @\<presentation name\> to reference it. For example: “Create a presentation based on @old_project.”

4.  Click Send .
    - **Tip:** Keep the tab open until Gemini generates your presentation; this may take up to several minutes. You can switch to other tabs or windows but do not close the existing tab.

5.  To refine your presentation plan, you can answer Gemini’s follow-up questions. You may also find recommended sources that you can add for Gemini to use as it generates your presentation.

6.  Click **Next**.
    - Keep the tab open until Gemini generates your presentation, which may take up to several minutes. You can switch to other tabs or windows, but do not close the existing tab.
    - Once the presentation is generated, you can open it in Google Slides to view and edit. [Learn how to generate presentations with Gemini in Google Slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/17111393).

### Use Ask Gemini with third-party integrations

**Important:** Before you begin, you may need to ask your administrator to install the third-party integrations from the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/category/integrations).

You can use Ask Gemini to connect with third-party apps to manage tasks, find information, and automate workflows across Google Workspace and external tools.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .
    - [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17036303#find-gemini).
2.  In the message field, type `@` followed by the third-party integration name. For example, “@HubSpot,” “@Asana,” “@Salesforce.”
    - Or, you can enter the full name of the app in your prompt without the use of @.
3.  Enter your prompt. For example:
    - “Summarize my Asana tickets.”
    - “@Salesforce find customer information for \[Customer Name\].”
    - “@Hubspot create a new lead for \[Lead details\].” or “Create a new lead for \[Lead details\] in Hubspot.”
4.  Click Send .

**Tips:**

- Ask Gemini only connects to the third-party integrations you have successfully connected in the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace). If you encounter an authorization error, you must authorize the app through the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace).
- When you use Ask Gemini, it cites the third-party app as a source for the information it provides. It often includes a link to the source in the app.

### Use Ask Gemini to invoke skills

**Important:** Before you can use a skill in Chat, you must create the skill and turn it on in [Workspace Studio](https://studio.workspace.google.com).

To analyze chat conversations, use skills in Ask Gemini in Google Chat.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .
    - [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17036303).
2.  In the message field, type `@` followed by the skill name. For example, “@Project Summary.”
3.  Enter your prompt.
4.  Click Send .

**Key use case:** To analyze recent chat conversations within a particular chat space and generate a structured status update, use a “Project Summary” skill.

### Use Ask Gemini to use Deep Research

You can use Ask Gemini to run complex, multi-step research tasks about any topic. Gemini synthesizes information from the web and any sources you include to create comprehensive research reports with cited sources. [Learn more about Deep Research in Gemini](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/17127708).

##### Start a Deep Research report

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .
    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17036303#find-gemini).
2.  In the message field, enter a prompt. Explain what you want to research. For example, "Deep research: Research current renewable energy trends and compare them with my project drafts in Google Drive."
3.  Click **Send** .

##### Add sources and review your research plan

After you submit your prompt, a "Deep research sources" card shows in your chat. Ask Gemini uses these sources to base its research.

1.  In the "Deep research sources" card, select one or more options:
    - Drive: Analyze files and folders in your Google Drive.
    - Gmail: Include info from your emails.
    - Chat: Include info from your chat messages.
    - Web: Search the public web for the latest info.
2.  **Optional:** To choose specific sources for your research, click **Add sources** and select files from your Google Drive.
3.  Click **Next**.
    - Ask Gemini creates a research plan for your topic with the questions the report aims to answer.
    - To update the research plan before you create a report, click **Customize**.
4.  Click **Start research**.

##### Track your research

- While you work, Ask Gemini researches your topic based on the plan.
- It can take some time to generate your report. You can close Ask Gemini, start a new chat, or switch to a different Google Workspace document. The research task continues in the background.
- When the research is complete:
  - Ask Gemini generates the report in a Google Docs document.
  - You get an email about your plan’s completion.

##### Open your report

1.  In your conversation with Ask Gemini, click **Open doc**.
    - A new Google Docs document opens in a new tab.
    - It includes the findings, citations, and an organized summary of the data found.
    - The report automatically saves to your Google Drive.
2.  In the document, you can ask follow-up questions about the report.
    - You can also use Ask Gemini to refine specific sections.

1.  Below the generated output, select Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .

3.  Select **Submit**.

To provide general feedback about Google Chat, at the top, click Help Help & Feedback and then **Send feedback to Google**.

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .
    - [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17036303#find-gemini).
2.  In the message field, type `@` followed by the third-party integration name. For example, “@HubSpot,” “@Asana,” “@Salesforce.”
    - Or, you can enter the full name of the app in your prompt without the use of @.
3.  Enter your prompt. For example:
    - “Summarize my Asana tickets.”
    - “@Salesforce find customer information for \[Customer Name\].”
    - “@Hubspot create a new lead for \[Lead details\].” or “Create a new lead for \[Lead details\] in Hubspot.”
4.  Tap Send .

**Tips:**

- Ask Gemini only connects to the third-party integrations you have successfully connected in the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace). If you encounter an authorization error, you must authorize the app through the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace).
- When you use Ask Gemini, it cites the third-party app as a source for the information it provides. It often includes a link to the source in the app.

### Use Ask Gemini to use Deep Research

You can use Ask Gemini to run complex, multi-step research tasks about any topic. Gemini synthesizes information from the web and any sources you include to create comprehensive research reports with cited sources. [Learn more about Deep Research in Gemini](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/17127708).

##### Start a Deep Research report

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .
    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/17036303#find-gemini).
2.  In the message field, enter a prompt. Explain what you want to research. For example, "Deep research: Research current renewable energy trends and compare them with my project drafts in Google Drive."
3.  Click **Send** .

##### Add sources and review your research plan

After you submit your prompt, a "Deep research sources" card shows in your chat. Ask Gemini uses these sources to base its research.

1.  In the "Deep research sources" card, select one or more options:
    - Drive: Analyze files and folders in your Google Drive.
    - Gmail: Include info from your emails.
    - Chat: Include info from your chat messages.
    - Web: Search the public web for the latest info.
2.  **Optional:** To choose specific sources for your research, click **Add sources** and select files from your Google Drive.
3.  Click **Next**.
    - Ask Gemini creates a research plan for your topic with the questions the report aims to answer.
    - To update the research plan before you create a report, click **Customize**.
4.  Click **Start research**.

##### Track your research

- While you work, Ask Gemini researches your topic based on the plan.
- It can take some time to generate your report. You can close Ask Gemini, start a new chat, or switch to a different Google Workspace document. The research task continues in the background.
- When the research is complete:
  - Ask Gemini generates the report in a Google Docs document.
  - You get an email about your plan’s completion.

##### Open your report

1.  In your conversation with Ask Gemini, click **Open doc**.
    - A new Google Docs document opens in a new tab.
    - It includes the findings, citations, and an organized summary of the data found.
    - The report automatically saves to your Google Drive.
2.  In the document, you can ask follow-up questions about the report.
    - You can also use Ask Gemini to refine specific sections.

### Use Ask Gemini to start and use Sessions

You can manage your conversations with Ask Gemini by starting new sessions to keep discussions focused on single topics. Sessions help organize your interactions and improve the clarity of Gemini's responses.

To start or open a session:

1.  On your iOS device, open Google Chat.

2.  Find Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

3.  **To start a new session**:
    1.  In the message field, enter a new question or prompt.
    2.  Select one of the suggested topics displayed at the top of the chat interface.

4.  **To open a past session**: Select the desired session from your recent conversations.

When a session opens, all chats from that conversation will be displayed and stored together.

You can get summaries of your day's to-do items and things to follow up on from Ask Gemini that are sent to you automatically. This feature is on by default.

The Google Workspace Experiments is a trusted tester program that users with personal accounts can use to try new AI features. [Learn more about Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).

To access your daily brief:

1.  In Google Chat, open Ask Gemini .

    **Tip:** [Learn where to find Ask Gemini in Google Chat](#find-gemini).

2.  Locate the most recent session called "Daily brief" from Ask Gemini. Your daily brief will appear with the date, a summary of your daily tasks and events, and links to related resources.
    1.  **Note:** You can access your daily brief from the Chat home screen, the Ask Gemini conversation thread, or the Ask Gemini home screen within Chat.

3.  **Optional:** To unsubscribe from daily briefs, follow the prompt at the bottom of a brief.
