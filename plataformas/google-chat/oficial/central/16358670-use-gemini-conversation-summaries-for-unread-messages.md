# Use Gemini conversation summaries for unread messages

To catch up on important discussions in a space in Google Chat, you can use Gemini to summarize unread messages.

**Feature availability**

- This feature is available as part of Gemini Beta.
- Your administrator controls access to Gemini Beta. If you can’t find these features, your administrator may not have [Beta features enabled for your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14170809).
- This feature is also available as part of our early access testing program, [Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).

## Summarize unread messages in a conversation

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](http://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](http://gmail.google.com/).
2.  Choose a conversation with unread messages that you want to summarize.
3.  Click **Summarize unread**.
    - You can find a summary list of the recent messages that you haven’t read under the "Recent discussions" section.

      **Tip:** For messages with many unread messages, summaries will automatically expand without clicking.
4.  Click **Close summary**.

## Give feedback about Gemini conversation summaries

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can submit feedback. Your feedback can help improve AI-assisted Workspace features and broader Google efforts in AI.

Because feedback may be human readable, do not submit data that contains personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

1.  At the top right of the generated output, select Good summary or Bad summary .
2.  In the pop-up window, you can select the data you want to share along with your feedback, with additional context and output. If you don’t want to share certain data, you can uncheck it.
    - If you select Bad summary : To select the issue and enter additional feedback, select **Next**.
3.  Select **Submit**.

To provide general feedback on this feature, select Help Help & Feedback and then **Send feedback to Google**. [Learn how to send feedback in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13985546).

To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).
