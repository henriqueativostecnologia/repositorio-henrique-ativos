# Use the Feeds app in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To get messages about updates from news, blog posts, or other content through the Atom or RSS data format, use the Feeds app in Chat. The integration sends new posts from the feed to Chat.

### Add Feeds to a direct message or space

1.  On your computer, open [Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  To find the Feeds app, enter: `/Feeds`.
    1.  Or, on the left side of the compose bar, click Add .
    2.  Enter: `/Feeds`.
3.  Select **Manage your subscriptions**.
    - The Feeds module pops up.
4.  In the text box, under “Add a subscription,” enter the subscription URL.
5.  Click the **Add Subscription** button.

**Tips:**

- You need to set up subscriptions in each space where you add the app.
- The app sends notifications to all spaces where it's a member and it’s configured.

### Edit or delete Feeds subscriptions

1.  On your computer, open [Chat](https://chat.google.com/).

2.  Open a direct message or go to a space where you have a feed set up.

3.  Edit or delete a subscription.

    - **To edit:**
      1.  Open the **Manage Subscriptions Module**.
      2.  Next to the subscription you want to edit, click **Edit** and then **Save Subscription**.

    Or, you can:

    > 1.  Enter: `/Feeds`.
    > 2.  Select **Manage your subscriptions**.
    > 3.  In one of the feed outputs, click More and then **Manage Subscription**.

    - **To delete:** Next to the subscription you want to delete, tap Delete .

**Tips:**

- If you remove the app from a space, it deletes all the space’s subscriptions.
- Only the person who created the subscription can edit or delete the Feeds. The space manager can delete it, but not update.
