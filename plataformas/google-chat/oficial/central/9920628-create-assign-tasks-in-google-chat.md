# Create & assign tasks in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

As a member of a space in Google Chat, you can create and manage group tasks and assign tasks to other space members. You can view tasks in the space where they're created. If a task is assigned to you, it appears in your personal task list in Google Tasks. When someone creates or updates a task, a notification appears in the space.

**Tips:**

- If you use a work or school account and don't have the "Tasks" tab in a space, contact your Google Workspace administrator to turn on this feature.
- When a space becomes too large, you can no longer:
  - Assign tasks
  - Mention anyone through "@"

### Create & assign a task in a space

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail**: At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Spaces and then select the space.
3.  On the top, tap the **Tasks** tab.
4.  At the bottom right, tap New task .
5.  Enter a title for the task.
6.  Optional: To add a description or more details, tap Details and enter the information.
7.  Optional: To add a date, tap Google Calendar and then select a date and then tap **Done**.
    **Tip**: If you add a date to your task, you get notifications at the scheduled dates.
8.  Optional: To assign the task to a member of the space, tap Person and then tap the name of the person. To assign the task to someone who isn't a member of the space, invite them to the space first.
9.  Tap **Save**.

**Important:** When you delete a task, it’s deleted for all members of the space and from the assignee’s personal task list.

### Edit a task

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the bottom, tap Spaces and then select the space.
3.  At the top, tap the **Tasks** tab and then select the task.
4.  Tap the title, details, date, or assignee and make the changes.
5.  Tap Back .

**Tips:** If you have a task in your personal task list:

- You can edit the task title, details, and date from your personal task list.
- Changes update the task for everyone in the space and a notification displays.

### Mark a task complete

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  On the top, tap the **Tasks** tab and then select the task.
3.  At the top right, tap Mark as complete .

**Tips:** If you have a task in your personal task list:

- You can edit the task title, details, and date from your personal task list.
- Changes update the task for everyone in the space and a notification displays.

### Mark a task incomplete

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  On the bottom, tap Spaces and then select the space.
3.  On the top, tap the **Tasks** tab.
4.  Next to "Completed," tap Expand to view the tasks.
5.  Next to the task, tap Complete to change the status to Incomplete .

### Delete a task

**Important:** When you delete a task, it’s deleted for all members of the space and from the assignee’s personal task list.

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the bottom, tap Spaces and then select the space.
3.  At the top, tap the **Tasks** tab and then select the task.
4.  At the top right, tap Delete .

## Version: Computer

As a member of a space in Google Chat, you can create and manage group tasks and assign tasks to other space members. You can view tasks in the space where they're created. If a task is assigned to you, it appears in your personal task list in Google Tasks. When someone creates or updates a task, a notification appears in the space.

**Tips:**

- If you use a work or school account and don't have the "Tasks" tab in a space, contact your Google Workspace administrator to turn on this feature.
- When a space becomes too large, you can no longer:
  - Assign tasks
  - Mention anyone through "@"

### Create & assign a task in a space

**Important:** When you delete a task, it’s deleted for all members of the space and from the assignee’s personal task list.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - In Gmail, on the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Select the space where you want to create a task.
3.  At the top right, click Tasks .
4.  At the bottom of the panel, click **Add**.
5.  Enter details about the task, such as:
    - Title
    - Details
    - Date
    - Assignee
6.  Click **Add**.

**Tips:**

- If you add a date to your task, you get notifications in Chat for the scheduled dates.
- If the assignee leaves the space, the task remains in the space and in their personal task list in Google Tasks.
- Users may have restrictions when they assign tasks to a subset of members. [Learn more about group restrictions](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13535086).

### Edit a task

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the top right, click Tasks .
3.  Click the title, details, date, or assignee and make the changes.

**Tips:** If you have a task in your personal task list:

- You can edit the task title, details, and date from your personal task list.
- Changes update the task for everyone in the space and a notification displays.

### Mark a task complete

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the top right, click Tasks .
3.  Next to the task, point to Incomplete and click Complete .

**Tips:** If you have a task in your personal task list:

- You can edit the task title, details, and date from your personal task list.
- Changes update the task for everyone in the space and a notification displays.

### Mark a task incomplete

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  At the top right, click Tasks .
3.  Next to "Completed," to view the tasks, click Expand .
4.  Next to the task, click Complete to change the status to Incomplete .

### Delete a task

**Important:** When you delete a task, it’s deleted for all members of the space and from the assignee’s personal task list.

1.  In Chat, select the space.
2.  Select the space.
3.  At the top right, click Tasks .
4.  At the top right, click Delete and then **Delete**.
