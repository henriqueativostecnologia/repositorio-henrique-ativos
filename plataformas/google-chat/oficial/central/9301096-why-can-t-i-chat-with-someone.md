# Why can't I chat with someone?

When you try to send someone a message in Google Chat, you might get a banner that says you can't message them. This problem happens if you have incompatible settings.

## Reasons you can't chat with someone

- Settings that block you in Google Chat or another product. [Learn more about blocked accounts](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6388749).
- Settings that don't allow the other person to chat outside their organization.
  - If they use a Google Workspace account and their administrator disabled chats with external people, you can’t send each other messages. [Learn how to turn Chat history on or off](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7664687).
- History settings aren't compatible.
  - **Important:** If you have a work or school account, your administrator may set your history settings and may not allow users to change their history settings.
  - You might be able to fix this problem if you update your history settings to be compatible with the other person’s. For example, if your chat history is on, and you send a message to someone who doesn't have their history on, you may be able to chat by updating those settings to match. [Learn how to turn Chat history on or off](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7664687).
    - **Tip:** If both of you can turn off history, it automatically turns off when you chat with someone in a different data region.
- Your Workspace administrator may have configured policies that limit certain capabilities. For example, you can send messages in existing conversations but can’t start new ones.
  - Contact your Workspace administrator if this happens.
