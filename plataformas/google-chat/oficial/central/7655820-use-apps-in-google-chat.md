# Use apps in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Apps are special accounts you can message that connect you to services in Chat, so you can:

- Look up information.
- Schedule a meeting.
- Do tasks.

### Learn how apps work in Google Chat

When you interact with an app in Chat, the app can see your:

- Email address
- Avatar
- [Basic information](https://developers.google.com/chat/api/reference/rest/v1/User) (such as your name)

The app can see others' basic information in a chat, but it can't see their email address or avatar unless they interact directly with the app.

Google creates and maintains some apps, such as the [Google Drive](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7660707) and [Poll](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/15667559) apps.

Third-party software vendors create and maintain other apps. If you use one, review the vendor's Terms of Service and privacy policy for more information on the third-party use of your data.

**Important:**

- On your work or school accounts, your admin can install apps for you. Afterwards, the apps can send you direct messages. You can't uninstall the apps but you can turn off notifications for conversations with the app.
- If you're an external member of a Google Chat space created by a work or school account, you can't add, remove, or interact with any apps.

**Tips:**

- [Learn how to create custom apps for your organization](https://developers.google.com/hangouts/chat).
- For your organization's guidelines on app creation, [contact your Google Workspace administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

#### Learn about unverified & personal apps in Google Chat spaces

Developers with personal Gmail accounts can create and add personal apps to Google Chat spaces, group direct messages (DMs), and DMs they join. Any member of the space can then interact with these apps.

**Important:** Developers must explicitly provide consent in their app's configuration to enable apps to join spaces and group conversations. This allows their email to be visible to other members.

Google Chat displays an "Unverified app" warning badge next to messages sent by these apps to ensure transparency. When you hover over or click this badge, it reveals the creator's email address. For example, "Unverified—built by \[developer_email\]." This badge appears on all app interaction surfaces, such as messages, link previews, slash/quick commands, message actions, and system messages.

If a personal app's creator leaves or is removed from a space, the app is automatically removed from that conversation to help prevent spam and abuse. [Learn more about how to block an app creator](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9277792#block-an-app).

### Find & install a Chat app

**Important:** If you have a work or school account, your organization controls which apps are available to you.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select an option:
    - Click New chat and then **Find apps**.
    - In the Chat sidebar, click Get Add-ons .
3.  Search or browse for an app.
4.  Select an app.
5.  To create a dedicated 1:1 direct message with the app and allow you to interact with the app’s features individually, click **Install**.
    - Or, to add an app to a space and allow all space members to use the app’s features, click **Add to Space**.

**Tips:**

- You can [find and install an app in the Marketplace](https://support.google.com/marketplace/answer/6067029).
- To perform tasks on your behalf, the app sends you a prompt to get your permission to access your Google Account. To grant permission, go to the prompt and click **Allow**. [Learn how to remove third-party app access to your account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3466521).
- To uninstall an app in a direct message, at the top, click the app name and then **Uninstall**. For work or school accounts, you can’t uninstall apps that your admin installed.

### Add apps to a conversation or space

Your installed apps can be used in your conversations and spaces. When you add an app to a space, you grant the app the ability to:

- Fetch basic information about the space.
- Create messages in the space.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select a conversation or space.
3.  At the top, click the space name or conversation participants.
4.  Click **Apps & integration** and then **Add apps**.
5.  Enter the app name.
6.  Select one or more apps.
7.  Click **Add**.

**Tips:**

- When you add an app to a space, it grants the app access to messages that triggered the app, not to read all the messages in the space.
  - Apps that require access to the full message context can only do so with explicit permission.
- When you add an app to a space, the app gets installed in your Apps section.
  - Now the app is available in every other space you’re in and may be available in other Workspace products.
- Do not manually add the app to the conversation. To use the app in a different conversation or space, add the app in the space with:
  - An @mention or /mention
  - A command
  - A relevant link preview
- When you add an app to space, it grants the app access to manage cards that it owns in the space, including cards attached to human user messages.

### Remove apps from a conversation or space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  Select a conversation or space.
3.  At the top, click the space name or conversation participants.
4.  Click **Apps & integrations**.
5.  To remove an app, click More options and then **Remove from space.**

**Tips**:

- When you remove an app from a conversation or space, it’s only removed from that conversation or space, not from your Apps section.
- You can uninstall the app from the Apps section. This also uninstalls the app from other Workspace products.

### Uninstall an app

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  On the left sidebar, under “Apps,” open an app.
3.  At the top, click the app's name and then **Uninstall**.

### Use app commands

Use app commands in Google Chat to communicate quickly with apps. You don’t have to remember the commands. Instead, you can enter a forward slash `/` or click Add .

The app commands you use in a conversation are private. The app's responses appear in the conversation.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
2.  Select a conversation or space.
3.  In the reply area, enter a forward slash `/`.
    - Or, at the left of the reply area, click Add .
4.  Start typing and select the option from the list that matches what you're trying to do.
    - If the command requires more information, type your input in the reply area and press **Enter** to activate.
    - If the command does not require more information, click the command to activate it.

**Tips:**

- If the app isn’t already in the conversation or space, the app is added when you message it.

- To perform a custom behavior, add an argument. An argument is an additional input that you provide to a command.

### Get help with apps

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
2.  Open the conversation with the app.
3.  At the top, click **About**.
