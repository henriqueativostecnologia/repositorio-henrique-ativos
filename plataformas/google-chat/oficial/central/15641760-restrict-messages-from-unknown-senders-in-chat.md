# Restrict messages from unknown senders in Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To increase security and reduce the number of unwanted messages, which includes spam, you can restrict Chat message requests from unknown senders in 1:1 conversations and spaces.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  At the top right, click Settings .
3.  To choose who can message you in:
    - **1:1 conversations:** Under "Who can message you?," select:
      - **Anyone:** You can receive invitations from anyone.
      - **Known senders only:** For increased security, you can receive invitations only if the sender is in your contacts or if you’ve interacted with them before. Other invitations go to the spam folder.
    - **Spaces:** Under “Who can invite you to spaces?,” select:
      - **Anyone:** You can receive invitations from anyone.
      - **Known senders only:** For increased security, you can receive invitations only if the sender is in your contacts or if you’ve interacted with them before. Other invitations go to the spam folder.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To increase security and reduce the number of unwanted messages, which includes spam, you can restrict Chat message requests from unknown senders in 1:1 conversations and spaces.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app .
2.  At the top left, tap **Menu** and then **Settings**.
3.  Under "Manage invites," to choose who can message you in:
    - **1:1 conversations:**
      1.  Tap **Who can message you?**
      2.  Select:
          - **Anyone:** You can receive invitations from anyone.
          - **Known senders only:** For increased security, you can receive invitations only if the sender is in your contacts or if you’ve interacted with them before. Other invitations go to the spam folder.
    - **Spaces:**
      1.  Tap **Who can invite you to spaces?**
      2.  Select:
          - **Anyone:** You can receive invitations from anyone.
          - **Known senders only:** For increased security, you can receive invitations only if the sender is in your contacts or if you’ve interacted with them before. Other invitations go to the spam folder.

[Go to Chat settings](https://chat.google.com/settings)

## Version: iPhone & iPad

To increase security and reduce the number of unwanted messages, which includes spam, you can restrict Chat message requests from unknown senders in 1:1 conversations and spaces.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Chat app .
2.  At the top left, tap **Menu** and then **Settings** and then **Manage invites**.
3.  To choose who can message you in:
    - **1:1 conversations:** Under “Who can message you?,” select:
      - **Anyone:** You can receive invitations from anyone.
      - **Known senders only:** For increased security, you can receive invitations only if the sender is in your contacts or if you’ve interacted with them before. Other invitations go to the spam folder.
    - **Spaces:** Under “Who can invite you to spaces?,” select:
      - **Anyone:** You can receive invitations from anyone.
      - **Known senders only:** For increased security, you can receive invitations only if the sender is in your contacts or if you’ve interacted with them before. Other invitations go to the spam folder.
