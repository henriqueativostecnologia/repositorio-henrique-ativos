# Pin messages, files & links in spaces & messages

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can pin your important messages, files, links, images, and videos in spaces and in direct messages with an individual or group with the Board tab in Chat.

- **Pin what's important:** You can place important messages, files, links, images, and videos on the Board.
- **Access key items easily:** To make sure that everything is neatly organized and easily curated, the Board is readily available at the right panel.
- **Save time:** To save time, important messages and files are always easily accessible.
- **Use smart suggestions:** To save time, use the suggestions feature to quickly find the best and most relevant files to pin.

### Open a Chat board

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open any conversation or space.
3.  At the top right, click Board .

### Pin or unpin a message

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open any conversation or space.
3.  Click the message you want to pin and then More actions and then Pin to board .
    - To unpin, click Unpin .

### Pin a file or link

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  In Chat, open any conversation or space.
3.  At the top right, click Board and then Add resource .
4.  To upload a file from Drive, select **Upload or attach from Drive.**
    1.  Select the file name.
    2.  Click **Insert**.
    3.  In the pop up, to give access, select **Share with people**.
    4.  Click **Share**.
5.  To add a URL, select **Link**.
    1.  In the pop up, enter the link and the link title.
    2.  Click **Add Link**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

- **Access key items easily:** To make sure that everything is neatly organized and easily curated, the Board is readily available at the right panel.
- **Save time:** To save time, important messages and files are always easily accessible.
- **Use smart suggestions:** To save time, use the suggestions feature to quickly find the best and most relevant files to pin.

### Find Chat board

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Chat:** Open any conversation or space.
    - **In Gmail:** Tap Chat .
2.  Open any conversation or space.
3.  Tap Chat and then Board .

### Pin or unpin messages

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Chat:** Open any conversation or space.
    - **In Gmail:** Tap Chat .
2.  Open any conversation or space.
3.  Touch and hold a message.
    - **To pin:** Tap **Pin to board**.
    - **To unpin:** Tap Unpin .
