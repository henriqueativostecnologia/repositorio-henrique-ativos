# Use threads in Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In addition to the main conversation in the space, you can reply to any message and create a separate thread. With a thread, you can track important discussions and prevent the main conversation from being cluttered.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ViMCcUaOn1Q

### Learn how threads work in Chat

To create a single, streamlined flow of conversation in Google Chat, use threads for conversation. In a thread, you can either:

- Send a message to the entire group in the main conversation window.
- Quote a previous message.
- Reply to a single message to start a thread.
  - If you use threads to reply to a single message, your message branches into a separate conversation with up to 500 replies.

A conversation with threads allows users to:

- Follow specific threads and receive notifications for replies and mentions in the thread.
- Find out who replies to a thread. Each thread shows avatars of users that reply to the thread.

Find the number of unread thread updates:

- **For followed threads:** Check the number on the Threads icon .
- **In the main conversation stream:** The number of unread replies is underneath the initial message.

**Note:** A special thread icon badge may also appear on bolded conversations when there is only followed thread activity in that conversation.

### Reply in a thread

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).

    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.

2.  Select a conversation.

3.  Point to the message.

4.  In the menu, click Reply in thread .

5.  In the thread panel, to the right of the conversation, enter your message.

6.  Click Send .

**Tips:**

- To open the thread panel for a thread with replies, click below the message where it says “**X replies.**"
- To resize the thread panel, click and drag the bar on the left border of the panel.

### Follow or unfollow a thread

1.  At the top right corner of the conversation, click Threads .
    - Note: The icon may also show the number of unread followed threads.
2.  In the thread panel, choose:
    - **To follow a thread:** Click **Follow**.
    - **To unfollow a thread:** Click **Following**.

**Tip:** The **Unread** and **Following** filters apply automatically when the threads panel opens initially; if you update the filters, they will apply to all conversations on your device.

A thread is automatically followed if:

- You’re the author of the main message that initiates the thread.
- You reply via thread or were mentioned.
- You set the notification setting of a specific conversation to notify for “All.”

### Manage thread notifications

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  At the top of the conversation, click the conversation and then **Notifications**.
3.  Select an option for notifications:
    - **All:** Notifications for all new messages and replies to threads.
      - If you select “All,” you automatically follow all new threads for that conversation.
      - To unfollow a thread, click Threads and then **Following**.
    - **Main conversations:** Notifications for all new main conversations, @mentions, and replies to threads you follow.
    - **For you:** Notifications for @mentions and replies to threads you follow.
    - **None:** No notifications, just an indicator for @mentions.
4.  Optional: If you don’t want the conversation to be in bold and moved to the top of your conversation list, select the box next to "Mute conversation."
5.  Click **Save**.

**Tip:** In your conversation list, you may see or a blue dot that indicates an unread message in a thread you follow.

### Find threads in Home or in a conversation

##### Find followed threads in Home

To find your followed threads in Home:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  In "Home," click **Threads** .

##### Find threads in a conversation

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  To open a conversation, click on a conversation.
3.  At the top right corner of the conversation, select **Threads** .

**Tips:** When the threads panel opens, the **Unread** and **Following** filters apply automatically. You can also filter for threads where you are mentioned by clicking "**Mentions me.**"

### Reply in a thread

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections and then select a conversation.
3.  Tap and hold a message and then **Reply in thread**.
4.  At the bottom, enter your message.
5.  Tap Send .

Tip: If a thread already has replies, tap the message to open the thread panel.

### Follow or unfollow a thread

1.  At the top right corner of the conversation, tap Threads .
2.  In the thread panel, to the right of the thread, choose:
    - **To follow a thread:** Tap **Follow**.
    - **To unfollow a thread:** Tap **Following**.
3.  At the top of the thread panel, to filter the list of available threads, tap:
    - **Mentions me:** Threads where you’re mentioned.
    - **Following:** Threads that you follow.
    - **Unread:** Threads that you have not read.

### Manage thread notifications

After you start a thread, you can adjust how and when you’re notified of new messages.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  At the top of the conversation, tap the conversation  and then **Notification settings**.
3.  Select an option for notifications:
    - **All:** Notifications for all new messages and replies to threads.
      - If you select “All,” you automatically follow all new threads for that conversation.
      - To unfollow a thread, tap Threads and then **Following**.
    - **Main conversations:** Notifications for all new main conversations, @mentions, and replies to threads you follow.
    - **For you:** Notifications for @mentions and replies to threads you follow.
    - **None:** No notifications, just an indicator for @mentions.
4.  Optional: If you don’t want the conversation to be in bold and moved to the top of your conversation list, select the box next to "Mute conversation."
5.  Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** In your conversation list, you may see or a blue dot that indicates an unread message in a thread you follow.

### Find threads in Home or in a conversation

##### Find followed threads in Home

To find your followed threads in Home:

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Tap Home and then Thread .
    - **Tip:** To show only unread threads, tap Unread .

##### Find threads in a conversation

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  At the top of the conversation, tap and then Threads.
3.  Optional: To show only threads where you’re mentioned or that you followed, at the top, tap **Mentions me** or **Following**.
