# Edit or delete a message in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** This page provides instructions on how to edit or delete messages in Google Chat. To learn how to do this in Google Messages, go to [Update an RCS sent message](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/14929292) in the [Google Messages Help Center](https://support.google.com/messages).

If you make a mistake or need to update a message, you can edit or delete the message.

When you edit a message:

- You can’t go back to the previous edit or the original message.
- Chat notifications may include the original message.

When you delete a message:

- The message is permanently removed from your Google Account and can’t be recovered.
- You can’t undo the message deletion.

### Edit a sent message

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  Open a conversation, such as a direct message, group direct message, or space.
3.  Hover over the message you want to edit.
4.  Click Edit .
5.  Make your changes.
6.  Click **Update**.

### Delete a sent message

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  Open a conversation, such as a direct message, group direct message, or space.
3.  Hover over the message you want to delete.
4.  Click More and then Delete .
5.  To confirm, click **Delete**.

### Learn why you can't edit or delete a message

- You can delete messages from apps, but you can’t edit them.
- If you use a personal account, you can’t delete messages when:
  - You’re in a space a different organization started.
  - You send a direct or group message to a Google Workspace account.

### Edit a sent message

2.  Open a conversation, such as a direct message, group direct message, or space.
3.  Touch and hold the message you want to edit.
4.  Tap **Edit**.
5.  Make your changes.
6.  Tap Send ****.

### Delete a sent message

1.  On your Android device, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  Open a conversation, such as a direct message, group direct message, or space.
3.  Touch and hold the message you want to delete.
4.  Tap **Delete**.
    - To find the “Delete” option in the pop-up menu, scroll to show all options.
5.  To confirm, tap **Delete**.
