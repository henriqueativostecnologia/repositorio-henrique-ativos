# Use the Giphy app in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Use the Giphy app in Chat to add GIFs to your chats.

**Tip:** You can also send GIFs from your computer. To send a GIF, click Add GIF .

### Before you begin

- You need permission to install apps from your Google Workspace administrator.
- Add the app to Chat. [Learn how to find apps and add them to Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7655820).

### Use the Giphy app in Chat

1.  Open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open a direct message with the app or go to a space with the app.
3.  Enter a search query, using any keyword, such as "cats" or "funny:"
    - *query*—Adds the first GIF that matches the query with the set rating.
      - In spaces, enter **@Giphy** *query* or **/Giphy** *query*.
      - To remove the GIF, click the GIF and then **Try again** or **Delete**.
    - **choose** *query*—Adds a group of 25 GIFs that match the query with the set rating.
      - In spaces, enter **@Giphy choose** *query* or **/Giphy choose** *query*.
      - To move through the GIFs, click **Next** or **Back**.

### Change the content rating of GIFs

The app shows GIFs that are rated G unless you change the default setting. You can find the current rating and change it.

1.  Open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open a direct message with the app or go to a space with the app.
3.  Enter **rating**.
    - In spaces, enter **@Giphy rating** or **/Giphy rating**.
    - The app displays the current content rating.
4.  Enter **rating** \[**Y,G,PG,PG-13,R**\].
    - In spaces, enter **@Giphy rating** \[**Y,G,PG,PG-13,R**\] or **/Giphy rating** \[**Y,G,PG,PG-13,R**\].
    - For example, to set the content rating of GIFs to G, enter **rating** **G**.
