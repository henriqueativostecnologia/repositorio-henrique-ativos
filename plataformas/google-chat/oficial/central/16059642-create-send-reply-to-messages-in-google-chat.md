# Create, send & reply to messages in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can send a direct message to a person or group of people in Google Chat.

### Create a direct message

To send a new message:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  At the top left, click New chat .
3.  Enter the person’s name you want to message.
    - To create a group direct message, enter multiple names.
4.  Click **Start chat**.

### Send & reply to messages

To send or reply to a message, you can use the message field in Chat. There are multiple ways to reply.

When you reply, you can:

|                          |                                                                               |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Send a message**       | Send the message you wrote in the message field.                              |
| **Share files and more** | Share Google Drive files, set up a Calendar invite, or add apps to a message. |
| **Start a meeting**      | Start a video call with [Google Meet](https://meet.google.com).               |
| **Add a video meeting**  | Add a Google Meet link to a video meeting.                                    |
| Edit **Edit a message**  | Edit a sent message.                                                          |
| **Delete a message**     | Delete a sent message.                                                        |
| **Format messages**      | Bold or italicize text, use bullet points, or add color.                      |
| **React to messages**    | Add an emoji.                                                                 |
| **Upload a file**        | Share a file from your computer.                                              |
| **Share a GIF**          | React with an animated GIF.                                                   |
| **Quote a message**      | To directly respond, quote a previous message in Chat.                        |
| **Start a thread**       | In a space, create a new thread based on a message.                           |

### Troubleshoot permissions

Contact the Workspace administrator if you:

- Can’t create a new conversation
- Can’t add members to a space
- Get a permission error like "You don't have permission to start a new conversation”

[Learn how to manage space settings](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13340792).

### Schedule messages

You can write a message, then schedule it to be sent later.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://mail.google.com/chat) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Select a Space or Chat.
3.  Type your message.
4.  To the right of the "Send" arrow, click the Down arrow More send options .
5.  A pop-up appears with suggested send times, which includes the recipient's time zone.
6.  Select a suggested time or click **Pick a custom time**.

#### Confirm a scheduled message

After you schedule your message delivery, at the bottom of your chat, a banner appears. This confirms the message's scheduled delivery time and recipient.

To find and manage all your scheduled messages, click **See all scheduled messages**.

#### Manage your scheduled messages

On the left navigation panel, in the "Drafts" tab, you can access and manage all your scheduled messages.

In "Drafts," you can:

- Find all your pending scheduled messages.
- Get the send time in your time zone.
- Get the send time in the receiver's time zone.
- Perform different actions on scheduled messages.

#### Perform actions on scheduled messages

In "Drafts," you have full control over your scheduled messages. For each scheduled message, you can:

- **Edit message:** Modify the content of a scheduled message.
- **Reschedule:** Change the scheduled delivery time of a message.
- **Send immediately:** Bypass the scheduled time and send the message immediately.
- **Delete:** Remove a scheduled message.

#### Identify conversations with scheduled messages

A draft icon Draft icon next to a conversation indicates that it contains either a draft or scheduled message.

If there’s "Drafts Draft icon " next to a conversation, it means that the conversation contains either a draft or a scheduled message.
