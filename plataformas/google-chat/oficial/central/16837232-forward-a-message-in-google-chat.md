# Forward a message in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can share important or relevant messages with other people or spaces in Google Chat. This allows you to:

- Quickly relay information
- Share updates without needing to add them to another chat
- Bring a message to a new conversation without copying and pasting the original text

### Forward a message

**Important:**

- You can forward conversations with people outside your organization into your internal conversations.
- You can't forward messages from any internal conversations to any external conversations.
- You can’t forward messages from external conversations to other external conversations.

Forward messages

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com).
2.  Open a DM or space.
3.  Hover over a message.
4.  Select More Actions .
5.  Select Forward message .
6.  Search for and select all the people or space you want to forward the message to.
    - When you forward a message from a DM or group DM, a reminder about sensitive information appears.
7.  Select **Next**.
    - Optional: You can add your own message before you share the forwarded message.
8.  Select Send message .

**Tips:**

- To send any attachments or images included in the original message, forward the message.
- To increase the message's visibility, forward a message from a thread to the main stream of the same space.
