# Hide or delete a 1:1 direct message

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can hide or delete a conversation between you and another person in Google Chat and Chat in Gmail.

- **Hide a 1:1 direct message**
  - Hides your copy of a conversation, but doesn't remove the conversation for other participants.
  - If you hide a conversation with someone and then message them again, the conversation chat history shows up again.
- **Delete a 1:1 direct message**
  - Permanently hides the conversation history from your view. The content remains visible to the other participant.

You can also choose to [edit or delete a specific message](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653281). If you edit or delete a sent message, it updates the message for you and other participants in the conversation.

### Hide a conversation or space

You can hide a 1:1 direct message, a group direct message, or a space.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Hover over the 1:1 or group direct message name.
3.  Click Options and then Hide conversation.

#### Hide a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Hover over the Space name.
3.  Click Options and then Hide Space.

### Delete a 1:1 direct message

**Important:** You can’t delete a group direct message with more than 2 participants.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Hover over the person's name.
3.  Click More and then Delete conversation .
4.  To confirm, click **Delete**.

To delete a single message instead of a conversation, [learn how to edit or delete a message in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653281).

### Hide a conversation or space

You can hide a 1:1 direct message, a group direct message, or a space.

#### Hide a direct message

1.  On your Android or Android tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - In Gmail: At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Direct messages .
3.  From the list, select the 1:1 or group direct message.
4.  At the top of the conversation, select the message name.
5.  Select **Hide conversation**.

#### Hide a space

1.  On your Android or Android tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - In Gmail: At the bottom, select Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, select Spaces .
3.  From the list, select the space name.
4.  At the top of the space, select the space name.
5.  Select **Hide space**.

### Delete a 1:1 direct message

**Important:** You can’t delete a group direct message with more than 2 participants.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Direct messages .
3.  In the list of direct messages, tap the person's name.
4.  At the top of the conversation, tap the person's name and then Delete conversation .
5.  To confirm, tap **Delete**.
    - **Note:** Deleting hides the conversation history from your view. The content remains visible to the other participant.

To delete a single message instead of a conversation, [learn how to edit or delete a message in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653281).
