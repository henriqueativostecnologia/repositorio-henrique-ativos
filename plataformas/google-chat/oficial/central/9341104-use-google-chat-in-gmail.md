# Use Google Chat in Gmail

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To manage your communication, stay connected, and collaborate in one place, add Google Chat to your Gmail inbox. If you use a work or school account, your organization determines if this option is available.

**Important:** Your child can use Chat in the Gmail app, or in the web version of Gmail, even if the Chat app is blocked. [Learn how to manage your child's Google Account](https://support.google.com/families/answer/7100427).

### Turn Chat on or off in Gmail

An animation showing how to turn Chat on or off in Gmail on desktop

1.  On your computer, open [Gmail](https://gmail.google.com).

2.  At the top right, click Settings   and then  **See all settings**.

    [Go to Gmail settings](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/chat)

3.  At the top, click **Chat and Meet**.

4.  Next to “Chat,” to turn Chat on or off in Gmail, select **Google Chat** or **Off**.

5.  Click **Save Changes**.

### How is Chat in Gmail different from Chat?

You get the same features in Chat and Chat in Gmail, but the integrated Gmail experience provides a central location to communicate with friends, family, or coworkers between emails.

- **Chat:** Use when you prefer a dedicated chat experience and don't mind switching between different apps.
- **Gmail:** Use when you like to multi-task and prefer to get all of your communications in one place. [Learn more about the new integrated Gmail layout](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/11555490).

### Turn Chat on or off in Gmail

An animation showing how to turn Chat on or off in Gmail on Android

1.  Open the Gmail app .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu   and then **Settings**.
3.  Choose an account to opt in to Google Chat.
4.  Under “General,” next to “Chat,” turn on **Show the chat and spaces tab**.

An animation showing how to turn Chat on or off for Gmail on iOS

1.  Open the Gmail app .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Under "Apps in Gmail," tap **Chat**.
4.  Turn "Chat" on or off.
