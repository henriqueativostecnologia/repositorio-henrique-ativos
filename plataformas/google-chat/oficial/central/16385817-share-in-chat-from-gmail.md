# Share in Chat from Gmail

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Users with a work or school account can quickly share an email in a Chat message to keep the email context and avoid long email threads.

**Important:**

- Users with a personal account don’t have access to this feature.
- To share an email in Chat, you must turn on [Chat in Gmail](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9341104).

### Share an email in Chat

1.  On your computer, open [Gmail](https://gmail.google.com/).
2.  Open an email.
3.  At the bottom of the email, select **Share in chat** .
    - **Tip:** To share a specific email in a multiple-email thread, next to the email, click More and then **Share in chat** .
4.  To add recipients, you can:
    - Click **Add everyone from the email**.
    - Search or select from listed suggestions.
5.  Optional: To remove a recipient, next to the name or space, click **Remove** .
6.  Click **Next**.
7.  Enter your message.
8.  Click **Send** .
    - The recipient gets a link to the email in Chat.
    - Gmail adds an automated reply to the email thread that links to the Chat message.

### Learn why you’re unable to share an email in Chat

You can’t share an email in Chat if:

- You use a personal account.
- The admin for your work account turned off Chat.
- You haven’t turned on [Chat within Gmail on your computer](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/9341104).
- The email is spam, phishing, scheduled, in trash, in drafts, or in confidential mode.
- You open the email in another email app, like Apple Mail or Microsoft Outlook.
- The email is encrypted with Client-side encryption. [Learn about Gmail Client-side encryption](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/13317990).
- You try to share an email in Chat to more than 50 recipients or to a Google Group.
