# Add or remove someone in a space

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To add more people to a space in Google Chat, add them to the member list or mention them directly in the space.

**Tips:** If you use Chat with a Google Workspace account:

- You can add a Google Group to the space. [Learn how to add a Google Group to a space](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13535086).
- Your administrator can create a space that everyone in your organization can find. For example, your administrator can add a group named `everyone@yourcompany.com` to a space.
- By default, any member can remove another member without administrator approval or restrictions, unless the space settings explicitly restrict membership management.

### Add someone to a space

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#inbox).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  In the chat header, click the dropdown and then **Manage members**.
    - A list of current members displays.
4.  At the top right, click **+ Add**.
5.  Enter the name or email address of the person or group.
6.  Click **Add**.

**Tips**:

- To quickly add a new person to a space, in the reply area, enter **@** followed by the person’s name.
- To view people that you’ve invited to the space, click **Manage members** and then **Invited**.

##### Learn how space invites work

- An email invite is sent to someone if:
  - They haven’t had a conversation with you before.
  - They belong to another organization and the space was created by your Google Workspace account.
- Someone you invite to a space is added directly if:
  - They’ve had a conversation with you before.
  - You're in the same organization.
- People added directly to spaces don't get email notifications.
- If you have a Google Workspace account, members of your organization may get an email invite or added directly to a space.

### Remove someone from a space

**Important**: If you have the ability to manage members, you can remove members from a space. After you remove someone, they can't:

- Participate in the conversation.
- View that space’s message history.
- Access shared files in the space.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  In the chat header, click the dropdown and then **Manage members**.
4.  To the right of the person or group name, click More options and then **Remove from space**.

**Tip**: If a person was added as part of a Google Group, remove them from the group first and then from the space.

### Copy space member email addresses

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  In the chat header, click the dropdown and then **Manage members**.
4.  Click Copy member emails .

    - **In Gmail**: At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections .
3.  Open a space.
4.  At the top left, tap the space name and then **Manage members**.
5.  Under "Members," tap Add people .
6.  Enter the name or email of the person or group you want to add.
7.  At the top right, tap **Done**.

- To quickly add a new person to a space, in the reply area, enter **@** followed by the person’s name.
