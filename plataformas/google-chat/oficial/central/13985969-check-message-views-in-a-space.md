# Check message views in a space

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To understand the popularity of a message in spaces, you can check the number of users who have viewed a post.

### Use message views

**Important:** Message view counts aren't available for in-line replies.

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or your [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under "Spaces," select a space.
3.  Hover over a message.
4.  Click More options and then **See message views**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To understand the popularity of a message in spaces, you can check the number of users who have viewed a post.

### Use message views

**Important:** Message view counts aren't available for in-line replies.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
2.  At the bottom, tap Spaces .
3.  Select a space.
4.  Touch and hold a space message.
5.  Tap **See message views**.
