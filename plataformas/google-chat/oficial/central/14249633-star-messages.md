# Star messages

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To track important conversations in Google Chat, you can star messages. To find starred messages, go to the "Starred" shortcut.

### Star or unstar a message

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  Open a conversation.
3.  Hover over a message you want to star.
4.  Click More and then **Star** or **Unstar**.

### Find your starred messages

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under “Shortcuts,” click **Starred**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To track important conversations in Google Chat, you can star messages. To find starred messages, go to the "Starred" shortcut.

### Star or unstar a message

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  Open a conversation.
3.  Touch and hold a message.
4.  Tap **Star** or **Unstar**.

### Find your starred messages

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap More and then **Starred**.
