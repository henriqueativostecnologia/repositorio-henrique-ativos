# Send a video message in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In Google Chat, you can send a video message to a person or group.

### Send a video message

**Important:** If you send a message to someone for the first time, they must accept your message request before you can chat with them.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Click **New chat**.
3.  Enter a name or an email address.
4.  Click **Start chat**.
    - When you enter a name or email address, you can get suggestions.
    - To start a group direct message (DM), add more names or email addresses.
5.  At the bottom, in the reply area, click and then Video message .
6.  In the popup, to start recording, click Record .
7.  To pause recording, click Pause .
8.  Select an option:
    - Send
    - Preview
      - After you preview the video, you can choose to restart or send the video message.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Chat, you can send a video message to a person or group.

### Send a video message

**Important:** If you send a message to someone for the first time, they must accept your message request before you can chat with them.

1.  On your Android device, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  At the bottom, in the navigation menu, tap New chat .
3.  Enter a name or email address.
    - When you enter a name or email address, you can get suggestions.
    - To start a group direct message (DM), add more names or email addresses.
4.  Tap **Done**.
5.  At the bottom, in the reply area, tap and then Video message .
    - Your video message starts to record.
6.  Tap the stop button and then Send .
7.  To delete your recording, tap Delete .
