# Create a guest account in Google Chat

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can join another organization's Google Chat with a Google Workspace guest account. At no cost to you, collaboration and communication with other organizations is easy. To join, you must be invited.

**Tip:** If you're part of a Workspace and want to invite a guest to your Chat, you can [send an invitation](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/16997417).

### What you can do with a guest account

Once you create an account, your invitation lets you chat directly with the person who invited you and join any approved spaces.

- **Google Chat:** Use chats and spaces to coordinate and communicate.
- **Google Meet:** Join huddles, Google Meet calls, or access Meet sessions that are shared through links.
- **Google Drive:** Open and edit shared Google Drive files like Docs, Sheets, or Slides.

**Tip:** Guest accounts can't use chat in Gmail.

### Create a new guest account

1.  Open the invitation email.
    - For the best experience, use a computer web browser to create your account.
    - **Tip:** If there’s a sign-in conflict with a Chrome or Google Chat account that’s already logged in:
      1.  Copy the invitation link.
      2.  Paste it into a new incognito or private browsing window.
2.  To accept the invitation, select **Join the conversation**.
3.  To create your guest account, follow the on-screen instructions.

**Tips:**

- Save your guest account email ID and password for future use. Log-in details
- You can find the guest account email ID in the invitation email if you didn’t save it during setup.
  Invite for guest account

### Sign in to your guest account on another device

#### Sign in on web

1.  Go to [chat.google.com](https://chat.google.com/).
2.  Sign in with your guest account email ID and password.
    - If you’re already signed into other Google Chat accounts:
      1.  Select your **Profile picture** and then **Add another account**.
      2.  Sign in with your guest account.
