# Search for a space

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can use search to find spaces in Google Chat you're interested in or have been invited to.

### Search for a space in Chat or Gmail

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  Next to "Spaces," click Section actions and then Browse spaces .
3.  Enter the name of the space that you want to find.
4.  Click the space name or **View**.
    - If the space is open, click **Join**. You can also preview the messages.
    - If the space is private or discoverable and the space allows it, you can submit a request to join.

[Learn how to join a space in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653963).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can use search to find spaces in Google Chat you're interested in or have been invited to.

### Search for a space in Chat or Gmail

1.  Open the Gmail app or Chat app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the search bar at the top, enter a keyword.
3.  On the “Search results” page, tap **Spaces**.
4.  Tap on the space to open it.

[Learn how to join a space in Google Chat](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653963).
