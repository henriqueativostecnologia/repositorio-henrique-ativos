# Learn about your role as a space owner or manager

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

When you create a space in Google Chat, you automatically become the space owner. As a space owner, you can:

- Remove and add participants.
- Promote other members to be space managers, or remove them from the list of space managers.
- Delete a space.
- Delete messages.
- Edit the space name, avatar, and description.

As a space manager, you have all the permissions of a space owner except the ability to:

- Promote other members to be space owners.
- Remove space owners from the list of space owners.
- Delete a space.

Space owners who use a work or school account can update additional settings, such as space access and permissions. [Learn how to change space access and permissions](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13340792).

### Add members

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  Click the space name and then **Manage members**.
4.  Click **+ Add**.
5.  Enter the name or email of the person or group that you want to add.
6.  Click **Add**.

### Remove members

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  Click the space name and then **Manage members**.
4.  Point to the person and click More actions and then **Remove from space**.

### Change a user's role

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  Click the space name and then **Manage members**.
4.  Next to the person's name, under "Role," click the dropdown.
    - **To make someone a space owner:** Select **Owner**.
    - **To make someone a space manager:** Select **Manager**.
    - **To make someone a space member:** Select **Member**.

**Tips:**

- Not all users can change other users' roles.
- The last space owner can't leave a space unless they delete the space or make someone else a space owner.

### Delete messages

**Important:** The “Delete messages” feature is only available to specific subscriptions and might not be available on your work or school account.

Space owners and managers can delete any message in a space. Space members can only delete their own messages. [Learn how to delete a message as a space member](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653281).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#chat/home).
    - **In Gmail**: On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  On the left, select the space.
3.  Point to the message you want to delete.
4.  Click More actions and then Delete .
5.  In the window that opens, click **Delete**.

**Tip:** When a message is deleted in a space, a time stamp may appear. The time stamp remains for 30 days or longer based on the retention policy of your company.

Space owners who use a work or school account can update additional settings, such as space access and permissions. [Learn how to change space access and permissions](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/13340792).

### Add members

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections .
3.  Select a space.
4.  At the top left, tap the space name and then **Manage members**.
5.  Enter the name or email of the person or group you want to add.
6.  At the top right, tap **Done**.

### Remove members

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections .
3.  Select a space.
4.  At the top left, tap the space name and then **Manage members**.
5.  Under "Members," next to the person's name, tap More options and then **Remove from space**.

### Change a user's role

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections .
3.  Select a space.
4.  At the top left, tap the space name and then **Manage members**.
5.  Under "Members," next to the person’s name, tap More options .
    - **To make someone a space owner:** Tap Change to Owner .
    - **To make someone a manager:** Tap Change to Manager .
    - **To make someone a member:** Tap Change to Member .

**Tips:**

- Not all users can change other users' roles.
- The last space manager can't leave a space unless they delete the space or make someone else a space manager.

### Delete messages

**Important: **The "Delete messages" feature is only available to specific subscriptions and might not be available on your work or school account.

Space owners and managers can delete any message in a space. Space members can only delete their own messages. [Learn how to delete a message as a space member](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/7653281).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  In the bottom navigation menu, tap Sections .
3.  Select a space.
4.  To delete, tap and hold a message.
5.  In the window that opens, tap **Delete**.

**Tip:** When a message is deleted in space, a time stamp may appear. The time stamp remains for 30 days or longer based on the retention policy of your company.
