# Search for Google Chat messages

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In Google Chat, you can search:

- In all your messages.
- For messages that include specific people.
- For messages that include certain types of content, such as images.

**Important:**

- To search for Chat messages in Gmail, turn on Chat in Gmail.
- You can search for messages in conversations you joined or can join.
- To filter your search results, you can also use [search operators](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/14179758).
- You can’t search for messages using the Gmail API.
- Even if you’re invited to join, you can’t search for messages in conversations or spaces that someone from a different organization created.

### Search for messages in Chat & Gmail

1.  On your computer, open [Google Chat](https://chat.google.com/) or [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
    - **In Gmail:** On the left, click **Chat**.
2.  At the top, click the search box.
3.  Enter search text.
    - **For Gmail:** You can also use the search operator` in:chats`.
4.  Select an option:
    - **For Gmail:** Under the search box, click **Conversations**.
    - Select a recent search.
5.  To open a result, click it.

### Search for messages in a space or conversation

1.  In Chat, select the space or conversation you want to search.
2.  At the top right, click Search in this chat .
3.  Enter search text.
4.  Select an option:
    - Press **Enter**.
    - Select a recent search.
5.  To open a result, click it.

### Filter & sort messages

1.  In Chat, at the top, click the search box.
2.  Enter search text.
    - As you enter text, suggestions appear below. Press "tab" to autofill the rest of a query.
3.  Press **Enter**.
4.  Select a filter to find the following types of messages:
    - **From:** Messages sent from specific people.
    - **Said in:** Messages said in a specific conversation or space.
    - **Date:** Messages sent during a specific date range.
    - **Has file:** Messages that include documents, spreadsheets, slides, and more.
    - **Has link:** Messages that include links.
    - **Mentions me:** Messages that mention you.
    - **Only conversations I’m in:** Messages from conversations that you’re a member of.
5.  Optional: To sort messages, below the filters, click **Sort menu**.
    - **Most relevant:** Sort messages by how relevant they are to your search text.
    - **Most recent:** Sort messages by how recent they are.
6.  To open a result, click it.
    - Results from spaces you haven't joined may appear. To join, next to the message, click **Join**.

### Delete suggested search results

When you enter text in the Chat search bar, suggestions may appear based on your search history. To find messages, you can select a suggestion.

- Suggestions are stored for 90 days after use.
- Search history for suggestions isn’t shared between Chat and Chat in Gmail.
- You can delete your recent searches or your entire search history.

1.  In Chat, click the **Search bar**.
2.  Under “Recent searches,” you can:
    - Delete a recent search: Click a search and then **Delete**.
    - Delete all searches: Click **Delete all**.

### Fix issues to find Google Chat messages

- Check if history was turned off for the Chat conversation.
  - If Chat history was turned off, then the messages were permanently deleted.
- **For work or school accounts, and for anyone in a 1:1 direct message with a work or school account user:** Messages may not be available after a certain time period. This depends on the retention policy of the organization of the person who started the direct message. [Learn about message history for conversations with work or school accounts](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/16053860).

### Auto-save & sync message drafts

Google Chat automatically saves your unsent messages as drafts across your computer and mobile devices in the conversation compose box and in a new Drafts shortcut. This allows you to start a message on one device and finish it on another.

This feature is available to all Google Workspace customers, Workspace Individual subscribers, and users with personal Google accounts.

**Draft messages are automatically saved when you:**

- Pause typing for 5 seconds.
- Switch to a different window or application where Google Chat is not in focus.
- Navigate to another screen within Google Chat, such as your chat list or Home.

**Access and manage drafts:**

1.  In Chat, in the left sidebar, select **Drafts** to view and manage all active drafts.
    **Note:** If you have no active drafts, this shortcut won't be visible.
2.  Select a draft. You can edit a draft, which opens the conversation with the draft pre-filled, or delete it.
3.  **Optional:** You can access drafts from your chats. When you open a conversation, any saved draft for that conversation automatically appears in the compose box and a new draft icon appears next to conversations that have an unsent draft. This icon takes priority over other indicators like pinned conversations or meeting notifications.

**Important:**

- Google Chat can store up to 100 drafts. When this limit is reached, the oldest draft is removed to make space for new ones.
- Drafts are kept for 30 days from the last time they were edited.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Chat, you can search:

- In all your messages.
- For messages that include specific people.
- For messages that include certain types of content, such as images.

**Important:**

- To search for Chat messages in Gmail, turn on Chat in Gmail.
- You can search for messages in conversations you joined or can join.
- To filter your search results, you can also use [search operators](https://support.google.com/chat/answer/14179758).
- You can’t search for messages using the Gmail API.
- Even if you’re invited to join, you can’t search for messages in conversations or spaces that someone from a different organization created.

### Search for messages & spaces in Chat & Gmail

You can search for messages and spaces in Chat.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Chat app or the Gmail app .
    - **In Gmail:** At the bottom, tap Chat .
2.  At the top, enter search text.
3.  Select an option:
    - At the bottom, on your keyboard, tap Search .
    - Tap a recent search.
4.  To open a result, tap it.
5.  After you finish your search, you can switch between “Messages” and “Spaces” results at the top of the screen.
    - To view the list of matching spaces, tap **Spaces**.
    - Select a filter to find the following types of spaces:
      - **All spaces:** Joined or unjoined spaces.
      - **Internal and external:** Filter internal or external spaces for your organization.

### Search for messages in a space or conversation

1.  In Chat, open the conversation to search.
2.  At the top right, tap More options and then Search in conversation .
3.  Enter your search text.
4.  To open a result, tap it.

### Filter & sort messages & spaces

1.  In Chat, at the top, enter search text.
    - As you enter text, filters appear below.
2.  Select a filter to find the following types of messages:
    - **From:** Messages sent from specific people.
    - **Said in:** Messages said in a specific conversation or space.
    - **Attachment:** Messages that include documents, spreadsheets, slides, and more.
    - **Date:** Messages sent during a specific date range.
    - **Has link:** Messages that include links.
    - **Mentions me:** Messages that mention you.
    - **Only conversations I’m in:** Messages from conversations that you’re a member of.
3.  Optional: To sort messages, below the filters, tap **Sort menu**.
    - **Most relevant:** Sort messages by how relevant they are to your search text.
    - **Most recent:** Sort messages by how recent they are.
4.  On the “Search results” page, you can switch between message and space results.
    - To view the list of matching spaces, tap **Spaces**.
    - Select a filter to find the following types of spaces:
      - **All spaces:** Joined or unjoined spaces.
      - **Internal and external:** Filter internal or external spaces for your organization.
5.  Tap a search result.
    - Results from spaces you haven't joined may appear. To join, next to the message, tap **Join**.

### Delete suggested search results

When you enter in the Chat search bar, suggestions may appear based on your search history. To find messages, you can select a suggested search result.

- Suggestions are stored for 90 days after their use.
- Search history for suggestions isn’t shared between Chat and Chat in Gmail.
- You can delete your recent searches or your entire search history.

1.  In Chat, tap the search bar.
2.  Under “Recent searches,” you can:
    - **Delete a recent search:** Tap and hold a search item and then tap **Delete**.
    - **Delete all searches:** Tap **Delete all**.
