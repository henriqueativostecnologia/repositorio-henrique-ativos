# Invite others to edit your site

## Add an editor

**Important:** If you use a Google Account through work or school, your admin may limit your ability to add an editor.

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top, click Share Share with others .
3.  Under **Share with people and groups**, enter an email address.
4.  Click **Editor**.
    - Editors with a non-Google account can view a draft of your site, but cannot edit it.
    - To view a draft, an Editor with a non-Google account must enter a verification code. You receive an email with this code when you’re invited to be an editor.
    - Editors with a Google account can view and edit your site without a verification code.
5.  Click **Done**.

## Follow a collaborator on Google Sites

You can follow a collaborator if you edit the site at the same time. When the collaborator moves to another part of the site, you also move there.

#### Follow a collaborator on Google Sites

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  Open a site.
3.  At the top right, click the collaborator's profile picture to follow them to the same page or tile.
    - If the collaborator moves to another page or tile after you follow them, you won’t move to the new page or tile.

#### Issues when you follow a collaborator

You can't follow a collaborator who:

- Uses an unsupported device
- Is anonymous
- Doesn't have permission to edit the site
- Is on the same page as you are, but hasn’t selected a page element

## Stop, limit, or change sharing

After you share a site, you can stop sharing a site at any time. You can also prevent someone you’ve shared with from changing or sharing your site.

#### Change who can edit your site

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top, click Share Share with others .
3.  Under **Share with people and groups,** find the name of the person whose access you want to change.
4.  Choose an option.
5.  Click **Done**.

#### Limit who can edit your site

Choose to restrict edits to only editors or allow anyone with a link to your site and a Google account to edit.

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top, click Share Share with others .
3.  Click the **Links** section.
4.  Adjust the settings for draft and published versions of your site:
    1.  **Draft:** You can restrict access to people added under “Share with people and groups,” or allow anyone to edit with a link to your site and a Google account.
    2.  **Published site:** You can restrict views to people added under “Share with people and groups,” or allow anyone to view it.
5.  Click **Done**.

#### Remove an editor

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top, click Share Share with others .
3.  Under **Share with people and groups** find the editor you want to remove.
4.  Next to the editor’s name, click **Remove** and then **Save**.

#### Change the site owner

The new owner can change what you can edit or remove you from the site.

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).

2.  At the top, click Share Share with others .

3.  Under **Share with people and groups,** find the name of the person you want to be the new owner.

4.  Next to the person’s name, click the Down arrow Down and then **Make owner**.

5.  Click **Save changes** and then **Done**.

#### Prevent others from sharing your site

If you’re sharing a site, the owner or anyone with edit permissions can change the sharing settings for the site. To make it so only you can share:

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top, click Share Share with others .
3.  At the top of the window, click Settings Settings .
4.  Uncheck **Editors can publish, change permissions, and add new people**.
