# How to use Google Sites

You can create a website to share info with others.

**Tip:** [Google Accounts managed by parents](https://support.google.com/families/answer/7103262?hl=en&ref_topic=7336731) can't use Google Sites, but are able to see and edit pre-existing sites in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).

## Create a site

1.  On your computer, open [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top, under "Start a new site," select a template.
3.  Edit your site.
4.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.
