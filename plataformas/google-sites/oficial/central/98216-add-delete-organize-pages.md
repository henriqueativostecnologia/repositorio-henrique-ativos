# Add, delete & organize pages

You can add or delete pages, insert a table of contents, edit headers and footers, and update site info settings in Google Sites.

#### Add a page

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  At the bottom right, hover over Add New page .
4.  Click New page Add .
5.  Enter the page name.
6.  Click **Done**.
7.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

## Copy or delete a page

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  On the page you want to duplicate or delete, click More More .
    - To copy a page, choose **Duplicate page**.
    - To delete a page, choose **Delete**.
      - **Tip**: You can recover deleted pages by [restoring to a previous version](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/9622187).
4.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

### Add a table of contents

You can add a table of contents to help viewers navigate your page structure. The table of contents automatically updates when you edit your page.

1.  On your computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the top right, click **Insert** and then **Table of contents**.
    - **Important:** Make sure your site has titles, headings, and subheadings which will be included in your table of contents. [Learn how to add titles, headings, and subheadings](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/90538).
3.  Publish your site to use the table of contents. [Learn how to publish your site](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/6372880).

**Tip:** To delete the table of contents, click the table and then Delete tile  Remove .

## Edit a table of contents

The table of contents automatically indents based on the structure of your titles, headings, and subheadings. To remove the indent:

1.  On your computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  Click on your table of contents.
3.  Click Flatten Decrease indent .

**Tip**: To re-indent, click Indent Increase indent .

## Hide a heading from a table of contents

You can hide titles, headings, and subheadings from site viewers in your table of contents. 

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  In your table of contents, point to the heading that you want to hide or unhide.
    - To hide the heading, click Hide Hide .
    - To unhide the heading, click Show Hide .
3.  In the top right, click **Publish**.

**Tip:** Your heading will still be visible when you edit your site, but it won't be visible on the published site.

## Organize your pages

## Move a page

You can move a page under another page to create a subpage.

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  Drag the page where you want to move it.
4.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

### Create a new subpage

To create a new subpage, click **Pages**.Then, choose a page and click More  More   and then ** Add subpage**.

**Tip**: You can create up to 5 levels of sub pages.

## Choose your homepage

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  Choose a page and click More More and then **Make home page**.
4.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

## Create a custom URL

You can create a custom path to a page on your site.

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  Choose a page and click More More and then **Properties**.
4.  Click **Advanced** and then **Custom path** and then enter the path you’d like to use.
5.  Click **Done**.

**Tip:**

- Publishing a custom path to a page or renaming a page will create a new URL and might affect data tracking with Analytics.
- If site viewers reject cookies for tracking usage, the data about your site's visits and activity might be inaccurate and underestimate actual usage. [Learn more about cookies](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/9396802).

## Add a link to your site menu

1.  On your computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  Click the page with the menu you want to update.
4.  At the right, point to Add Add .
5.  Click New link New link .
6.  Add a link name.
7.  Add a web address or choose a page from your site.
8.  Click **Done**.

## Hide a page

When you hide a page, it won’t show up in the menu, but people can still get to it with a link.

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  On the page you want to hide, click More More and then **Hide from navigation**.
4.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

You can’t hide the home page, but when you hide any other page:

- Subpages are also hidden.
- Other sites and pages can link to the page.

If you don’t want your page to appear in search results, [learn how to hide your site](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/6372880).

 

## Nest pages inside a dropdown menu

1.  On your computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Pages**.
3.  Click the page with the menu you want to update.
4.  At the bottom right, hover over Add New page .
5.  Click New menu section .
6.  Add a menu section name.
7.  Click **Done**.
8.  You can drag and drop pages into your new menu.

**Tip:** When you preview your site, you can see your nested pages by hovering over the new menu.

## Resolve errors with page path character limits

In Google Sites, page paths are limited to 1024 characters. If you nest pages under other pages or inside menu sections, the paths for those nested pages will be longer.

To resolve any errors, you can:

- Shorten page or menu section titles.
- Shorten custom path names.
- Reorganize pages to reduce nesting.

## Add or edit headers & footers

The header and footer are the areas at the top and bottom of each page on your Google site.

## Remove or add header

When you create a site or a page, it will have a header. To remove it:

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  Point to the header.
3.  At the left, click Delete Delete .

**Tip**: To re-add a header, at the top, point to "Enter site name" and click **Add header**.

## Change header image

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  Point to the header and click **Change image**.
3.  Click **Upload** or **Select image**.
4.  Upload or choose an image and click **Open** or **Select**.
5.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

## Change header size

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  Point to the header and click **Header type**.
3.  Choose a size for the header.
4.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

## Add or change a footer

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  Point to the bottom of the page and click **Add footer** or **Edit footer**.
3.  Enter your text or make other edits.
    - To change the background, point at the highlighted box. At the left, click Background Background .
4.  When done, click anywhere else on the page.
5.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

To hide a footer, point to the footer, and at the bottom left click Hide  Hide .

## Change your site info settings

#### Show or hide a page last updated time

1.  On your computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  In the top right corner, click Settings Settings .
3.  In the "Settings" window, go to **Viewer tools**.
4.  Turn “Show page last updated time” on or off.
5.  At the top right, click **Publish**.
