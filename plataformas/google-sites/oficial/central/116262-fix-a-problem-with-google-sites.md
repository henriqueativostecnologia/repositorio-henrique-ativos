# Fix a problem with Google Sites

If you can't see a site or page, you can try to fix it.

## "You need permission"

You'll see this error if you don't have permission to see an item on the page.

Here are some steps you can try:

- **Switch accounts**: You’re currently signed in as . If you have more than one Google account, or an account for school, work, or your website, [switch to a different account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977).
- **Contact the site owner**: Ask the site owner to change what you can see on the site.
- **Ask for permission to see a file**: If you can't see a Google Doc, calendar, video, or other file, [ask the file owner for permission](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6211862).
- Ask for help on the [Google Sites Help Forum](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/sites).
- **Contact your administrator**: If you have a Google Account through work or school, [contact your admin for help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6208960).

#### Can't use Google Sites

Here are some steps you can try:

- **Switch accounts**: You’re currently signed in as \[email placeholder\]. If you have more than one Google account, or an account for school, work, or your own website, [switch to a different account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1721977).
- **Check your browser**: Use a browser that [works with Google Sites](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/6372874).
- Ask for help on the [Google Sites Help Forum](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/sites).
- **Contact your administrator**: If you have a Google Account through work or school, [contact your admin for help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6208960).

#### "404" errors

You'll see this error if you visited a site or page that doesn’t exist, or that you don’t have permission to see.

If you use Google Sites for school or work, [contact your admin for help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6208960).

You can also ask for help on the [Google Sites Help Forum](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/sites).

#### Report a problem or illegal activity

You can report a Google Site if you think it breaks one or more of our [Program policies](https://www.google.com/intl/en/sites/help/program_policy.html).

1.  In your Google site page, at the top right, next to “Settings,” click More More and then **Report abuse**.
2.  Select the reason.
3.  Click **Submit**.

[Learn how to report illegal activity.](https://support.google.com/legal/troubleshooter/1114905)
