# Redesign your Google sites

You can change the look and style of your site at any time.

## Choose a theme for your site

## Use pre-made themes

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Themes**.
3.  Under "Created by Google," select a pre-made theme.

## Import a custom theme from another site

1.  On a computer, open a site in [Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new).
2.  At the right, click **Themes**.
3.  Under "Custom," select Import theme .
4.  Select any site and then Select a theme.
5.  Click **Import theme**.

###

### Use custom themes

With custom themes, you can:

- Create and apply different backgrounds and text colors throughout your site.
- Set default text styles.
- Add brand images or favicons.
- Customize other site properties such as:
  - Buttons
  - Dividers
  - Links
  - Image carousels
- Change the site width.
- Change the color of your menu text and background.
  - **Tip:** To change your menu's location, click **Settings** Settings and then **Navigation**.

#### Create a custom theme

1.  On a computer, [open a site in Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new) .
2.  On the right panel, click **Themes**.
3.  Under "Custom," select Create theme  Plus .
4.  Enter a name for your theme.
5.  Select **Next**.
    - You can also add a logo and banner image.
6.  Select a color palette  and then  **Next**.
    - To choose specific colors for different parts of your site, select **Customize colors**.
7.  Choose your fonts.
8.  Select **Create theme**.
    - In the right panel, you can continue to customize your theme.

#### Edit a custom theme

1.  On a computer, [open a site in Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new) .
2.  On the right panel, click **Themes**.
3.  Under "Custom," select a theme.
4.  At the top right corner, select More More and then **Edit**.

#### Show & customize frame

1.  On a computer, [open a site in Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new) .
2.  After you create a custom theme, on the right panel, click **Themes**.
3.  Under "Custom," select a theme.
4.  At the top right corner, select More More and then **Edit**.
5.  Under "Spacing," check the "Show frame" checkbox.
6.  Select the color of the frame.

**Important:** To use page frames, you have to place your site menu at the top of the site. Under "Navigation," set the "Mode" to **Top**.

#### Adjust site width

1.  On a computer, [open a site in Google Sites](http://sites.google.com/new) .
2.  After you create a custom theme, on the right panel, click **Themes**.
3.  Under "Custom," select a theme.
4.  At the top right corner, select More More and then **Edit**.
5.  Under "Spacing," select the site width.
    - **Default:** Recommended for sites that have less content and will appear spread out
    - **Full:** Recommended for sites that have more content and will appear densely packed
    - **Wide:** Recommended for sites that fall between default and full

## Change your site's style

**Tip:** You must have more than one page to change the menu location.

#### Change the font

#### If you're using a pre-made theme

1.  On the right, click **Themes**.
2.  Click **Font style** and choose an option.
3.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

#### If you're using a custom theme

1.  On the right, click **Themes**.
2.  Under “Custom,” find the custom theme you want to edit. 
3.  In the upper right corner, select **More**  More   and then  **Edit.**
4.  Click **Text** 
5.  Select a text style you want to edit, and make changes to the font and formatting.
6.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.

#### Change how a section looks

1.  Point to a section on the page. Then, at the left, click Background Color .
2.  Choose a style or add a photo.

### Move the menu to the top or left

**Tip**: You must have more than one page to change the menu location.

1.  In the top right corner, click Settings Settings .
2.  In the "Settings" window, go to **Navigation  and then  Mode.**
3.  Choose **Top** or **Side**.
4.  At the top right, click **Publish**.

### Change the color of the menu

#### If you're using a pre-made theme

1.  In the top right corner, click Settings Settings .
2.  In the "Settings" window, go to **Navigation and then  Color.**
3.  Choose **Transparent,** **White,** or **Black. **
4.  At the top right, click **Publish.**

#### **If you're using a custom theme**

1.  On the right, select the “Themes” panel.
2.  Under “Custom,” find the custom theme you want to edit. 
3.  In the upper right corner, select **More** More   and then  **Edit.**
4.  Click **Navigation**.
5.  Under "Background," change the color of the menu, or select "Transparent at top."
6.  To publish your changes, at the top right, click **Publish**.
