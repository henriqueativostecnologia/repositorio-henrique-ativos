# What you need to use Google Sites

You can view Google Sites on a computer, phone, or tablet. When you edit your site, you must use a computer.

## Browsers

You can view your sites with the 2 most recent versions of the following browsers:

- Chrome
- Firefox
- Internet Explorer
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari

When you edit your site, make sure:

- You use [Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/) version 24 or up, or the latest version of Firefox or Safari.
- [Cookies](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416) and [JavaScript](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/114662) are turned on.
