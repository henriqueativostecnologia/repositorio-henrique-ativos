# Use Analytics with your site

You can collect basic data from your website with Analytics.\*

## Step 1: Get an Analytics Property ID

1.  If you don't already have one, [sign up for an Analytics account](https://www.google.com/analytics).
2.  [Find your Analytics Property ID](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9310895).

## Step 2: Add the Analytics Property ID to your site

1.  On your computer, open a [Google Sites](https://sites.google.com/new).
2.  In the top right corner, click Settings Settings .
3.  In the “Settings” window, go to **Analytics.**
4.  Enter a valid Analytics Property ID.

It can take up to 24 hours for data to show up in Analytics.

## Step 3: See your data

1.  Open Analytics.
2.  View your data. [Find out how to use Analytics](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/2604608).

**Tip:**

- Publishing a custom path to a page or renaming a page will create a new URL and might affect data tracking with Analytics.
- If site viewers reject cookies for tracking usage, the data about your site's visits and activity might be inaccurate and underestimate actual usage. [Learn more about cookies](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/9396802).
