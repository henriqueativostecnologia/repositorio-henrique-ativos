# Cookies notification in European Union countries

European Union (EU) laws require giving EU visitors information about cookies used on your site. In many cases, these laws also require obtaining consent.

We have added the following notice on your site to help meet these regulations:

“This site uses cookies from Google to deliver its services and to analyze traffic. Information about your use of this site is shared with Google. By clicking "accept," you agree to its use of cookies.”

The notice lets visitors know about the use of certain Site and Google cookies on your site, including Google Analytics. Learn about [Google’s privacy practices](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/) and how [Google uses data on partner sites](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/).

If you’re a site owner or editor, and use Google Analytics for your site then site viewers are shown the cookie consent dialog. You can use Google Analytics with your site with the following steps:

1.  Add a Google Analytics ID in **Settings** and then **Analytics**.
2.  To get an Analytics ID, [use Analytics with your site](https://support.google.com/sites/answer/97459).

If you're a site viewer:

- You can see the consent notice if your EU location requires notification of nonessential cookies.
- Click **Accept** to consent to nonessential cookies for the site.
- Click **Reject** to decline nonessential cookies for the site.
