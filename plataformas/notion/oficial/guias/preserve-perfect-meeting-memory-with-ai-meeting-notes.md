## [Get started with AI Meeting Notes](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/preserve-perfect-meeting-memory-with-ai-meeting-notes#get-started-with-ai-meeting-notes)

AI Meeting Notes is built right into Notion, so you save yourself the hassle of juggling separate transcription tools that don't connect with the rest of your work. You can take meeting notes in two ways:

- **As a block inside a page.** Your notes sit next to the rest of the page's content, like a project doc or an agenda.

- **As their own page.** Your notes are a full Notion page that you can share on its own, store in a database, and find anywhere in your workspace.

To get started, simply type `/meet` or click the `Meet` pill at the bottom of a new Notion page. This adds meeting notes as a block. When you start notes from a meeting notification, the compose menu, or the `Meetings` tab in your sidebar, Notion creates them as their own page instead. Meeting notes created from `/meet` are embedded on the page, but you can expand them into a full page and share the meeting note separately from the rest of the page.

For more details, learn how to [take AI Meeting Notes in Notion → ](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes)

### [Weave AI Meeting Notes into your daily schedule](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/preserve-perfect-meeting-memory-with-ai-meeting-notes#weave-ai-meeting-notes-into-your-daily-schedule)

AI Meeting Notes is quick to set up and works seamlessly with how and *where* you already work. When you connect your Notion calendar to your workspace, all your meetings appear right on your Notion homepage—making it easy to access meetings, details, and notes without ever leaving Notion.

Access your meetings and take notes directly from your Notion homepage.

**Here’s how you can get set up:**

- **Access your meeting notes from anywhere—** Whether you’re rushing to your next call or catching up between meetings, AI Meeting Notes is right there on your Notion homepage— alongside your daily schedule. For example, if you forgot to attach AI Meeting Notes to your event, you can still launch it directly from the menu bar at the top of your screen during a Zoom call.

- **Choose where your notes live—** In your Notion calendar settings, set a default location for new meeting notes, like a dedicated meetings database. That way, every conversation is automatically saved in one organized place, ready when you need it.

- **Meeting notes pages come with built-in details—** `Title`, `Date`, and `Attendees`. If your note is linked to a calendar event, these fill in automatically and show up as properties in your database, so your notes stay organized without extra setup. If you don't have a default meetings database yet, Notion creates a private `My meetings` database for you. You can move a note to a different meetings database when it belongs with a team or project.

Set it and forget it with auto-share and auto-add

Select your preferred meeting notes database in your calendar settings, then enable AI Meeting Notes to automatically appear each time you create a new page.

- **Add AI Meeting Notes to any event—** When creating a new calendar event, just use the AI Meeting Notes and Docs section to attach a notes page. For existing pages, click the microphone icon to turn them into meeting notes, and AI will instantly add the note-taking block to the top.

## [Make your meetings more effective](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/preserve-perfect-meeting-memory-with-ai-meeting-notes#make-your-meetings-more-effective)

Good meeting documentation reduces confusion, keeps everyone accountable, and ensures important decisions don't get lost in the shuffle. Here are five ways to keep your meetings productive and your notes actionable:

Get everyone's okay first

- **Set your agenda beforehand—**Share a clear agenda beforehand in the notes tab so everyone comes prepared. This helps keep discussions focused and ensures you cover key topics in the time available.

- **Pick your meeting style**— Click the three dots menu to choose from four formats: *Auto*, *Sales*, *Stand-up*, or *Team Meeting*. Each one organizes your notes differently to match your meeting type, and you can customize them for your conversation.

Choose the format option for your meeting so Notion AI can organize your conversation more effectively.

- **Capture notes collaboratively and in real time—**Since Notion AI looks at both the notes and transcription when creating summaries, encourage everyone to add their thoughts and highlights in the Notes tab. More input means better summaries!

Use the notes tab to collaborate in real time, enabling Notion AI to synthesize context from both your transcript and notes and generate a truly holistic summary.

- **Tag teammates in action items—**When you jot things down in the Notes tab or share thoughts during the meeting, use `@` mentions to tag teammates—this helps Notion AI turn those mentions into clear next steps everyone can act on.

  Notion AI automatically tags the right people in the summary tab when you tag them in your notes.

Save time by converting action items to tasks

- **Set up a meeting notes database—**Keep all your meeting notes in one place by creating a dedicated database. This makes it super easy to find past notes and helps everyone build better documentation habits, especially when AI Meeting Notes is already part of your templates. Check out our course on [creating a meeting notes database](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-academy/lesson/meeting-notes). Because meeting notes can be their own pages, they fit naturally in a database. Their built-in `Title`, `Date`, and `Attendees` details fill in automatically, so you can sort and filter your meetings without typing in the basics yourself. You can also turn an existing database into a meetings database. Existing pages stay the same. Turn your database into a meetings database from its settings and choose which date and attendees properties to use. New pages in the database can then be meeting notes. For recurring meetings, move the meeting note to the right database and choose to save future notes from that event series there too.

Set up a meeting notes database to organize your meetings and make them easy to find—all in one place within your workspace.
