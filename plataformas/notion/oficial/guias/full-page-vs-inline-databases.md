# Full page vs. inline databases

## Databases are the key to unlocking some of Notion's most powerful features. The inline and full-page views give you even more flexibility!

- Notion Basics

3 min video

Vídeo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7o3Hc-7qf2Q

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- There are two ways to display a Notion database: inline with your other content, or full page.

  - Should you need to focus, full-page databases are your best option, giving you easy access to all the menus you need.

  - Compact and neat, inline databases can be tucked under other pages, keeping your workspace tidy. You can easily drag and drop them around your page.

- If you wish, you can transform database entries into simple, non-database pages. To do this, select your entries, and drag them away from your database with your mouse.
