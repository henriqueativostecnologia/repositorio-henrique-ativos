# Using Notion for product roadmaps

## For many companies, important information is scattered across various tools, creating a fragmented picture of what’s on their Product Roadmap. Product teams on Notion don’t have to worry about this problem, because they can create a living plan that connects to company goals, documentation, responsibilities and more.
