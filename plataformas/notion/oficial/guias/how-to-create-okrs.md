# How to create OKRs

## Using this framework for goal setting, your team can assign key results, timelines, owners, and more to these objectives. Plan, track, and organize goals in one place — so your team hits them.

- Goals

15 min video

Vídeo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LY1W_zmJTdQ

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- OKRs (objectives and key results) are a framework for setting goals and keeping track of their progress.

- Notion’s versatility can help you carry out all the planning and tracking of these OKRs.

- Plus, when goals are all in one place, this creates transparency across your entire company.

- This video shows you how to build an OKR database from scratch, and add different database views for each team or quarter.

  - You can assign specific key results, timelines, or owners to each objective.

  - You can neatly link your OKR database in other Notion pages for easy access and updates.

### [Here’s the formula we use in this video to see OKR progress](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/how-to-create-okrs#here%E2%80%99s-the-formula-we-use-in-this-video-to-see-okr-progress)

Remember, it’s specific to the names of the columns used in this video. If you name columns differently, you’ll have to adjust the formula.  
  
`(prop("Current Value")-prop("Initial Value"))/(prop("Target Value")-prop("Initial Value"))`
