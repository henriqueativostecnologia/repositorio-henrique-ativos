# Using Notion for product requirement documents

## Explore how to create a central place for all PRDs, customize templates for easy workflows, bring in important information from other tools, and provide a space for feedback and approval.

- Demo
- Documentation
- Product

4 min video

Vídeo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hI1aSnb6fr0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Notion can help you easily write, share, and collaborate on Product Requirement Documents, or PRDs.

- Create a central place where all PRDs live to provide everyone with full context on projects happening across the team.

- Customize a template for consistent and efficient workflows to ensure each PRD includes all the information needed.

- Integrate important information from other tools directly into Notion.

- Facilitate idea-sharing and feedback through synced blocks, comments, and AI assistance to improve writing or change the tone.

- No matter what you're building, Notion's flexibility and adaptability make it easy to plan and work, improving team organization and focus.
