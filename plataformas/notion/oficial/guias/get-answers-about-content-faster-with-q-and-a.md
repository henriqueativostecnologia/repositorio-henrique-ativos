# Get answers about work content faster with Notion Agent

## Learn how to use Notion AI to find answers, understand information in your workspace, and get more out of its capabilities.

- Notion AI

10 min read

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your company's Notion workspace stores all the information that teams and individuals rely on — product roadmaps, PTO policies, onboarding docs, and more.

Answers to important questions live inside these pages, but team members often spend longer than they should hunting for vital information.

Notion AI surfaces answers immediately, so you can leverage all the information in your Notion workspace.

For employees at Remote (a global HR platform of over 1,000+ distributed teammates), it used to take 5-10 minutes to find an answer to every question, multiple times a day. They're now using Q&A across their entire workspace to get answers in seconds.

Instead of digging for information and context-switching throughout the day, you can stay focused.

In this guide, we'll show how Notion AI helps everyone work faster and move forward with work independently.

## [Notion Agent gives you the answers you need in seconds](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a#notion-agent-gives-you-the-answers-you-need-in-seconds)

Regardless of your role, function, or company size, you probably spend a fair amount of time searching for the information you need to do your work.

Even with a comprehensive knowledge base in Notion, team members don't always know how or where to find what they need. Often, information lives in a separate place from where they're working, or doesn't exist in a form they can make sense of. So they ping a DM or email to a colleague and have to wait for an answer.

As a result, progress stalls and focus is broken. Team members are blocked until they get their answers, and the knowledge-keepers of your company are bombarded with routine questions that pull them away from more impactful work.

But Notion Agent acts as a personal assistant to each team member so you can spend less time searching for vital information.

Thanks to its ability to search thousands of docs in seconds, Notion Agent gives team members what they need, in a fraction of the time it takes to DM your colleague and get a response.

## [Ask your Notion Agent anything](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a#ask-your-notion-agent-anything)

Whenever a question about work arises, you can consult Notion Agent first to find out if the answer is already documented in Notion.

To open Notion Agent, click on the circular face in the bottom right of your Notion window and start typing your request into the text box as a naturally-worded question.

Your agent can surface relevant content from pages you have access to, summarize the main takeaways from one or more pages, and even answer more complex specific questions about what the content means.

#### Ask Notion Agent to

- **Search for mentions of a particular topic across the workspace** — For example, asking your agent to "find mentions of plans for new user education" which might surface training materials for employees.

- **Answer onboarding questions** — Even the most robust onboarding page will lead to questions. Your agent is an onboarding buddy for your new hires to ask anything, from how to set up their 401k, to what to do when they get locked out of the office.

- **Get up to date with a meeting you missed** — Couldn't make yesterday's standup? Ask your agent to summarize what was discussed and find tasks assigned to you after the meeting.

- **Suggest explanations for results of a campaign** — Seen unusually fast growth in one market? Your agent can help you make sense of results. Ask "why have we experienced such high organic growth in Korea?". It can look at the results of marketing campaigns, paid ads, user research and client feedback that relates to Korea, and suggest possible explanations.

With access to all the information stored in your workspace, Notion Agent understands your OKRs, sprint commitments, campaign goals, and everything else you use Notion for. When you ask a question, it will provide an accurate answer from the library of information you've built as a company.

## [How to get more value from Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a#how-to-get-more-value-from-notion-ai)

Notion AI helps you find and make sense of information across your workspace using natural language. Ask questions, get answers based on your workspace content, and connect ideas across documents. You can also connect external tools and enable web to pull from context beyond your workspace.

### [Writer assists you in drafting, editing, brainstorming and research](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a#writer-assists-you-in-drafting-editing-brainstorming-and-research)

To edit with AI on a page, select the text or blocks you want to change, then choose `Edit with AI`. Use it to draft and edit text, brainstorm ideas, research topics, and more. To draft something from scratch, open your Notion Agent from the sidebar or the bottom corner and describe what you want.

Notion AI can understand the context of the page you’re currently on. You can ask it to make changes to the content you selected, but it won’t necessarily take wider workspace information into context.

### [Autofill extracts key information at database level](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a#autofill-extracts-key-information-at-database-level)

Autofill is a database property that can summarize, extract insights, translate content from a database entry.

In the context of project management, it can summarize the progress of a project or task, and the results will update as new tasks are added and lifecycle changes.

It helps you glean high-level insights from a database at a glance.

Writer, Autofill, and Notion Agent: three distinct AI tools in your workspace

## [Grow a company knowledge hub that supports team members in their work](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/get-answers-about-content-faster-with-q-and-a#grow-a-company-knowledge-hub-that-supports-team-members-in-their-work)

To maximize the value you get from Notion Agent, your company's Notion workspace needs to contain all the information your team members need to get on with their work and understand their roles.

If you’re still building out your organization’s workspace, here are some tips to bear in mind:

- **Create a company home or wiki containing key information about your company** — Your [company wiki](https://www.notion.so/help/guides/build-a-docs-first-culture-with-a-beautiful-team-wiki-powered-by-a-database) might contain process documents, company policies, your mission statement and values, and overarching goals.

- **Build wikis for each team** — In addition to the company wiki, each [team should have their own homepage](https://www.notion.so/help/guides/team-wiki) where relevant resources are easy to find.

- **Use our project and task management system** — You can duplicate Notion’s [Projects & tasks template](https://www.notion.so/help/guides/getting-started-with-projects-and-tasks) into your workspace. With projects and tasks connected, Notion Agent will be able to answer questions about project status and workload.

- **Import content from other tools** — If you’ve switched from another tool, you can import content from [Confluence](https://www.notion.so/help/guides/migrate-from-confluence-to-notion) or [Asana](https://www.notion.so/help/guides/import-projects-asana-connected-work), you can import your content straight into Notion in just a few clicks. You can also create [synced databases](https://www.notion.so/help/guides/synced-databases-bridge-different-tools) and pull information from other tools such as Github and Jira.
