# A designer's ultimate guide to using Notion

## Explore our comprehensive guide to equip you with the skills to streamline your design workflow using Notion. Perfect for designers at any level.

- Notion is a versatile tool for designers, offering a clean surface for content addition, collaboration, task management, and more.

  - It features templates for inspiration, a Design System database for product design and development, and properties around which to organize each entry.

  - Notion databases can be viewed in multiple ways, including galleries, lists, or boards. You can share pages with external parties via a public URL, and embed content from various apps like Figma, Whimsical, Miro, and more.

  - As a designer, you will find custom Notion templates useful for recurring meetings, as well as the ability to add code blocks to any page.
