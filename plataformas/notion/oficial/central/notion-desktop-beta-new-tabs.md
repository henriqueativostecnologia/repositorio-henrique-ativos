# Notion desktop beta: new tabs

In this help doc

## Try a faster, lighter way to work with tabs in the Notion desktop app. The desktop beta is opt-in, and you can go back to the normal app whenever you want.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Notion desktop beta allows you to try new Notion Mac and Windows desktop features early, and share your feedback to help us improve Notion. Joining is your choice, and you can leave at any time. This doesn't change Notion on the web or on your phone.

Before joining a desktop beta, make sure your desktop app is updated to version 7.39.0 or later. You can check for desktop app updates from Notion in the Mac menu bar or File in the Windows menu bar.

You may also need to refresh Notion’s client inside the app. On Mac, press `Command + Shift + R` to refresh the client. This only refreshes the client, and doesn't update the desktop app, so you may need to do both.

## [New tabs beta](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#new-tabs-beta)

With new tabs, all the tabs in a window share one running copy of Notion. Here's what that means day to day:

- Switching between tabs is faster.

- The sidebar stays put instead of reloading each time you switch.

- Your open tabs keep their place, including how far you scrolled and what you had selected.

- On average, Notion uses less memory when you have multiple tabs open.

## [How to join](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#how-to-join)

There are two ways to join the beta.

- You can join any time from `Settings` → `Preferences` → `Desktop beta`.

- The `Try Desktop Beta` button will appear in the tab bar when the beta becomes available. Click it, then choose `Restart and try now`.

Notion restarts when you join the beta, and your open tabs come with you.

## [What happens to your windows and tabs](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#what-happens-to-your-windows-and-tabs)

A few things move around when you join, so it helps to know what to expect:

- Each window holds one workspace. If a window had pages from more than one workspace, it splits into separate windows, one per workspace.

- Tab groups become their own windows.

- If you open a link to a page in another workspace, it opens in a different window.

- Your open tabs carry over, and your old tab layout is saved. If you leave the beta, that layout comes back.

## [How to go back to classic tabs](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#how-to-go-back-to-classic-tabs)

There are two ways to leave the beta and go back to classic tabs. You can leave the beta whenever you want.

- Click the Beta button in the tab bar, choose `Leave beta`, and confirm.

- Turn off `Desktop beta` in `Settings` → `Preferences`.

Either way, Notion restarts, and your classic tabs come back.

## [Send feedback or report a problem](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#send-feedback-or-report-a-problem)

We'd love to hear how it's going. To share your feedback:

- Click the Beta button in the tab bar and choose `Report a bug` or `Send feedback`.

- This opens an email that's already filled in with your app version and system details. Add what happened and send it.

**Note**: Support can't troubleshoot beta issues. If something isn't working and it's getting in your way, leave the beta, then reach out if you still need help.

## [Things that aren't smooth yet in the beta](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-desktop-beta-new-tabs#things-that-aren't-smooth-yet-in-the-beta)

The beta is still a work in progress, so a few things have rough edges:

- AI Meeting Notes on desktop can have trouble with the recording indicator, with pop-up and floating windows, and with ending a recording cleanly if the app restarts.

- Some full-screen views may hide the tab bar.

- The beta is for the Mac and Windows desktop app only.

## FAQs

Will I lose my tabs when I join or leave?

No. Your open tabs carry over, and your old layout is saved and comes back when you leave.

Why did my window split into two?

Each window holds one workspace. If a window had pages from more than one workspace, it splits so each workspace gets its own window. Tab groups also become their own windows.

Does this change Notion on the web or on my phone?

No. The beta is only for the Mac and Windows desktop app.
