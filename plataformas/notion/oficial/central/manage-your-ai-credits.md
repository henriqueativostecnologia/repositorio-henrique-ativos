# Manage your AI credits

In this help doc

## Learn how the Standard and Select credit allowances included with Business and Enterprise plans work, how to track your usage, and how Notion credits can keep your team working.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Business and Enterprise plans give each workspace member a set amount of Notion AI credits each month. Additional usage via [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits) is a separate, workspace-managed balance an owner can turn on so you can keep going once your Standard or Select allowances run out.

- **Standard credits**: Used for work where Notion picks the model for you. Each member gets 1,500 Standard credits a month.\*

- **Select credits**: Used for work where you pick the model yourself, or use a [Custom Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/how-ai-credits-work-for-custom-agents) that isn’t funded by your workspace. Each member gets 500 Select credits a month.\*

\* Your included amount may vary depending on your plan and purchase arrangement. To check your allowance, go to `Settings` → `Notion AI` → `Me`.

[**Auto**](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) **is Notion’s model router.**

It sends each task to the model that best balances quality, speed, and cost, so you don’t have to choose a model yourself.

Standard and Select allowances are separate. Using one doesn’t affect the other. You can’t combine them or transfer them between members. Each resets monthly, and unused credits don’t roll over. Notion lets you know when you’re close to using up your Standard or Select credits. Once you use up all Standard or Select credits, impacted work will pause until your credits reset.

A workspace owner can set up a Notion credit limit allowing you to continue using your workspace’s [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits), which can cover eligible usage beyond the Standard or Select allowances included each month. Notion credits can also pay for high credit consumption models, and [Custom Agents](https://www.notion.com/help/how-ai-credits-work-for-custom-agents) funded by the workspace.

For the full feature list, see [Standard, Select, and Notion credits: eligible features →](https://www.notion.com/help/standard-select-and-notion-credits-eligible-features)

## [What counts toward Standard & Select credits](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#what-counts-toward-standard-&-select-credits)

**Standard credits** cover work where Notion picks the model, like:

- Your [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent) on [Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) (including chat)

- AI Meeting Notes \* (starting December 1, 2026)

- Writing and translation

- AI blocks, formulas, and inline editing

**Select credits** cover:

- Picking a model yourself in Notion Agent instead of leaving it on [Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto)

- Custom Agents you set up for your own use

Some work uses your workspace’s [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits) instead of your Standard or Select credits:

- High-credit-consumption models not included in your plan, such as Fable and Astra

- Custom Agents funded by the workspace

- Running Notion Agent or a Custom Agent through the Notion API or a connected app

## [Track your credit usage & get alerts](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#track-your-credit-usage-&-get-alerts)

You can see how much of your Standard and Select allowances you’ve used, how much is left, and when they reset by going to `Settings` → `Notion AI` → `Me`.

On your mobile phone, you can open your Notion AI settings to check your own credits. Buying credits and the workspace usage dashboard aren't available in the mobile app.

Notion also alerts you in Notion Agent chat when your Standard or Select credits are running low or have run out, so you have time to adjust.

## [What happens when your credits run out](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#what-happens-when-your-credits-run-out)

Standard and Select allowances are separate. Using one doesn’t affect the other.

- **If you run out of Standard credits:** Features that use Standard credits pause until your next monthly reset. Any Select credits you have left won’t cover Standard features. A workspace owner can set up a Notion credit limit allowing you to continue using your workspace’s [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits), which can cover eligible usage beyond the Standard or Select credits included each month.

- **If you run out of Select credits:** Features that use Select credits pause until your next monthly reset, unless an owner allows you to continue with Notion credits. If you still have Standard credits, [switch Notion Agent to Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) to keep using features covered by Standard.

- **If you run out of both:** You can continue with Notion credits if an owner allows it and your workspace has credits available.

**If your subscription is billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play:**

When your Standard or Select credits run out, features that use them pause until they reset at the start of your next billing cycle. You can't buy extra credits with an app-store subscription. To buy extra credits, a workspace owner must first cancel the existing plan, wait for it to fully expire, then move billing to [notion.com](http://notion.com) on a Business or Enterprise plan that includes AI.

## [Ask a workspace owner for more Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#ask-a-workspace-owner-for-more-notion-credits)

If you use up your Standard or Select credits and want to keep going, you can ask a workspace owner to raise your credit limit. You pick how many credits you want and add a short reason, so the owner has what they need to decide.

Here's how it works:

- Your request stays pending until a workspace owner approves or declines it. While you wait, the credits included in your plan keep working. Only the work that needs the extra credits pauses.

- If your request is approved, you get a higher limit just for you. It doesn't change anyone else's limit.

- Approving a request raises your limit, but it doesn't buy credits. If your workspace runs out of Notion credits, you still can't spend until an owner adds more.

## [For owners: let members keep going on Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#for-owners-let-members-keep-going-on-notion-credits)

By default, members who use up their Standard or Select allowances have to wait for them to reset. Workspace owners can set up a Notion credit limit letting members keep going using the workspace's [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits), which can cover eligible usage beyond the Standard or Select credits included each month.

To give members more usage, set a credit limit by going to `Settings` → `Notion AI`, and finding the `Credits` tab.

When a credit limit is set, members who use up their Standard or Select allowances will keep going with Notion credits. When this setting is not on, members will need to wait for their credits to reset, or can request more credits.

## [For owners: track credit usage from the dashboard](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits#for-owners-track-credit-usage-from-the-dashboard)

If a workspace owner sets a credit limit allowing members to use [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits) past their Standard or Select allowances, they can [track credit usage in the Notion credits dashboard](https://www.notion.com/help/track-usage-in-the-notion-credits-dashboard) by going to `Settings` → `Access & billing` → `Notion credits`. Each member’s usage appears as its own row so you can see who is spending credits and how much.

Credits spent on high credit consumption models show up in the same rows, so you can see who is using them and how much they cost.

## FAQs

What's the difference between Standard, Select, and Notion credits?

Standard and Select credits come with workspaces on a Business or Enterprise plan.

- **Standard credits** cover work where Notion picks the model.

- **Select credits** cover work where you pick a model yourself, or use a Custom Agent you set up.

- **Notion credits** are a separate, workspace-managed balance an owner can turn on so you can keep going once your Standard or Select credits run out.

Standard and Select credits are separate per-member balances. These balances can't be pooled across the workspace, transferred between members, or exchanged for other credit types. They’re not "Notion Credits" as defined in the [Notion AI and Notion Credit Terms](https://notion.notion.site/Notion-AI-and-Notion-Credit-Supplementary-Terms-fa9034c8b5a04818a6baf3eac2adddbb) and don’t count toward your Notion Credit Limit. The workspace-managed Notion credits described above are "Notion Credits" under those terms.

How many credits does each task use?

It depends on the task. In Notion’s estimates, simple tasks use about 5 credits, medium tasks about 10, and complex tasks about 25. Actual use varies with the task and its context.

See [estimated credits by task](https://www.notion.com/help/auto#compare-credit-use-across-tasks-in-notion) →

What is Auto?

[Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) is Notion’s model router. It chooses the model that best balances quality, speed, and cost for each task, so you don’t have to choose one.

Can I combine or transfer my credits?

No. Standard and Select credits are separate, and each person has their own. You can't combine them, move them between people, or trade one kind for another.

When do my credits reset?

Standard and Select credits each reset monthly, at the start of your billing cycle, and they don't roll over.

If you use one up, you can wait for it to reset, or keep going on [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits) if a workspace owner has turned that on. The other keeps working as usual.

How will I know I'm close to running out?

You can track your Standard and Select credits from `Settings` → `Notion AI` → `Usage`.

We'll also notify you as you get close and when you've used up your Standard or Select credits.

Can my workspace get additional Notion AI usage?

Yes. Workspace owners can let members keep using Notion AI beyond their Standard or Select credits by spending [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits).

This is off by default. Workspace owners can turn it on in `Settings` → `Notion AI`.

Which credits does Notion Agent use?

On [Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto), Notion Agent uses your Standard credits. When you pick a model yourself, it uses your Select credits.

How do Custom Agents use credits?

A Custom Agent you set up uses your Select credits, even when you share it with your team. Once your Select credits run out, it uses any extra credits a workspace owner has given you. After that, it switches to your workspace's [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits) automatically (if an owner allows it). An owner can fund the agent with Notion credits at any time. You can also [ask an workspace owner](https://www.notion.com/help/how-ai-credits-work-for-custom-agents#ask-a-workspace-owner-to-fund-your-custom-agent) to do this.

Learn more about how [AI credits work for Custom Agents →](https://www.notion.com/help/how-ai-credits-work-for-custom-agents)

Do connected apps and the Notion API use credits?

Running Notion Agent or a Custom Agent through a connected app or a Notion API uses Notion credits. Using connected apps or a Notion API without running Notion Agent or a Custom Agent does not itself use Standard, Select, or Notion credits, though rate limits may still apply.

Does AI Meeting Notes consume credits?

AI meeting notes uses [Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) and will begin consuming monthly Standard credit allowance starting December. A one hour meeting is estimated to use ~10 Standard credits.

I pay through the App Store or Google Play. How do my credits work?

If your plan includes AI, you get the same monthly Standard and Select credits as Business plans.

You can't buy credits with an app-store subscription. When you run out, that work waits until your credits reset at the start of your next billing cycle. To buy more credits, a workspace owner must first cancel the existing plan, wait for it to fully expire, then move billing to [notion.com](http://notion.com) on a Business or Enterprise plan that includes AI.
