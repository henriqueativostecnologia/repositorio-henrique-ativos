# What are Notion credits?

In this help doc

## Learn what Notion credits are, and the difference between monthly vs annual credits.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/what-are-notion-credits#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notion credits are an add-on for Business and Enterprise plans. They’re shared across your workspace and let you run [Custom Agents](https://www.notion.com/help/custom-agents), [Workers](https://www.notion.com/help/run-custom-code-with-workers), [high credit consumption models](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-ai-models-and-member-credit-spend), and additional Notion AI usage for [eligible features beyond your Standard and Select usage](https://www.notion.com/help/standard-select-and-notion-credits-eligible-features).

If you manage billing in-product, workspace owners can [purchase credits](https://www.notion.com/help/purchase-notion-credits) and track usage in the [Notion credits dashboard](https://www.notion.com/help/track-usage-in-the-notion-credits-dashboard). If you work with an account team, they can help you estimate credit needs and review credit options. If your subscription is billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, you can't buy credits. To buy more credits, a workspace owner must first cancel the existing app-store subscription, wait for it to fully expire, then move billing to notion.com on a Business or Enterprise plan that includes AI.

## [**Monthly vs. annual Notion credits**](https://www.notion.com/help/what-are-notion-credits#monthly-vs.-annual-notion-credits)

There are two types of Notion credits you can buy: monthly and annual. The type of credit you can purchase is tied to your core subscription plan.

**If you manage billing in-product:**

- Users on monthly plans can buy monthly credits.

- Users on annual plans can buy annual credits.

**If you work with an account team:**

- You can buy monthly, annual, or on-demand credits.

- Your credit usage will be drawn in the order of: monthly, annual, then on-demand.

- To purchase credits, work with your account team.

[TABLE]

**Monthly credits** are a good fit if your workspace uses a steady amount of credits each month, like [Custom Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/custom-agents) workflows with predictable usage. Unused credits reset each month.

**Annual credits** are a good fit if you want flexibility throughout the year, like usage that ramps up, spikes, or varies between teams and quarters. They’re also useful for experimentation and time-bound projects. Because you can use annual credits anytime during your subscription year, they cost more and work well as a flexible pool.

## [Billing date](https://www.notion.com/help/what-are-notion-credits#billing-date)

You can view your billing date, as well as your credit reset and expiration dates, in the [Notion credits dashboard](https://www.notion.com/help/track-usage-in-the-notion-credits-dashboard).

## [**How monthly credits work**](https://www.notion.com/help/what-are-notion-credits#how-monthly-credits-work)

Monthly credits cost \$10 per 1,000 credits per month and you can adjust the amount based on your needs.

- **Purchase anytime.** If you run out of credits before the next reset, you can buy more mid-cycle to keep features running. You’ll get the full amount right away, and credits aren’t prorated.

- **Credits reset monthly.** Your credits reset to the amount you purchased. Unused credits don’t carry over to the next month.

- **Match your credits to your monthly usage.** Increases are available immediately, while decreases take effect at the start of the next month.

- **Credits are billed monthly.** Monthly credits are billed alongside your monthly Notion subscription.

## [**How annual credits work**](https://www.notion.com/help/what-are-notion-credits#how-annual-credits-work)

Annual credits cost \$13 per 1,000 credits. Owners on annual Business or Enterprise plans can buy annual Notion credits.

- **Purchase anytime.** Buy annual credits at any time and use the full amount right away. If you need more later, you can add credits without waiting for renewal.

- **Adjust as your needs change.** Increase your annual credit amount anytime, with new credits available immediately. You can schedule a decrease or cancellation for your next renewal. If you work with an account team, you can add credits during your contract term, up to three times a year. To lower your committed amount, renew your contract.

- **Use your existing credits first.** If your workspace has multiple types of credits, Notion uses free credits first, followed by monthly credits and then annual credits.

- **Use credits flexibly throughout your subscription year.** Annual credits remain available until your subscription renews, giving you more flexibility to accommodate changes in usage. Unused credits expire at renewal and don’t carry over, even if you bought them partway through the year.

## [What happens if a credit payment fails](https://www.notion.com/help/what-are-notion-credits#what-happens-if-a-credit-payment-fails)

Your Notion plan and your Notion credit purchases are billed separately. That means a problem with a Notion credit payment doesn't affect your Notion plan, and a problem with your Notion plan payment is handled on its own.

If a Notion credit payment doesn't go through:

- Your team keeps using the credits you've already paid for.

- Credits above the amount you've paid for are put on hold. Once you update your payment details and the payment goes through, the rest of your credits unlock automatically.

- You'll see an alert in Notion when a payment needs attention, so an owner can fix it. You can't buy more credits until all unpaid invoices are paid. You can view invoices on your billing page.

Some payment methods, like bank transfers, take a few days to clear. While a payment is still clearing, your team can keep using credits up to the amount you set out to buy. Nothing is put on hold unless the payment actually fails.

## FAQs

Are annual credits cheaper than monthly credits?

No. Annual credits cost \$13 per 1,000 credits, compared to \$10 per 1,000 for monthly credits.

The benefit is flexibility. You can use annual credits anytime during your subscription year, while monthly credits reset each month.

What happens to unused annual credits at the end of my subscription year?

They expire and don't carry over. If you have a remaining balance, you can reduce your credits for the following year.

Can I buy annual credits on a monthly plan?

**If you manage your billing in-product:** No. Annual credits are only available on annual Business and Enterprise plans. You'll need to switch to an annual plan first.

**If you work with an account team:** You can purchase monthly, annual, or on-demand credits.

What happens to my Notion subscription if a Notion credit payment fails?

Your Notion plan and your Notion credits are billed separately. A failed Notion credit payment only pauses the credits you haven't paid for yet. Your plan, and the credits you've already paid for, keep working while you sort out the payment.

How are Notion credits different from Standard and Select credits?

[Standard and Select credits](https://www.notion.com/help/standard-select-and-notion-credits-eligible-features) come with each Business or Enterprise seat and reset monthly:

- **Standard credits** cover work where Notion picks the model.

- **Select credits** cover work where you pick a model yourself, or use a Custom Agent you own.

- **Notion credits** are a separate, workspace-managed balance owners can purchase. They cover eligible usage beyond Standard or Select credits when an owner allows it, plus high credit consumption models and Custom Agents funded by the workspace.

Monthly Notion credits are purchased for the workspace. They’re separate from the Standard and Select credits included with each seat.

Standard and Select credits can’t be pooled across the workspace, transferred between members, or exchanged for other credit types. They’re not “Notion Credits” under the [Notion AI and Notion Credit Terms](https://notion.notion.site/Notion-AI-and-Notion-Credit-Supplementary-Terms-fa9034c8b5a04818a6baf3eac2adddbb) and don’t count toward your Notion Credit Limit. Workspace-managed Notion credits are “Notion Credits” under those terms.⁠

Can I change my credits before my plan or contract renews?

If you manage billing in-product, you can raise or lower monthly credits each month, and add annual credits anytime (reductions happen at your next service period).

If you work with an account team, you can add credits during your contract term, up to three times a year. You can't lower your committed amount until you renew your contract.
