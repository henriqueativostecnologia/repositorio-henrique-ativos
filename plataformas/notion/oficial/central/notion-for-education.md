## [What's included in the Education Plan](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#what's-included-in-the-education-plan)

There are two types of free Education Plans:

- **The Education Plan for individual students and teachers:** a free plan for a one-member workspace. It’s available for students or teachers signed in with their education institution's email per the WHED.

- **Education Plan for student organizations:** a free plan for a workspace with multiple members. It’s available for verified student orgs who have submitted an application [here →](https://www.notion.so/notion-for-student-orgs-apply)

## [Upgrade to the Education Plan](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#upgrade-to-the-education-plan)

**Note**: Each school email can claim the Education Plan once, for one workspace. Upgrading it to a Plus, Business, or Enterprise plan permanently ends that workspace's Education eligibility.

### [Individual students & teachers](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#individual-students-&-teachers)

Individual students and teachers at accredited colleges and universities can upgrade to the Education Plan for free at any time.

To stay on the plan, verify your student status once a year with your school email. You can keep using the plan as long as you can verify that you have a valid education email.

We'll remind you before your anniversary date. If you can no longer verify with a valid education email, your workspace (including all your content) moves to the Free Plan.

Before you upgrade, make sure of the following:

- **You’re signed in with your education institution email.** Your school email must be the main email on your Notion account. A school email added as an extra address won't count. If you’re using a personal email address with your Notion account, you can change it to your educational email address following the instructions [here →](https://www.notion.so/help/account-settings#account-security-settings)

- **Your school email hasn't already claimed the offer.** Each school email can claim the student offer just once, for one workspace.

- **You can verify your school email each year.** To stay on the Education Plan, your school email must stay active and remain the main email on your Notion account.

- **Your school is an accredited institution recognized by and listed in the World Higher Education Database** [**here →**](https://www.whed.net/home.php)

- **You’re on the Free Plan.** If you’re currently paying for the Plus Plan, you can still take advantage of this offer. Downgrade to the Free Plan first by going to `Settings` in your sidebar → `Billing` → `Change plan`.

- **You only have one member in your account.** The Education Plan for individual students and teachers can only contain one member. Remove any additional workspace owners and members before upgrading.

- **You're upgrading using Notion for desktop or Notion for web**, not mobile.

Once you’ve met the above requirements, follow these steps in the desktop or web app to upgrade to the free Education Plan:

1.  Go to `Settings` in your sidebar.

2.  Select `Upgrade plan`.

3.  Select `Get free education plan`. If your school is recognized by our system, you’ll be upgraded automatically.

4.  If you satisfy all requirements above for access to the plan but don't see `Get free education plan`, you can get in touch with [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#027667636f426f6369676c6d766b6d6c2c616d6f) for more help.

You must have a valid educational email address to upgrade to the Education Plan. We don’t accept student IDs or other documentation to verify a student or educator status.

### [Student organizations](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#student-organizations)

Before you apply, make sure of the following:

- **You're a student representing a student organization at an accredited institution recognized by and listed in the World Higher Education Database** [**here →**](https://www.whed.net/home.php)

- **You're a member of the student organization's workspace.**

- **All members in your student organization’s workspace have the same domain as your valid educational email address.**

- **Your organization is recognized by your institution.**

If you want to upgrade to the Education Plan for your student organization’s workspace, you can find more info and apply [here](https://www.notion.so/notion-for-student-orgs-apply). Only one application per organization will be approved.

Once you’ve applied, our team will reach out as soon as we can to let you know if you’ve been approved.

## [Set up your workspace](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#set-up-your-workspace)

If you’re brand new to Notion, check out the articles [here](https://www.notion.so/help/category/new-to-notion) to learn the basics. You can also use our templates to build a foundation for your education workspace. To find our templates for students and teachers:

1.  Go to `Marketplace` in your sidebar.

2.  In the dropdown, select `School`. You can also use the search bar to look for a more specific template.

3.  Select a template to see what it looks like.

4.  When you’re ready to use the template in your own workspace, select `Get template`.

## [Verify your student status each year](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#verify-your-student-status-each-year)

To keep using the free Education Plan, you’ll need to verify your student status once a year with your school email. Once a year, we'll ask you to confirm that you still have a valid education email. There's no limit to how many times you can re-verify. Your school email must stay the main email on your Notion account.

## [If you can't verify your school email](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education#if-you-can't-verify-your-school-email)

If you can no longer verify with a valid education email, your workspace (including all your content) moves to the Free Plan. You don't need to do anything, and your workspace features change to match what's included in the Free Plan.

## FAQs

Are there any discounts for students?

The Education Plan for individual students is a free plan for a one-member workspace. It’s available for students signed in with their education institution's email per the World Higher Education Database.

With the Education Plan, students can organize plans, tasks and notes across school and life with included pages & blocks, file uploads, more features to customize and publish your site, and 30-day version history.

What's Notion's student organization plan?

Student-led organizations at select verified higher education institutions around the globe can get access to our Education Plan and add all of their org members to collaborate entirely for free.

With the Education Plan, student orgs can upload blocks and files, add members to their workspace, get 30-day page history, add up to 100 guests, and more, free of charge.

[Sign your org up for the free offer here](https://www.notion.so/notion-for-student-orgs-apply).

**Note:** This discount is only eligible for workspaces for volunteer student organizations. Workspaces that are not designated for student organizations with will not qualify for the discount.

How do I sign up for the free Education Plan?

You’re eligible for the free Education Plan if:

1.  You’re a student or educator at an accredited college or university.

2.  You have a school email address. We don’t accept student IDs or other documentation to verify.

You’re not eligible if:

1.  You’re a K-12 student or K-12 educator. You can still use our Free Plan with included storage for free! Learn more on our [pricing page](https://www.notion.so/pricing).

2.  Your Notion account is associated with a personal email address, such as a gmail.com or email address.

Eligible users can sign up for the plan by following these steps:

1.  Go to `Settings` in your sidebar.

2.  Select `Upgrade plan`.

3.  Select `Get free education plan`. If your school is recognized by our system, you’ll be upgraded automatically.

How long does the free plan for individual students last?

There is no set end date. To stay on the free Education Plan, verify your status once a year with your school email. You can keep using the plan as long as you can verify that you have a valid education email.

Do I need to verify my status again after I upgrade to the Education Plan?

Yes. You'll verify your status once a year to stay on the plan. There's no limit to how many times you can re-verify. We'll remind you before your anniversary date.

Does my school email need to be the main email on my account?

Yes. Your school email must be the main email on your Notion account. A school email added as an extra address won't qualify you for the plan.

I’m a student who loves Notion. How can I get more involved?

Amazing! Thanks for being part of our community. The [Notion Campus Leaders Program](https://notion.notion.site/Notion-Campus-Leaders-Program-5817b00cbaa244bca9e0e498804cbab4?pvs=4) enables students around the world to share and teach Notion at their universities, while building strong Notion communities on campus. We encourage you to [attend an upcoming CL event](https://www.notion.so/notion/Notion-Campus-Leaders-Program-5817b00cbaa244bca9e0e498804cbab4?pvs=4#e736841f35fb47f0b408a47d7ea5fd1f) on your campus. Or, if you’re feeling extra passionate, you can [apply](https://www.notion.so/notion/Notion-Campus-Leaders-Program-5817b00cbaa244bca9e0e498804cbab4?pvs=4#e642b1878294419282aeb5c7ee9456ce) to become a Campus Leader yourself!

Can I claim the student offer more than once?

No. Each school email can claim the student offer once, for one workspace. Here's what that means:

- Your school email works for the student offer one time, on one workspace.

- Once your school email has claimed the offer, you can't use it to claim the offer again on a new workspace. This is true even if you delete the first workspace you used it on.

- If a workspace has already been on the Education Plan, you can't upgrade that same workspace to the Education Plan again. This stays true even if you upgrade the workspace to a paid plan and later move it back to the Free Plan.
