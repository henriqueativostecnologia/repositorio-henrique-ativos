# Notion AI settings overview

In this help doc

## Learn how workspace owners use the AI Command Center in Notion AI Settings to manage AI access, controls, credits, and usage.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use Notion AI settings to manage how your workspace uses [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent) and [Custom Agents](https://www.notion.com/help/custom-agents). Manage model controls, credit balances and limits, usage, connected apps, and AI insights in one place.

This article is for workspace owners on the Business or Enterprise plan. If you're a member, read [Manage your AI credits](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-ai-credits) instead.

## [Before you start](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#before-you-start)

- Your workspace needs to be on the Business or Enterprise plan.

- You must be a workspace owner. Some settings, like turning on a model or setting credit limits, are also available to admins.

## [Open Notion AI settings](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#open-notion-ai-settings)

1.  In your sidebar, go to `Settings`.

2.  Select `Notion AI`.

3.  Use the tabs across the top to move between sections.

## [What's in Notion AI settings](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#what's-in-notion-ai-settings)

### [Overview](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#overview)

See how your workspace uses credits by month, day, credit type (Standard, Select, or Notion), or feature. Find members who are nearing their limit or have requested more credits. The tab also lists each agent, its owner, type, number of runs, and credits used. You can change an agent’s model from the table.

### [Controls](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#controls)

Choose which model providers and models people can use with [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent) and Custom Agents. Set the default model for new Custom Agents, and choose who can create agents: everyone, workspace owners only, or selected members and groups. Restricting a model provider changes the quality and efficiency of Auto. Learn more about [restricting a model provider for your workspace →](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-ai-models-and-member-credit-spend#restrict-a-model-provider-for-your-workspace)

### [Credits](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#credits)

See each credit type's balance, reset date, and expiration date. Buy more Notion credits, and set default or custom credit limits for members, groups, and agents. You can also choose `Unlimited` to remove an individual limit, and choose members to help manage and review credit requests.

### [Usage](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#usage)

See a detailed breakdown of credit usage by member, group, and agent to understand where your workspace’s credits are going. Export usage data as a CSV or use the [Admin API](https://www.notion.com/help/admin-apis-for-enterprise-organizations) to create your own reports.

### [Connectors](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#connectors)

See which apps your workspace can connect to Notion AI and which apps are already connected.

### [Me](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#me)

Your own credit usage breakdown and personalization settings, plus a list of the agents you own. All members have this tab.

### [Insights](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#insights)

**Insights is temporarily paused.** Insights help you understand how your team uses AI. This page is disabled for now, so use cases and conversation counts aren't available. Check back soon.

## [Review requests for more credits](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#review-requests-for-more-credits)

When a member or an agent needs more Notion credits, they can send a request instead of waiting on the default limit. Go to `Settings` → `Requests` to review, approve, or decline requests for workspace credits, Custom Agent funding, and higher limits. Learn more in [Review requests for Notion credits →](https://www.notion.com/help/review-requests-for-notion-credits)

## [Who can use Notion AI settings](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#who-can-use-notion-ai-settings)

- Workspace owners and admins on the Business or Enterprise plan can open Notion AI settings, including the `Overview`, `Controls`, `Credits`, `Usage`, `Connectors`, and `Insights` tabs.

- Members can see their own usage and personalization settings under the `Me` tab, and any Custom Agents they own, but can't change workspace-wide controls or limits.

- On a mobile device, use the `Me` tab to check your own credits. Workspace-wide tabs like `Overview` and `Usage`, buying credits, and setting limits need the desktop app or a browser.

**Note**: Only workspace owners can review and approve credit requests, and they can route requests to a chosen group instead of handling them alone. Learn how to [review requests for Notion credits →](https://www.notion.com/help/review-requests-for-notion-credits)

## [Best practices for handling credit requests](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-settings-overview#best-practices-for-handling-credit-requests)

- **Decide who reviews requests ahead of time.** Assign a group or a few people to review and approve requests, so they don't pile up on one person.

- **Find what's driving the spend before you approve.** Start with the member, then look at the agents they own and the model each agent uses. For example, a busy agent running on a costly model may be the real cause. Switching it to a more efficient model can reduce its spend without adding credits.

- **Give access with a limit instead of raising the default.** Assign the requester to a credit limit that fits, or make a new one, so one person or team gets more room while everyone else stays the same. You can set a limit for a whole group at once.

- **Remember approving doesn't buy credits.** Access still depends on the credits your workspace has available. Keep an eye on usage over time so you can plan your budget and buy more before you run low.

- **Encourage** **`Auto`.** When [members use ](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) for everyday work, their credits go further, which means fewer requests.

- **Encourage fund shared agents.** Shared Custom Agents are funded with workspace Notion credits, so they don’t draw down one member's balance.

## FAQs

Does Notion AI settings replace Access & billing?

No. `Access & billing` still covers your Notion plan and payment details. Notion AI settings is where you manage AI-specific controls, credits, and usage.

Can I export the usage data?

Yes. You can export usage data as a CSV or pull it with the [Admin API](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-custom-agents-with-the-admin-api) to bring it into your own reporting.

What happens if my workspace runs out of Notion credits?

Custom Agents and premium models pause automatically until credits reset or an admin adds more. Learn more about [what happens if you run out of credits](https://www.notion.com/help/track-usage-in-the-notion-credits-dashboard).

Can members see what other members or agents are spending?

No. Members only see their own usage and agents under the `Me` tab. Workspace-wide breakdowns in `Overview` and `Usage` are only visible to owners and admins.
