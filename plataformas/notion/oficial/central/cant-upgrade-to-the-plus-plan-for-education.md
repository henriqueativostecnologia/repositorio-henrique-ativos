# Can’t upgrade to the Education Plan

In this help doc

## Try the following fixes if you’re not able to upgrade to the free Education Plan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## [Downgrade to the Free Plan first](https://www.notion.com/help/cant-upgrade-to-the-plus-plan-for-education#downgrade-to-the-free-plan-first)

If you’re already subscribed to a paid Notion plan, you’ll need to [downgrade](https://www.notion.so/help/upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan#downgrade-your-plan) to the Free Plan first in order to see the option to upgrade to the Education Plan for students and teachers. The upgrade option will appear only after the plan has been fully downgraded to the Free Plan at the end of your existing billing term.

## [Your workspace already used the Education Plan](https://www.notion.com/help/cant-upgrade-to-the-plus-plan-for-education#your-workspace-already-used-the-education-plan)

If this workspace has been on the Education Plan before, you can't put it back on the Education Plan. This is true even after you downgrade to the Free Plan. Each school email can also claim the Education Plan only once, for one workspace, even if you delete the first workspace you used it on. Learn more in [Get the Education Plan for students & teachers](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education).

## [Make sure your educational email address is tied to your Notion account](https://www.notion.com/help/cant-upgrade-to-the-plus-plan-for-education#make-sure-your-educational-email-address-is-tied-to-your-notion-account)

Your account might not be tied to a valid educational email address. Make sure you [change your account email](https://www.notion.so/help/account-settings#account-security-settings) to your educational email. Your school email must be the main email on your account. A school email added as an extra address won't count.

## [Remove any other workspace members](https://www.notion.com/help/cant-upgrade-to-the-plus-plan-for-education#remove-any-other-workspace-members)

Your workspace might have multiple workspace owners or members. The Education Plan for individual students and educators can only contain one member. Remove any additional admins or members and try again.

## [Verify your student status each year](https://www.notion.com/help/cant-upgrade-to-the-plus-plan-for-education#verify-your-student-status-each-year)

If you already have the Education Plan, you'll verify your student status once a year with your school email. You can stay on the plan as long as you can verify that you have a valid education email.

## [Contact support](https://www.notion.com/help/cant-upgrade-to-the-plus-plan-for-education#contact-support)

Your educational institution may not be recognized by our system. Our team can help. Email us at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#0e7a6b6f634e636f656b60617a676160206d6163).
