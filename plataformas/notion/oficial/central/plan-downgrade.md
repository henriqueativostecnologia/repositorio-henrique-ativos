# What happens when I downgrade my plan?

In this help doc

## When you downgrade your Notion plan, some of the features available with your previous plan may be affected. Here’s what to expect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## [Downgrade your plan](https://www.notion.com/help/plan-downgrade#downgrade-your-plan)

As your needs change, you might want to downgrade your Notion plan. To do this:

1.  In your sidebar, click `Settings` → `Billing` → `Change plan`.

2.  Select the plan you want to change to, then click `Continue`.

3.  Provide some feedback for us, then click `Downgrade`.

4.  You’ll receive confirmation of your downgrade, and see when your current plan ends.

## [How downgrading impacts your Notion features](https://www.notion.com/help/plan-downgrade#how-downgrading-impacts-your-notion-features)
