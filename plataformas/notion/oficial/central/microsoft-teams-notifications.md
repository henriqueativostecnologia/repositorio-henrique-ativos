# Get Notion notifications in Microsoft Teams

In this help doc

## Learn how to connect Microsoft Teams to Notion so a Notion bot sends your mentions, comments, and other notifications straight to Teams.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/microsoft-teams-notifications#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

You can get your Notion notifications in Microsoft Teams. Connect your Teams account in Notion, and a Notion bot will message you in Teams when there is activity you follow, like mentions, comments, and page invites.

## [Connect Microsoft Teams](https://www.notion.com/help/microsoft-teams-notifications#connect-microsoft-teams)

1.  Go to the workspace switcher → `Settings` → `Notifications` → `Connected apps`.

2.  Find `Microsoft Teams`, select the dropdown, then select `+ Add new account`.

3.  You’ll be redirected to our Microsoft Teams account in your browser. Click through the prompts to add the app and connect to Notion.

4.  You’ll be redirected back to Notion to continue the process. Click through the prompts to allow Microsoft Teams to access Notion.

5.  When prompted, copy the 6-digit code from Notion.

6.  Open Microsoft Teams and find the Notion bot, then enter the 6-digit code shown in Notion into the bot.

7.  Once the code is accepted, your account is connected.

## [What you’ll be notified about](https://www.notion.com/help/microsoft-teams-notifications#what-you%E2%80%99ll-be-notified-about)

A Notion bot sends you a message in Teams when:

- Someone @mentions you, or mentions a group you’re in.

- Someone comments on a page you follow, or replies in a thread you’re part of.

- A page property changes on a database page you follow. You can get all updates, or only important ones like status, assignee, and due date.

- You’re invited to a page.

- Someone asks for access to a page you own.

- You’re added to a teamspace.

These follow your notification settings and your page permissions. You won’t get a notification about a page you can’t open, and mentions of pages you can’t see are removed.

## [Manage your connection](https://www.notion.com/help/microsoft-teams-notifications#manage-your-connection)

- More than one Notion account can send notifications to the same Microsoft Teams account.

- You can manage the connection with bot commands in Teams: `list`, `connect`, `help`, and `signout`.

## [Turn Microsoft Teams notifications off](https://www.notion.com/help/microsoft-teams-notifications#turn-microsoft-teams-notifications-off)

1.  Go to the workspace switcher → `Settings` → `Notifications` → `Connected apps`.

2.  Select the dropdown next to `Microsoft Teams` and select `Off`.

## [Troubleshooting](https://www.notion.com/help/microsoft-teams-notifications#troubleshooting)

- Your connection never finished: open the Notion bot in Microsoft Teams and enter the 6-digit code from Notion. The connection is not complete until you do.

- You’re not getting notifications: go to the workspace switcher → `Settings` → `Notifications` → `Connected apps` and check that Microsoft Teams is connected.

- Messages are slow: notifications can take up to two minutes to arrive, because related updates are grouped together.

- You don’t see the option yet: this is rolling out over time, so it may not be in your workspace yet.

## FAQs

Is this the same as the Microsoft Teams AI Connector?

No. The [Microsoft Teams AI Connector](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-connector-for-microsoft-teams) lets Notion AI search your Teams messages. This feature sends your Notion notifications to Teams. They are set up in different places.

Can I connect more than one Notion account to the same Teams?

Yes. One Microsoft Teams account can get notifications from more than one Notion account.
