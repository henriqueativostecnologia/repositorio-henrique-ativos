# Manage sidebar presets for your workspace

In this help doc

## Sidebar presets allow you to set up ready-made sidebar tabs for your workspace. You can choose which tabs new members get automatically, and which ones they can add on their own.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Presets establish a starting point for members. They don't lock anyone's sidebar, so members can still change, remove, or reset any tab.

**Note**: To set and update workspace presets, you need to be a workspace admin.

## [Default and optional presets](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#default-and-optional-presets)

There are two kinds of presets:

- **Default presets** which are added for every member automatically. New members get them when they join.

- **Optional presets** which aren't added automatically. Members can add them on their own.

Every workspace needs at least one default preset.

## [Create a preset](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#create-a-preset)

1.  Go to `Settings` → `Sidebar`.

2.  Select `Create preset`.

3.  Add the shortcuts and sections you want the tab to hold.

4.  Give the preset a name.

You can also save a sidebar tab you already have as a preset, as long as that tab isn't already tied to one.

## [Rename or reorder a preset](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#rename-or-reorder-a-preset)

- To rename a preset, open it in `Settings` → `Sidebar` and change its name.

- To reorder presets, drag them into the order you want.

## [Move a preset between default and optional](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#move-a-preset-between-default-and-optional)

In `Settings` → `Sidebar`, drag a preset into the `Default` group or the `Optional` group. Presets in `Default` go to every member automatically. Presets in `Optional` are there for members to add themselves.

## [Presets that come from Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#presets-that-come-from-notion)

Some presets come built in from Notion. You can preview these or make your own copy, but you can't rename or change the originals.

To start from a built-in preset, duplicate it. You'll get an editable copy your workspace owns, and you can change that copy however you want.

## [Delete a preset](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#delete-a-preset)

When you delete a preset, it doesn't disappear right away. It moves to a `Deleted` list first, where you can review it. The preset is only removed when you save your changes.

## [What members can do](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-sidebar-presets-for-your-workspace#what-members-can-do)

Presets give members a starting point. Members stay in control of their own sidebar. They can:

- Get default presets automatically when they join.

- Add an optional preset in `Customize sidebar` → `Add tab`.

- Change or remove a tab that came from a preset.

- Reset a changed tab back to its preset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## FAQs

Does every workspace need a default preset?

Yes. You need at least one default preset so new members start with a sidebar.

Is this available on mobile?

These steps are for Notion on desktop only.
