# Manage your inbox with Notion Agent

In this help doc

## Ask Notion Agent to catch you up, triage, and clear your Inbox, without manually reviewing every notification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Instead of scanning and triaging everything in your inbox yourself, you can ask your Notion Agent to read your notifications, summarize what needs attention, group them, and take action - all from a chat.

This works alongside your existing inbox. Notion Agent can help you triage it, but the inbox remains your source of truth for notifications.

## [Prompt Notion Agent from your inbox](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-inbox-with-notion-agent#prompt-notion-agent-from-your-inbox)

You can prompt Notion Agent directly from your inbox:

1.  Open your Inbox from the top of the sidebar.

2.  Click the Notion Agent icon to have your Agent prioritize your notifications. The results will appear in a chat.

You can also ask your Notion Agent about your Inbox from any chat, not just from within the inbox.

## [What Notion Agent can do in your inbox](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-inbox-with-notion-agent#what-notion-agent-can-do-in-your-inbox)

Once you prompt your Notion Agent, it can:

- **Read your notifications** - summarize what's new, what needs a reply, and what can be cleared.

- **Group notifications** - organize by notification type, by read status, by project, or by page.

- **Mark notifications as read or unread.**

- **Archive or unarchive notifications** - including bulk archiving (for example "Archive everything I've already read").

You can ask your Notion Agent questions that require context, like finding all notifications tied to a specific project. Try these prompts:

- Are there any notifications related to \[project name\]?

- Summarize what needs my attention.

- Group my notifications by project.

- Group by notification type.

- Group by read status.

- Archive all my read notifications.

- Show me all @mentions from this week.

## [Grouping options](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-inbox-with-notion-agent#grouping-options)

You can ask Notion Agent to group your inbox notifications by:

- **Notification type** - @mentions, comment replies, page invites, reminder alerts.

- **Read status** - unread vs. already read.

- **Project or page** - to see all updates related to a specific area of your workspace.

## [What Notion Agent can't do in your inbox](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-inbox-with-notion-agent#what-notion-agent-can't-do-in-your-inbox)

There are some notification types Agent can't act on:

- Embedded approval and access-request workflows  - Notion Agent can surface these notifications, but can't approve, deny, or complete the action on your behalf.

- Notion Agent can't create new notifications or change your notification settings.

## [Tips triaging your inbox with Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/manage-your-inbox-with-notion-agent#tips-triaging-your-inbox-with-notion-agent)

- **Start broad, then narrow.** Ask "What's new in my inbox?" first to get an overview, then follow up with specific actions like archiving or grouping.

- **Use grouping to prioritize.** If you have a lot of notifications, ask your Agent to group by type or by read status so you can focus on @mentions and replies first.

- **Bulk archive to keep things clean.** Once you've reviewed everything, ask your Agent to archive all read notifications to keep your Inbox tidy.

- **Agent results appear in chat.** Notification results and actions show in the Agent chat transcript (they’re not buried in output), so you can see what your Agent did and act on it inline.
