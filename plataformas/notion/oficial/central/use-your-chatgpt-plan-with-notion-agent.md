# Use your ChatGPT plan with Notion Agent

In this help doc

## Connect your ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go account to use your plan with Notion Agent.

[Jump to FAQs](https://www.notion.com/help/use-your-chatgpt-plan-with-notion-agent#faq)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

If your workspace is on the Business or Enterprise plan and you have a ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go account, you can connect your ChatGPT account to Notion. Once connected, [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent) can use your ChatGPT plan instead of your Notion plan. You’ll use your ChatGPT plan only when you select a specific GPT model. The model picker shows which plan is in use before you send a message.

## [Before you start](https://www.notion.com/help/use-your-chatgpt-plan-with-notion-agent#before-you-start)

Each person connects their own ChatGPT account. Before you connect, check that:

- Your Notion workspace is on the Business or Enterprise plan.

- You're a member of the workspace (guests can't use this).

- You have a ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go account.

- You’re using [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent). This feature doesn’t work with [Custom Agents](https://www.notion.com/help/custom-agents).

## [Connect your ChatGPT account to Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/use-your-chatgpt-plan-with-notion-agent#connect-your-chatgpt-account-to-notion-agent)

Connect your account in `Settings` or from the model picker.

**Connect from Settings**

1.  Go to `Settings` → `Notion AI` → `Usage`.

2.  Under `Connected subscriptions`, select `Connect` next to ChatGPT.

3.  Sign in to ChatGPT and allow Notion to connect to your account.

4.  Return to Notion.

**Connect from the model picker**

1.  In Notion Agent, open the model picker.

2.  Under `Subscriptions`, select `Connect ChatGPT plan`.

3.  Sign in to ChatGPT and allow Notion to connect to your account.

4.  Return to Notion.

## [How sessions work](https://www.notion.com/help/use-your-chatgpt-plan-with-notion-agent#how-sessions-work)

After you connect your account, Notion Agent can use either your ChatGPT plan or your workspace’s Notion plan allowance. The model you choose determines which plan gets used. The model picker shows which plan is in use before you send a message.

- **Select a specific GPT model:** use your ChatGPT plan for the session.

- **Select Auto or a non-GPT model:** use your AI credits for the session. [Auto](https://www.notion.com/help/auto) draws on your Standard credits, and picking a non-GPT model yourself draws on your Select credits. Learn more about [Standard and Select credits →](https://www.notion.com/help/standard-select-and-notion-credits-eligible-features)

## [If you run out of ChatGPT usage](https://www.notion.com/help/use-your-chatgpt-plan-with-notion-agent#if-you-run-out-of-chatgpt-usage)

If you hit the limit of your ChatGPT plan, Notion Agent will ask you to switch to your workspace’s [Notion credits](https://www.notion.com/help/category/notion-credits) to continue the session. When your ChatGPT usage becomes available again, Notion Agent will automatically switch back to your ChatGPT plan.

## [Turn off ChatGPT funding or disconnect your account](https://www.notion.com/help/use-your-chatgpt-plan-with-notion-agent#turn-off-chatgpt-funding-or-disconnect-your-account)

You can stop using your ChatGPT plan without disconnecting your account, or disconnect your account from Notion entirely.

1.  Go to `Settings` → `Notion AI` → `Usage`.

2.  Find ChatGPT under `Connected subscriptions`.

3.  To stop using ChatGPT funding, turn off `Use your ChatGPT plan`. To remove your ChatGPT account from Notion, select `Disconnect`.

After you turn off ChatGPT funding or disconnect your account, your [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent) usage will run on your Notion plan again.

## FAQs

Does it work with every model?

No. You'll use your ChatGPT plan only when you select a specific GPT model. If you choose `Auto` or a non-GPT model, the session uses your workspace's Notion plan.

Does it work with Custom Agents?

No. You can only use your ChatGPT account with [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent).

Why don't I see the option to connect to ChatGPT?

Make sure your workspace is on the Business or Enterprise plan, you're using [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent), and you have a ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Go account.

Why did my session use my Notion plan instead of my ChatGPT plan?

This can happen if you selected `Auto` or your ChatGPT usage ran out. In either case, [Notion Agent](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-agent) uses your workspace's Notion plan.

For requests processed using a ChatGPT plan, do Notion’s agreements with its AI subprocessors still apply, or do the personal ChatGPT plan’s terms and data settings apply?

Notion’s agreements with its AI subprocessors still apply, including commitments that customer data is not used for training and applicable no-data-retention terms.

Are conversations that use a ChatGPT plan saved to or shown in ChatGPT chat history or memory?

No. The ChatGPT connection is used only for billing and usage attribution.

Does Notion AI’s standard data-retention policy still apply?

Yes. Notion AI’s data-retention policy applies, including no retention for Enterprise and 30-day retention otherwise.

Can workspace owners see which members have connected a ChatGPT plan?

Workspace owners can use audit logs to see connection and disconnection events. Usage volume is not available.
