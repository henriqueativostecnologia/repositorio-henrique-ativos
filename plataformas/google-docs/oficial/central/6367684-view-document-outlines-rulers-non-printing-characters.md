# View document outlines, rulers, & non-printing characters

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can structure your Google Doc with the “document outline” feature. The outline detects and lists headings from your text to help organize your document. In the outline, you can also add a summary for your document. You can also show or hide rulers and non-printing characters to help format your document.

### Work with a document outline

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/).
2.  To open the outline, click **View ** and then  **Show outline**. The outline opens on the left.

##### Close a document outline

1.  To temporarily close the document outline: At the left, next to "Outline," click **Close document outline ** Back .
2.  To completely hide the document outline: At the top, click **View** And then  **Show outline**. 

**Tip**: If there's a checkmark next to "Show outline," the document outline icon Docs outline icon  will still be visible in the top left of your document. 

##### Add headings to an outline

Google Docs will automatically add headings to the outline, but you can also add them manually.

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/).
2.  Select text for an outline heading.
3.  At the top, click **Normal text**.
4.  Click a heading style. The heading will be added to the outline.

**Note**: Subtitles will not appear in the outline.

[Learn how to re-add a removed heading to your document outline.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6367684#remove_headings)

##### Remove headings from an outline

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/).
2.  On the document outline, point to the heading you want to remove.
3.  On the right of the heading, Click Remove from outline Remove from outline .

##### Re-add a heading to an outline

1.  Select the removed heading text.
2.  Right-click on the selection.
3.  Click **Add to document outline**.

##### Use an outline to move around in a document

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/).
2.  Click **View ** and then  **Show outline**. The outline opens on the left.
3.  Click the heading you want to go to in the document. You’ll move to that place in the document.

##### Find your past document summaries

Summary in Google Docs will no longer be available starting from March 21, 2024.

You can no longer find your document summaries in Google Docs’ left panel. To find the summaries of documents you have access to, open the documents in Drive.

1.  On Google Drive, select the document you want to open.
2.  On the right, click More More .
3.  Click the **Down arrow**  and then  **File information** and then **Details**.
4.  Scroll down to find the Description tab.
    - Users can add, edit or delete the summary from here.

### Show or hide rulers

You can use horizontal and vertical rulers to help you align text, graphics, or tables in your document.  The rulers will automatically show when you open a document. To hide the rulers:

1.  At the top of the document, click **View**.
2.  Click **Show ruler**.

**Tip**: To show the ruler again, click **Show ruler**.

### Show or hide non-printing characters

You can use non-printing characters to see how your document is formatted. Non-printing characters appear in blue and will show you where your document has:

- Paragraph breaks
- Line breaks
- Section breaks
- Page breaks
- Column breaks
- Tabs
- Spaces

To show non-printing characters:

1.  At the top of the document, click **View**.
2.  Click **Show non-printing characters**.

**Tip**: To hide the characters, click **Show non-printing characters** again.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can structure documents with the document outline in Google Docs. The outline detects titles and headings to help frame your document. You can also show or hide rulers.

### Work with a document outline

1.  Open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&hl=en).
2.  To open the outline, tap More More .
3.  Tap **Document outline**. The outline will open on the bottom.
4.  To close, go to the left side of "Document outline" and tap Close document outline  Close document outline .

##### Add headings to an outline

Google Docs will automatically add headings to an outline, but you can also add them manually.

1.  Open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&hl=en).
2.  Tap Edit  Edit .
3.  Select the text you want to make a heading.
4.  Tap Format Format .
5.  Tap **TEXT** and then **Style**.
6.  Tap a heading style.
7.  Tap Done Done . The heading will be added to the outline.

**Note**: Subtitles will not appear in the outline.

##### Remove headings from an outline

1.  Open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&hl=en).
2.  Open the document outline.
3.  On the heading you want to remove, tap More  More .
4.  Tap **Remove from outline**.

**Note**: You can add a heading back to the outline. Select the text, then tap More More and then **Add to document outline**.

##### Use an outline to move around in a document

1.  Open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&hl=en).
2.  Open the document outline.
3.  Tap the heading you want to go to in the document. You will move to that place in the document.

##### Quickly scroll through documents

1.  Open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&hl=en).
2.  Scroll through your document. If the document is longer than one screen length, a little bar will show on the right.
3.  To scroll, tap the bar. A list of available headings will show.
4.  Drag the bar down on the screen to any heading.
5.  To quickly jump to any section in the document, tap the relevant heading.

### Show or hide rulers

To use rulers, open Google Docs on a computer. [Learn how to show or hide rulers on a computer](6367684#rulers).
