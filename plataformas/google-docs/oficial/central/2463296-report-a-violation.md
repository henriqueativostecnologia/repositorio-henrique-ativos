# Report a violation

You can report behavior that you consider to be a violation of [Google's Terms of Service](https://www.google.com/drive/terms-of-service/) or program policies. [Learn about abuse program policies and enforcement](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/148505).

## Report abuse

You can report abusive content from your computer.

#### Google Docs or Slides

To report a violation:

1.  On your computer, open a file.
2.  At the top, click **Help** and then **Report**.
3.  Select the type of abuse you want to report.
4.  Click **Submit report**.

**Tip:** You can also send feedback if you click **Help Docs improve** or **Help Slides improve**.

#### Google Sheets

To report a violation:

1.  On your computer, open a file.
2.  At the top, click **Help** and then **Report**.
3.  Select the type of abuse you want to report.
4.  Click **Submit report**.

**Tip:** You can also send feedback if you click **Help Sheets improve**.

#### Google Forms

If you’re an editor, to report a violation in a form:

1.  At the bottom left, click Help and then **Help Forms improve**.
2.  Select **Report an issue**.
3.  In the “Choose an option” drop down, select an action you tried to perform.
4.  Add a description.
5.  Optional: To add a screenshot, follow the on-screen instructions.
6.  Click **Next** and then **Send**.

If you’re a responder, to report a violation in a form:

1.  At the bottom left, click Help and then Report .
2.  Choose the type of abuse found in the form.
3.  Click **Submit Abuse Report**.

**Tip:** You can also send feedback if you click **Help Forms improve**.

#### Google Vids

To report a violation:

1.  On your computer, open a file.
2.  At the top, click **Help** and then Report Abuse .
3.  Select the type of abuse you want to report.
4.  Click **Submit report**.

#### Google Drive

You can report PDFs, images, or other content stored in Google Drive.

To report a violation:

1.  On your computer, open a file.
2.  At the top, click **Help** and then **Report**.
3.  Select the type of abuse you want to report.
4.  Click **Submit report**.

**Tip:** You can also report a violation from a notification.

- Android or iOS device notification: Expand the sharing or comment notification and tap **Report and block**.
- Sharing email notification: Find the "Does this item look suspicious?" line above the email footer and click **Report**. You can report the file or block the sender even if you no longer have access to open the file. You can only report files shared from external organizations.

#### Google Pics

To report a violation:

1.  On your computer, open a Google Pics file.
2.  At the top, click Open menu Down arrow and then **Help** and then **Help**.
3.  Scroll to the bottom.
4.  Select **Send feedback**.
5.  Add a description.
6.  Optional: To add a screenshot, follow the on-screen instructions.
7.  Click **Send**.

Reports don’t guarantee removal of the file or any other action on Google’s part. Content that you disagree with or deem inappropriate is not always a violation of Google’s Terms of Service or program policies.

## Actions we may take on abusive material

- Remove the file from the account.
- Restrict sharing of a file.
- Limit who can view the file.
- Disable access to one or more Google products.
- Delete the Google Account.
- Report illegal materials to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

## Appeal a violation

If a file you own has a violation notice, you can [request a review of the violation](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2463328).
