# Zoom or change your document view

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can change how you view a document, presentation, or spreadsheet, like making the text bigger or hiding the toolbar at the top.

To zoom in a slide, you can use [keyboard shortcuts.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1696717)

### Zoom in or out

### Change the zoom on a Google Docs or Sheets file

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/) or a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  In the toolbar, click **100%**.
3.  Choose how big you want your text or enter a number from 50 to 200.
    - **Tip:** In Google Docs, to make the document as wide as the browser window, click **Fit**.

### Change the zoom on a Google Slides file

1.  On your computer, open a presentation in [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/).
2.  In the toolbar, click **Fit**.
3.  Select a zoom option from the dropdown or enter a number from 25 and 1600.
    1.  **Tip:** To make the canvas as wide as the browser window, click **Fit**.

### Change your view

1.  On your computer, open a document, spreadsheet, or presentation.
2.  On the toolbar, click **View**, then click an option.
    - **Full screen**: Hides the menu and toolbar. To exit full screen, on your keyboard, press **Esc** or **Escape**. If you're using a touch screen, touch and hold to open the context menu  **and then**  **Exit full screen**.  
    - **Show print layout**: This option is only available in Google Docs that are [in pages format](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/11528737). See your headers, footers, and margins between pages as if you’d printed the document.

**Tip:** Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides remember your most recent view. When you change your view, it doesn’t change for anyone else looking at the file.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can change how you view a document, presentation, or spreadsheet, like making the text bigger or hiding the toolbar at the top.

To zoom in a slide, you can use [keyboard shortcuts.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1696717)

### Zoom in or out

You can make a file look bigger or smaller in Google [Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc), [Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc), or [Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides&referrer=utm_source%3Dslides_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dslidess_hc) with zoom.

- To zoom in, pinch open.
- To zoom out, pinch closed.

To zoom in a slide, you can use [keyboard shortcuts.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1696717)
