# Work with links & bookmarks

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can use links and bookmarks to help people find content or move around your file in Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/), or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/).

### Use links

You can add, update, or remove links in Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/), or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/).

##### Create a link

1.  Open a doc, sheet, or slide.
2.  Click where you want the link, or highlight the text that you want to link.
3.  Click **Insert** and then **Link**.
4.  Under "Text," enter the text you want to be linked.
5.  Under "Link," enter a URL or email address, or search for a website.
6.  Click **Apply**.

**Tip:** You can add multiple links in a single cell in a spreadsheet. To add multiple links in a cell, highlight the text you want to link. Then, at the top, click Insert link  Insert link or press **CTRL + k or  ⌘ + k** on your keyboard.

##### Change or remove a link

1.  Open a doc, sheet, or slide.
2.  Click the link you want to remove.
3.  To remove the link in Google Docs or Slides, click **Remove**. To update the link, click **Change** and choose a new link or text.
4.  To remove a link in Google Sheets, right-click the cell with the link and click **Unlink**. To change the link, right-click the cell and click **Edit link**.

##### Link to a range of cells in a spreadsheet

 

1.  Open a [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Click the cell you want to link.
3.  Click **Insert** and then **Link**  and then ** **Select a range of cells to link  Grid . 
4.  Select the range of cells you want to link to.
5.  Click **Ok ** and then ** Apply**.

### Get info about linked documents & websites

You can preview thumbnails and request access for documents linked in Google Docs.

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com).
2.  In the top left, click **Tools ** and then ** Preferences**.
3.  You can either:
    - Turn on this setting: Check **Show link details.**
    - Turn off this setting: Uncheck **Show link details**.

**Tip:** If you don’t have access to the document, you’ll get the option to request access.

### Use bookmarks

You can use bookmarks to link within a document to a specific section or page. Bookmarks aren't available in Google [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/) or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/).

##### Create a bookmark

1.  Open a [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Click where you want the bookmark.
3.  Click **Insert** and then **Bookmark**.

**Tip**: To remove a bookmark, click the bookmark  and then  **Remove**. 

##### Link to a bookmark

1.  Open a [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Select the text you want to link from.
3.  Click **Insert** and then **Link**.
4.  In the window that opens, click **Bookmarks**. A list will show the bookmarks in your document.
5.  Click the bookmark you want to link to  and then ** Apply**.

To remove the link or bookmark, click it, then click **Remove**.

**Tip**: To remove a bookmark, click the bookmark  and then  **Remove**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can insert, update, or delete links in the Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides app.

### Add a link

1.  Open a file in the Google [Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc), [Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc), or [Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides&referrer=utm_source%3Dslides_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dslidess_hc) app.
2.  **Docs:** Tap Edit Edit .
3.  Highlight text or tap the area in the file where you want the link to appear.
4.  In the top right, tap Create Insert .
5.  Tap **Link**.
6.  In the "Text" field, type the text you want to be linked.
7.  In the "Link" field, enter a URL or email address, or search for a website.
8.  To save, tap Done Done .

### Change or remove a link

1.  Open a file in the Google [Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc), [Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc), or [Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides&referrer=utm_source%3Dslides_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dslidess_hc) app.
2.  **Docs: **Tap Edit Edit .
3.  Tap the text, cell, or shape with the link you want to remove.
4.  To remove the link, tap **Remove link**. To change the link, tap **Edit Link **or More More and then **Edit link**.

### Add a bookmark

Bookmarks are not available on Android; you’ll have to use a computer.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can insert, update, or delete links in the Google Docs, Sheets or Slides app.

### Add a link

1.  Open a file in the Google [Docs](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt=9008&ct=docs_hc&mt=8), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets), or [Slides](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id879478102?pt=9008&ct=slides_hc&mt=8) app.
2.  **Docs:** Tap Edit Edit .
3.  Highlight text, tap the cell, or tap the area in the file where you want the link to appear.
4.  In the top right, tap Insert  Insert .
5.  Tap **Link**.
6.  In the "Text" field, type the text you want to be linked.
7.  In the "Link" field, enter a URL or email address, or search for a website.
8.  To save, tap Done Done . 

### Change or remove a link

1.  Open a file in the Google [Docs](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt=9008&ct=docs_hc&mt=8), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets), or [Slides](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id879478102?pt=9008&ct=slides_hc&mt=8) app.
2.  **Docs:** Tap Edit Edit .
3.  Tap the text, cell, or shape with the link you want to remove.
4.  To remove the link, in portrait view, tap Right arrow right arrow and then **Remove link**. To change the link, tap Right arrow right arrow and then **Edit link**.

**Note:** for Google Sheets, when a cell contains multiple links, only the first link would appear when editing the links in the cell. Editing the link will overwrite the existing links.

### Add a bookmark

1.  Open a file in the [Google](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8) [Docs](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8) [app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8).
2.  Tap Edit Edit .
3.  Tap where you want the bookmark.
4.  In the top right, tap Insert Insert   and then  **Bookmark**.

**To copy or remove a bookmark**, tap the bookmark, then tap **Copy link** or **Remove**.
