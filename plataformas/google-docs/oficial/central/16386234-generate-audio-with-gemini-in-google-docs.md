# Generate audio with Gemini in Google Docs

**Important:** This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).

You can use audio generation with Gemini to:

- Listen to audio versions of your document tabs.
- Listen to audio summaries of your documents.
- Add audio buttons to documents.

## Use Gemini to listen to your documents

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
    - Your document should have content before you can generate audio.
2.  To generate audio, you can:
    - At the top, click **Tools** and then **Audio** and then Listen to this tab .
      - Or, in the toolbar, click Listen to this tab .
3.  To generate an audio summary, click Listen to document summary .
4.  To insert an audio button or add an audio chip:
    - **Insert an audio button:**
      1.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Audio buttons** and then Listen to tab .
      2.  Click the inserted button.
    - **Add an audio chip in your selected section:**
      1.  Select the section you want to add an audio chip.
      2.  Enter **@**.
      3.  Select **Listen to tab**.
      4.  Click the inserted button.
5.  You can change the speed and the voice of the audio with the audio player.
    - To change the speed, click Playback speed and then Choose the speed.
    - To change the voice, click More More and then Change voice .
      1.  You can preview and select a voice:
          - Narrator
          - Educator
          - Teacher
          - Persuader
          - Explainer
          - Coach
          - Motivator
      2.  Click **Select**.
6.  Optional: To send feedback, in the audio player, click More More and then Useful audio or Not useful audio .
    1.  If you click Not useful audio :
        1.  Select the issue you found.
        2.  Enter additional feedback.
    2.  Click **Submit**.

**Tips:**

- You can pause, resume, and check how long it takes to read the entire doc.
- You can move the audio player around the screen.
- You can edit the labels of the audio button.
- You can edit the size and color of the button.
