# Find & manage your Gemini in Workspace conversation history

**Important:** This article explains how to manage conversation history for Gemini in Workspace. To manage history in Gemini Apps, [use these instructions instead](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746).

You can find and manage your conversation history with Gemini in:

- Docs
- Drive
- Gmail
- Sheets
- Slides

Your conversation history is app-specific. For example, a conversation with Gemini in Gmail only appears there and not in Gemini in Docs.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yQtfncyURM8

## What you need

To check your conversation history, you need one of the following:

- **A personal Google Account**:
  - [*Google AI Pro*](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/14534406)
  - [*Google AI Ultra*](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16286513)
  - [*Google Workspace Experiments*](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104?hl=en)
- **A work or school account**:
  - You need an eligible Google Workspace plan with access to Gemini Beta. Your administrator controls access to Gemini Beta. If you can't find this feature, your administrator may not have [Beta features enabled for your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14170809).

## Find your conversation history

**Important:** Your conversation history does not include any interactions with the Gemini side panel that occurred before the release of this feature.

1.  On your computer, open a Google Workspace app, like Gmail or Docs.
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  At the top left, click More options .
4.  Under "History," select a recent conversation.
    - To find more conversations, click **View all conversations**.

**Tip:** Your conversations are organized by date, with the most recent at the top.

## Resume a past conversation

When you return to a previous conversation, you can continue where you left off.

1.  On your computer, open a Google Workspace app, like Gmail or Docs.
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  At the top left, click More options .
4.  Under "History," click the conversation you want to open.
    - To find more conversations, click **View all conversations**.
5.  At the bottom, enter a new prompt in the box.
6.  Press **Enter**.

## Rename or delete a conversation

To organize your history, you can give your conversations custom names or remove them permanently.

1.  On your computer, open a Google Workspace app, like Gmail or Docs.
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  At the top left, click More options .
4.  Under "History," hover over a conversation.
    - To find more conversations, click **View all conversations**.
5.  Click More .
6.  Select an option:
    - **Rename:**
      1.  Enter a new title for the conversation.
      2.  Press **Enter**.

    &nbsp;

    - **Delete:** To remove the conversation, click **Delete**.
      - After you delete a conversation, it’s permanent and can’t be undone.

**Tips:** For work or school accounts:

- If "Delete" is disabled, your organization’s administrator has turned off your ability to delete your history.
- By default, your conversation history is kept forever. However, your administrator can set an auto-deletion period for your organization.
- Your administrator can’t recover or access deleted conversations.

## Delete your conversation history automatically

**Important**: For work or school accounts, your administrator controls whether you can change your auto-delete settings. If your auto-delete settings are grayed out, you can't change them.

You can choose how long Google keeps your Gemini in Workspace history. Only inactive conversations are automatically deleted after the time period you select. For example, if an older conversation is still active, it's not automatically deleted.

1.  On your computer, open a Google Workspace app, like Gmail or Docs.
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  At the top left, click More options .
4.  Click **Settings**  and then ** History**.
5.  Under "Delete after," choose an option:
    - **3 months**
    - **18 months**
    - **36 months**
    - **Manual deletion only**
6.  Click **Apply**.

## Learn why some responses aren't saved

Some responses are temporary and don't save to your conversation history. For example:

- System status alerts, such as "You are offline."
- Messages not generated by Gemini, such as a confirmation after you export a response to Google Docs.
