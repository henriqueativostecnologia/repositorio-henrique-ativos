# How to use Google Docs

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Want advanced Google Workspace features for your business?

[Try Google Workspace today!](https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome?utm_source=gwslearningcenter&utm_medium=support&utm_campaign=7068618)

Google Docs is an online word processor that lets you create and format documents and work with other people. [Get our top tips for using Google Docs.](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/9299931)

### Step 1: Create a document

To create a new document:

1.  On your computer, open the Docs home screen at [docs.google.com](https://docs.google.com/document/).
2.  In the top left, under "Start a new document," click Blank New .

You can also create new documents from the URL [docs.google.com/create.](https://docs.google.com/create)

### Step 2: Edit and format

To edit a document:

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).

2.  To select a word, double-click it or use your cursor to select the text you want to change.

3.  Start editing.

4.  To undo or redo an action, at the top, click Undo Undo or Redo Redo .

**Note:** To edit a document on a touchscreen device, like a Pixel Book, double-tap the document to start typing.

You can add and edit text, paragraphs, spacing, and more in a document.

- [Format paragraphs or font](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1663349)
- [Add a title, heading, or table of contents](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/116338)

###  Step 3: Share & work with others

You can [share files and folders](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822) with people and choose whether they can view, edit, or comment on them.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

Want advanced Google Workspace features for your business?

[Try Google Workspace today!](https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome?utm_source=gwslearningcenter&utm_medium=support&utm_campaign=7068618)

You can create, view, and edit Google documents, as well as Microsoft Word® files, with the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs) for Android.

### Step 1: Download the Google Docs app

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Play app Google Play .
2.  Find the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc)  Docs .
3.  Tap Install.

### Step 2: Get started

- [Create a document](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/148833?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&oco=1)
- [Format paragraphs or font](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1663349)
- [Add a title, heading, or table of contents](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/116338)

##### Edit a document

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a document in the Google Docs app  Docs .
2.  Tap Edit Edit .
3.  To select a word, double-tap it. Move the blue markers to select more text.
4.  Start editing.
5.  To undo or redo an action, click Undo Undo or Redo Redo .

### Work with different file formats

You can upload and download files with the Google Docs app for Android.

- **Import**: You can open and edit DOC, DOCX, ODT, TXT, RTF, and HTML files.
- **Export**: You can export DOCX, EPUB, RTF, TXT, PDF, ODT, and as a web page (HTML as a ZIP).

### Step 3: Share & work with others

You can [share files and folders](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822) with people and choose whether they can view, edit, or comment on them.
