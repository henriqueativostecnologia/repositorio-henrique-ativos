# Insert third-party smart chips from other applications

You can preview URLs from a third-party partner in Google Docs and Sheets. You can also create third-party resources in a smart chip with the @ menu in Google Docs.

**Important:** You can only create and use a third-party smart chip if a developer has enabled it for that application. Also, you or your domain administrator will need to install the specific add-on for that application. To [install an add-on](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2942256), go to **Extensions** and then **Add-ons** and then **Get Add-ons**. If you’re unable to install the add-on, you can try contacting your administrator.

## Create a third-party resource in a smart chip

**Important:** To find the third-party application in the integration section, you must have the third-party application add-on installed.

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](http://docs.google.com/).
2.  To create a new resource, enter “@”.
3.  Under the “Integration” section, click your third-party application.
    **Tip:** You can also type the name of the third-party application after the "@" symbol.
4.  Fill out the form.
5.  Click **Create**.

[Create a third-party resource as a developer](https://developers.google.com/apps-script/add-ons/editors/gsao/create-insert-resource-smart-chip).

## Preview link with smart chips

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](http://docs.google.com) or a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets) that has a third-party smart chip.
2.  Hover or click the smart chip to see information about the website.
    **Tip**: If you don’t have the add-on installed, you’ll be able to see the chip, but won’t be able to see more information when you hover over the chip. You can click **Install** to get the add-on.
3.  If you want to:
    - Go to the website, click the smart chip title.
    - Copy the URL, click Copy link .
    - Learn more about the application’s add-on, click Add-on information Information .

[Create preview links with smart chips as a developer](https://developers.google.com/apps-script/add-ons/editors/gsao/preview-links).

### Convert a hyperlink to a third-party smart chip

If you install the add-on for a third-party application, you can also use the URLs in your documents or spreadsheets as smart chips or convert them to smart chips.

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](http://docs.google.com/) or a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets).
2.  Enter a link from a third-party website.
3.  To replace the URL with the smart chip, press **Tab**.
4.  Click the URL to get information about the website.
5.  (Optional) To convert the URL to a smart chip, click the URL, then click **Chip**.
