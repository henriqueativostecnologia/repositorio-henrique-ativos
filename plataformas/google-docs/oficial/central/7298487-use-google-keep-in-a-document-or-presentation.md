# Use Google Keep in a document or presentation

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can create, view, and insert your Google Keep notes in a document or presentation.

### See your Google Keep notes

1.  On your computer, open a document or presentation in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [Google Slides](https://slides.google.com).
2.  At the right, choose Keep  Keep .

### Save text or image as a note

1.  On your computer, open a document or presentation in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [Google Slides](https://slides.google.com).
2.  Highlight and right-click the text or image you want to save as a note.
3.  From the menu that appears, click **Save to Keep**.

### Add a note to a document

1.  On your computer, open a document or presentation in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [Google Slides](https://slides.google.com).
2.  At the right, choose Keep  Keep .
3.  In the side panel, find the note you want to add.
4.  Click and drag the note to your document.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can create, view, and insert your Google Keep notes in a document or presentation.

This feature isn’t available on the [Google Docs app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&hl=en) for Android.
