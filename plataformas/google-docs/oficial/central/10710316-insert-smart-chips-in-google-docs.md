# Insert smart chips in Google Docs

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can put smart chips in your Google Doc to add details about:

- Names of users users with Gmail or Workspace email addresses
- Other Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides files
- Dates or Google Calendar events
- Places and map directions

You and the document collaborators can hover or click on a smart chip in your document to get more information.

You can also insert placeholder chips, time trackers, and dropdowns, and use building blocks to track projects, files, and more.

### Add smart chips for people, files, dates, events & places

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/).
2.  Type "@" anywhere in your document or click the "@" button on a blank line.
    - **Tip:** To find all the chips, click **Insert** and then **Smart chips**.
3.  To narrow your suggestions, select from the list of suggestions or enter letters, numbers, or symbols.
    - **Tip**: To add a people smart chip, start typing the name or email address of the person you want, or type `@me` to add yourself.
    - **Tip:** To add a file smart chip, enter the file name or related keywords.
4.  To add a smart chip that a collaborator can fill in, select "Placeholder chip." Then select the type of information you want your collaborator to add when they fill in the smart chip.
5.  Hover over the chip to view related information.

**Important:** When you mention another user in a smart chip, they don't automatically get access to your document. To grant access to another user, you must [share your document](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822).

### Create and edit date chips

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/).
2.  To add a date chip, type:
    - `@today`
    - `@tomorrow`
    - `@yesterday`
    - `@date`
    - A specific date, such as `@Jan ` or `@1/1/2021`
    - A relative date, such as `@monday`,` @next tuesday`,` `or `@last wednesday`
3.  To edit the:
    - Date: Click the text field.
    - Date format: Click **Settings** Settings .

After you add a date chip, it shows on the doc for all collaborators in the language of the person who added it. Date chips will appear the same to collaborators in all time zones. To see how far away the date is relative to your time zone, hover over the date chip.

### Create Calendar event chips

You can add a smart chip for a Calendar event to see information about the event and join the video meeting from your Google Doc.

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/).
2.  You can either:
    1.  Type “@” and scroll down to "Calendar events".
    2.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Smart chips** and then **Calendar event**.
3.  Choose an event.

**Tip**: If the calendar event has a video meeting and is starting soon or has started already, you can [join the video meeting from your Google Doc](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/10540294). Click the Calendar event chip, then at the bottom right, click **Join**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can put smart chips in your Google Doc to add details about:

- Names of users users with Gmail or Workspace email addresses
- Other Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides files
- Dates or Google Calendar events
- Places and map directions

You and the document collaborators can hover or click on a smart chip in your document to get more information.

You can also insert placeholder chips, time trackers, and dropdowns, and use building blocks to track projects, files, and more.

### Add smart chips for people, files, dates & checklists

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) Docs .
2.  In the document, type: `@`
3.  Select from the list of suggestions or, to find a smart chip, enter letters, numbers, or symbols.
    - To add a people smart chip, start typing the name or email address of the person you want, or type @me to add yourself.
    - To add a file smart chip, enter the file name or related keywords.
4.  To insert a chip, tap on it.

**Tip:** When you mention another user in a smart chip, they don't automatically get access to your document. [Learn how to share files from Google Drive](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822).

##### Create & edit date chips

**Add a date chip**

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) Docs .
2.  To add a date chip, type:
    - `@today`
    - `@tomorrow`
    - `@yesterday`
    - `@date`
    - A specific date like `@Jan `or `@1/1/2021`
    - A relative date like `@monday`, `@next tuesday`, or `@last wednesday`
3.  Tap on the chip to add it to your document.

**Edit a date chip**

1.  Tap the date chip and then tap \[Editing pencil icon\]
2.  Edit the date.
3.  Tap **OK**.

**Tips:**

- Date chips appear the same to collaborators in all time zones.
- To know how far the date is relative to your time zone, tap the date chip.

##### Create checklist chips

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) Docs .
2.  Type: `@`.
3.  Scroll to “Lists.”
4.  Select **Checklist**.

##### Add a dropdown menu

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a document in the [Google Docs app](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) Docs .
2.  Type: `@`
3.  Scroll down and tap **Dropdown**.
    1.  To insert a pre-configured drop down: Scroll down to find options and tap on the one you want.
    2.  To build a dropdown:
        1.  Tap **New dropdown**.
        2.  Enter a name and the color and text for each option.
        3.  Tap **Save.**
