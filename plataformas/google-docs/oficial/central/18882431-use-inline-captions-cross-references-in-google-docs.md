# Use inline captions & cross-references in Google Docs

You can insert inline captions, cross-references, and tables of captions to organize, link, and index visuals (figures, charts, tables, images, or equations) in Google Docs documents like academic papers, reports, and technical documentation.

## Add an inline caption

You can insert inline captions, which act like labels to your visuals, with or without sequential numbering to figures, charts, tables, images, or equations across your document.

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Select a figure, chart, table, image, or equation.
3.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Page elements** and then **Inline caption** **.**
4.  On the right, in the “Inline Caption” section you can:
    - Enter caption text.
    - Select a caption type: **Figure**, **Table**, **Equation**, or **Add custom label**.
    - To add the caption type in the caption check **Include label in the caption**.
    - Select the numbering style.
5.  Select **Insert**.

## Insert a cross-reference

You can create text that links to the caption of a visual element and allows you to navigate directly to it. Unlike traditional links which have static display text, these cross-references can dynamically update their display text based on the caption.

**Important:** You must insert at least one caption in the document before you can add a cross-reference,

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Place your cursor where you want the reference link to appear.
3.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Page elements** and then **Cross reference** and then **Insert cross reference** .
4.  On the right, in the "Add cross reference" section:
    - **Select a** reference type, such as **Figure**, **Table**, **Equation**, or a custom label.
    - In the drop down menu, select from the list of the captions in your document, the caption to link to.
    - Select a display format.
5.  Click **Insert**.

**Update cross-references**

If you update a caption of a visual element, you can refresh to update the link.

- To update one: Click on the linked text and then click Refresh cross-reference Refresh .
- To update all references at once: Click **Insert** and then **Page elements** and then **Cross reference** and then **Refresh all** Refresh .

## Create & manage a table of captions

Generate a structured list that details every captioned element alongside its exact document location.

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Place your cursor where you want to insert the table of captions.
3.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Page elements** and then **Table of captions**.
4.  On the right, in the "Table of Captions" section:
    - Select a "Caption label type" .
    - To add the label name and numbers in the caption check: “Include label and numbers”.
5.  Select **Insert**.

**Update, edit or delete to a table of captions**

- To update a table after modifying your document: Select the table and then click Update table of captions Refresh .
- To delete a table: Select the table and then  click More and then Delete table of captions.
- To adjust the layout Select the table and then click More and then Edit table of captions.

**Tip**: If your table of captions is not displaying numbers: Pageless format hides page numbers and dot or dash separators in a table of captions. To display page numbers and line separators, [switch your document to Pages format.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/11528737)
