# Switch between multiple Google accounts

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can add and switch between multiple Google accounts on Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides.

### On a browser, like Chrome

1.  On your computer, sign in to Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/), [Sheets](https://sheets.google.com), or [Slides](https://slides.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click your **profile photo or email address**.
3.  Click the account you want to use.
4.  A new window will open for this account.

**Tips:**

- If you frequently switch between accounts, learn how to [set up different profiles in Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2364824).
- To sign in to only one account, [sign out of all your accounts](https://accounts.google.com/Logout?service%253Dwise%2526continue%253Dhttps://drive.google.com/accounts?continueUrl%25253Dhttps://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1ef6DnSY74P353AD5lcSlVV9VQXQcOvzY4B_Ot1uTXiY/edit?usp%2525253Ddrive_web%25252526usp%2525253Ddocs_home%25252526ths%2525253Dtrue%252526ddrp%25253D1%252526urp%25253Dhttps://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?passive%2525253D1%2526hl%253Den%2526continue%253Dhttps://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1ef6DnSY74P353AD5lcSlVV9VQXQcOvzY4B_Ot1uTXiY/edit?usp%25253Ddrive_web%252526usp%25253Ddocs_home%252526ths%25253Dtrue). Then sign in to the account you want to use.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can add and switch between multiple Google accounts on Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides.

### In the apps

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the [Google Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc), [Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc), or [Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides&referrer=utm_source%3Dslides_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dslidess_hc) app.
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  Tap your email address.
4.  Choose the account you want to use.

### On a browser, like Chrome

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com/).
2.  In the top right, tap your **profile photo or name**.
3.  Tap **Sign out** or **Manage accounts** and then **Sign out**.
4.  Sign in with the account you want to use.
5.  Open the file in Docs, Sheets, or Slides.
