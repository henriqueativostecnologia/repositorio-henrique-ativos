# Collaborate with Gemini in Google Docs

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

With Gemini in Google Docs, you can:

- [Write and edit with Gemini in Google Docs (Gemini Beta and Workspace Experiments)](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447609).
- [Create personalized documents with Gemini in Google Docs](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15541879).
- [Summarize your files from Google Drive and emails from Gmail](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696#summarize_text_with_gemini).
- [Reference other files to edit or refine content](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696#reference_other_files).
- [Create images](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14206696#create_images).

**Feature availability**

- This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/13952129).
- [Learn about supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14925782).
- This feature is available in Google Workspace Experiments, a trusted tester program for users with personal accounts to try new AI features. [Learn how to get started with Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).

### Identify supported languages

You can use the Gemini in Docs side panel in some languages. [Learn more about language availability](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13607340).

### Get started with Gemini in Docs

An animation that shows how to ask Gemini in Docs about a proposal for a new electric bike

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).

2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .

3.  In the side panel, select a suggested prompt or write your own prompt.

    ##### Select a suggestion

    1.  Select from the list of suggestions.
        - To get more options, select **More suggestions**.
    2.  At the bottom, in the prompt box, replace the example text with the information you want.
    3.  Press **Enter**.

    ##### Enter a prompt in Gemini

    1.  At the bottom, in the prompt box, enter a prompt.
    2.  Press **Enter**.

4.  Optional: To remove your history, click More options More and then **Clear history**.

**Tips:**

- To avoid the loss of your history, insert generated output in the document.
- You lose your conversation history when:
  - You refresh your browser.
  - You close and reopen the document.
  - Your computer goes offline.

### Things to ask Gemini in Docs

##### Summarize text with Gemini

1.  On your computer, open a document from [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
    - When you open a document that has text, on the side panel, you’ll find a summary of your document.
    - To get a summary of your document, ask Gemini.
      - **Examples:**
        - “Summarize this document in 3 bullet points.”
        - “Explain this document like I'm 5 years old.”
      - To summarize a specific section of your document:
        1.  Highlight the text.
        2.  On the side panel, ask Gemini to summarize your text.
3.  You can also:
    - **Add the text to your document:** Click Insert .
    - **Find a different version:** Click Retry Refresh .
    - **Send feedback about the suggested text:** Click Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .

##### Create documents or new text

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/).
2.  At the bottom bar, in the prompt field, enter a prompt that describes the document you want to create.
3.  Optional:
    - [Learn how to reference other files to create content](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15541879#reference_other_files).
    - [Learn how to match a writing style](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15541879#match_a_writing_style).
    - [Learn how to match a document format](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15541879#match_format).
4.  Click **Submit**.
5.  Optional: To review sources used, select Source .

##### Edit or refine content

You can use Gemini to:

- Rewrite specific sections.
- Adjust the tone of your writing.
- Substantiate your arguments with facts.
  - Gemini can pull stats, evidence, and citations directly from your Google Drive, Gmail, or the web into your document.

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Select the text you want to rewrite.
3.  On the floating bar, click Refine .
4.  To refine the content how you want, you can:
    - **Select:**
      - **Match writing style**
        - [Learn how to match a writing style](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15541879#match_a_writing_style).
      - **Enhance**
      - **Rephrase**
      - **More** and then **Shorten**, **Elaborate**, **More formal**, or **More casual**
    - **Enter a prompt:**
      - To target a specific section, enter a prompt like “Add evidence to my Research section.”
      - To review the whole document, you can enter a prompt like “Suggest places where I can better substantiate my argument.”
5.  Review the suggested changes.
    - To apply the changes, click **Accept all**.
    - To reject the changes, click **Reject all**.

**Tip:** If you paste new content to an existing doc and want to improve the text, select **Match writing style**.

##### Create images with Gemini

You can ask Gemini to generate images in Docs.

1.  On your computer, open a document from [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  On the side panel, create your own prompt.
    - **Example:** “Create an image of a dog with glasses.”
4.  On the image that you like, you can hover over to:
    - **Insert an image:** Click Insert .
    - **Find similar images:** Click Generate more .
5.  You can also:
    - **Find more images:** At the bottom, click Generate more .
    - **Change your prompt:** Click Edit prompt edit .
    - **Send feedback about the generated images:** At the bottom, click Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .

##### Reference other files to edit or refine content

You can ask Gemini to use specific files to write, edit, or refine content. For example: “Update the project timeline based on the latest updates from our meeting notes doc.”

1.  At the bottom bar, click Sources and then **Add from Drive** or other location.
    - Or, you can type “@” and then search and select from the menu.
2.  Select one or more files.
3.  Click **Add**.

#### Select where Gemini can search for information

You can select the locations where Gemini can proactively search for content related to your prompt. [Learn how to use sources with Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/16813283).

##### Get help with Google Docs

- “How do I make my document pageless?”

- “Can I add markups to my document?”

##### Get answers from the web

**Important:** To get answers from the web, make sure to include phrases like “Use Google Search” or “Using web search” in your prompt.

- “Write a paragraph describing the weather today in Mountain View, California using web search.”

- “Help me write a paragraph explaining if lightning can strike the same place twice using only information from the web.*”*

##### Use a Gem

1.  On your computer, at the top right, click Ask Gemini .
2.  On the right, in the side panel, click **Gems**.
3.  Click the Gem you want to use.
4.  Select a custom or premade Gem.
    - To start a conversation with the Gem, type a question or prompt at the bottom.

**Tip:** To create a Gem, go to [gemini.google.com](http://gemini.google.com). You can then use your Gem in the Gemini app and Gemini side panel. [Learn how to use Gems in Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780).

##### Generate a spreadsheet

You can use Ask Gemini to create a Google Sheets spreadsheet.

1.  In Google Docs, open Ask Gemini .
2.  In the side panel, describe the spreadsheet you want to build. For example:
    - Create a sheet to track my monthly expenses with columns for date, category, amount, and notes.
    - Create a table for a project tracker in Google Sheets with columns for task, assignee, deadline, and status.
    - Create a 10-day itinerary for a first-time visitor covering Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka.
    - Create a visual dashboard in Sheets of this sales data.
3.  Click Submit

##### Generate a presentation

You can use Ask Gemini to create a fully editable presentation in Google Slides with a single prompt.

1.  In Google Docs, open Ask Gemini .
2.  In the side panel describe the presentation you want to create. For example: "Create a slide on quantum computing for a general audience with a modern design."
3.  Optional: To give your presentation the same style as an existing slide deck, you can use @\<presentation name\> to reference it. For example: "Create a presentation based on @old_project".
4.  Click Submit
    - **Tip**: Keep the tab open until Gemini generates your presentation; this may take up to several minutes. You can switch to other tabs or windows but do not close the existing tab.
5.  Once the presentation is generated, you can open it in Google Slides to view and edit. [Learn more about how to generate presentations with Gemini in Google Slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/17111393).

##### Use Ask Gemini to draft communications

You can use Ask Gemini to draft email or chat responses.

1.  In Google Docs, open Ask Gemini
2.  In the side panel, enter a prompt. For example:
    1.  "Draft an email to Kevin summarizing our progress on Project Clover."
    2.  "Refine this chat message I am sending to Lori: ‘We are good to start Project Clover.’ Make sure it’s professional and concise."
3.  Click Submit

##### Use Gemini to manage Drive files

You can use Ask Gemini to move, rename, share, and copy files in Drive.

1.  In Google Docs, open open Ask Gemini .
2.  IIn the side panel, enter a prompt. For example:
    - “Copy Q2 Operational Plan and name it Q2 Operational Plan v2”
    - "Save the attachment from this email to my Prospects folder"
    - “Share the @Q3 Marketing Plan with @dougm as commenter.”
    - “Who is my Quarterly Review document shared with?”
    - “Create a new folder called Working Drafts and add @December Brainstorming to it”
3.  Click Submit

**Important:** Review suggested file management actions before confirmation.

##### Create a skill with Skill Builder

**Important:** To create and save your skill, make sure to complete Steps 8 and 9 below. If you only generate instructions in Step 5, your skill won’t be created or saved.

1.  On your computer, open a doc in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  Click Tools and then Skill builder .
4.  In the prompt box, describe the skill you want to create. Then, review the skill instructions that Gemini generates.
5.  Optional: Edit the generated instructions as needed.
6.  Share the Google Doc file with others.
7.  When you’ve all agreed on the skill, click Make this skill in Studio .
    - This creates a copy of the skill for each collaborator who clicks this option.
8.  When you’re redirected to Workspace Studio with the new preloaded skill, to enable the skill, at the top right, click **Turn on**.

If you make updates or enhance your skill instructions in Google Doc, you can sync these instructions inside Workspace Studio. To sync updates from your document, at the top right of your skills page, click More options and then Sync with Docs .

**Tip:** If you have your skill open already, the sync option won't show unless you refresh your page.

##### Use skills with Ask Gemini

**Important:** Before you can use a skill, you must create the skill and turn it on in [Workspace Studio](https://studio.workspace.google.com).

You can use skills in your conversation in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/) or [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/?tgif=c).

1.  On your computer, open a file in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/) or [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/?tgif=c).
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  In the message field, type `@` followed by the skill name in your prompt. For example: “@Project Summary.”
4.  Enter your prompt.
5.  Click Submit .

### Give feedback on generated output

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request. Because feedback may be human readable, please do not submit data that contains personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can submit feedback. Your feedback can help improve AI-assisted Workspace features and broader Google efforts in AI.

1.  In the bottom bar, click Leave feedback .
    - Or, in the side panel, click Good response or Bad response .
2.  Select the data you want to share. This includes prompts and outputs.
3.  Enter additional feedback.
4.  Click **Submit**.

To provide general feedback on this feature, at the top, go to **Help** and then **Help Docs improve**.

To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).

    2.  Tap Send .

        - **To download, share, or print the image:** At the top right, tap More More .
