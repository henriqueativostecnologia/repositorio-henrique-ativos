# Learn how to generate an image with Gemini in Google Docs

**Feature availability:**

- This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn how to get started with Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).
- This feature is also available through our trusted tester program, [Google Workspace Experiments](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104).
- [Learn about supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14925782).

To generate cover images for your document with Gemini in Docs, you can use the “Generate an image” option. For example, you can request Docs to create a “Joyful illustration of a desk with a laptop, coffee cup, and plants in front of a window with sunshine pouring in.”

## Use Gemini to generate an image

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).

2.  Click the document where you want to add the image.

3.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Image** and then Generate an image .

4.  In the panel on the right, enter a prompt. For example:

    - "A beautiful photograph of a Hawaiian beach at sunset with palm trees in the distance"
    - "A plate of freshly baked blueberry muffins on a wooden kitchen table next to a large window"
    - "Watercolor painting of hydrangeas in a water pitcher"
    - "A futuristic cityscape with skyscrapers and flying cars set at night with HD details and shadows"

    **Tips:**

    - For better results, consider the subject, setting, distance to subject, materials, or background. For example: "A close up of a boat made out of wood on a lake surrounded by trees at sunrise."
    - Avoid figurative language in your prompt.

5.  To get several suggested images, click **Create**.

6.  Optional: After you click “Create,” you can:
    - **Send feedback about the suggested image:** Under each image, click Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .
    - **Edit your prompt:** At the top of the panel on the right, you can:
      1.  Click the prompt.
      2.  Edit your prompt.
      3.  Click **Create**.
    - **See more suggested images:** Click **View more**.

7.  To insert an image, click on it.

## Edit an image

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Click an image and then Edit image .
    - If this action opens Google Pics instead, your account already has access to Google Pics. [Learn how to edit your images in Docs with Google Pics](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/17499836)
3.  Enter a prompt that describes how to adjust the image. For example:
    - “Place this image within a frame hanging in an art gallery.”
    - “Add a fluffy white cat.”
4.  Click **Create**.
5.  To replace the original image with the new generated image, click **Accept.**

## Use Gemini to generate a cover image

**Important:** Cover images are only available in Pageless mode. When you change your document to Pages mode, the cover image is hidden.

1.  On your computer, open a new document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/?tgif=d).
2.  To insert a cover image you can either:
    - At the top, click **Insert** and then **Cover Image** and then Generate an image .
    - In your document:
      1.  Enter: `@cover image`.
      2.  Click Cover image and then Generate an image .
3.  In the panel on the right, enter a prompt.
    - For example, "A top-down view of a white desk with neatly arranged sticky notes, a pen, pencil, notebook, and potted plant."
4.  Optional: To customize the image you create:
    1.  Click **Add a style**.
    2.  Select the preferred style.
5.  Click **Create**.
6.  To add the image to your document, click a generatged image from the gallery or click **View more**.
7.  Optional: After you generate a cover image, select the cover image. To:
    - **Replace image:** Click Replace image .
    - **Reposition:** Click Reposition .
    - **Find alt text:** Click Alt text .
    - **Delete:** Click Delete Delete .
8.  Optional: After you generate an image, in the panel you can:
    - **Send feedback about the suggested image:** Under each image, click Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .
    - **Edit your prompt:** At the top of the panel, you can:
      1.  Click the prompt.
      2.  Edit your prompt.
      3.  Click **Create**.
    - **To find more suggested images:** Click **View more**.

**Tip:** You can also insert cover images from your computer or a stock gallery. [Learn how to add a cover image](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/97447#zippy=%2Cadd-a-cover-image-to-your-document).

## Give feedback on a generated image

Google Workspace with Gemini constantly learns and may not be able to support your request.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can submit a feedback.

- To provide feedback on the generated input of this feature, click , and follow the prompts.
- To provide general feedback on this feature, at the top, go to Help and then Help Docs improve.
- To report a legal issue, [learn how to report content for legal reasons](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).
