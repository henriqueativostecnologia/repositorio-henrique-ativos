# Create summary videos from documents in Google Vids

**Feature availability**

- This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13952129).
- [Learn about supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14925782).

You can generate summary videos from your documents using Google Vids.

## Generate a summary video from Google Vids

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Vids](https://docs.google.com/videos/create?usp=hc).
2.  In the File menu, click **Docs to video**
3.  Select a file (.doc, .docx, .pdf) and then **Next**.
4.  Optional: You can review the generated script, where each section becomes a scene.
    1.  To delete a section click
    2.  To edit text write directly in the sections/scenes
    3.  To choose the AI voiceover voice click
5.  Click **Create**
6.  You can edit, share or export the generated video.
