# Check your spelling & grammar in Google Docs

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

Spelling and grammar suggestions are available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian.

### Check spelling & grammar

You can check your spelling and grammar, then accept or ignore the corrections. 

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  In the top left, click Spelling and grammar check Spell check . A box will open in the top right corner.
    - To use a suggestion, click **Change**.
    - To ignore a suggestion, click **Ignore**.
    - To accept or ignore all suggestions, click **More ** More ** ** and then ** Accept all** or **Ignore all**.

If you have spelling and grammar suggestions turned on, misspelled words are underlined in red, and grammar suggestions are underlined in blue. [Learn how to turn suggestions on or off](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/12022089?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&oco=1#turn_spelling_suggestions_on_or_off). 

To quickly accept or ignore the corrections: 

- Press **Tab** to accept the suggestion.
- Right-click the red or blue underline. 
  - To accept the suggestion, click the edited word. 
  - To ignore the suggestion, click **Ignore**.
  - To report an incorrect suggestion, click **Feedback on suggestion**.

##### Add a word to your personal spelling suggestions dictionary

You can add words to your personal dictionary so Docs doesn't identify them as misspelled.

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  At the top, go to **Tools ** and then  **Spelling and grammar**  and then ** Personal dictionary**.
3.  Enter a word.
4.  Click **Add**.

You can also add words to your personal dictionary after they’re marked as misspelled.

1.  Open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Right-click the word that's underlined in red.
3.  Click **Add to personal dictionary**.

### Remove a word from your personal dictionary

You can remove a word that you've added to your personal dictionary.

1.  Open the document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  At the top, go to **Tools ** and then  **Spelling and grammar**  and then ** Personal dictionary**.
3.  Find the word you want to remove.
4.  To the right of the word, click Delete  Delete .

### Spell-check your document

1.  Open the Google Docs app Docs .
2.  Open a document.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  In the top right, tap More More and then **Spellcheck**.
5.  Spelling suggestions will show at the bottom:
    - To use a suggested spelling, tap **Change**.
    - To ignore a suggestion, tap **Ignore**.
    - To use the suggestion for the whole document, tap More More and then **Change all**.
    - To ignore all instances of the word, tap More More and then **Ignore all**.
6.  To hide "Spellcheck", tap Close Close .

**Note:** Autocorrect is only available on your desktop computer.
