# Delete files in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

- [Deleting files in Drive for desktop](#d4d_delete_file)

### Learn about files you can delete and how to delete them

**Important:** Once you delete the file by moving it to your Trash, remember that :

- Anyone you shared your file with can continue to access it until the file is permanently deleted.
- Files in your Trash still count towards your storage until they are permanently deleted.

If you're the owner of the file, you can delete the file by moving it to your Trash.

To put a file in the Trash:

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com).
2.  Right-click the file and:
    - Click Move to Trash .
    - If someone else is the owner of the file, click Remove .

### Learn about files you cannot delete

If you're not the owner of a file, you won't be able to delete it. Instead, you will only be able to remove it from your Drive.

To remove a file:

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com).
2.  Right-click the file and click **Remove** .

Other people will have access to the file since you're not the owner.

### Learn how to permanently delete a file

#### Learn about permanently deleted files in Drive

**Important:** If you're the owner of the file, anyone you shared the file with loses access when you permanently delete it. [Learn how to make someone else the owner of your file](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494892).

- When you click **Empty trash**, the files in your trash are deleted forever.
- Files you move to the Trash are deleted forever after 30 days.
- [Learn how to recover a deleted file in Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/1716222).

#### Permanently delete all files in your trash

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com).
2.  On the left, click **Trash**.
3.  Make sure there are no files you want to keep.
4.  At the top right, click **Empty trash** and then **Delete** **forever**.

#### Permanently delete an individual file:

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com).
2.  On the left, click **Trash**.
3.  Select a file.
4.  At the top, click **Empty Trash** and then Delete forever .

**Tip:** If you want others to have access to the file, you can transfer ownership to someone else. [Make someone else the owner of your file](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494892).

### Delete files in Drive for desktop

**Important:** If you back up to Google Photos, only photos and videos will be uploaded. Changes (including deleting images) won't sync between your computer and Google Photos.

If you mirror or stream files between Google Drive and your computer, any files you put in the trash are put in the trash everywhere.

### Learn about files you can delete and how to delete them

**Important:** Once you delete the file by moving it to your Trash, remember that:

- Anyone you shared your file with can continue to access it until the file is permanently deleted.
- Files in your Trash still count towards your storage until they are permanently deleted.

If you're the owner of the file, you can delete the file by moving it to your Trash.

To put a file in the Trash:

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the [Google Drive app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs).
2.  At the bottom right, tap Files Files .
3.  Next to the file you want to delete, tap More More and then **Remove**.

### Learn about files you cannot delete

If you're not the owner of a file:

- You don't have the option to remove the file.
- Others still have access to the file.

### Learn how to permanently delete a file

#### Learn about permanently deleted files in Drive

**Important:** If you're the owner of the file, anyone you shared the file with loses access when you permanently delete it. [Learn how to make someone else the owner of your file](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494892).

- When you click **Empty trash**, the files in your trash are deleted forever.
- Files you move to the Trash are deleted forever after 30 days.
- [Learn how to recover a deleted file in Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/1716222).

#### Permanently delete an individual file

You can permanently delete an individual file or empty your trash. After you delete a file, anyone you've shared the file with loses access to it. If you want others to still access the file, you can [give ownership to someone else](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494892).

1.  In the upper left, tap Menu Menu .
2.  Tap **Trash**.
3.  Next to the file you'd like to delete, tap More More .
4.  Tap **Delete forever**.

#### Permanently delete a file in your trash

1.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu and then **Trash**.
2.  Next to the file you want to permanently delete, tap More More and then **Delete forever**.

#### Permanently delete all files in your trash

1.  In the upper left, tap Menu Menu .
2.  Tap **Trash**.
3.  Make sure that there are no files that you'll want later.
4.  In the top right, tap More More .
5.  Tap **Empty trash**.
