# Insert emojis & special characters

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can add emojis to your Google Docs and Sheets. In Google Docs and Slides, you can also add special characters, like arrows, shapes, or accent marks.

### Insert emojis in your Google Doc

1.  On your computer, open a [*Google Doc*](https://docs.google.com/).
2.  You can either:
    - At the top, click **Insert** and then **Symbols** and then **Emoji**.
    - Enter `@emoji` and press **Enter**.
3.  Choose the emoji you want to add or enter search terms for an emoji.
    - You can also directly search for emojis in the doc by entering `:`and entering search terms. For example, to find a list of cat-related emojis, enter `:cat` and press **Enter**.

To disable emoji insertion through colon characters, go to Tools and then **Preferences**, and uncheck **Insert emojis using the colon character**.

### Insert emojis in your Google Sheets

1.  On your computer, open a [*Google Sheet*](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets).
2.  You can either:
    - At the top, click Insert and then ** Emoji**.
    - Enter `@emoji `and press **Enter**.
3.  Choose the emoji you want to add or enter search terms for an emoji.

### Insert special characters

1.  On your computer, open [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation). You can’t insert special characters directly in Google Sheets.
2.  Open or create a document or presentation.
3.  At the top, click **Insert** and then **Symbols** and then **Special characters**.
4.  Find the character you want to insert:
    - Pick from categories.
    - Enter the character's Unicode value.
    - In the box on the right, write the character.
5.  To add a character to your file, click it.
6.  Optional: If you want to add the special character to a spreadsheet, copy it and paste it in a cell on the sheet.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can add emojis to your Google Docs and Sheets. In Google Docs and Slides, you can also add special characters, like arrows, shapes, or accent marks.

To insert emojis, open [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [*Sheets*](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets)  on your computer. To insert special characters, open [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation) on your computer.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can add emojis to your Google Docs and Sheets. In Google Docs and Slides, you can also add special characters, like arrows, shapes, or accent marks.

To insert emojis, open [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [*Sheets*](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets) on your computer. To insert special characters, open [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/) or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation) on your computer.
