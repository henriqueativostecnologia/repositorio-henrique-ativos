# Learn how to use drawings & markups

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Want advanced Google Workspace features for your business?

[Try Google Workspace today!](https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome?utm_source=gwslearningcenter&utm_medium=support&utm_campaign=179740)

You can create, insert and edit drawings with Google Drawings.

### Create a drawing in Google Docs

1.  On your computer, open a document.
2.  In the top left, click **Insert  and then Drawing  and then New**.
3.  Insert shapes, lines or text with the editing tools.

### Create a drawing in Google Drive

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Drive](https://drive.google.com).
2.  In the top left, click **File  and then New  and then More**  **and then**  **Google Drawings**.
3.  Use the editing tool to insert:
    - Shapes
    - Lines
    - Text

### Insert a drawing from Google Drive

1.  On your computer, open a document.
2.  In the top left, click **Insert  and then Drawing  and then  From Drive**.
3.  Click the drawing you want to insert. 
4.  Click **Select**.

The inserted drawing is linked to the original drawing.

- **Update the drawing:** Click the drawing. In the top right, click Update.
- **Unlink the drawing:** Click the drawing. In the top right, click Unlink Unlink .

### Use the editing tools

You can use the editing tools to add lines, shapes, text boxes and images.

##### Draw a line

1.  At the top of the page, find and click **Select line**.
2.  Pick the type of line you want.
3.  Place the line on your drawing:
    - **Line, Elbow Connector, Curved Connector or Arrow**: Click to start, then drag across the canvas.
    - **Curve or Polyline**: Click to start, then click at each point you want the line to bend. Double-click or complete the shape to finish.
    - **Scribble**: Click to start, then drag across the canvas.
4.  Click **Select**.

##### Draw a shape

1.  At the top of the page, find and click **Shape**.
2.  Choose the shape you want to use.
3.  Click and drag on the canvas to draw your shape.

##### Insert text

1.  At the top of the page, click **Insert**.
    - To place text directly on the canvas as a shape, click **Word** **Art**.
    - To place text inside a box or confined area, click **Text Box** and click where you want to put it.
2.  Type your text and press **Enter**.
3.  You can select, resize and format the word art or text box, or apply styles like bold or italics to the text.

##### Insert an image

1.  At the top of the page, find and click **Image**.
2.  Follow the instructions to pick or upload an image.
3.  You can move and format the image like any other shape.
    - **Move the image**: Drag it on the canvas.
    - **Resize the image**: Drag the blue boxes at the corners.
    - **Rotate the image:** Drag the blue dot that’s outside of the shape. Hold **Shift** to rotate 15° at a time.
    - **Change border color, line thickness, or border/line style**: Use the buttons above the canvas.

### Select and format shapes

1.  On your computer, open a drawing in [Google Drive](http://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top of the page, find and click **Select**.
3.  On the canvas, click the shape, line, or text box you want to change.
    - **Move a shape**: Drag it on the canvas.
    - **Resize a shape:** Drag the blue boxes at the corners.
    - **Rotate a line**: Drag the blue dots at the corners. Lines can be rotated this way.
    - **Rotate a shape**: Drag the blue dot that’s outside of the shape. Hold Shift to rotate 15° at a time.
    - **Change fill color, line color, line thickness, or border/line style**: Use the buttons above the canvas.
    - **Put text inside a shape**: Double-click an enclosed shape and start typing.

### Copy a shape

To copy a shape, hold **Option** (on Macs) or **Ctrl** (on Windows) and drag the copy to a new location.

### Change your drawing size

1.  On your computer, open a drawing in [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Go to the menu and click **File** and then **Page setup.**
3.  Select a size from the dropdown menu. To choose your own size, select **Custom**.

### Hide & show markups

If your document has markups, you can hide and show markups. To hide and show all markups, select an option:

1.  Click **View menu** and then Show markups Checkmark .
2.  Right click anywhere on the document.
3.  Click Hide all markups .

**Tip:** On touchscreen devices, you can tap to show or hide all markups.

### Delete markups

1.  On your computer, open a document with markups.
2.  To delete a markup:
    1.  Right click on the markup.
    2.  When the menu shows, click Delete Delete .

**Tip:** You can use your keyboard to delete a markup. While you select a markup, you can press the **Backspace** button or **Delete** button.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

Want advanced Google Workspace features for your business?

You can create, insert and edit drawings with Google Drawings.

This feature is currently available for Android devices.

### Add a markup

To enter markups mode:

1.  On handhelds, tap Insert Insert and then Add markups .
2.  On large screens, in the top toolbar, tap the Markups toggle .

To add a markup:

1.  Tap the Pen or Highlighter Highlight text from the markups toolbar.
2.  Draw on the document to add a markup.
    - When you add a markup in the document, it links to the nearby content and highlights it briefly.
    - When you edit the linked content, the markup will stretch or shrink.

After you add a markup, you can exit markups mode. On the markups toolbar, tap Close Close .

- On handhelds, in the top toolbar, tap the Checkmark Checkmark .
- On large screens, in the top toolbar, tap the Markups toggle .

### Change the Pen or Highlighter size & color

In the markups toolbar, to adjust size and color, tap the Pen or Highlighter Highlight text .

- To change the markups toolbar position, you can touch and hold, then drag anywhere.

### Hide & show markups

To hide and show all markups, select an option:

1.  While in markups mode, in the markups toolbar, tap the Eye .
2.  At the top right, tap More More .
3.  Turn **Show markups** on or off.

### Delete a markup

To delete a markup, select an option:

- Tap Undo Undo .
- Tap the Eraser and then **Delete**.

**Tip:** While in markups mode, you can tap a markup and then **Delete**.
