# Use templates

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can use Google-created templates like resumes, budgets, and order forms.

Some template features are only available for work or school accounts, and you’re not currently signed in. [To sign in to your work or school account, click here](https://www.google.com/accounts/Logout?continue=https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/docs/answer/148833).

### Use a Google template

1.  On your computer, go to Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/), [Sheets](https://sheets.google.com), [Slides](https://slides.google.com), or [Forms](https://forms.google.com).
2.  At the top right , click **Template Gallery.**
3.  Click the template you want to use.
4.  A copy of the template opens.

Some template features are only available for work or school accounts, and you’re not signed in. ​

On your computer, open a Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/), [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/), or [Forms](https://docs.google.com/forms/).

1.  Open the file you want to copy.
2.  In the menu, click **File and then Make a copy**.
3.  Type a name and choose where to save it.
    - To copy any comments to your new file, click **Copy comments and suggestions** or **Copy comments**.
4.  Click **Ok**.

If you have a Google Account through work or school (that doesn’t end in @gmail.com), [sign in here](https://www.google.com/accounts/Logout?continue=https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/docs/answer/148833).

### Show or hide templates

1.  Open Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/), [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/), [Forms](https://docs.google.com/forms/), or [Sites](http://sites.google.com) homepage.
2.  At the top left, click Menu Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Turn **Display recent templates on home screens** on or off.

### Insert templates in Google Docs

You can find and insert customizable templates in Google Docs for a wide range of use cases, like:

- Blog posts, press releases, business proposals, and journals.
- Project roadmap, marketing plan, and sales emails for business users.
- Interview guide, onboarding guide, and training manual for human resource teams.
- Reading list and class notes templates for education users.

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/).
2.  Click **File** and then New and then **From a template**.
3.  To pick the template of your choice, scroll through the gallery.
4.  Select the template you want.
5.  To preview other tabs of the template, at the top left, select the dropdown.
6.  Click **Insert**.

**Tips:**

- If you already have content in a document, the template will open in a new document tabs.
- To remove a template, you can:
  - At the top left, click Undo Undo .
  - Restore previous version through Version history.
  - Delete the template-related document tabs. [Learn how to use tabs in Google Docs](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15499791).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can use Google-created templates like resumes, budgets, and order forms.

Some template features are only available for work or school accounts, and you’re not currently signed in. [To sign in to your work or school account, click here](https://www.google.com/accounts/Logout?continue=https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/docs/answer/148833).

### Use a Google template

1.  On your Android device, open the Google [Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc), [Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc), [Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides&referrer=utm_source%3Dslides_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dslidess_hc), or [Sites](http://sites.google.com) app.
2.  In the bottom corner, tap New New .
3.  Tap **Choose template**.
4.  Tap the template you want to use.

To use templates for Google Forms, go to [forms.google.com](https://forms.google.com) on a computer.

- To create templates, you need a Google Account through work or school and a computer.
- You can't create templates when you're signed out, but you can make a copy of a file.

[Learn how to make a copy of a file on your Android device](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/49114#make_a_copy).
