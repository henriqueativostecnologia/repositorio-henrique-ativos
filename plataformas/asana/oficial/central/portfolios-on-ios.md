# Portfolios on iOS

**Available on Asana [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [legacy tiers](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Business and Legacy Enterprise.**

With Asana for iOS, you can organize your tasks for the day and communicate with your team from anywhere. This article takes a look at creating and using portfolios on iOS. Download the [Asana app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/asana-work-in-one-place/id489969512) for your iPhone and iPad from the App Store. Asana's mobile app is only compatible with iOS 16 or later.

[Portfolios](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolios-overview?language=en_US) help you keep track of projects across your team or your whole organization. Portfolios let you categorize Asana projects, see and request status updates, and organize projects.

## Creating a portfolio

#### To create a Portfolio:

1.  Navigate to your **Portfolios** page and tap **New portfolio**
2.  Enter the portfolio name and click **OK**
3.  Start adding projects or portfolios

## Portfolio overview

Once you've created a portfolio and added a few projects, you can keep track of their health in one place.

From the Portfolio view, you can view the following:

- Each project's latest status
- The project's progress as a percentage
- Each project's due date or date range
- Custom field values
- The number of overdue tasks
- The project owner
