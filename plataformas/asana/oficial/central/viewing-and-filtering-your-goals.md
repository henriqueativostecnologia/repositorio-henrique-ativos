# Viewing and filtering your goals

**Available on Asana [*Advanced*](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [*Enterprise*](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [*Enterprise+*](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [*legacy tiers*](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Business and Legacy Enterprise.**

**Visit our [*pricing page*](https://asana.com/pricing) for more information.**

When navigating and reviewing goals, you can customize your view with filters to focus on what matters the most.

New to Asana Goals? Check out our [Get started with Asana Goals article](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/goals "Get started with Asana Goals").

Video: https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/JHoYYMp7V68

## Company goals

#### From your **Company goals** tab, you can filter by:

1.  Goal owner
2.  Status
3.  Time period

## Team goals

When searching for team goals, you can use the team selector to view goals that belong to specific teams.

#### From the **Team goals** tab, you can filter goals by:

1.  Teams to which the goals belong to
2.  Goal owner
3.  Status
4.  Time period

> You can select teams you’re a member of or public and request-to-join teams. Private teams, you’re not a member of won’t appear in your search.

## My goals

#### From the **My goals** tab, you can filter goals by:

1.  All goals
2.  Open goals
3.  Closed goals

## Using time periods to filter

When filtering company or team goals, you can choose to view goals from specific time periods.

 

To filter using time periods:

 

1.  Click **Time Periods**
2.  Use the arrow to move between different fiscal years
3.  Choose an entire fiscal year, halves, or quarters
4.  Click **Apply**
