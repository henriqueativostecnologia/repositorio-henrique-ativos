# Using Asana for your product roadmap

Marketers rely on product innovation to tell a compelling story to customers, which is why marketing teams need to be constantly up to date with what’s on the product roadmap. Knowing the roadmap means staying ahead of how you’ll bring these new products or features to market effectively.

Use one project in Asana as the source of truth for the product roadmap across your product and marketing teams. Sales and other teams across the company can also follow the project to stay informed about launch plans. Using Asana to track your product launches will ensure everyone knows what is happening, when it’s happening, and who’s responsible for all the different pieces of work.

This article will guide you through building your own product roadmap project, and using Asana to streamline and optimize your product launch process.

### Set up your project structure

- Use [sections](https://help.asana.com/s/article/sections?language=en_US) like "Planning", "In Progress", "Review", "Complete" to organize your project. You could also base your sections on release dates (e.g., month, quarter or sprint cycle).
- Add [tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-tasks?language=en_US) for each launch or campaign and break down each of those launch tasks into [subtasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/subtasks?language=en_US) for specific activities.
- Create a [task template](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-task-templates?language=en_US) that the product team will copy for each upcoming product release. Name the task “Duplicate me - Product launch template.” Add subtasks that can be assigned to the appropriate teammate for each new product release.
- When your product team decides to start building a new feature, they should duplicate and fill out the template task. They should include [basic information](https://help.asana.com/s/article/create-tasks-in-asana?language=en_US) in the task description, like the shipping schedule and an overview of the product functionality. They should also keep the followers of the template task, add a due date, fill out appropriate custom fields, and move the task to the right section of the project. Or simply create a [form](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US) to capture all of this information - you can learn more about that below.
- [Invite members](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-owner-and-members?language=en_US) from other teams to the project. Teammates will be notified in their [inbox](https://help.asana.com/s/article/inbox?language=en_US) when tasks are created in the project so they can stay updated on product changes.

### Customize your project

Add [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields?language=en_US) to track important information like campaign type, target audience, channel, priority, and status.

You can also use timeline to [outline your project's critical path](https://help.asana.com/s/article/critical-path-on-timeline?language=en_US).

## Planning and assigning work

### Use forms for project initiation

Create a [form](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US) to standardize the information collected for each new launch.

Include compulsory fields for objectives, target dates, and key contacts, this ensures essential details are collected upfront.

### Assign tasks and set dependencies

[Assign tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/assign-tasks-to-teammates?language=en_US) to team members to ensure everyone knows who is responsible for what.

Use [task dependencies](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-dependencies?language=en_US) to establish the order of work. You can mark several tasks as dependent on another task. For example, you could make all subtasks dependent upon “Launch messaging.” Once the messaging task is complete, task assignees of dependent subtasks will receive a notification. Your copywriters, social media managers, and email marketers can all begin on their respective subtasks and keep the launch on schedule

### Set milestones and manage workload

Add [milestones](https://help.asana.com/s/article/create-tasks-in-asana?language=en_US#h_01HEJK4705T0HNBS76KM92K8MC) for key events like kickoff, content approval, launch date, and post-campaign analysis.

Use [workload](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolio-workload-and-universal-workload?language=en_US) view to monitor team capacity and balance assignments.

### Convert tasks to projects

If a launch becomes too big for a task, [convert the task into a project](https://help.asana.com/s/article/converting-tasks-into-projects?language=en_US). Keep a reference task in the launches roadmap project that links to the new project so the launch is accounted for in your roadmap.

## Executing and monitoring launches

### Automate routine tasks

Set up [rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules?language=en_US) to automatically move tasks between sections, notify team members, and send reminders.

Use [Asana AI features](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US) like [smart rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-rules?language=en_US) to automate your workflows with custom rules powered by AI, ensuring that routine processes are handled efficiently.

### Integrate with other tools

Connect Asana to tools like [Slack](https://help.asana.com/s/article/slack-and-asana?language=en_US), [Google Drive](https://help.asana.com/s/article/google-and-asana?language=en_US), and [Adobe Creative Cloud](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-for-adobe-creative-cloud?language=en_US) to avoid context switching.

### Monitor progress

Use [portfolios](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolios-overview?language=en_US) to track the status of all ongoing campaigns in one place.

Create [dashboards](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-dashboards?language=en_US) to monitor key performance indicators like task completion rates, adherence to deadlines, and resource utilization. Use [reporting features](https://help.asana.com/s/article/reporting-with-dashboards?language=en_US) to generate insights for weekly or monthly status meetings.

## Post-launch analysis

### Evaluate campaign performance

Use [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields?language=en_US) and [reporting tools](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting?language=en_US) to gather insights and measure success against initial [goals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-goals?language=en_US) and identify areas for improvement.

### Gather feedback

Create a "Feedback" section within your project and add a new [form](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-a-form?language=en_US) to collect feedback from team members and stakeholders about the campaign process. Automate this workflow by adding a rule which will move any feedback form submissions to the "Feedback" section.

### Refine your process

[Convert your project into a template](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-templates?language=en_US) to save time when planning your next product roadmap. Update your project template based on learnings to continually improve your launch process.

By following these steps, you can effectively manage your product launch roadmap, ensure timely deliveries, and maintain high visibility across your teams. This holistic approach empowers your teams to work efficiently, reduce bottlenecks, and achieve better campaign outcomes.
