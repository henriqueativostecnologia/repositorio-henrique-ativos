# Profile settings

Make the most of your Asana profile by personalizing your preferences and setting up a customized experience with everything from notifications to your profile picture.

#### To access **Profile settings**:

1.  From the sidebar, click on your profile photo
2.  Select **Settings** from the menu
3.  Select **Profile**

 

#### From the **Profile** tab, you can:

1.  Change your **profile photo**
2.  Change your **profile name**
3.  Add your preferred **pronouns**
4.  Add a **name pronunciation** audio clip 
5.  Add your **job title**
6.  Add your **department** or **team**
7.  View your **email** address
8.  Add a brief description about yourself in the **About me** field
9.  Set your **personalization settings**
10. Set your **collaborative work management certifications** preferences
11. Set yourself as** out of office** and choose to enable **push notifications** while you're away

## Profile name and photo

A profile name and photo helps your team identify who you are. Your profile name and photo will appear:

- Under the [teams](https://asana.com/guide/help/organizations/team-basics "teams") you belong to in the [sidebar](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/navigating-asana#gl-sidebar "sidebar")
- Next to tasks [assigned](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/understanding-tasks#gl-assignee "assigned") to you (except in your My tasks list) in the [main pane](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/navigating-asana#gl-main-pane "main pane")
- Next to your [comments](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/comments "comments") and [collaborator](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/understanding-tasks#gl-collaborators "collaborator") fields in the [task details pane](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/navigating-asana#gl-right-pane "task details pane")

We accept the following file types under 5MB:

- PNG
- JPEG
- GIF
- BMP

It's best if the image is square and at least 128x128px, but not necessary.

> If you do not upload a photo, we will use your initials as a placeholder.

The information from your **About me** section of your profile can be seen whenever you hover over your profile photo.

## Out of office

When you’re not going to be working or available to collaborate with your teammates, use the **out of office** indicator to let them know. You can create and manage multiple out of office entries to reflect different time away periods.

> This setting is per domain, so you will need to update your OoO in all of the organizations and workspaces you belong to.

#### From your **Profile** tab:

1.  Click **Set out of office**
2.  Enter the date ranges you'll be away
3.  Click **Save**

Once you’ve set your status as out of office, your profile photo will appear dimmed, and your out of office status will appear whenever anyone hovers over your profile photo.

For more information on how to set up your profile, [read this article.](https://help.asana.com/s/article/setting-up-your-asana-profile?language=en_US)

> "Away through today" means that person is out today and will back tomorrow. "Away through Monday" means that they will be back on Tuesday.
