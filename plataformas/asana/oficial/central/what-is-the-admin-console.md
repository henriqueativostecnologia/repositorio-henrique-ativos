# What is the admin console?

The [admin console](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-access-the-admin-console?language=en_US) is your central hub for managing your organization's Asana workspace. It gives you complete control over user management, security settings, billing, and organizational insights—all in one place.

Using the admin console, organizations can ensure efficient management, robust security, and insightful analytics to optimize their use of Asana.

## How to access the admin console

Only admins and super admins can access this feature. Team admins do not have access to the admin console.

#### To access the admin console:

1.  Click on your profile photo.
2.  Select **Admin console** from the drop-down menu.

## Tabs in the admin console

### Insights tab

Get data-driven insights about your team's Asana usage:

- See daily activity data for teams and individuals
- Track project and task activity over time
- Identify where teams are succeeding
- Identify recently added and most influential members

### Users tab

Manage your organization's users and roles:

- View, search, and sort every member of your organization
- View paused, invited or removed users
- Add new members or remove existing ones
- Change roles, status, licenses and divisions
- See member activity, guest accounts, and pending invites
- Track available seats on your plan
- Import and export membership data to CSV for reporting
- Control user access to billable consumption metrics

### Teams tab

View and manage teams across your organization:

- Search for specific teams
- Filter by team status and privacy settings

### Divisions tab

Organize and manage members with divisions.

### Billing tab

Handle your organization's subscription:

- View your current plan details
- Update payment information
- Change plans or add more seats
- Access invoices and billing documentation
- View and reassign your billing owner
- Use timesheets and budgets
- Manage AI Studio
- Contact support for billing-related questions

### Security tab

Implement and manage security measures:

- Set up SAML and two-factor authentication
- Control password requirements and strength
- Manage external sharing permissions
- Set boundaries for integrations and file types
- Configure mobile security settings (downloads, screenshots, copy-paste)

### Products tab

Here you can find the settings for your different products:

- Work Management 
- Client Management
- Service Management
- Command
- Timesheets and budgets

### Settings tab

Configure organizational preferences:

- Customize your organization's name and logo
- Set up custom branding for your workspace
- Manage domain settings and announcements
- Configure organization-wide preferences
- Create a customized onboarding experience

### Apps tab

Manage integrations and connected tools:

- View all third-party apps connected to your organization
- Block or configure app settings as needed
- View last activity and most active members for each integration
- Create new service accounts

### Resources tab

Access training and support materials:

- Find video tutorials and Help Center articles
- Access learning resources without leaving Asana
- Get help with admin console features
- Share training materials with your team

## Asana AI

The **Asana AI** section of the admin console gives admins control over how AI features are used across their organization. From one place, you can monitor usage, manage access, and configure spending limits.

- Super admins can enable or disable **Asana AI** for your entire organization from the admin console. This setting controls whether AI is available at all and is on by default.
- The **Overview** tab shows a snapshot of AI usage across your organization, from active user adoption to use of AI products.
- The **AI requests** tab tracks included and on demand usage for **Dash** and **AI Teammates**. Your organization receives a monthly request allotment that resets automatically. Admins and billing owners receive email alerts at first use, then at 65%, 85%, and 100% of the monthly allotment.
- When your monthly request allotment is reached, users cannot take any request-based actions until the allotment resets or on demand billing is enabled. On demand billing lets usage continue past your allotment at an extra charge per request, with the option to set a spend limit.
- The **AI Studio** tab tracks credit usage for **AI Studio** workflows. On demand billing is also available for credits and works the same way.
- From the **Users** tab, you can see who has AI request access, add or remove licenses at any time, and use bulk export and import to manage access for specific people or teams.
