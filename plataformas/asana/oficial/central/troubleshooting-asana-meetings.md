# Troubleshooting AI Meetings

If you are having issues using [AI Meetings](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-meetings?language=en_US), start with the FAQs below. If you can’t find a fix, ask the chatbot in the bottom right corner of this page. If you think you’ve found a product issue that these steps don’t resolve, [report a bug](https://form-beta.asana.com/?k=pdFi2h5bjBm8irdBhJ8iQ&d=15793206719).

## Detailed Troubleshooting Guides

### I'm unable to find Asana in my system tray

**Why can’t I find the Asana mini-app?**

The mini-app is in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows), and its icon can easily get buried among your other icons. The steps below show you how to check that it’s turned on and how to move it somewhere you’ll always see it.

**How do I make sure the mini-app is turned on?**

Check both of these settings:

1.  In Asana, go to **Settings \> General** and turn on **Tray App Settings**.
2.  On a Mac, open **System Settings \> Menu Bar** and make sure **Asana** is turned on.

**How do I keep the mini-app icon visible?**

Move the icon to a permanent spot where it won’t get hidden. The steps depend on your operating system.

*macOS 26 (Tahoe) and earlier
* Hold **⌘ Command** and drag the Asana icon next to your clock. Icons near the system icons stay put, but icons farther left can get hidden.

*macOS 27 (Golden Gate)
* Hold **⌘ Command** and drag the Asana icon next to your clock. If your menu bar is full, click the **\<\<** arrow at the edge to show hidden icons, including Asana.

*Windows 10 and 11
* Click the **^** arrow in your taskbar tray, then drag the Asana icon onto the main taskbar.

### How do I turn on Asana notifications manually?

If Asana still isn’t showing notifications, you can turn them on yourself in your computer’s settings. Follow the steps for your computer below.

*On a Mac*

1.  Click the **Apple icon** in the top-left corner of your screen.
2.  Click **System Settings**.
3.  In the list on the left side of the window, click **Notifications**.
4.  On the right side, scroll down to the list of apps and click **Asana**.
5.  Turn on the switch next to **Allow notifications**. The switch turns blue when it’s on.
6.  Your change saves automatically, so you don’t need to click a save button. Close the System Settings window by clicking the red circle in its top-left corner.
7.  Quit Asana: click **Asana** in the menu bar at the top of your screen, then click **Quit Asana**.
8.  Reopen Asana: open **Finder**, click **Applications** in the list on the left, then double-click **Asana**.
9.  Try again.

*On Windows 11*

1.  Click the **Start** button (the Windows icon) on your taskbar.
2.  Click **Settings** (the gear icon).
3.  In the list on the left, click **System**.
4.  Click **Notifications**.
5.  Make sure the **Notifications** switch at the top of the page is on.
6.  Scroll down to **Notifications from apps and other senders** and find **Asana**. Turn on the switch next to it.
7.  Your change saves automatically, so you don’t need to click a save button. Close Settings by clicking the **X** in the top-right corner of the window.
8.  Quit Asana: click the **^** arrow on the right side of your taskbar, right-click the **Asana** icon, then click **Quit**.
9.  Reopen Asana: click the **Start** button, type **Asana**, then click **Asana** in the results.
10. Try again.

*On Windows 10*

1.  Click the **Start** button (the Windows icon) on your taskbar.
2.  Click **Settings** (the gear icon).
3.  Click **System**.
4.  In the list on the left, click **Notifications & actions**.
5.  Make sure the **Get notifications from apps and other senders** switch is on.
6.  Scroll down to **Get notifications from these senders** and find **Asana**. Turn on the switch next to it.
7.  Your change saves automatically, so you don’t need to click a save button. Close Settings by clicking the **X** in the top-right corner of the window.
8.  Quit Asana: click the **^** arrow on the right side of your taskbar, right-click the **Asana** icon, then click **Quit**.
9.  Reopen Asana: click the **Start** button, type **Asana**, then click **Asana** in the results.
10. Try again.

### Why do I see a 'Transcription failed to start' error on my Mac?

If you see this error message when you try to transcribe a meeting, the device control and data access setting for Asana may not have applied correctly. Resetting the setting usually clears the error. Before you begin, make sure macOS and Asana Desktop are both up to date.

1.  On your Mac, open **System Settings** and go to **Device Control and Data Access**.
2.  Turn off the setting for **Asana**.
3.  Force quit Asana Desktop.
4.  Return to **Device Control and Data Access** and turn the setting for **Asana** back on.
5.  Open Asana Desktop and start transcription again.
