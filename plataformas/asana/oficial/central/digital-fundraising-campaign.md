# Managing digital fundraising campaigns in Asana

Digital fundraising campaigns require careful planning, execution, and monitoring to achieve successful outcomes. Learn how to leverage Asana features to streamline your campaign management process and maximize donor engagement.

## Getting started

Create your campaign project using one of these methods:

1.  Start with the [digital fundraising campaign template](https://app.asana.com/0/projects/new/template/digital_fundraising_campaign/preview) to save time and follow best practices.

2.  [Create a new project](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/26291209296155-How-to-create-a-project) from scratch.

3.  [Import](https://help.asana.com/s/article/preparing-data-for-csv-import?language=en_US) existing campaign data from a spreadsheet.

4.  Use [Smart projects](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-projects?language=en_US) to have Asana AI create a project for you. Simply enter a project name, and smart projects will generate a detailed description, organize sections, and create relevant custom fields.

## Set up your campaign structure

Organize your project into key phases:

1.  Add [sections](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/26452846500379-Sections) for different campaign stages like Preparation, Launch, Engagement, and Wrap-up.
2.  Create [tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-tasks?language=en_US) for each campaign activity.
3.  Break down complex activities into [subtasks](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14101387791899-Subtasks).
4.  Use [milestones](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-actions?language=en_US#gl-actions-list) to outline critical points like the campaign launch, significant donor events, and end-of-campaign reports. Ensure each milestone has a due date and assigned owner to maintain accountability.

## Plan your timeline

Use [Gantt view](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/18619303446939-Gantt-view) to:

1.  Visualize your campaign schedule.
2.  Set [task dependencies](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-dependencies?language=en_US).
3.  Adjust timelines as needed.
4.  Switch to [timeline](https://help.asana.com/s/article/timeline?language=en_US) view to track [critical path](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/23059681321755-Critical-path-on-timeline) tasks.

## Track campaign progress

Add [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14110099577883-Custom-fields) to monitor:

1.  Donor engagement levels.
2.  Target amounts.
3.  Campaign phases.
4.  Task status.

## Collaborate with stakeholders

1.  Invite external agencies to your project as [guests](https://help.asana.com/s/article/guests-faq?language=en_US).
2.  Use comments for clear communication.
3.  Set up [approvals](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14076905203483-Approvals) for campaign materials.
4.  Share updates using [status updates](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14148168179099-Project-progress-and-status-updates). Use [Smart status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-status?language=en_US) to speed up the process by having [Asana AI](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US) generate the status update for you.

## Collect donor information

Collecting donor testimonials brings your nonprofit’s work to life and inspires others to contribute to your cause.You can easily capture their stories in the format you need by having donors fill out a form.

Once submitted, the form response turns into a task that you can categorize with custom fields so you always have the right quote or story to share, whether it’s via email or your social channels. This is an easy way to showcase supporters and build more momentum.

You can add [forms](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US) to your project to:

1.  Gather donor stories.

2.  Collect testimonials.

3.  Track engagement metrics.

4.  Organize feedback.

## Optimize with Asana AI

Use AI features to enhance campaign effectiveness:

1.  Use [Smart chat](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/26093133817627-Smart-answers) to get answers to campaign questions.
2.  Create [Smart summaries](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/26160590826779-Smart-summaries) of campaign progress.
3.  Use [Smart rule creator](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/26093391969435-Smart-rules) to automate workflows.

## Monitor multiple campaigns

For organizations managing several campaigns:

1.  Use [portfolios](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14212495456539-Portfolios-overview) to track all campaigns.
2.  Monitor [workload](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14075775804827-Portfolio-workload-and-Universal-workload) to balance team capacity.
3.  Create [advanced search reports](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14074042291995-Search-and-search-views) to identify bottlenecks.

## Connect your tools

Integrate Asana with:

1.  [Mailchimp](https://asana.com/apps/mailchimp) for email campaigns.
2.  [Slack](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/articles/14244009654683-Slack-and-Asana) for team communication.
3.  [Google](https://help.asana.com/s/article/google-and-asana?language=en_US) for document collaboration.

Nonprofit organizations can optimize their campaign workflows, boost team collaboration, and refine donor engagement strategies by leveraging Asana's tools, ultimately achieving greater success in their fundraising initiatives.
