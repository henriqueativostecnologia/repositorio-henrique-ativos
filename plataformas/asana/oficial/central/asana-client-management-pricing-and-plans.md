# Asana Client Management pricing and plans

**To purchase Client Management, your organization must have an active Starter or Advanced base subscription. Client Management is not included in these plans by default.**

This article covers the two Client Management tiers, how AI usage works, and available add-ons.

## Pricing options

**Starter:** For agencies that need one shared workspace to manage client communication, project status, and client-facing work.

**Advanced:** For agencies that need to connect client-facing work, meetings, projects, and resource management in one place.

Pricing is composed of two parts: your Client Management tier (Starter or Advanced), billed by number of seats, and AI consumption, determined by your AI usage.

Asana Client Management includes a set amount of AI, with the option to purchase more based on your needs. 

### AI Teammates and Dash usage

Usage of AI Teammates and Dash are measured separately from your base plan. The unit of consumption is a request, which is one successful unit of work completed by AI on a user's behalf. AI Teammates and Dash use the same request pool, so they do not have separate allowances.

Requests are shared across your Asana account rather than assigned per user. This means one person can use more requests if others use fewer, as long as the account stays within its limit. Included requests reset at the start of each billing month and do not roll over.

If you use more than your included requests, you can buy additional AI usage through usage packs, which are prepaid, or on demand, which is pay-as-you-go. Purchased usage stays available for the length of your contract. 

### Timesheets and Budgets (TBAO)

Timesheets and Budgets (TBAO) is an optional add-on available on Starter and Advanced. It lets teams track time on projects and monitor budgets in real time. For details on the TBAO add-on, see [Timesheets and Budgets help center article](https://help.asana.com/s/article/timesheets-and-budgets-add-on).

### Trials

Asana Client Management and Timesheets and Budgets are available in a 14-day trial for new and existing Asana customers. Admins and billing owners can start the trial from the admin console. During the trial, everyone in your organization gets access automatically, and you do not need to assign licenses individually.

## Asana Client Management: Starter vs Advanced

Asana Client Management is available on two plans: Asana Starter and Asana Advanced.

Both plans include clients, contacts, engagements, and built-in AI Teammates. Advanced also includes the client portal and team capacity planning.

| Capability                        | Starter               | Advanced |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------------|----------|
| Clients, contacts and engagements | Included              | Included |
| Client Portal                     | Available on Advanced | Included |
| Built-in AI Teammates             | Included              | Included |
| Team capacity (Workload)          | Available on Advanced | Included |
