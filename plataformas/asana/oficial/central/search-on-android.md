# Search on Android

Use search to find tasks, projects, teams, messages, tags, portfolios, goals, or people in your workspace or organization.

#### Related articles

- [Android basics](https://help.asana.com/s/article/android-basics?language=en_US)
- [Creating tasks and projects on Android](https://help.asana.com/s/article/creating-tasks-and-projects-on-android?language=en_US)
- [Portfolios on Android](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolios-on-android?language=en_US)
- [Inbox on Android](https://help.asana.com/s/article/inbox-on-android?language=en_US)

From the **Search** page, you'll see recent searches you've done, quick filters you can apply and shortcuts to useful searches.

#### To search:

1.  Tap on the **Search** icon at the bottom of your screen
2.  Start typing to look for objects.

You can tap on the filters below the search bar to narrow your search.

> You can also use your Google search bar to find search results from Asana

When searching, the results will display:

- Teams - select a team to view projects and members within it
- Projects - select a project to view its corresponding list of tasks
- Tasks - select a task to view its details
- Messages - select a message to view its details
- Tags - select a tag to view its corresponding list of tasks
- People - select a person to view their [My tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/my-tasks?language=en_US "their My tasks")
- Portfolios
- Goals
