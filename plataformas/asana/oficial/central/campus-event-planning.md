# Planning campus events in Asana

Planning campus events requires coordinating multiple stakeholders, managing numerous tasks, and staying on top of deadlines. Whether you're organizing a small club meeting or a large-scale campus festival, Asana provides the tools needed to keep your event planning on track and ensure everything runs smoothly from conception to execution.

### Get set up

The first thing to do is to [create a project](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-a-project) for your event. Click the **+ Create** button in the top bar, and either select **Blank project** or choose our [event planning template](https://asana.com/templates/event-planning) to get started quickly. Give your project a descriptive name like "Fall Festival" or "Career Fair," and [invite](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-invite-team-members-to-asana) your planning committee members using their email addresses.

## Create and organize your tasks

Break your event planning down into specific [tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-tasks). If you're using [list](https://help.asana.com/s/article/list-view?language=en_US) or [board](https://help.asana.com/s/article/board-view), create [sections](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-and-use-sections?language=en_US) to organize tasks by time period e.g., 3 months before, 1 month before, Week of event, or by category e.g., Venue, Marketing, Vendors.

[Assign tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/assign-tasks-to-teammates?language=en_US) to team members responsible for different aspects of the event. Remember, you can only assign a task to one person in Asana - this ensures clear ownership and accountability.

Set [due dates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times) for each task, working backward from your event date. This creates a clear timeline and helps prevent last-minute rushes. For day-of tasks, you can even set specific times to create a detailed schedule of the event itself.

## Track vendors and sponsorships

Create tasks or [milestones](https://help.asana.com/s/article/all-asana-features#gl-reporting) to track each vendor or sponsor you're working with. Use [subtasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/subtasks) to track important steps like contract signing, payment processing, and day-of logistics. You can [attach](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-comments-and-attachments?language=en_US#gl-attachments) relevant documents like contracts or invoices directly to the tasks for easy reference.

Keep track of your budget using [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields?language=en_US). Create fields for cost estimates, actual costs, and payment status to maintain clear financial oversight of your event. Use [formula custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/formula-custom-fields?language=en_US) to perform calculations directly in Asana, this is useful when comparing your estimated budget vs actual budget.

## Manage marketing and promotion

Set up a [section](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-and-use-sections?language=en_US) for marketing tasks including social media posts, email announcements, and physical promotional materials. Switch to [timeline view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/timeline?language=en_US) to visualize your marketing schedule and ensure promotional activities are properly timed leading up to the event.

Create [forms](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US) for event registration, volunteer sign-ups, or event feedback. When someone fills out the form, it automatically creates a task in your project, making it easy to track attendees, volunteers, and feedback.

## Day-of coordination

Create a detailed schedule for the day of the event using tasks with [specific times](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US#h_01HBB2EJQT8TTQ9SBQ2MEZ3WEB). Your team can access this schedule from their phones using the [Asana mobile app](https://asana.com/download) for iOS or Android, making it easy to check what needs to happen, and when.

Use [comments](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-comments-and-attachments?language=en_US#h_01GWMF0T91VJ8XVGP9NC8VWDYH) to communicate with team members in real-time during the event. This keeps all communication in one place and creates a record you can reference for future events.

## Tips for success

To make the most of Asana for your campus events:

- Use templates - Save your event project as a [template](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-templates?language=en_US) to make planning future events easier.
- Keep documentation centralized - Store all event-related files and documents as attachments in your Asana project.
- Create checklists - Use subtasks for detailed setup and teardown procedures.
- Track attendance - Use custom fields to monitor RSVPs and actual attendance.
- Document feedback - Create a section for post-event feedback to improve future events.
