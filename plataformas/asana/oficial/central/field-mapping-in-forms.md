# Field mapping in forms

**Forms are available on Asana [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter), [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [legacy tiers](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Premium, Business, and Legacy Enterprise.**

This article covers how to leverage field mapping to capture specific information using [forms](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US) and connect it to your workflow via custom fields.

## Custom field mapping

With custom field mapping, you can connect the answers to your form's questions directly to custom fields in the project.

When adding a new question, click the **Connect to** **field **box and choose from available fields. You can also see which fields are already in use.

To create a new field directly from the form, start typing in the **Connect to field** box and the **+ Add** option will pop up.

Once you've connected the field, it will appear in the form.

> To connect a custom field to a form question, the custom field's permissions must be set to allow access for both organization members and guests. If a custom field's access is restricted to organization members only, it will not appear in the **Connect to field** suggestions in the form builder.

To check or update a custom field's permissions:

1.  Open the custom field's settings and click **Manage access**.
2.  Under **Field members**, confirm that **Guests** are listed with at least **User** access.
3.  If guests have **No access**, update their permission level to **User** so the field can be linked to a form question.

## Date field mapping

For date questions, you can choose to have the form question responses connect to the following date types:

1.  Single day
2.  Date range

When **Single day** is selected, the date input field will be **Due date**.

When **Date range** is selected, there will be a **Start date** and **End date** input field.

### Required fields

Click **Required** to make the date a required field in your form.

## Adding project custom fields in bulk

You can bulk add or remove custom fields to streamline the form creation process. This process allows for easy management of form fields.

### To bulk add custom fields:

1.  Navigate to the form you want to update within your project
2.  From the **Questions** tab, click **Fields**
3.  Select the custom fields you want to add
4.  Click **Update**
5.  Click **Save**

To bulk remove custom fields from your form, deselect the custom fields and click **Updated**.
