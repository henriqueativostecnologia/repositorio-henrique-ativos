# Smart status

Save time by using Asana AI to draft status updates to help identify blind spots, open questions, roadblocks, and an efficient path towards your goals. You can use smart status to draft status updates for projects, portfolios, and goals.

## Smart status for projects

1.  Navigate to your project **Overview** tab and choose the current status of your project on the right hand side to begin drafting a new status update.
2.  Click the **Draft with AI** button to draft a status update using Asana AI.
3.  Select the project status and choose the dates in which you’d like to include insights.
4.  Click + Add guidance to add any instructions or content you'd like Asana AI to include.

Once a draft is generated, you can choose to Discard, or Edit the draft. Once you've edited the draft to your liking, you can post the status by clicking **Post** in the top right corner.

You can view the status update at any time via the **Overview** tab.

You can also draft a status update using Asana AI in any project view from the drop-down menu next to the project name.

## Smart status for portfolios

**Smart status on portfolios is available on Asana [*Advanced*](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [*Enterprise*](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [*Enterprise+*](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers. **

To create a status update with Asana AI for portfolios, click **Set status** and select **Draft** **update with AI**.

 

You can then choose the projects from the portfolio that you want to focus on for the status update and click **+ Add guidance** to add further instructions.

## Smart status for goals

**Smart status on goals is available on Asana** **[*Advanced*](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [*Enterprise*](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [*Enterprise+*](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers**

To create a status update with Asana AI for goals, navigate to the goal’s details page, click the three dot icon, and click **Draft update with AI**.

You can then choose the sub-goals, portfolios, and projects that you want to focus on for the status update and click **+ Add guidance** to add further instructions.

 

## Adding guidance to smart status

You can provide guidance to Asana AI to structure your status update in a specific way.

1.  Format the section names as a header to specify a status update structure.
2.  All other input will be interpreted as instructions for Asana AI to follow or content to include.

If instructions are not provided, Asana AI will use the previous status update structure (if one exists) or a default structure.

**Example 1:** Giving instructions - “Bullet out current known or potential blockers that you foresee. Consider calling out where you might need help”.

**Example 2:** Specifying structure, format the titles as headings in this case - “Initiatives this work supports, Current Blockers, Next Steps”.

Once everything is added, click **Draft update**. Wait for Asana AI to analyze your project, portfolio, or goal and draft a status update. You will be notified once the update is complete.

Once a draft is generated, you can choose to:

1.  **Discard** the draft by click the **X** or back button
2.  **Edit** the draft

Clicking **Edit draft** will insert the draft into the editor.

Once you’ve inserted your status update, you can review and make changes to the AI-generated status update in the editor and then click **Post** in the top right corner.
