# How to sign up for a free Asana trial

You can try out Asana for free, before deciding which plan is right for you and your team. There's no credit card required, and no commitment to pay.

## Begin a free trial now

If you'd like to jump in right away, click the link to [begin a free trial now](https://app.asana.com/-/upgrade).

Or you can visit our [pricing page](https://asana.com/pricing) and begin a free **Advanced** trial yourself by clicking **Get started**. If you're interested in Asana [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise) or [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise), please [contact sales](https://asana.com/sales) directly.

If you're new to Asana, you'll be brought through the sign up process while setting up your free trial.

## Try out some Asana features

Once set up, you'll have full access to paid features for the duration of the trial.

During your trial you can explore [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter) features like [rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules?language=en_US), [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields?language=en_US), and [project templates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-templates?language=en_US). A The Starter tier also includes any features available on Asana's free Personal plan.

You'll also explore [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced) features such as [goals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-goals?language=en_US), [portfolios](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolios-overview?language=en_US), and [universal reporting](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting?language=en_US). The Advanced tier also includes everything available on the Starter tier.

Be sure to check out Asana's [Help Center](https://help.asana.com/hc/en-us/categories/13974184441243-Help-Center) and [Academy](https://academy.asana.com/) resources to make the most of your trial.

## Evaluate Asana with your team

Your Asana trial is the perfect opportunity to get key teammates involved in evaluating Asana's features. Invite others into your Asana organization and have them test out capabilities that are relevant to their roles and workflows.

Once the trial period ends, you can choose a plan that provides the most value for your team.
