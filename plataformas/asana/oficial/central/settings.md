## Access settings

 

#### To access **Settings**:

1.  From the sidebar, click on your profile photo
2.  Select **Settings** from the menu

> [Click here to go straight to Settings](https://app.asana.com/-/user_settings "Click here to go straight to My Settings").

In the **Settings** menu, you will find 7 tabs, each with different options:

- [**Profile**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/profile-settings) - change your profile details or set yourself as away with the vacation indicator
- [**Notifications**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/notification-settings) - schedule your **Do not disturb**, access **Email notifications** to assign your preferred email and **Browser notifications** for updates and @mentions
- [**Email Forwarding**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/email-forwarding-settings) - change which of your email addresses can create tasks in your various workspaces and organizations
- [**Account**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/account-settings) - change your password or deactivate your account
- [**Display**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/display-settings) - change the background image or add task row numbers to your Asana account's interface
- [**Apps**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/apps-settings) - manage your integrations and create Personal Access Tokens
- [**Hacks**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/hacks) - enable and disable experimental features in Asana
