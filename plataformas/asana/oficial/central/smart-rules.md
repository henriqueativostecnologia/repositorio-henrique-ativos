## How to use smart automation creator

1.  Navigate to the automation builder and select **Start from scratch**
2.  Click **Create automation with AI** in the top right corner
3.  Provide simple instructions on the workflow you would like to automate or select one of the suggestions provided
4.  Click the up arrow or press enter

Once the preview is available, you can choose to discard, or publish the automation.
