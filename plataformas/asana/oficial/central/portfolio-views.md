# Portfolio views

**Available on Asana [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [*legacy tiers*](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Business and Legacy Enterprise.**

**Visit our [pricing page](https://asana.com/pricing) for more information.**

Portfolios help you organize and view related projects in one place. In this article, we'll focus on how you can customize your portfolio view to track project progress efficiently. You'll learn to apply filters, sort and manage custom fields, view milestones, and use portfolio timeline.

## Portfolio list view

The portfolio list view shows a list of the projects and portfolios within the main portfolio. From the list view you can see each object's owner, priority, [rollups](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolio-progress-and-reporting?language=en_US#gl-custom-field-rollups), status, due date, and either task or milestone progress.

### Custom fields

Adding custom fields to your portfolio will allow you to report on project-level data and help you track the progress of your work. You can add, hide, or remove custom fields from your portfolio.

Click the **Customize** menu to add and manage your custom fields, or click the **+** button next to the last custom field to add a new one. You can hide custom fields by clicking the drop-down arrow next to the custom field title and select **Hide column** or remove them by clicking **Remove field from portfolio**.

### Milestones

Milestones show key events and map forward movement in the project plan. They act as signposts throughout your project, helping ensure you stay on track.

To view milestones, click **Options**, select **Show/hide columns**, and toggle on **Milestone progress**.

A maximum of 12 milestones can be displayed in your portfolio view before the list becomes truncated.

## Filter, sort, and group by

Filtering and sorting are effective ways to quickly find the information you need by organizing it in a meaningful way.

#### To change your portfolio view:

1.  Click **Filter** and/or **Sort** to apply a single filter
2.  Choose either tasks or milestones as the progress type displayed
3.  Click the three dot icon and select **Save layout as default**

If there is a specific way you and your team prefer to sort portfolios, you can save it as the default view for everyone.

### Sort by custom fields

From the **Sort** button in the project view, you can sort by a range of custom field options. You can categorize, prioritize, and sort based on your particular needs.

### Multi-filter and sort

With multi-filter and sort, you can create custom views within portfolios that will allow you to manage, monitor, and report on work at scale.

#### Multi-filter

You can apply up to 10 filters at a time on your portfolio list view and can use the following filters:

- Status
- Team
- Owner
- Multi-select custom field
- Single-select custom field
- People custom field
- Start/Due date
- Date custom field
- Goals

#### Multi-sort

You can apply up to 10 sorts at a time on your portfolio list view and can use the following sorts:

- Name
- Status
- Owner
- Start/Due date
- Duration
- Time remaining
- Priority
- Multi-select custom field
- Single-select custom field
- People custom field
- Date custom field
- Goals

### Group by

Group by allows you to group portfolios by specific fields such as owner, status, and due date.

## Portfolio timeline

Portfolio timeline allows you to visualize, share, manage and adjust how initiatives are sequenced so you can best utilize your resources and hit your deadlines. With a grid-backed timeline view, increased item capacity, and filtering and sorting options, you can better track and adjust project schedules while maintaining clear visibility of your portfolio's progress. Note that a project's tasks are not visible in portfolio timeline view, but project milestones do appear. See the screenshot in the below section for a visual example.

### Accessing your portfolio timeline

You can access the portfolio timeline by clicking the **Timeline** tab within the portfolio.

#### Portfolio timeline view includes the following:

- Grid-backed timeline for improved visual organization
- Increased capacity to display up to 1,500 items
- Multi-filter and sort capabilities
- Enhanced toolbar for better navigation and control

### Grid-backed timeline

The new grid-backed timeline provides a more structured view of your portfolio items. This enhancement makes it easier to:

- Align projects and initiatives along precise time intervals
- Visualize project durations and dependencies
- Navigate between items with improved accuracy

### Increased item limit

Portfolio timeline supports up to 1,500 items, allowing you to:

- Track more projects within a single portfolio
- Maintain comprehensive visibility across larger initiatives
- Reduce the need to split portfolios due to size limitations

### Multi-filter and sort

Apply multiple filters and sorts simultaneously to customize your timeline view:

- Click **Filter** in the toolbar to apply multiple criteria
- Use **Sort** to organize items by various attributes
- Combine different filters and sorts to create precise views of your portfolio

### Enhanced toolbar

The improved toolbar provides quick access to:

- Timeline view controls
- Filtering and sorting options
- Display preferences
- View customization settings

Use the portfolio toolbar to:

- Filter projects by day
- Filter your view by day, week, month, quarter, half-year or year
- Set colors or choose custom fields with a particular color

## Connected goals in portfolio list view

You can view connected goals within a portfolio's list view. The **Connected goals** column shows goals connected to the work in the portfolio, increasing the visibility of the relationships between work and goals.

From the portfolio list view, you can:

- View connected goals
- Add existing goals to a specific portfolio item
- Create a new goal from within list view

### Viewing connected goals

**[](https://help.asana.com/hc/article_attachments/22897939670427)**

#### To view connected goals:

1.  Open your portfolio in **List** view and click on the **Customize** menu
2.  Click **Show more fields**
3.  Toggle on the **Connected goals** field
4.  The connected goals field will now be visible in list view

### Adding connected goals  **[](https://help.asana.com/hc/article_attachments/22897939675035)**

#### To add existing goals:

1.  Click into the **Connected goals** field
2.  Choose an existing goal by scrolling through the pop-up list or start typing the goal name into the field
3.  Click on the existing goal to add it as a connected goal

### Creating a new goal  **[](https://help.asana.com/hc/article_attachments/22897907219867) **

#### To create a new goal from the connected goals field:

1.  Click on the **Connected goals** field
2.  Click **+ Create new goal** at the bottom of the pop-up menu
