# AI Studio pre-built AI automations

AI Studio is available for [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter), [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) customers. Visit [asana.com/ai](https://asana.com/product/ai) to learn more about Asana AI.

This article will show you how to use and customize pre-built AI Studio automations to quickly add AI to your workflows. If you’d like to go further, you can also earn the [AI Studio Foundations skill badge](https://academy.asana.com/ai-studio-foundations-skill-badge) in the Asana Academy. You’ll learn how to write effective prompts, plan Smart workflows, and troubleshoot common issues.

AI Studio's pre-built AI automations represent simple use cases that add the power of AI to a workflow. Found in the automations gallery, these AI-powered automations are a great starting point for AI Studio creators to familiarize themselves with Smart workflows without needing to build an AI automation from scratch. These pre-built automations can be added to any project and will work without any editing, though creators are encouraged to make edits to tailor automations to their workflows.

Pre-built automations are visible in the automations gallery to customers who have [AI Studio enabled](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-studio#How_to_enable_AI_Studio) and have been added to the list of AI Studio creators by an admin.

## How to access pre-built automations

Access the AI Studio pre-built automations from within the automations gallery.

1.  Click the **Customize** button in the top-right of your project.
2.  Under **AI Studio**, click **Automations**.
3.  Next, click the **+ Add** button to open the automations gallery

## Choose a pre-built automation from the gallery

When you open the automations gallery, you will see an AI-powered text box at the top of the screen. Type a description of what you want to automate and the automation builder will generate a customized automation based on your prompt.

Below the text box, you can also browse the **Featured** section, which contains a curated selection of pre-built automations ready to add to your project.

To add a featured automation to your project:

1.  Scroll to the **Featured** section and click the tile for the automation you want to add
2.  On the pop-up screen, click **Add to project**
3.  Wait a moment for the automation to be added

## Editing a pre-built automation

To make changes to an existing automation:

1.  Navigate to your automations list under **AI Studio**
2.  Click the **three dot icon** next to the automation you want to edit
3.  Select **Edit** from the menu that appears
4.  Make your changes to the trigger, condition, action, or guidance, then save
