# Invite others during your trial

It's a good idea to invite your teammates to join Asana during your trial period. By getting your team involved early, you can test all the paid features together and see how they can improve your work processes before you buy.

Before inviting them, you might like to set up some sample workflows using [rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules?language=en_US), [forms](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US), or [task templates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-templates?language=en_US). 

## Invite your team

The quickest way to invite others to Asana during your trial is via the **Create** button. The button is located in the top-left corner of Asana and can be accessed while working in any project, portfolio, or team.

Invite the teams and colleagues that you usually work with. People who explore Asana with their teammates are more likely to figure out if it works for them.

## Set them up for success

Inviting your teammates via the **Create** button lets you choose specific teams and projects for them in advance. You may have set up some rules, templates, or workflows in specific projects and wish to test them out with your team. It's a good idea to list those projects in the **Add to projects** field when sending invites.

1.  Click the **Create** button and select **Invite** from the list.
2.  In the new window, enter the email addresses of the invitees. Copy and paste email addresses from a CSV file to speed this part up.
3.  Under **Add to team**, choose a team for them to join.
4.  Under **Add to projects**, choose specific projects to add them to. This is optional, but recommended.
5.  Click **Send** when you're done.

Your colleagues will be invited to join your Asana organization, placed in the correct team, and given access to the relevant projects. By selecting the team and projects for the invitees, you ensure a smooth collaboration experience during the trial phase. They will immediately have access to the paid features you want to explore and can enjoy testing out their usual workflows.
