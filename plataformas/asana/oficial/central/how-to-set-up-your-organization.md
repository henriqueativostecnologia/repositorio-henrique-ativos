# How to set up your organization

Setting up your organization in Asana is the foundation for effective team collaboration and work management. Your organization is the highest level of hierarchy in Asana. This article will walk you through the essential steps to configure your organization for success.

## Set up organization settings

From the admin console, navigate to the **Settings** tab. Here, you can manage your organization’s domain settings, announcements, and company profile. Update your fiscal year start date to align with your business operations.

## Configure your profile

Start by customizing your organization's basic information:

1.  In the admin console, navigate to **Settings**
2.  Under **Company profile ** you can:
    1.  Update your **organization name** to reflect your company
    2.  Upload your company logo for custom branding
    3.  Add a preferred email to receive security and privacy-related notifications 

## Custom branding

Upload your company's logo to Asana to personalize its appearance, ensuring your brand is represented accurately throughout the Asana experience.

#### To add your organization's logo:

1.  Navigate to the **Settings** tab in your admin console
2.  Under **Company profile**, click **Custom branding**
3.  Upload your primary logo for light mode
4.  Optionally, upload a separate logo optimized for dark mode
5.  Use the preview function to verify how your logos appear across different surfaces

### Logo guidelines

- We recommend that your logo’s resolution be 60x60 pixels minimum, but a higher resolution image is preferred.
- Upload a PNG image with a transparent background.
- Logos will appear next to your profile picture in the admin console, work exports, and forms (if enabled).  
- The shape of the logo should be square or rectangular. If not, Asana will scale the logo to fit onto different surfaces.

## Customize onboarding

[Custom onboarding](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-onboarding?language=en_US) helps tailor the Asana experience to fit your organization’s needs. This includes setting up welcome messages, creating an onboarding project, and providing guides on how to use Asana effectively. Offering clear instructions and resources will engage your team from the start.

1.  **Create onboarding projects**: Design templates to streamline new member onboarding.
2.  **Provide training materials**: Link to tutorials, webinars, and help articles to help new users learn the ropes quickly.

To create a custom onboarding:

1.  Navigate to the **Settings** tab
2.  Under **New user onboarding**, click **Customized onboarding**
3.  Click **Create onboarding**

## Enable Asana AI features

Optimize your workflow with [Asana’s AI capabilities](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-features-and-admin-controls?language=en_US). Super admins can turn these features on or off via the **Settings** tab under **Domain settings**.

1.  Navigate to the **Settings** tab
2.  Under **Domain settings** click **Asana AI**
3.  Turn AI features on or off as needed

## Manage security settings

Security is crucial for protecting your organization’s data. Set up [global authentication settings](https://help.asana.com/s/article/global-authentication-settings?language=en_US), including:

- [**Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/two-factor-authentication?language=en_US): Enhance security by requiring a second verification form
- [**SAML Single Sign-On (SSO)**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/authentication-and-access-management-options-for-paid-plans?language=en_US): Simplify the login process using your organization’s SSO provider
- [**Password settings**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/managing-password-settings-in-the-admin-console?language=en_US): Enforce strong password practices to prevent unauthorized access

## Configure domain export

Super admins can request a [full export of your organization’s data](https://help.asana.com/s/article/privacy-and-security?language=en_US) in JSON format. This is particularly useful for maintaining comprehensive records or conducting audits.

1.  Go to the **Settings** tab in the admin console
2.  Under **Domain settings**, select **Domain export** and follow the prompts to export data

By following these steps, you can ensure your organization is set up for success in Asana, with customized settings and robust security measures in place.
