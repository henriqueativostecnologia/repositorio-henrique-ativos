# Asana AI features and admin controls

**Asana AI refers to a feature set powered by **generative AI and large language models (LLMs) from Asana’s AI partners (e.g., [OpenAI](https://openai.com/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/), [Google](https://cloud.google.com/), [Fireworks.ai](https://fireworks.ai/), and Recall.ai). Models from Asana’s AI partners may be hosted on third-party servers or [AWS Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/), a service offered through Asana’s long-standing cloud service provider.

Customers can choose to enable Asana AI features for their domain (i.e., [organization](https://help.asana.com/s/article/managing-an-organization?language=en_US) or [workspace](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-workspaces?language=en_US)). If Asana AI features are not enabled:

- Users in the organization/workspace will not have access to the AI features.
- Organization/workspace's data will not be processed to power these features.

## Data and Safeguards 

Asana AI features powered by AI partners use metadata and user generated content from your organization/workspace (e.g., content you enter into Asana like task titles and task descriptions, which may include user names) to help users in your domain optimize their work with AI.

**Neither Asana nor Asana’s AI partners use customer data to train AI models of any kind**. Moreover, Asana’s AI partners configure their services to promptly delete input and output data following completion of the relevant query and/or response.

To learn more about how Asana processes your information, please see [Asana’s Privacy Statement](https://asana.com/terms#privacy-statement). For further details and answers to other frequently asked questions, please visit the [Asana AI FAQ](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-faq) and [Asana Trust Center](https://security.asana.com/).

## Asana AI features

The following features can be enabled/disabled using the **Enable Asana AI** control in the admin console.

- [Smart editor](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-editor?language=en_US)**:** Write clearer, more compelling responses that strike the right tone.
- [Smart summaries:](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-summaries?language=en_US) Available for tasks, projects and portfolios. Use smart summaries on tasks to get action items and highlights from conversations, tasks, and comments without another meeting. Use smart summaries on projects and portfolios to keep up to date with an easily scannable summary of all recent activity and highlights in one place.
- [Smart fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-fields?language=en_US)**:** Organize projects to support cross-functional collaboration with auto-generated custom fields.
- [Smart status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-status?language=en_US)**:** Identify blind spots, open questions, and roadblocks to find an efficient path towards your goals with automatic status updates.
- [Smart goals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-goals?language=en_US)**:** Standardize and improve the effectiveness of goal-setting with smart goals. Smart goals analyzes existing goals and offers suggestions for improvement, including adding success criteria, impact summaries, parent goals, supporting subgoals, and assigning accountable teams.
- [Smart projects](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-projects?language=en_US)**:** Quickly create customized projects with smart projects. Smart projects suggests descriptions, sections, and custom fields all based on the project's name and purpose.
- [Smart rule creator](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-rules?language=en_US)**:** Create rules with AI by providing simple, and natural language instructions via prompts.
- [AI Studio](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-studio?language=en_US): Build no-code smart workflows to pass off your teams’ busywork.
- [AI Teammates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-teammates): AI Teammates are ready-to-go AI agents that help teams automate routine work and accelerate outcomes across workflows. AI Teammates can be configured to handle specific roles and responsibilities within your organization.
- [Asana Dash](https://help.asana.com/s/article/chat-with-dash): Asana Dash serves as your AI Chief of Staff, providing intelligent assistance across your work in Asana. Dash helps you stay on top of priorities, surface insights, and take action on important work.
- [AI Meetings](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-meetings): Instead of meeting notes living in a separate tool, Asana transcribes your meetings and generates an AI summary and suggested action items that you can convert into tasks without leaving Asana. 

To learn more about the specific AI partners powering the Asana AI features above, see [Asana AI Customizations](https://help.asana.com/s/article/Asana-AI-customization?language=en_US).

## Enabling and disabling Asana AI features

Super admins of organizations and workspace billing owners and creators can enable or disable Asana AI features as follows:

1.  Click your profile picture in Asana and navigate to the admin console.
2.  Click **Asana AI.**
3.  Select **Settings**.
4.  Scroll down to **Advanced **
5.  Click **Providers and APIs**
6.  Enable or disable specific providers by clicking the relevant check boxes.
7.  Click **Save changes.**

From this page, designated admins can choose to enable or disable Asana AI for their entire domain. After disabling, Asana AI experiences will no longer appear or process data from your organization.

To enable these features, you will need to complete the [super admin verification process](https://help.asana.com/s/article/admin-and-super-admin-roles-in-asana?language=en_US) or contact the [Support team](https://asana.com/support). This behavior also applies to [divisions](https://help.asana.com/s/article/a-guide-to-divisions-in-asana?language=en_US) in Asana, as divisions do not have super admins.

Please note that if you are on a division plan, it is still possible to enable AI features via your admin console. However, only a super admin will be able to take action on this. If there is no super admin, a current admin will first need to complete the super admin verification.

To learn more about Asana AI **Advanced Settings**, check out [Asana AI Customizations](https://help.asana.com/s/article/Asana-AI-customization?language=en_US).If you are an admin of an organization that does not have at least one [super admin](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/admin-console-admin-roles#gl-super-admin-vs-admin?), you can disable Asana AI features, but cannot enable them. To enable these features, you will need to complete the [super admin verification process](https://help.asana.com/s/article/admin-and-super-admin-roles-in-asana?language=en_US) or contact the [Support team](https://asana.com/support). This behavior also applies to [divisions](https://help.asana.com/s/article/a-guide-to-divisions-in-asana?language=en_US) in Asana, as divisions do not have super admins.
