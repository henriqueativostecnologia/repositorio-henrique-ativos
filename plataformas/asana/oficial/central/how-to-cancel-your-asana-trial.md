# How to cancel your Asana trial

If you’ve been trying out Asana’s paid features with a trial and wish to cancel it, you can do so at any time. Trials without payment information will simply expire. This article guides you through the steps for canceling trials, whether payment information has been added or not.

## Allow the trial to run its course

Asana trials are designed to end automatically, so you won’t have to manually cancel. Unless you’ve manually added payment details, you can simply let the trial run its course and you won’t be billed.

## Cancel a trial after adding payment details

If you have manually added payment details to your trial, your chosen subscription is set to begin once the trial period has ended. In such cases, you would need to manually cancel your trial.

To cancel a trial after you’ve added payment details, follow these steps:

1.  Click your profile photo in the top-right corner
2.  Select **Admin console**
3.  Navigate to the **Billing** tab
4.  Under **Plan details**, select **Cancel plan**
5.  Follow the steps to confirm cancellation

You can cancel at any time during the trial period. If you cancel on or before your trial expiration date, your account will not be charged.

After canceling, when you reach your plan renewal or trial expiration date, you will revert to the Asana tier you were using before starting the trial. You will still have access to paid features until the trial end date.

## Remove a trial immediately

There may be cases where you require a trial to be removed immediately, even if you haven't added payment information. If necessary, please [contact our Support team](https://asana.com/support).

Be aware that trials without payment information will simply expire without any cost to you.
