# Calendars and Asana

The Sync to Calendar option helps you meet deadlines by syncing your tasks to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any calendar that allows you to subscribe by URL.

You can sync any [project](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-projects?language=en_US) [tag](https://help.asana.com/s/article/tags?language=en_US), or [My tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/my-tasks?language=en_US) list to an external calendar. Once synced, you will see tasks with due dates from those projects as all day events. You can click each event to visit the task in Asana.

A few things to note:

- Only tasks with [due dates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US?language=en_US) will sync
- Calendar sync is one-way: updates to your calendar will not be reflected in Asana
- [Completed](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-fields?language=en_US) tasks will not sync
- [Subtasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/subtasks?language=en_US) also sync to external calendars
- [Team calendar](https://help.asana.com/s/article/team-basics?language=en_US) cannot be synced
- It's not possible to sync calendars by URL to Outlook for Mac 2011
- The initial sync to your calendar should be instant, but subsequent syncs may take up to a day
- The maximum number of synced tasks is 5000

## To sync a project

#### Navigate to the project you want to sync, from here:

1.  Click the drop-down arrow to access the [project actions](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-projects?language=en_US#gl-actions "project actions")

2.  Hover over the Export/Print option

3.  Select **Sync to calendar...**

    > These steps can also be used to sync your My tasks.

    ## Sync to Google Calendar

    From the **Sync to Calendar** popup window in Asana, highlight and copy the entire link found in the **Google Calendar** tab. The **asana.biz** domain is used to avoid potential issues with Asana projects not syncing after more than 24 hours.

    > Projects will need to be synced individually.

    #### In Google Calendar:

    1.  Click **+ Other calendars**
    2.  Select **From URL**

    #### In the popup window:

    1.  Paste the link you previously copied from Asana into the **URL** field
    2.  Click **Add calendar**

    > Once synced, you will see tasks with due dates from that project as all day events on your Calendar.

    You can click each event to find a link to the task in Asana.

    To remove a synced project from Google Calendar navigate to the calendar options, click **Settings**, scroll down, click **Unsubscribe** and click **Remove calendar**.

    ## AI Meetings

    [AI Meetings](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-meetings) helps you prepare for and follow up on your meetings. When you connect your Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar to Asana, AI Meetings can work with your upcoming calendar events so you can keep your related tasks and projects close at hand. Learn more about [AI Meetings](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-meetings).

    ## Sync to Apple Calendar

    From the **Sync to Calendar** popup window in Asana, click the **iCAL, Outlook, or Other Calendar** tab and highlight and copy (but do not click) the entire link.

    #### In Apple Calendar:

    1.  Select **File**
    2.  Select **New Calendar Subscription**

    Paste the link you previously copied from Asana into the **Calendar URL** field and select **Subscribe**.

    Edit the Calendar's options. We recommend setting auto-refresh to **Every 5 minutes**. Once you're done, select **Ok**

    > Once synced, you will see tasks with due dates from that project as all day events on your calendar. You can click each event to find a link to the task in Asana.

    #### To remove a synced project from Apple Calendar:

    1.  Right click (or `⌘` + click on a Mac) the Asana project's name
    2.  Select **Unsubscribe**
