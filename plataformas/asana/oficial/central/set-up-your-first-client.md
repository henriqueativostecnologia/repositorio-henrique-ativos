# Set up your first client

**To purchase Client Management, your organization must have an active Starter or Advanced base subscription. Client Management is not included in these plans by default.**

A client record is the central place to manage everything related to a client relationship.

Each record has four sections: **Profile**, **Projects**, **Contacts**, and **Portal**. 

Together, they give your team one place to store client information, track the work you deliver, manage your relationships, and share updates with clients.

## Creating a client

To get started, click **Clients** in the **Client Management** sidebar. 

Select **+ Create client**, then enter your client's name. 

You can also add a description, and details like their website, address, client health status, start date and renewal date.

## The client record

Once created, open the client record to find the four tabs: **Profile**, **Projects**, **Contacts**, and **Portal**. The sections below walk through how to use each one.

### Complete the client profile

The **Profile** tab is where your team stores key details about the client relationship. This information is internal and visible to your team only. Use it to record account ownership, contract dates, client health, and other details that help your team stay aligned.

### Add a project

The **Projects** tab is where you connect the work you do for a client to their record. Open the client, go to **Projects**, and select + **Add project** to link an existing project or create a new one.

> Adding your first project automatically creates the client's portal. You do not need to set it up separately.

### Add contacts

Contacts are the people at a client organization that you work with. You can add them from the client's **Contacts** tab.

**To add a contact:**

1.  Click on a client 
2.  Click **View client**
3.  Select the **Contacts** tab.
4.  Click **Add contact**.
5.  Enter the contact’s name and email, and consider populating any other fields you use.

### Set up the client portal

**Client Portal is available on the Advanced tier.**

The **Portal** tab is where your client-facing work lives. Once a project is added, the portal is created automatically. Before sharing it, you can upload a cover image and review the welcome update so your client sees a polished, relevant space when they sign in.

When the portal is ready, select **Share with client** and invite your client by email. They will receive a link to log in and view the work you have shared with them.

For full details on managing the portal, see [Understanding the client portal](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-the-client-portal).

**Share work into the portal**

After your client has access, you can begin sharing work into the portal. Add tasks, post a status update, and share files from your project so your client can follow progress in one place.

This helps you keep communication and delivery connected. Instead of sending updates across different tools, you can share the work directly where your client already expects to find it.

> Only tasks added directly to the portal are visible to your client. Clients cannot see tasks or comments from your internal projects, and this work is never shared to the portal automatically.

## Another way to create a client

If you are working elsewhere in Asana, you can also create a client from the global **+** button. Select **Client** to start a new client without needing to open the **Clients** page first.
