# Ideas and brainstorms

Brainstorming is essential for generating innovative ideas, but managing and prioritizing these ideas can often be challenging. Asana provides a powerful platform to capture, organize, and prioritize ideas efficiently, helping teams turn creative concepts into actionable plans.

## Set up a brainstorming project

### Create a project in board view

Start by creating a project in [board view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/board-view), which acts as a digital whiteboard, providing a visual layout that’s ideal for brainstorming. This format allows participants to see and move ideas around easily, fostering a dynamic and interactive session. The clear visual representation helps in categorizing thoughts and setting the stage for prioritization.

## Structure the brainstorming session

**Add columns to your board
**Organize your brainstorming process by adding columns that represent different stages of idea development, such as "Idea Capture," "Voting," "In Review," and "Top Ideas." Each column marks a phase in the idea lifecycle, ensuring a systematic approach to collecting, evaluating, and refining ideas.

**Create tasks for ideas
**In the "Idea Capture" column, create a task for each new idea. Each task represents a single idea, making it easy to track and manage during the session.

## Gather ideas before the session

**Forms
**To streamline idea collection, use [forms](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms) to gather input before the session begins. Share the form link with participants so they can submit their ideas in advance, ensuring everyone’s voice is heard, especially in larger or remote teams. This pre-session ideation allows the brainstorming meeting to focus on refining and expanding upon these collected ideas.

**Template task for idea submission
**Alternatively, create a [template task](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-templates) that participants can clone and fill with their ideas if forms are not used. This ensures consistency in how ideas are submitted.

## Customize and track ideas

**Add custom fields
**Enhance idea tracking by adding [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields) like "Owner," "Votes," "Category," and "Status" to each idea task. These fields allow for detailed tracking and evaluation, making the process of assigning responsibility, categorizing ideas, and monitoring their popularity or feasibility straightforward and transparent.

**Attachments and comments
**Encourage team members to enrich idea tasks by [attaching](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-comments-and-attachments) relevant documents, images, or links. Utilize comments for additional insights, enabling collaborative brainstorming where ideas can be built upon, critiqued, and supported with contextual information.

## Conduct real-time brainstorming

**Virtual collaboration
**For remote teams, integrate Asana with tools like [Zoom](https://help.asana.com/s/article/zoom-and-asana-integration) and [Slack](https://help.asana.com/s/article/slack-and-asana) to ensure everyone can participate actively, regardless of location. Share the project link within these tools to keep the session interactive and inclusive.

**Live idea capture
**During the session, use [board view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/board-view) to capture ideas in real-time. Participants can add tasks directly or through the pre-shared form, ensuring that all contributions are documented as they happen.

**Voting on ideas
**Utilize [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields) to allow participants to vote on ideas as the session progresses. This democratizes decision-making, ensuring that the most popular ideas rise to the top.

## Organize and prioritize ideas

**Move ideas across columns
**Transition top-voted ideas from "Idea Capture" to "Voting," then to "In Review," and finally to "Top Ideas" [columns](https://help.asana.com/s/article/organize-projects-with-custom-fields). This process ensures that only the best ideas move forward to implementation.

**Asana AI
**Leverage [Asana’s AI](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai) capabilities to suggest grouping or prioritization based on votes or comments. 

## Convert ideas into actionable tasks

**Assign tasks and track progress
**Once the top ideas are identified, convert them into actionable tasks with clear ownership and deadlines. Use [dependencies](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-dependencies) and task tracking to monitor progress from conceptualization to implementation, ensuring that the brainstorming leads to tangible outcomes.

## Review and follow-Up

**Create dashboards and reports
**Set up [dashboards](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-dashboards) to visualize the progress of idea implementation and generate reports for review in subsequent meetings. This continuous monitoring ensures that ideas are developed and refined over time, leading to better outcomes.

**Encourage continuous feedback
**Promote ongoing feedback and iteration on implemented ideas within the project. This keeps the creative process alive and ensures that the best ideas continue to evolve.

By leveraging these features for brainstorming, your team can centralize idea management, foster effective collaboration, and ensure that every idea receives the attention it deserves. This structured approach not only organizes the brainstorming process but also ensures that the best ideas are effectively executed, driving innovation and results.
