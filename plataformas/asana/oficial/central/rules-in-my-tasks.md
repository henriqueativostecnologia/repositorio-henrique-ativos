# Automations in My tasks

This article delves into how you can add automations to My tasks to save time, reduce manual effort, and increase efficiency when managing your tasks.

Automations are available for customers on **Asana** [**Starter**](https://asana.com/plan/starter)**,** [ **Advanced**](https://asana.com/plan/advanced)**,** [ **Enterprise**](https://asana.com/enterprise)**, and** [ **Enterprise+**](https://asana.com/enterprise) **tiers**, **as well as** [ **legacy tiers**](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) **Premium, Business, and Legacy Enterprise.** **Due date is approaching** is the only preset automation in My tasks available to Asana Personal.

The [custom automation builder](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules?language=en_US) is available on **Asana** [ **Starter**](https://asana.com/plan/starter)**,** [ **Advanced**](https://asana.com/plan/advanced)**,** [ **Enterprise**](https://asana.com/enterprise)**, and** [ **Enterprise+**](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as legacy tiers **Business** and **Legacy Enterprise**. When adding a automation to My tasks, select **Create custom automation** to get started.

## Adding automations to My tasks

Adding automation to My tasks will help you save time on manual processes like triaging and automate the movement of tasks through your sections. From the gallery of preset automations, you can pick an option that already has the trigger and action set. You can then specify the parameters needed.

Choose from a selection of preset automations with common triggers such as **Due date is approaching**, **Task is overdue**, and **Task marked complete**.

###

### Recreating Today, Upcoming, Later auto-promotion behavior

To recreate the behavior where tasks moved from **Later** to **Upcoming** and from **Upcoming** to **Today**, you'll need to add 2 automations to your My tasks. You'll need to add a automation to move tasks to the **Upcoming** section when the due date is approaching and a automation to move tasks to the **Today** section when the task is due today.

#### To add these automations:

1.  Click **Customize** 
2.  Click **Automations **and select **Add automation**
3.  Click to add the **Due date is approaching** automation
4.  Choose the due date you would like the automation to move a task to a certain section on
5.  Choose the section you want tasks to move to on your chosen date

You can see that a automation has been deployed when the automations icon (a lightning bolt) appears beside the section that has a automation.

> You can add an additional automation for each section to which you would like your tasks to move. For example, you would need to create two automations if you would like your tasks to move first to an **Upcoming** section when the due date is 3 days away, and a second separate automation to move them to a **Today** section as the task's due date nears.

When you've created these automations, tasks will move from anywhere in your My tasks into these sections as the due date approaches. They'll move right from your **Recently assigned** section, or any other section, between midnight and 1am when these automations update your My tasks.
