# How to use Asana for candidate tracking

Managing the recruitment process effectively requires clear organization, seamless collaboration, and consistent communication. Asana provides a comprehensive solution for tracking candidates through your hiring pipeline while maintaining visibility and efficiency. This article guides you through setting up and optimizing your candidate tracking workflow in Asana. Candidate tracking project

## Project setup

#### Create your candidate tracking project:

- Start with the [candidate tracking template](https://asana.com/templates/candidate-tracking) or [create a new project](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-a-project) from scratch.
- Customize [sections](https://help.asana.com/s/article/sections) to match your recruitment stages (e.g., Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, Hired).
- Add [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields) to track essential candidate information.

#### Custom field setup:

- Utilize custom fields to track candidate information such as position, interview stages, and hiring status.
- Update these fields as candidates progress through the hiring pipeline—leveraging [Asana AI](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai) to predict potential bottlenecks and suggest timeline adjustments.
- Create a "Status" field with options like Active, On Hold, Declined, or Hired.
- Add fields like; Position applied for, Department, Source, Interview stage and Expected start date.

#### Task management:

- Create [tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/create-tasks-in-asana) and task templates.
- Use a [task template](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-templates) for each new candidate titled “\[COPY ME\] To add a candidate.”
- Include a clear description detailing the information required for onboarding each candidate: resumes, contact details, and source.
- Use [subtasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/subtasks) to break down the recruitment process into steps, for example, you can add a subtask to collect interview feedback. 

## Segment and organize the project

#### Break the project into sections by department or team or by hiring stage:

- Include distinct sections for candidates who declined offers or were not selected, maintaining a comprehensive historical record.
- Designate a [project owner](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-owner-and-members#h_01HQ14CG830T486JSSMETEK9GW) for oversight.
- [Assign specific tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/assign-tasks-to-teammates) to relevant hiring managers while using comments for collaboration.

## Automate your workflow

#### Set up rules to streamline processes:

- Use [rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules) to notify hiring managers when new candidates are added.
- Have a rule move tasks between sections based on interview stage.
- Send reminders for scheduled interviews.
- Use [ rule creator](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-rules) to easily build rules, automating repetitive tasks and maintaining consistency.

#### Collect candidate information using Asana forms:

- Add a [form](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms) to your project which will automatically create a task for new applications.
- Use forms to gather basic candidate information, resumes and team referrals.

## Communication and collaboration

#### Maintain consistent candidate communication:

- Use [comments](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-comments-and-attachments) to centralize feedback from interviewers.
- @mention team members to bring them into discussions.
- [Attach](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-comments-and-attachments#gl-attachments) interview notes and evaluation forms directly to tasks.

#### Enhance candidate interaction:

- Collect referrals or direct applications using Asana form, ensuring seamless data capture and task creation.
- Automate candidate feedback reminders, using rules and [Asana AI](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai) to ensure timely and consistent communication.

## Resource management

#### Monitor hiring team workload:

- Use [workload](https://help.asana.com/s/article/portfolio-workload-and-universal-workload) to balance interview schedules.
- Track interviewer availability and capacity.
- Reassign tasks when team members are overloaded.

#### Review and optimize the workflow:

- Regularly review the project dashboard for insights generated by Asana AI on workflow efficiency.
- Adapt strategies based on AI feedback, focusing on bottlenecks and candidate satisfaction trends.

## AI-powered insights

#### Leverage Asana AI features:

- Get AI-generated insights about pipeline bottlenecks.
- Use [Smart status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-status) for quick updates on hiring progress.

## Reporting and analytics

#### Track recruitment metrics:

- Create [dashboards](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-dashboards) to monitor; time-to-hire, pipeline status, source effectiveness.
- Generate [reports](https://help.asana.com/s/article/reporting-with-dashboards) for stakeholder updates.

## Integration with tools

#### Connect with your existing tools:

- Integrate with [calendar apps](https://help.asana.com/s/article/calendars-and-asana) for interview scheduling.
- Connect with [Slack](https://help.asana.com/s/article/slack-and-asana) for real-time notifications.
- Link to [cloud storage](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules-integrations-for-cloud-storage-and-file-sharing) for document management.

By following these steps, your recruitment team can create an efficient, transparent, and collaborative candidate tracking process in Asana. This system helps ensure a positive experience for both candidates and hiring teams while maintaining organization throughout the recruitment lifecycle.
