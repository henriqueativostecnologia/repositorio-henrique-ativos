# Modify your Asana plan

From time to time it may make sense to upgrade your plan's tier, expand the number of seats on your plan, or downgrade to a smaller number. This article outlines how you can take those actions.

#### Related articles

- [How to cancel your Asana plan](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-cancel-your-asana-plan?language=en_US)

## How to downgrade to Asana Personal, our free tier

If you’re looking for personal or basic task management, you can at any time downgrade back to Asana Personal. If you choose to downgrade, the downgrade will take effect at the end of your current subscription period.

- From the **Billing** tab on your admin console select **Cancel plan/trial** or **Edit plan**
- Contact our [Support team](https://asana.com/support "Support team") at any time if you are having any issues with canceling your plan, editing your plan, or switching back to Asana Personal.

### Downgrade and other options

If you choose to terminate your subscription to a paid tier, you and your team will lose access to paid features, including advanced team and project permissions, custom fields, timeline view, and task dependencies.

**All cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.**

There are other options you may want to consider. If you believe that your team is unsure of how to make the most out of your paid features, take advantage of the [free trainings](https://academy.asana.com/series/master-premium-features "free trainings") that we offer to our paid users.

You can read more about plan options and [pricing](https://asana.com/pricing "pricing") in Asana.

### Note for trial plan users

Once you trial finishes, you'll be prompted to select a plan. Click **Select your plan**.

If you'd like to downgrade to the free Asana Personal plan, the click **Not ready? Downgrade to a free Asana Personal plan** on the next screen.

Check the **Confirm downgrade** box, and click **Downgrade to Personal. **

## How to edit your current plan

You can edit or switch your plan from the plan details section via the **Billing** tab in the admin console.

#### Credit card customers:

If you pay by credit card, simply select **Edit plan** from the **Billing** tab of your admin console.

#### Manual invoice customers:

When you click into **Edit plan**, you will be routed to a data capture form where you will be asked to fill in details about your project, team, and the process you want to track in Asana.

After you have filled out your preferences, click **Submit** and an Asana specialist will be in touch after processing your request.

## How to change your paid plan size

Add seats to your plan through the admin console. If you’re on a manual invoice you will be directed to Asana sales to request the change.

You can change the size of your plan via the **Seats** drop-down menu when you choose to update the plan.

#### On the **Change Plan Details** page, you can:

1.  Choose your type of paid plan
2.  Choose whether to be billed **monthly** or **yearly**
3.  Choose the size of your plan you wish to upgrade with the **Seats** drop-down
4.  Choose whether to upgrade the entire organization or specific teams in a division plan

When you have selected all of your options, click the **Update Plan** button

Asana does not offer paid subscriptions for 1-user plans but offer set increments of seats for smaller teams; such as 2, 3, 4 and up to 5-seat plans. When upgrading, you'll need to choose the most appropriate seat tier for your team size.

## Managing AI Requests in the admin console

With the launch of **AI Teammates**, your organization now consumes **AI Requests**; the unit of usage that powers AI Teammate work across your domain. To help admins track and manage this new resource, we've added AI usage and billing tools directly to the admin console.

### What's new

**1. AI consumption dashboard**

Admins can now view a dedicated AI usage dashboard in the admin console. This gives visibility into:

- Total AI Requests consumed across the domain

- Usage trends over time

- Which AI Teammates or workflows are driving consumption

This helps you understand how AI Teammates are being used across your organization and plan ahead for future needs.

 

**2. Buy, manage, and cancel AI Requests**

A new section of the **Billing** tab lets admins:

- **Purchase** additional AI Requests for the domain

- **Manage** existing AI Request allocations

- **Cancel** AI Requests when they're no longer needed

These controls sit alongside your existing Asana billing settings, so managing seats and AI Requests happens in one place.

### How AI Requests work

- **Pooled at the domain level.** AI Requests aren't assigned to individual users or teams, they're shared across your entire domain, so any AI Teammate activity draws from the same pool.

- **Do not roll over.** Unused AI Requests expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not carry forward. Plan your purchases based on expected usage for the upcoming cycle, since unused capacity won't be banked for later.

**Where to find these settings**

1.  Go to the **Admin Console**

2.  Select the **Billing** tab

3.  Look for the **AI Requests** section to view your dashboard, purchase additional requests, or manage your current allocation
