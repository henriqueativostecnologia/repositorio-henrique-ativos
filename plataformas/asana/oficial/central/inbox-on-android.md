# Inbox on Android

Inbox is your notification center for Asana. Your Inbox displays updates on all [projects](https://asana.com/guide/help/projects/basics "projects") you're a member of and tasks that you collaborate on or are [assigned](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/understanding-tasks#gl-assignee "assigned") within a given workspace or organization.

To get to your inbox, tap on the **Inbox** icon from your **Home** screen.

#### From your inbox, you can:

1.  Sort and filter
2.  Press and hold on a task to take action
3.  Tap the three dot icon to view more options

### Create follow-up tasks

Inbox follow-up tasks allow users to get updates from their team and respond in real-time, straight from your inbox.

To create a follow-up task press and hold on the task. Click on **Follow up**.
