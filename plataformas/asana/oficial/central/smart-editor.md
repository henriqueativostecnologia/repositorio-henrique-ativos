# Smart editor

**Available on Asana [*Starter*](https://asana.com/plan/starter), [*Advanced*](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [*Enterprise*](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [*Enterprise+*](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [*legacy tiers*](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Premium, Business, and Legacy Enterprise.**

Write clearer, more compelling responses with smart editor. The **Edit writing** and **Improve format** features help you strike the right tone, adjust the length of your writing, and improve the structure of your responses.

Smart editor can be used within task descriptions, comments, and status editor. Simply click the **AI assist options** button in the rich text toolbar to get started.

## Edit writing

Use **Recommend edits** to quickly perfect your text’s grammar and spelling, as well as tone and length. Tone adjustment options include: **Direct**, **Polite**, **Professional**, **Casual**, and **Friendly**. You can also adjust the length of your text to make it shorter or longer, depending on your need.

1.  Write a task description or comment, or begin a status update in the status editor.
2.  Choose the **AI assist options** icon from the rich text toolbar as shown.
3.  Select **Recommend edits**. This feature is designed to correct any errors in grammar or spelling.
4.  Adjust the length and tone via the pop-up window or by clicking the **Tone and length** button.
5.  When adjustments are complete, click **Replace** to replace the existing text.
6.  Click **Discard** if you wish to use your original text instead.

## Improve format

Using **Format** allows you to focus on quickly capturing your thoughts, without getting bogged down in tedious formatting work.

1.  Write a task description or comment, or begin a status update in the status editor.
2.  Choose the **AI assist options** icon from the rich text toolbar as shown.
3.  Click **Recommend edits**.
4.  Select **Format**. This feature lets Asana AI handle the formatting work.
5.  When adjustments are complete, click **Replace** to replace the existing text.
6.  Click **Discard** if you wish to use your original text instead.
