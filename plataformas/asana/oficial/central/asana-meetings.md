# AI Meetings

**AI Meetings is available for Asana Command, [Asana Client Management](https://help.asana.com/s/article/what-is-asana-client-management), and AWM customers who have AI Meetings enabled.**

**If you have found an issue with AI Meetings, see** [Troubleshooting AI Meetings](https://help.asana.com/s/article/troubleshooting-asana-meetings)**.**

## What is AI Meetings?

AI Meetings connects the conversations where work gets discussed to the tasks that follow. Instead of meeting recaps living in a separate tool, Asana transcribes your meetings and generates an AI summary and suggested action items that you can convert into tasks without leaving Asana.

Additionally, AI Meetings leverages context from your Work Graph® to ensure summaries and task assignments are accurately grounded in your existing projects, goals, and team members.

## Consent for recording

To use AI Meetings to record and transcribe you need to get consent from the other participants in the meeting.

AI Meetings does not begin transcribing automatically. A reminder to obtain consent appears in the meeting notification before recording, and you are responsible for ensuring that all required consent has been obtained. If a participant does not consent, click **Stop** in the Asana application to end the recording.

When you join a meeting, Asana will post a chat message from your account to notify other participants that the meeting is being recorded.  However, you are still responsible for verbally informing and getting consent from all meeting participants before recording.

## How does AI Meetings use your data?

Asana Desktop captures audio directly from your device and works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams meetings. Learn more about how [Asana AI handles your data](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-features-and-admin-controls).

## Transcript and summary visibility 

Transcripts, summaries, and action items are private by default. Meetings can be assigned collaborators and to projects  during or after the meeting. 

When sharing a meeting with a collaborator, you can specify their level of access to the meeting. When sharing with a project, you can choose whether to grant access to all project members or restrict it to selected collaborators. Meeting content is only used as context for Asana’s AI tools for users who have been granted access to it.

## Cost

Asana charges [one AI Request](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-asana-ai-requests-and-usage-allocation?language=en_US) to generate a summary and suggested tasks from a transcript (whether from Asana Desktop or Zoom) for meetings up to 60 minutes long. Meetings over 60 minutes incur one additional **AI Request** for each extra 60-minute block or fraction thereof. 

## How to set up AI Meetings

AI Meetings works with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar to bring your schedule into Asana, and with either Asana Desktop or Zoom to handle recording and transcription. Follow the steps below to connect both.

### Sync your calendar

1.  In Asana, navigate to **Meetings** in the sidebar under **Knowledge**.
2.  Select **Connect calendar** or **Get started** from the top banner.
3.  Follow the prompts to authenticate your **Google Calendar** or Microsoft Outlook account.

### Set up recording and transcription

After connecting your calendar, choose how you want to record and transcribe your meetings. Asana supports two options: [Asana desktop app](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-desktop-app?language=en_US) and Zoom integration. Asana Desktop captures audio from your device for meetings on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. The Zoom integration pulls transcripts only from Zoom meetings you host, then generates a summary and action items.

### Set up Asana Desktop

1.  [Download](https://asana.com/download) Asana desktop or click **Download** from the setup screen.
2.  Follow the steps to install Asana Desktop.
3.  Enable notifications for Asana Desktop in your operating system settings so you are alerted when a meeting is starting.

> On macOS, Asana asks for microphone, system audio, and accessibility permissions during setup. On Windows, no additional system permissions are required.

### Setting up recording and transcription with Zoom 

Zoom transcription requires you to be the meeting host. After the Zoom recording ends, AI Meetings retrieves the transcript from Zoom and generates a summary and action items. It typically takes longer for Asana to receive Zoom transcripts than Asana Desktop transcripts.  Once you set up the Zoom integration, Asana will automatically pull the transcripts from all Zoom meetings that you record as the host going forward and generate summaries and action items for each.

 

To connect Zoom, select **Connect Zoom** from the setup screen and follow the steps.

## Transcribing your meetings

> If more than one person records the same meeting, Asana uses the first transcript to generate the summary and action items. All transcripts are still accessible; open the meeting and select **Show more** to view them.

### Transcribing your meetings with Asana Desktop

Calendar events must include a video call link from Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams.

Once Asana Desktop is set up, there are three ways to start transcribing a meeting:

1.  From the Meetings view
2.  From a desktop notification
3.  From the Asana Desktop mini app

**From the Meetings view**

1.  Navigate to **Meetings** in the sidebar.
2.  Select the  **Home** tab to see meetings that have not started yet. For any meeting starting within the next five minutes, select **Start**. 

Asana opens your call and displays a recording control at the top left of your screen which you can use to stop transcribing.

**From the Desktop notification**

If you have enabled notifications for Asana Desktop in your operating system, Asana alerts you before a meeting starts and when it detects you have joined a supported call. Select the notification to start transcribing directly.

**From the Asana Desktop Mini App**

Select the calendar icon in Asana Desktop’s Mini App to see your meetings and start transcribing:

### Transcribing your meetings with Zoom

To transcribe with Zoom, start a cloud recording as the meeting host. When the recording ends, AI Meetings retrieves the transcript from Zoom and generates a summary and action items automatically.

## Using AI-generated meeting summaries and action items

When your summary and action items are ready, you receive a [notification in your Asana inbox](https://help.asana.com/s/article/inbox?language=en_US). 

Summaries are available within minutes when using **Asana Desktop**; Zoom processing may take longer.

Select the notification to open the meeting. You will see the date, time, participants, and the AI-generated summary, which can be edited by any meeting member. Select **Show more** to view the host, meeting link, description, and full transcript (which cannot be edited).

Draft tasks appear at the bottom of the meeting record. You can edit a suggestion before accepting it, which creates an Asana task. Declining a suggestion removes it.

 
To return to any past meeting, navigate to **Meetings** under **Knowledge** and select the **Previous meetings** tab.

## AI Meetings settings

Select the gear icon at the top right of the **Meetings** page to open **AI Meetings settings**, which includes three settings.

**Preferences** allows you to set a default sharing setting for meeting summaries and action items, and to enable or disable desktop and browser alerts. Desktop notification preferences are managed from the gear icon in **Asana Desktop**, and browser preferences are managed from Asana on the web. 

**Calendar** lets you enable or disable your **Google Calendar** or **Outlook Calendar** integration. 

**Transcription** lets you manage your Asana Desktop or Zoom connections.
