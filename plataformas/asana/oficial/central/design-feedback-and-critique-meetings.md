# Managing design feedback and critique meetings

Design feedback and critique meetings are essential for creative teams to improve their work and maintain high-quality output. With Asana you can streamline your design feedback process, making your meetings more productive and ensuring that feedback is effectively captured and acted upon.

### Pre-meeting preparation

1.  Create a [new project](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-a-project?language=en_US) named "Design Feedback and Critique Meetings"
2.  Use a meeting agenda template or create a custom [template](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-templates?language=en_US) for your team's needs
3.  **Agenda Setup:** Create a [task](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-tasks?language=en_US) for each meeting in the project with [subtasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-tasks?language=en_US) for each agenda item, such as design showcase, feedback round, and action item assignments.

## **Scheduling and organizing meetings**

1.  **Calendar view:** Use [calendar view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/calendar-view?language=en_US) to schedule meetings and ensure all team members are aware of upcoming critique sessions to schedule meetings and keep team members informed
2.  **Automations:** Set [automated reminders](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US?language=en_US) for upcoming meetings to keep everyone on track

**Collecting feedback:**

1.  **Use tasks for agenda items:** Each agenda item can be a task with a due date and assignee to ensure that all items are covered during the meeting. 
2.  **Comments and collaboration:** During the meeting, use the comment function to note down feedback on specific designs. You can also create tasks directly from Zoom meetings using the [Zoom integration ](https://help.asana.com/s/article/zoom-and-asana-integration?language=en_US)[](https://help.asana.com/s/article/zoom-and-asana-integration?language=en_US))
3.  **Custom Fields:** Create [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields?language=en_US) to categorize feedback (e.g, major changes, minor tweaks, appreciation)

**Assigning action items:**

1.  **Subtasks for action items:** Create subtasks under each main task for action items raised during the critique.

2.  **Assign and set due dates:** Assign these subtasks to the appropriate team members with clear due dates.

**Managing design files:**

1.  **Attachments:** [Attach](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-comments-and-attachments?language=en_US) the design files and mockups to the agenda tasks. Use file version control to manage updated designs.
2.  **Google drive integration:** Integrate with [Google Drive](https://help.asana.com/s/article/google-and-asana?language=en_US) (or other cloud storage services) to synchronize design files seamlessly. 
3.  You can also use [approvals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/approvals?language=en_US) and [proofing](https://help.asana.com/s/article/proofing?language=en_US) to keep all information in the task and drive clarity on what changes are needed. 

**Post-meeting follow-up:**

1.  **Tracking progress:** Mark tasks as complete once the feedback is incorporated.
2.  **Project status updates:** Provide regular updates on the progress of feedback integration to keep the team informed.

**Continuous improvement:**

1.  **Retrospective meetings:** Schedule periodic retrospective meetings to evaluate the effectiveness of the critique process and make necessary adjustments.
2.  **Feedback loop:** Implement a feedback loop to continuously improve the critique process, leveraging custom fields and reporting features for insights.

**High-level overview:**

1.  **Project status:** Use [project status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-progress-and-status-updates?language=en_US) updates to provide broader visibility on the feedback process, letting stakeholders know the status of various feedback items.
2.  **Portfolios:** Create a [Portfolio](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-a-portfolio?language=en_US) to keep track of multiple design projects and their associated feedback cycles if managing feedback for multiple projects.

**Centralized notifications:**

1.  **Slack integration:** Integrate Asana with [Slack](https://help.asana.com/s/article/slack-and-asana?language=en_US) to receive real-time notifications about task updates, comments, and meeting schedules directly in the team's communication channel.
2.  **Email integration:** Use [email integration](https://help.asana.com/s/article/email-notifications?language=en_US) for those who prefer updates directly in their inbox.

By following this structured approach, you can make your design critique meetings more efficient and ensure that feedback is systematically captured and integrated into your projects.

## **Tips for success**

1.  Keep meetings focused by sticking to the agenda in your project
2.  Encourage team members to review designs and add comments before the meeting
3.  Use reporting features to track the progress of feedback implementation over time
4.  Regularly review and update your critique process based on team feedback and project outcomes
