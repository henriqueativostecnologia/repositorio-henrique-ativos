# Permissions management add-on

The permissions management add-on gives admins greater control over who can access, modify, or manage content within Asana. It includes tools for defining and customizing permission roles, managing third-party app usage, and global controls for guest access, mobile behavior, and file sharing. This add-on helps organizations better align Asana access with their internal policies and team structures.

## What is the permissions management add-on, and how does it work?

The add-on provides organization admins with additional controls on user permissions and access to the mobile app, or other third-party applications.

## Who is eligible for purchasing this add-on? 

Customers with organization-wide Asana Enterprise subscription are required to purchase access to the permissions management add-on. If you are on an existing workspace or divisions, you must upgrade to an organization-wide Asana Enterprise subscription. 

Customers on Enterprise+ subscriptions already have access to the features in this add-on.

## How do you access the add-on? 

If you meet the eligibility criteria and have purchased the add-on, your organization [super admin](https://help.asana.com/s/article/admin-and-super-admin-roles-in-asana?language=en_US#gl-super-admin-vs-admin) can access these via the [admin console](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-access-the-admin-console?language=en_US)

## How is the add-on priced? 

The add-on is charged on a per-user, per-month basis, based on the number of members with an Enterprise subscription.
