## HubSpot

The HubSpot + Asana integration allows teams to keep track of important context from deals and marketing campaigns across apps. Track campaign performance and collaborate with teams in one location, without having to switch between tools.

Easily attach details from HubSpot deals directly into an Asana task, to track relevant information like the deal amount and deal owner.

Quickly add your chosen campaign from HubSpot by clicking the HubSpot field from your task view. Attach campaigns relevant to the tasks you're tracking in Asana, so you can view accurate HubSpot campaign statuses and metrics while working on Asana tasks.

#### Here’s how to attach a HubSpot deal or marketing campaign to an Asana task:

1.  Click on **Install HubSpot for Asana**
2.  If you are not already logged into your Asana account, you will be asked to login
3.  Click **Connect to HubSpot**
4.  Click **Sign into your HubSpot account** or **Create a new HubSpot account** if you don’t have one already
5.  Once signed into your HubSpot account, **choose the HubSpot account** you'd like to connect Asana
6.  Choose **Connect app** to grant app permissions for HubSpot to access your Asana account
7.  Search for projects to add the HubSpot app to or select Skip for now
8.  You can also now navigate to your Asana account, and find the project where you want to enable the HubSpot widget
9.  Click **Customize** in the upper right-hand corner
10. Scroll down to the Apps section, then select **+Add app**
11. Select **HubSpot**
12. Click **Add to project** and follow the steps to connect HubSpot to Asana
13. Navigate to an Asana task
14. Link a deal or a marketing email to a task by clicking the **Add HubSpot deal or marketing email** button on the **HubSpot field** at the top of the task details pane
15. Insert the URL for your relevant HubSpot deal or email campaign
16. Attached HubSpot details will appear as a widget in the task pane

Note: This integration is already built into Asana, but you need to sign up for a HubSpot account to get started.

#### To disconnect HubSpot from Asana:

1.  Navigate to **Customize**
2.  Click **Apps**
3.  Select **HubSpot** and click **Remove from project**

#### To disconnect Asana from HubSpot:

1.  In your HubSpot account, navigate to **Reporting & Data \> Integrations**
2.  Click **Actions** on Asana and click **Uninstall**
3.  In the dialog box, type “**uninstall**” in the text field and click **Uninstall**

Uninstalling Asana, disconnecting the HubSpot widget, or removing an automation does not impact a user's HubSpot accounts or data; Asana does not delete or edit any data in HubSpot.

The HubSpot widget is available on Asana [*Starter*](https://asana.com/plan/starter), [*Advanced*](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [*Enterprise*](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [*Enterprise+*](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [*legacy tiers*](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Premium, Business, and Legacy Enterprise.
