Create tasks and conversations from emails with email forwarding.

#### To access **Settings**:

1.  From the sidebar, click on your profile photo
2.  Select **Settings** from the menu
3.  Select **Email forwarding**

 

From the **Email forwarding** tab, you can select the workspaces or organizations where you would like to create tasks and conversations from emails.

Add or remove emails by navigating to the **Account** tab.

Learn more about this feature in the [create tasks by email ](https://asana.com/guide/help/email/email-to-asana "create tasks by email") guide article.
