# Sharing feedback on AI features

Asana AI helps you work smarter, automate tasks, and gain valuable insights into your projects. This article provides information on the data shared when you choose to provide in-product feedback for our AI features. We value customer feedback as it helps us improve the AI experience.

When you enter feedback on our AI features in the box provided and click the **Share Feedback** button, your data is collected and used as described below.

## What data is collected?

The data collected refers to all information you provide during your interaction within the respective AI feature. This may include information you've directly provided to the feature, including task or project content deemed relevant to the AI feature, or information about you that is relevant to the feature.

**Note:** If your organization uses Asana's Enterprise Key Management (EKM) feature, please note that data sent to AI partners will not remain encrypted with your organization's keys. This is because data must be sent to our third-party AI partners in order to complete queries. To learn more about data protections provided by Asana's AI partners, please see [Asana AI FAQ](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-faq).

As an example, here is some data that is collected when you submit feedback for a collection of our features:

**Smart import
**Data collected includes the content of the file, additional instructions provided, and the project that was generated.

**Smart chat
**Data collected includes the question asked of the bot and information selected from your project and tasks used by the bot to answer the question.

**Smart editor
**Data collected includes the tone selected and the text selected in the text editor.

**Smart summaries
**Data collected includes the content of the task and the summary that was generated.

**Create subtasks
**Data collected includes the content of the task and the generated subtasks.

**Smart summaries
**Data collected includes the recent activity data of the project and the summaries that are generated.

**Smart fields
**Data collected includes information selected from your project and tasks used by the bot to create the custom fields. The bot can also look at each of the selected tasks, and try to extract the information in them to generate fields. Data collected also includes the generated custom fields, and the value of the custom field for each task that the bot fills.

**Smart status
**Data collected includes information selected from your project and tasks used by the bot to create the status update and the generated status update. The bot can also look at relevant information about those selected tasks, and try to extract decisions that were made.

**Smart rules
**Data collected includes project context such as name, custom fields, sections and forms. The user's input text used to describe the rule they wish to generate is also collected, as is the output rule that is generated.

Data is transferred, processed, and stored consistent with our [Privacy Statement](https://asana.com/terms#privacy-statement).

## What is that data used for?

The data collected will be used for feature improvement, as well as bug identification and fixing.

**Feature improvement**
By analyzing usage data, we will gain insights into what works well and what could be enhanced. We may use Large Language Models (LLMs) to summarize and categorize feedback, helping our teams more efficiently identify common themes and prioritize feature enhancements. This will help us provide you with new and improved features in Asana.

**Bug identification and fixing**
User data helps us identify and diagnose any technical glitches, ensuring a smoother and more reliable experience for everyone.

Your feedback is crucial in helping us refine and enhance our AI features. By sharing your experiences, you contribute to the continuous improvement of Asana's AI capabilities.
