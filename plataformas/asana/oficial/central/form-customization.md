# Form customization

This article covers how to customize your form header and add custom branding to your form. Visit [this article](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US) for more information on creating forms in Asana.

## Header customization

**Available on Asana [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) tiers, as well as [legacy tiers](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) Business and Legacy Enterprise.**

To add a cover image, click on the **Add cover image** icon at the top of your form.

The image dimensions for the Header are 800 x 120 pixels.

## Custom branding on forms

**[Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) and [Legacy Enterprise](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) users that have uploaded a logo to Asana via their [Admin Console](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-access-the-admin-console?language=en_US) will have the option to display this logo on their forms.**

To add custom branding to your form, first upload your company logo. You can do this through the **Settings** tab of your admin console.

Once the logo has been uploaded, navigate to your form and click on the form name / form description. From here, click **Show your organization's logo on this form**.
