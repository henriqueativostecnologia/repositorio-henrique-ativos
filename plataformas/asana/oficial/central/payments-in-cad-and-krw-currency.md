# Payments in CAD and KRW currency

Customers in Canada and South Korea can now upgrade to Asana [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter) and [*Advanced*](https://asana.com/plan/advanced) in their local currency when paying by credit card. Customers in Canada can also pay in their local currency with PayPal.

## Upgrade in your local currency

To upgrade in your local currency:

1.  Click on the **Upgrade** button on the right of the top bar

## Choose your plan

Select which plan you would like to upgrade to. Select from Starter or Advanced. It is not currently possible to upgrade to Enterprise or Enterprise+ using CAD or KRW.

> If you are currently paying in USD and would like to change to CAD or KRW, please [contact our Support team](https://asana.com/support "contact our Support team") to assist you.

Paying in CAD or KRW for manual invoices is currently not supported.
