# Outlook add-on for Asana Gov

**This feature is currently in limited availability and may not be visible to all users.**

The Outlook add-on for Asana Gov brings your Asana work into Outlook. You can turn emails into tasks, review task details, and add comments without leaving your inbox. The add-on runs entirely within the FedRAMP Moderate and GCC boundary, so teams in government environments can manage work from email in a compliant setting.

- [Asana for Outlook add-in](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-for-outlook-add-in)
- [Introducing Asana Gov](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-gov)

## Install the add-on

Your admin can install the Outlook add-on for Asana Gov for your organization. After installation, select the **Asana** icon in Outlook to open the add-on pane. The pane opens alongside the email you are reading.

## Log in and log out

Select **Log in** in the add-on pane. Sign in with your Asana Gov account, and the add-on opens to your task view. To log out, open **Settings** and select **Log out**.

## Choose a workspace

Open **Settings** and select the workspace you want to use. Tasks you create and comments you add go to the workspace you selected. You can switch workspaces at any time from **Settings**.

## Create a task from an email

Open the email you want to turn into a task and click the **Asana gov** button. Click the **Settings** button to choose the domain. Once you’ve selected your domain, click **Create new task**.

Enter a task name, assignee, due date, project, description, and select **Create task**. The task appears in your selected workspace, and the email details are available from the task.

You have the option to send the email as a comment and to make it public to your entire domain.

## View task details

Select a task in the add-on pane to view its details, such as the name, due date, and assignee. Review the details before you add a comment.

## Security and compliance

The Outlook add-on for Asana Gov operates within the FedRAMP Moderate and GCC boundary. Your task data and email content stay within your compliant environment while you work in Outlook.
