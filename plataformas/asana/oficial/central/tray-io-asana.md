# Tray.io + Asana

Access to tray.io templates allows you to integrate your Asana workflows with Google Forms and Salesforce. Learn more about using tray.io with Asana [here](https://asana.com/apps/tray-io "here").

## Salesforce

The Salesforce and Asana integration, connected by tray.io, is quick and easy to set up and allows you to further customize your workflows between Salesforce and Asana.

### Connect Salesforce and Asana to:

- Standardize your CRM processes
- Create Asana projects for your Accounts & Opportunities
- Automate your task lists

Opportunities created in Salesforce will automatically create an Asana task for the Opportunity, using a **template task** you've already created in Asana.

## Google Forms

Create Asana tasks and projects from new [Google Form](https://asana.com/guide/help/api/forms#gl-google-forms "Google Form") submissions to standardize internal processes, collect information and keep your team members on track.
