## Permissions available in the paid version of Asana

Generally, if someone can see something, they can edit it, but paid versions give you have the option to make projects [comment-only ](https://asana.com/guide/help/permissions/project-permissions#gl-comment-only-projects)or[ private to members](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-privacy-settings?language=en_US). Comment-only projects let you share information with your entire team or company without worrying about unwanted changes or accidental edits, except for the people you grant edit access to. Private to members projects ensure sensitive tasks or information is on accessible only to specific project members. [Tasks themselves inherit the permissions](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-permissions?language=en_US) of the project they are in.
