# Payments in MXN and BRL currency

Customers in Brazil and Mexico can now upgrade to Asana [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter) and [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced) in their local currency when paying with credit cards or PayPal.

## Upgrade in your local currency

To upgrade in your local currency:

1.  Click on the **Upgrade** button on the right of the top bar or [visit the upgrade page directly](https://app.asana.com/-/upgrade).

## Choose your plan

Select which plan you would like to upgrade to. Select from Starter or Advanced. It is not currently possible to upgrade to Enterprise or Enterprise+ using MXN or BRL.

> If you are currently paying in USD and would like to change to MXN or BRL, please [contact our Support team](https://asana.com/support "contact our Support team") to assist you.

Paying in MXN or BRL for manual invoices is currently not supported.
