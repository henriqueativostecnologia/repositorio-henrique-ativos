# Learn about Asana Advanced features

Asana Advanced provides all of the following features in addition to what's already included in Asana Starter. Visit our [pricing page](https://asana.com/pricing) to learn more, or [click here](https://app.asana.com/-/upgrade) to start a free Advanced trial.

## Asana AI

- [Smart fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Organize projects to support cross-functional collaboration with auto-generated custom fields.
- [Smart editor](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Write clearer, more compelling responses that strike the right tone.
- [Smart summaries](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Smart summaries offer visibility into the latest activities and progress about work. You can use smart summaries for tasks, projects, and portfolios.
- [Smart answers](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Use natural language to ask Asana questions, and get timely answers and insights about your projects, helping you to identify blockers, and determine next steps.
- [Smart status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Let AI draft status updates to help identify blind spots, open questions, roadblocks, and an efficient path towards your goals.
- [Smart rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Create rules with AI by providing simple, and natural language instructions via prompts.
- [AI Teammates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-teammates)—Collaborate with intelligent AI agents that seamlessly integrate into your workflows to take on routine tasks, triage work, and provide assistance so your team can focus on strategic goals.
- [Asana Dash](https://help.asana.com/s/article/chat-with-dash)—Start your day knowing exactly what to focus on with an AI Chief of Staff that proactively prioritizes tasks, flags blocked work, and summarizes key project updates.

## Workflow and automation

- [Time tracking](https://help.asana.com/s/article/time-tracking?language=en_US): Native time tracking feature allows you to estimate the time needed to complete a task, as well as record the actual time spent. Valuable time data helps inform decisions regarding workload and resourcing.
- [Critical path](https://help.asana.com/s/article/critical-path-on-timeline?language=en_US): Quickly identify which tasks are crucial for the completion of your project and visualize them on your timeline view
- [Forms with branching logic](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/forms#gl-branching "Forms with branching logic"): Based on initial responses, guide users through relevant follow-up questions. Craft forms that adapt to user input, providing up to five nested branch levels.
- [Proofing](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/proofing "Proofing"): Give clear feedback by leaving comments directly on images or PDFs that turn into tasks for easy tracking.
- [Approvals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/approvals?language=en_US "Approvals"): Request and give approvals on work in Asana.
- [Jira Cloud two-way sync](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-for-jira-cloud?language=en_US): Automatic updates between designated fields in Asana and Jira in either direction.
- [Rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules?language=en_US): Streamline routine tasks and establish workflows with ease.

## Reporting

- [Formulas](https://help.asana.com/s/article/formula-custom-fields?language=en_US): Perform calculations directly in Asana, making managing data easier and significantly enhancing your productivity.
- [Universal reporting](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting?language=en_US): Report on tasks and projects, as well as [portfolios](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting?language=en_US#h_01HETC8ZAZR6DZSRRBR9FW57YF) and [goals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting?language=en_US#h_01H9MV1GJWGXGYGYDV786EQ3VA).
- [Goals](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/goals "Goals"): Set, track, and manage company goals while connecting it to work to achieve it for a single source of truth for leads, executives, and individuals.
- [Portfolios](https://asana.com/guide/team/onboard/portfolio-management "Portfolios"): Organize strategic initiatives into portfolios to monitor the status, timelines, and teammate workloads across projects.
- [Portfolio workload](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/workload "Workload"): Asana’s resource management feature provides a single view of team bandwidth and helps you make informed staffing decisions and rebalance workloads if needed

## Privacy and security

- All of the privacy and security features included in Starter, such as SOC 2 Type II, Multi-factor authentication, private teams and private to members projects, Google SSO, and more.
