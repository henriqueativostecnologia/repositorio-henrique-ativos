# Asana Personal Plan details

Asana Personal is designed for personal productivity and includes Asana features to help you stay organized.

## What's included in Asana Personal

### Key features

- **Up to 2 seats per project and team** - Perfect for you plus one key person (partner, assistant, collaborator)
- **Unlimited projects** - Create as many projects as you need
- **Unlimited tasks** - No limits on what you can accomplish
- **Multiple views** - List, Board, and Calendar views
- **100+ integrations** - Connect with Google Drive, Slack, Zoom, and more
- **Mobile and desktop apps** - Access your work anywhere

### Perfect for

**Individual work** - Stay on top of deadlines and priorities - whether you’re managing your daily workload, running a business solo, or growing your side hustle.

**Big (and small) life events** - Organize weddings and moves with the same ease as tracking grocery lists and family chores. Coordinate the chaos of life’s biggest moments and daily routines in one place.

**Personal growth -** Keep reading lists, workout schedules, and learning plans perfectly organized to stay accountable and track every step toward your goals.

### Need more?

For teams of 3 or more people, or if you need advanced features like Asana AI, AI Studio, timeline view, workflow builder, and reporting dashboards, check out our [Starter plan](https://asana.com/plan/starter).

## Downgrading from a paid plan

You can downgrade from any paid Asana plan to Asana Personal at any time. Here's what you need to know about the downgrade process.

> Before downgrading, review what changes and make sure your team is prepared. Some features will become unavailable.

### How to downgrade

1.  Click your profile photo in the top right corner
2.  Navigate to the **Billing** tab
3.  Select **Edit plan** or **Cancel plan**
4.  Choose **Downgrade to Personal**
5.  Review the changes and confirm

### What happens when you downgrade

#### You'll keep:

✅ All your projects with 2 or less members

✅ All your data and work history

✅ List, board, and calendar views

✅ Basic integrations

✅ Mobile and desktop apps

#### You'll lose access to:

❌ Asana AI

❌ AI Studio

❌ Access to projects with paid features in it

❌ Projects with more than 2 members

❌ Public projects

❌ Timeline view (Gantt charts)

❌ Workflow builder and automation rules

❌ Advanced reporting and dashboards

❌ Forms

❌ Advanced search

❌ Admin console features

## Understanding seat limits

Asana Personal includes **up to** **2 seats** per project and team. If your team currently has more than 2 active users, you'll need to take action when downgrading.

### When you downgrade with more than 2 users:

- You'll be prompted to remove users to fit within the 2-seat limit
- Removed users will lose access to the workspace but their work history remains

### Can I change my mind?

Yes! You can upgrade back to a paid plan at any time. Your workspace and data will be exactly as you left it.

## Resolving common project issues

If you see an error message when trying to access a project on Asana Personal, one of the following three issues is usually the cause. If you are encountering more than one, work through them in the order listed below.

### Project has too many members

If a project has more than 2 members, you will see the message: "This project has more than 2 project members. You can remove members or upgrade to continue using this project."

To resolve this without upgrading:

1.  Click **More options**.
2.  Click **Manage project settings**.
3.  Remove all project members except yourself.
4.  Set **Access settings** to **Private**.

On Asana Personal, projects can only be private to yourself or shared with a team of up to 2 members. You cannot share a project directly with another individual user. If you want to collaborate with one other person, add them to a team first and then add the team as a project member. Teams on Asana Personal can also have a maximum of 2 members.

### Project privacy setting needs updating

If a project is shared with your organization, you will see the message: "You need a paid license to access this project. To access this project and keep it shared to organization, please upgrade."

To resolve this without upgrading:

1.  Click **Change access**.
2.  Click the **Access settings** dropdown.
3.  Select **Private**.

If **Private** is greyed out, you need to remove excess project members first, as described above. Once you are the only member, **Private** will become available.

### Project contains paid features

If a project includes paid features such as custom fields, start dates, or dependencies, you will see the message: "This project uses \[feature name\] which is a paid feature. Upgrade to get access."

To resolve this without upgrading:

1.  Click **Export project as CSV**.
2.  Open the CSV file and delete the columns for any paid features mentioned in the error message.
3.  Save the updated file.
4.  Create a new empty project in Asana.
5.  Click the project's **three dot icon** and select **Import**, then **CSV**.
6.  Import your saved file. The project will be created without the paid features.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I upgrade from Personal to Starter?

Yes! Both current and legacy personal plans can upgrade to any paid plan at any time. See [Upgrading your Asana plan](https://help.asana.com/s/article/upgrading-or-changing-your-asana-plan).

I need more than 2 seats but I'm on the current Personal plan. What are my options?

You can upgrade to Starter (starting at \$10.99/user/month) which includes unlimited users, plus advanced features like timeline view, workflow builder, and reporting. Learn more about [Starter plan features](https://asana.com/plan/starter).

> Still have questions? Contact our support team and we'll help you understand your specific plan details and options.
