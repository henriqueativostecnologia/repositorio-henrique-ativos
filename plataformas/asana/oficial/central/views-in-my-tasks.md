# Views in My tasks

Just like in projects, you can switch your view between [list view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/list-view?language=en_US), [board view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/board-view?language=en_US), and [calendar view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/calendar-view?language=en_US). Depending on the sections you’re using, you might prefer one view over another.

## Saved views

Saved views allows you to create new tabs in you My tasks with you preferred filter, sort, and group options.

### To create a saved view in My tasks:

1.  Click the **+ icon** next to your tabs
2.  Select your desired layout
3.  Enter a name for your new tab
4.  Choose your desired filters, sorts, and groups

Your view will be saved automatically. To remove the tab click on it and select **Remove**.

## Notes

You can add a notes tab to your My tasks allowing you to take meeting notes, jot down ideas, consolidate information and more.

To create a new **Notes** tab, click the **+ icon** next to your tabs and select **Note**.

## 7-day calendar view

View your My tasks calendar to keep track of important deadlines and upcoming tasks. Using the 7-day calendar view, you can reorder tasks due each day to keep your highest priority tasks at the top.

[](https://help.asana.com/hc/article_attachments/23285136423707)

### In My tasks 7-day calendar view, you can:

- Select **Week view** to toggle multi-day tasks on and off to prevent long-running tasks from cluttering up your weekly planning. Here you can also order your calendar by **Month view**, and show or hide weekends
- You can also organize your calendar view to show all tasks, incomplete tasks, or completed tasks by clicking on the **Task** button on your toolbar
- By dragging and dropping tasks, you can reorder tasks due each day to keep your highest priority tasks at the top
- Click on the **Unscheduled** button to bring up unscheduled tasks (tasks that have no due date). This also allows you to access a mini calendar so you can jump quickly to specific dates. You can drag tasks out of the **Unscheduled** sidebar into the week view to schedule tasks for specific days. The **Unscheduled** button is only visible in **Week view**.

### Weekly focus

[](https://assets.asana.biz/transform/07d17c5b-07e9-408b-9291-51e30a66fdfd/productui-helpcenter-asana_basics-getting-started-with-asana-s-mobile-app-001-en-us.png)

Kick off your work week more intentionally by setting a focus for the week. Capture your top priority for the week and see it displayed across mobile and web. Because this focus stays visible across platforms, setting a focus leads to better planning, actions, and outcomes.

On Monday mornings, mobile users are prompted to set a weekly focus. Weekly focus set on mobile will appear on desktop and web versions of Asana also but will not be editable there. Your weekly focus will appear at the top of your **My tasks** during the week to help guide your planning.

## View all your tasks across all your spaces

To view your My tasks list in each space you belong to (workspaces and organizations), you'll need to switch between these spaces in your [top bar](https://help.asana.com/s/article/navigating-asana?language=en_US "top bar").

However, you may wish to use this [third-party integration](https://asana.com/apps/taco "third-party integration"). Taco allows you to view all of your tasks in one list across workspaces and organizations.

> [Our Support team](https://asana.com/support "Our Support team") cannot, however, offer assistance with third-party tools as they are not developed by Asana.
