# Learn about Asana Starter features

Asana Starter provides all of the following features in addition to what's already included in Asana Personal. Visit our [pricing page](https://asana.com/pricing) to learn more, or [click here](https://app.asana.com/-/upgrade?tier=starter) to upgrade to a Starter plan.

## Asana AI

- [Smart fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Organize projects to support cross-functional collaboration with auto-generated custom fields.
- [Smart editor](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Write clearer, more compelling responses that strike the right tone.
- [Smart summaries](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Smart summaries allow users to get highlights from task descriptions and comments by summarizing task content and pulling out key action items as subtasks.
- [Smart status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US): Let AI draft status updates to help identify blind spots, open questions, roadblocks, and an efficient path towards your goals.
- [AI Teammates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-teammates)—Collaborate with intelligent AI agents that seamlessly integrate into your workflows to take on routine tasks, triage work, and provide assistance so your team can focus on strategic goals.
- [Asana Dash](https://help.asana.com/s/article/chat-with-dash)—Start your day knowing exactly what to focus on with an AI Chief of Staff that proactively prioritizes tasks, flags blocked work, and summarizes key project updates.

## Views

- All of the views available in Asana Personal, such as [list](https://help.asana.com/s/article/list-view?language=en_US), [board](https://help.asana.com/s/article/board-view?language=en_US), and [calendar](https://help.asana.com/s/article/calendar-view?language=en_US) view.
- [Timeline](https://help.asana.com/s/article/timeline?language=en_US): A live view of how your work fits together that helps you start projects on the right foot and hit your deadlines.
- [Gantt view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/gantt-view?language=en_US): This view equips project managers with the tools to visualize and manage project plans effectively, offering insight into task durations, delivery dates, dependencies, and baseline of your project plan.

## Workflow and automation

- [Over 100 free integrations](https://asana.com/apps?category=all-apps) including Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack and many more.
- [Forms](https://help.asana.com/s/article/forms?language=en_US): Use forms with internal and external teams, to collect and consolidate information and minimize any friction or misunderstanding for your team.
- [Custom templates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/create-projects-quickly-with-templates?language=en_US): Create custom templates for common workflows and projects to save time on future project setup and ensure that everyone is following the right processes.
- [Start dates and times](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US?language=en_US): Set a due date, due time, or a date range to let your teammates know when a task should be completed by.
- [Task dependencies](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-dependencies?language=en_US): Mark a task as blocking another task, or blocked by another task.
- [Rules](https://help.asana.com/s/article/rules?language=en_US): Streamline routine tasks and establish workflows with ease.

## Reporting

- [Custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields?language=en_US): Add additional data to tasks in your Asana projects.
- [Universal reporting](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting): Report on tasks and projects.
- [Project dashboards](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-dashboards?language=en_US): Dashboards are project-level tabs containing graphs and visualizations that let you zoom out from the day to day to quickly understand your team’s progress.
- [Advanced search](https://help.asana.com/s/article/search-and-search-views?language=en_US): Advanced search allows you to specify additional requirements for a search.
- [Milestones](https://help.asana.com/s/article/create-tasks-in-asana?language=en_US): Mark specific points in time along a project timeline to track major progress points that must be reached to achieve success.

## Privacy and security

- [Google SSO](https://help.asana.com/s/article/authentication-and-access-management-options-for-paid-plans?language=en_US): You have the option to require your members to authenticate via Google.
- [Private teams](https://help.asana.com/s/article/team-permissions?language=en_US) and projects with the [private to members](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-permissions?language=en_US) privacy setting: Manage access to projects and teams with Asana Starter.
- [Unlimited free guests](https://help.asana.com/s/article/guests-faq?language=en_US): Guests don't count towards your plan's seat count, and you don't pay for them.
- [Admin console](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-access-the-admin-console?language=en_US): Empowers all Asana admins and super admins with the administrative capabilities they need to champion Asana within their organizations.
- [Comment-only projects](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-permissions?language=en_US): Allow users to view or comment on projects while preventing any accidental or unnecessary changes.
- All of the privacy and security features included in Personal, such as SOC 2 Type II, Multi-factor authentication, cross-regional backups, and more.
