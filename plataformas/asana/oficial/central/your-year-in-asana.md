# Your year in Asana

Your year in Asana 2025 is a personalized review that showcases your accomplishments and activity in Asana throughout the year. This feature provides insights into your work patterns, collaboration efforts, and achievements during 2025, helping you reflect on your productivity and contributions.

Visualize your Asana data in new and insightful ways, including stats like **Tasks completed**, **Projects worked on**, **Total collaborators**, and even the number of **Minutes saved** using automations and workflows.

You can share your achievements using the sharing options provided at the end of the review. Remember to only share content that you have permission to share.

## How to access Your year in Asana

1.  Visit [asana.com/year-in-review](https://asana.com/year-in-review)
2.  Log in to your Asana account if prompted
3.  Cycle through the slideshow using the directional buttons on the side

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can access the feature?

Your year in Asana is available to users who have been active in Asana for more than 30 days in 2025. Users must also have signed up for Asana before November 1, 2025. They must be a full member of their domain, and must have sufficient collaborative activity to generate a meaningful experience.

If you believe you should be eligible for the feature but cannot access it, contact Asana support. Domain admins can request to enable the feature for their domain. Individual users can contact Asana support directly for assistance.

### How long will the review be available?

Your year in Asana will be available for a limited time, and will no longer be accessible in February 2026.

### Can I share my year in Asana with others?

Yes, share your year in Asana by using the download button, but don’t share anything that you don’t have permission to.

### Which Asana tier is the feature available to?

Your year in Asana is available to all tiers, including the free Asana Personal tier.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't log in to view my year in Asana

We recommend clearing your browser cookies, then trying to log into Asana again.

### I'm experiencing loading issues

Try clearing your cookies and logging back in to Asana. Rule out any issue with internet connection speed. If loading issues persist, contact Asana support.

You can use the account switcher to ensure you're logged into the correct workspace or organization before loading the experience. Check that you're logged into the expected space if you encounter issues.
