## What is Asana AI?

Asana AI is an intelligent tool integrated into Asana to help you manage your work more effectively. By leveraging artificial intelligence, Asana AI can automate routine tasks, provide insightful summaries, and assist with task creation and editing.

## AI Teammates

[AI Teammates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-teammates) are AI agents that work alongside your team in Asana. They can use the context in your work, follow guidance, and complete assigned tasks while keeping people involved in decisions, reviews, and handoffs.

 

You can use prebuilt AI Teammates for functions such as marketing, operations, and IT, or create custom AI Teammates for your workflows. Depending on your permissions and configuration, AI Teammates can connect to tools such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook Mail and Calendar, Slack, HubSpot, Figma, and Canva.

 

To get started, open a project or workspace and assign a task to an AI Teammate as you would assign work to a teammate. The AI Teammate uses its configured skills and the context in the task to complete the work. Review the result, provide guidance, or take over the decision when human judgment is needed.

## Asana Dash

[Asana Dash](https://help.asana.com/s/article/chat-with-dash) acts as an AI chief of staff for your work. It can surface priorities across goals, projects, meetings, email, and messages, helping you focus on what needs attention.

 

Dash can also help you ask questions about your work and capture follow-up actions from meetings, Slack conversations, and email. It connects decisions and next steps to structured work in Asana, so important actions do not remain disconnected from the projects they support.

## AI Studio

[AI Studio](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-studio) provides a no-code interface for building governed AI automations rooted in Asana work. Use it to create workflows for intake, routing, approvals, status updates, goal tracking, and capacity planning.

 

AI Studio helps you automate repetitive work while keeping the workflow visible to the people responsible for reviewing outcomes and making decisions.

## MCP and AI integrations

The Model Context Protocol, or MCP, is an open standard that enables AI tools to connect securely with Asana. With Asana’s MCP server, you can interact with your Asana work through natural language in supported tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

 

Depending on the connected tool and your permissions, you can search, create, update, and organize work directly from the conversation. Access and actions remain subject to the permissions and controls configured for your organization.

### 1. Smart status

Get quick updates on the status of your projects and tasks, making it easier to stay informed without digging through all the details.

Learn more about [smart status](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-status).

### 2. Smart chat

Get AI-powered answers to your project management questions, helping you make informed decisions quickly.

Learn more about [smart chat](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-chat).

### 3. Smart fields

Use AI to automatically fill in important fields, ensuring that your task details are always complete and up-to-date.

Learn more about [smart fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-fields).

### 4. Smart summaries

Receive concise summaries of your projects, tasks and portfolios, helping you understand the key points without reading through lengthy descriptions.

Learn more about [smart summaries](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-summaries).

### 5. Smart editor

Enhance your task creation and editing process with AI-powered suggestions, making it easier to write clear and effective task descriptions.

Learn more about [smart editor](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-editor).

### 6. Smart rule creator

Automate your workflows with custom rules powered by AI, ensuring that routine processes are handled efficiently.

Learn more about [smart rule creator](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-rules).

### 7. Smart goals

Standardize and improve the effectiveness of goal-setting with smart goals. Smart goals analyzes existing goals and offers suggestions for improvement, including adding success criteria, impact summaries, parent goals, supporting subgoals, and assigning accountable teams.

Learn more about [smart goals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-goals).

### 8. Smart projects

Use AI to quickly create customized projects. Smart projects suggests descriptions, sections, and custom fields all based on the project's name and purpose.

Learn more about [smart projects](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-projects).

### 9. Asana AI Studio

Asana AI Studio provides a no-code interface to set up smart workflows in minutes, combining the power of large language models (LLMs) and Asana’s rules engine. Smart workflows created in AI Studio can then be published for use across your organization.

Learn more about [Asana AI Studio](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-studio).

### 10. AI Meetings

AI Meetings connects live meeting discussions directly to the actionable work that follows by transcribing conversations within Asana. Rather than leaving meeting notes in a separate tool, it automatically translates key decisions and discussion points into structured tasks and updates to keep project workflows moving seamlessly.

Learn more about [AI Meetings.](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-meetings)

## Enable Asana AI

#### Enable Asana AI

To activate Asana AI, go to the **admin console** and enable Asana AI for your organization. Once enabled, team members can access the AI features available to them based on their permissions and plan.

Learn how to [enable Asana AI features](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-features-and-admin-controls).
