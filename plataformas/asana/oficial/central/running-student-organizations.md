# Running student organizations in Asana

Student organizations face unique challenges in coordinating activities, managing meetings, and maintaining continuity across leadership changes. Asana provides tools to streamline these processes and keep your organization running smoothly.

### Get set up

The first thing to do is to [create a workspace](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-workspaces) in Asana for your student organization. Once set up, [invite your members](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-invite-team-members-to-asana) using their email addresses.

## Create essential projects

Set up core [projects](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-a-project) to organize different aspects of your organization. Create an **Executive board meetings** project for agendas and action items, an **Events project** to plan and coordinate activities, a **Budget tracking project** to manage finances, and a **Member engagement project** to track participation and responsibilities.

## Managing meetings effectively

Structure your meeting projects by creating sections for different meeting types or timeframes. Add [tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/understanding-tasks) for agenda items and use [subtasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/subtasks) to break down action items. [Assign tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/assign-tasks-to-teammates) to specific members for follow-up, ensuring accountability and clear ownership of responsibilities. Don’t forget to use [due dates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times) to keep work on track.

## Tracking events and activities

Create a task for each event and break them down into subtasks for different responsibilities. Use [custom fields](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-fields) to track event status, budget allocation, venue details, and required resources. This helps maintain clear oversight of all your organization's activities. Take a look at our article on [Planning campus events in Asana](https://help.asana.com/s/article/campus-event-planning) for more on this topic.

## Maintaining organizational continuity

Create a **Knowledge base** or reference project to document important processes and procedures. Store important documents in this project, which you can keep track of using [Files view](https://help.asana.com/s/article/files-view). Update [templates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/task-templates) regularly to ensure tasks receive the right assignees and other details when template tasks are used. This ensures smooth transitions between leadership changes and preserves institutional knowledge for future officers as people leave the organization and new members take on leadership roles.

## Tips for success

To make the most of Asana for your student organization:

- Use templates - Create standardized templates for recurring events and meetings.
- Keep documentation centralized - Store all organization files and documents in Asana.
- Track member roles - Use custom fields to monitor positions and committee assignments.
- Document processes - Create clear handover documentation for future leadership.
- Set clear deadlines - Use due dates consistently to keep work on track.
