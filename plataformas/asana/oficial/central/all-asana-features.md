# All Asana features

**Please note that certain features may not be available in all Asana pricing tiers or plans.**

Explore Asana's capabilities with this ever-evolving list of features across all tiers, which includes links to detailed articles. To stay up-to-date on feature releases, head to our [Release notes article](https://help.asana.com/s/article/release-notes?language=en_US), updated monthly.

## Work, project, and task management

- [**Asana AI**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/get-started-with-asana-ai?language=en_US)—Asana AI drives clarity and accountability by eliminating guesswork in planning. It maximizes impact by speeding up workflows and allows you to scale work effortlessly. For more details on features and admin controls, visit our **[Asana AI features and admin controls](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-features-and-admin-controls?language=en_US)** article.
- **[Projects](https://asana.com/guide/help/projects/basics "Projects")**—Organize work into shared projects as lists or kanban boards for your initiatives, meetings, and programs.
- **[Tasks](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/actions "Tasks")**—Get clarity on who is doing what, by when.
- **[Subtasks](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/subtasks "Subtasks")**—Break a task into smaller parts, or show additional steps to complete a large task.
- **[Task assignees](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/understanding-tasks#gl-assignee "Task assignees")**—Give tasks a clear owner, so everyone knows who’s responsible.
- **[Sections](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-customization-and-views?language=en_US "Sections")** and **[columns](https://asana.com/guide/help/views/boards#gl-manage "columns")**—Group tasks into sections in list view, or columns in board view to keep tasks organized, or match your workflow stages.
- **[Custom fields](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/custom-fields "Custom fields")**—Create single-select, multi-select, number, or text custom fields in projects and portfolios to capture information uniformly, track the status, and sort and filter information like spreadsheet columns. You can also get [notifications when custom fields](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/custom-fields#gl-setting "notifications when custom fields") are updated.
- **[Forms](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/forms "Forms")**—Capture the right details every time for work requests. Forms are directly connected to projects, so you can track form submissions in one place.
- **[Due dates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US?language=en_US "Due dates")**—Due dates ensure every task gets completed on time. View tasks on an Asana calendar, or even your work calendar.
- **[Due times](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US?language=en_US#gl-due-date "Due times")**—Specify the time something is due so you don’t miss a deadline, and everyone will know when you need it by—no matter their time zone.
- **[Start dates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/scheduling-tasks-using-start-dates-due-dates-and-times?language=en_US?language=en_US#h_01HBB2EJQT8TTQ9SBQ2MEZ3WEB "Start dates")**—Start dates show when you should begin your work to hit your deadlines.
- **[Timeline](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/timeline "Timeline")**—A Gantt-style view of task and project deadlines to help you plan and schedule better.
- **[Attachments](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/comments#gl-attachments "Attachments")**—Add files from your computer, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or Box to any task or message.
- **[Likes](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/fields#gl-likes "Likes")**—Acknowledge a task or comment, say thanks, give a thumbs up, or vote with a like.
- **[Multi-home tasks](https://help.asana.com/s/article/multi-home-tasks-to-avoid-information-silos?language=en_US "Multi-home tasks")**—Keep the same task in multiple projects to show work in different contexts without duplicating efforts.
- **[Dependencies](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/dependencies "Dependencies")**—Make it clear which tasks are ready to start, and which tasks are waiting on others.
- **[Rules](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/rules "Rules")**—Automate manual processes like triaging, assigning tasks, updating fields, and more.
- **[Templates](https://asana.com/templates "Templates")**—Get started quickly with templates and reuse them every time you run a project. You can also [save your own projects as a template](https://asana.com/guide/team/advanced/create-use-asana-templates "save your own projects as a template") to never miss a step.
- **[Approvals](https://help.asana.com/s/article/approvals?language=en_US "Approvals")**—Request and give approvals on work in Asana.

## Communication

- **[Task comments](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/comments "Task comments")**—Comment directly on a task to clarify exactly what needs to be done, and @mention teammates or other work in Asana so everyone and everything stays connected.
- **[Proofing](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/proofing "Proofing")**—Give clear feedback by leaving comments directly on images or PDFs that turn into tasks for easy tracking.
- **[Project messages](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/messaging "Project messages")**—Discuss a project’s progress to keep the momentum going.
- **[Team pages](https://asana.com/guide/help/organizations/team-basics#gl-team "Team pages")**—See all of your team’s projects in one place, with a space for all-team messages and announcements.
- **Languages**—Asana is currently available in English, Traditional Chinese, Russian, Dutch, Polish, Italian, Swedish, Korean, French, German, Portuguese, Spanish and Japanese.
- **[Rich text](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/text#gl-rich-text "Rich text")**—Use rich text in larger text fields to make your message clear and organize your thoughts with numbered and bulleted lists.

## Views

- **[My Tasks](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/my-tasks "My Tasks")**—Plan your day with a prioritized to-do list.
- **[Inbox](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/inbox "Inbox")**—Receive and filter notifications only for the projects, messages, and tasks you’re following.
- **[List](https://asana.com/guide/help/views/list "List")**—The grid-like structure makes it easy to create, filter, and format tasks to actively collaborate with your team.
- **[Search](https://asana.com/guide/help/fundamentals/search "Search")**—Find the work you need quickly, without having to organize it painstakingly.
- **[Saved searches](https://asana.com/guide/team/advanced/advanced-search-reports "Saved searches")**—Save searches based on specific criteria to help you find work again, or make assessments based on your search criteria.
- **[Workload](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/workload "Workload")**—Asana’s resource management feature provides a single view of team bandwidth. It helps you make informed staffing decisions and rebalance workloads if needed.
- **[Calendars](https://asana.com/guide/help/views/calendar "Calendars")**—See any list of tasks on a calendar to get a clear view of when work is due.
- **[Files view](https://asana.com/guide/help/views/files "Files view")**—Find the project files you need quickly in a gallery view with all of the project’s attachments.
- **[Colorblind friendly mode](https://blog.asana.com/2017/01/new-accessibility-feature-colorblind-friendly/ "Colorblind friendly mode")**—See the full Asana color palette clearly in colorblind friendly mode.

## Reporting

- **[Goals](https://asana.com/guide/examples/project-management/goals-okrs-planning "Goals")**—Set, track, and manage company goals while connecting work to its purpose.
- **[Milestones](https://help.asana.com/s/article/create-tasks-in-asana?language=en_US "Milestones")**—Make project goals clear and keep your team motivated by setting task milestones as key markers of progress.
- **[Portfolios](https://asana.com/guide/team/onboard/portfolio-management "Portfolios")**—Organize strategic initiatives and monitor the status of all your important projects as a list or on a timeline in one place.
- **[Status updates](https://asana.com/guide/help/projects/progress#gl-status "Status updates")**—Craft a status update in seconds to share with relevant stakeholders and to populate portfolios.
- **[Dashboard](https://asana.com/guide/help/projects/dashboards "Dashboard")**—Provides custom real-time charts to help you understand where work may be blocked or off-track, with the ability to export charts.

## Team management

- **[Teams](https://asana.com/guide/help/organizations/team-basics "Teams")**—Create teams to organize your projects and connect teammates with a shared calendar and messages. You can also control team privacy settings.
- **[Collaborators](https://asana.com/guide/help/tasks/understanding-tasks#gl-collaborators "Collaborators")**—Add teammates as collaborators so they can follow task progress and receive relevant updates.
- **[Guests](https://asana.com/guide/help/organizations/basics#gl-people "Guests")**—Collaborate with vendors, contractors, and partners in Asana.
- **[View only](https://help.asana.com/s/article/view-only)**—View-only licenses in Asana are designed for users who need to monitor the progress of work without actively participating in tasks or projects.
- **[Permissions](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-permissions?language=en_US "Permissions")**—Limit access to any project, create private teams for sensitive work, or make public teams and projects for access by your entire organization.
- **[Admin controls](https://asana.com/guide/help/premium/admin-console-admin-roles "Admin controls")**—Designate organization super admins and admins who can add, remove, and manage members and their settings, and enforce password complexity. Customers can have additional controls with [SAML](https://help.asana.com/s/article/authentication-and-access-management-options-for-paid-plans?language=en_US) and our [Admin API](https://help.asana.com/s/article/audit-suite-api-and-integration-support?language=en_US).
- **[Privacy controls](https://help.asana.com/s/article/privacy-and-security?language=en_US "Privacy controls")**—Limit access to any project, create private teams for sensitive work, or make teams public.
- **Data security**—Easily export or delete data from Asana, and have your data backed up on a separate server.
- [**Domain-wide admin controls**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/domain-wide-admin-controls?language=en_US): Apply project defaults across your domain from the Admin console.
- [**Sandboxes**](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-sandboxes?language=en_US): Use a dedicated environment tailored for experimentation to test various workflows and integrations before deploying them.

## Integrations

Visit our [apps page](https://asana.com/apps "apps page") to see all integrations, or our [developers page](https://developers.asana.com/ "developers page") to learn about building your own.

### File creation and sharing

- **[Dropbox](https://asana.com/apps/dropbox "Dropbox")**, **[Google Drive](https://asana.com/apps/google-drive "Google Drive")**, and **[Box](https://asana.com/apps/box "Box")**—Attach files directly to tasks with a built-in file picker.
- **[Adobe Creative Cloud](https://asana.com/apps/adobe-creative-cloud "Adobe Creative Cloud")**—See new tasks, share designs, and incorporate feedback delivered in Asana—all without leaving your Creative Cloud tools.

### Communication

- **[Slack](https://asana.com/apps/slack "Slack")**—Move work forward faster by creating, completing, and editing tasks directly from Slack. When there are updates to tasks and projects in Asana, updates will automatically post to a Slack channel.
- **[Asana for Gmail Add-on](https://asana.com/apps/google-gmail "Asana for Gmail Add-on")** or **[Asana for Outlook](https://asana.com/apps/outlook "Asana for Outlook")**—Turn emails with clients, customers, and teammates into actionable tasks that are tracked in Asana—without leaving your inbox.
- **Gmail “Send Email” rules**: Add email recipients and details based on Asana rule variables when sending an email.
- **[Zoom](https://asana.com/apps/zoom "Zoom")**—Create or link to Zoom meetings right from an Asana task. When the meeting's over, you'll also have the transcript and recording attached in the task.

### Other top integrations

- **[Google](https://asana.com/guide/help/api/google "Google")**—[Connect your Google Drive](https://asana.com/guide/help/api/google#gl-drive "Connect your Google Drive") to Asana, log in with [Google SSO](https://help.asana.com/s/article/google-and-asana?language=en_US) or SAML, add tasks to Asana from any web page in [Chrome](https://asana.com/apps/chrome), and more.
- **[Microsoft](https://asana.com/apps/microsoft "Microsoft")**—Asana integrates with a suite of Microsoft tools like [Teams](https://asana.com/apps/microsoft "Teams"), [Office 365 and Outlook](https://asana.com/apps/microsoft-office365-connector "Office 365 and Outlook"), [OneDrive](https://asana.com/apps/microsoft-one-drive "OneDrive"), and [Power BI](https://asana.com/apps/powerbi "Power BI") to ensure that your team can coordinate work effectively across your most essential tools.
- **[Salesforce](https://asana.com/apps/salesforce "Salesforce")**—Drive better collaboration throughout your sales cycles by tracking follow-up work, automatically creating projects when customers reach key opportunity stages, and more.
- **[JIRA Cloud](https://asana.com/apps/jiracloud "JIRA Cloud")**—Create a smooth two-way sync between Asana and JIRA to prevent duplicate work while keeping information where teams want to see it.
- **[Harvest](https://asana.com/apps/harvest "Harvest")**—A built-in timer so you can track time on any task without leaving Asana.
