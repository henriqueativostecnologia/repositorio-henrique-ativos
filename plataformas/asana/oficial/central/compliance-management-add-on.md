# Compliance management add-on

**Available as add-on to organization-wide Enterprise subscription.**

The compliance management add-on provides features designed to help organizations meet security, audit, and regulatory requirements. It enables access to activity logs, integrations with tools like SIEM, DLP, and eDiscovery platforms, as well as domain access controls such as CASB and IP Allowlisting. This add-on is intended for teams with formal compliance and audit needs or those operating in regulated environments.

## What is the compliance management add-on, and how does it work?

The add-on provides organization admins the ability to monitor all data in their organization and restrict traffic to approved networks. 

## Who is eligible for purchasing this add-on? 

Customers with organization-wide Asana Enterprise subscription are required to purchase access to the permissions management add-on. If you are on an existing workspace or divisions, you must upgrade to an organization-wide Asana Enterprise subscription. 

Customers on Enterprise+ subscriptions already have access to the features in this add-on.

## How do you access the add-on? 

If you meet the eligibility criteria and have purchased the add-on, your organization [super admin](https://help.asana.com/s/article/admin-and-super-admin-roles-in-asana?language=en_US#gl-super-admin-vs-admin) can access these via the [admin console](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-access-the-admin-console?language=en_US)**.** For integration documentation, please reference our [developer documentation](https://developers.asana.com/reference/rest-api-reference). 

## How does this add-on integrate with other tools or third-party apps?

Customers purchasing this add-on can leverage new API endpoints to support their compliance needs. For integration documentation, please reference our [developer documentation](https://developers.asana.com/reference/rest-api-reference). 

## How is the add-on priced? 

The add-on is charged on a per-user, per-month basis, based on the number of members with an Enterprise subscription.
