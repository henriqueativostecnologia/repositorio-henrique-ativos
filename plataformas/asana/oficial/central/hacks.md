#### To access **Hack settings**:

1.  From the top bar, click on your profile photo
2.  Select **Settings** from the drop-down menu
3.  Select **Hacks**

Go to the **Hacks** tab of **Settings** to enable or disable any Hack. Click on the **Reload to Apply Changes** button to save your changes.

You can send us feedback by clicking on the **Send feedback** links found in the **Hacks** tab.
