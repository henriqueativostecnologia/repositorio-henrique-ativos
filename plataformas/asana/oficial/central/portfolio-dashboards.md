# Portfolio dashboards

**Available on Asana** [ **Advanced**](https://asana.com/plan/advanced)**,** [ **Enterprise**](https://asana.com/enterprise)**, and** [ **Enterprise+**](https://asana.com/enterprise) **tiers, as well as** [ **legacy tiers**](https://asana.com/pricing-legacy) **Business, and Legacy Enterprise.**

Portfolio dashboards provide a comprehensive view of your portfolio's performance, allowing you to track progress, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions across multiple projects. This powerful feature enables you to visualize key metrics and insights through various chart types, helping you to effectively manage and optimize your portfolio's performance.

## Creating a portfolio dashboard

Portfolio dashboards extend the power of Asana's reporting capabilities to the portfolio level. They allow you to create custom visualizations of your portfolio data, helping you to understand the overall health and progress of your initiatives.

To create a portfolio dashboard:

1.  Navigate to your portfolio

2.  Click on the **Dashboard** tab

3.  Click **+ Add chart**

You can choose from various chart types, including:

1.  Bar
2.  Stacked bar
3.  Grouped bar
4.  Line
5.  Burnup
6.  Burndown
7.  Donut
8.  Number
9.  Lollipop

Learn more about the [chart styles](https://help.asana.com/s/article/chart-styles) you can choose from.

With portfolio dashboards, you can choose to report on **Tasks**, **Projects**, or **Portfolios**.

Then, you can choose your **X-axis**, **Y-axis** and depending on the chart **Group by**.

Once you’ve finished customizing your chart, click **Add**.
