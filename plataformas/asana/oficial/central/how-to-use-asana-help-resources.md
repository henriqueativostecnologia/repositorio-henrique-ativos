# How to use Asana help resources

Getting the most out of Asana is easier when you know where to find help. Whether you're just starting out or looking to master advanced features, Asana offers multiple self-serve resources designed to support your journey. This article walks you through the three main help resources available to all users: the Help Center, Asana Academy, and Asana Forum.

## Asana Help Center

The [Asana Help Center](https://asana.com/guide/help "Asana Help Center") is your comprehensive resource for detailed, step-by-step instructions on how to use every feature in Asana. Whether you need guidance on basic features or advanced integrations, the Help Center is your go-to knowledge base and is a great first resource for any burning questions or quick fixes.

### Key features:

- **Feature guides**: Clear instructions for using specific Asana features
- **Screenshots and GIFs**: Visual guides that show you exactly what to do
- **Troubleshooting guides**: Solutions for common issues and questions
- **Getting started content**: Perfect for new users learning the basics
- **Advanced workflows**: In-depth guides for power users

### When to use the Help Center:

- You need quick answers about how a specific feature works
- You're troubleshooting an issue
- You want to learn about new features
- You need reference material while working

## Asana Academy

[Asana Academy](https://academy.asana.com/) is your hub for structured learning experiences that go beyond basic how-to instructions. It offers both self-paced courses and live training sessions to help you build work management expertise.

### Self-paced courses include:

- **Beginner courses**: Getting started with Asana fundamentals
- **Workflow building**: Creating effective project management systems
- **Advanced features**: Mastering automation, reporting, and integrations
- **Role-specific training**: Tailored content for managers, team leads, and individual contributors
- **Certification programs**: Earn credentials to validate your Asana expertise

> [Explore certifications and skill badges](https://academy.asana.com/page/asana-certification).

### Live training sessions:

- **Interactive workshops**: Real-time learning with Asana experts
- **Q&A opportunities**: Get your specific questions answered
- **Hands-on practice**: Apply what you learn in guided exercises
- **Weekly schedule**: Regular sessions covering different topics

> [Browse live trainings](https://academy.asana.com/page/upcoming-live-trainings).

### When to use Asana Academy:

- You want structured, comprehensive learning
- You prefer video-based instruction
- You're implementing Asana across your team or organization
- You want to earn certifications
- You learn better in live, interactive sessions

## Asana Forum

The [Asana Forum](https://forum.asana.com/ "Asana Community Forum") is where users, experts, and Asana team members come together to share knowledge, discuss best practices, and help each other succeed. Engage with a vibrant community of Asana users where you can ask questions, share ideas and issues, as well as learn from the experience of others. It's an ideal platform to connect with like-minded individuals, find inspiration, and stay updated with the latest product news.

Here, Asana Ambassadors answer your product questions and give tips and tricks on how they use Asana in their day-to-day. The Asana Forum is also a great place to offer product feedback and report or check on bugs or feature requests.

### Key features:

- **Real user experiences**: Learn from people actually using Asana in their work
- **Best practice sharing**: Discover creative ways to use Asana features
- **Product updates**: Stay informed about new features and improvements
- **Peer support**: Get help from experienced community members
- **Asana Ambassadors**: Connect with certified experts who provide guidance
- **Feature requests**: Share ideas for product improvements

### Popular forum sections:

- **Get started**: Perfect for new users with basic questions
- **Use cases**: Real-world use cases and workflow examples
- **Product feedback**: Suggest and vote on new features
- **Tips and tricks**: Community-shared productivity hacks

Remember, learning Asana is a journey, not a destination. These resources are designed to support you at every stage, from your first project to advanced workflow automation. Don't hesitate to use multiple resources together - they're all designed to complement each other and help you succeed with Asana.
