# AI add-on beta services

At Asana, we are committed to delivering high-quality, cutting-edge AI capabilities. We offer a range of AI Add-On features, including [AI Studio](https://asana.com/product/ai/ai-studio) and [AI Teammates](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-teammates?language=en_US).

Customers will be notified of opportunities to participate in AI Add-On Beta programs. Customers may elect to participate, as described in Asana’s [Product Specific Terms](https://asana.com/terms/asana-product-specific-terms). Participation in AI Add-On Beta Services is voluntary and requires the customer’s explicit consent.

In connection with these Beta Services, Asana may review and analyze customer data, including input and output data, associated with interactions with the AI Add-On Beta Services to evaluate, support, and improve Asana’s products and services. Asana does not use customer data for any other purposes, including to train AI models. 

Customers may opt out of Asana’s AI Add-On Beta Services program by contacting their account executive or submitting a request via our [Help Center](https://help.asana.com/s/get-support).
