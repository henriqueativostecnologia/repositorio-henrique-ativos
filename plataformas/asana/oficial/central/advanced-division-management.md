# Advanced division management

Licenses are assigned directly to the user, meaning they’ll have access to paid features anywhere they go in Asana. Users can be added and removed from your division at any time via the admin console. This article covers division FAQs relating to privacy and permissions, reporting and multiple division plans within one Asana organization.

## Permissions

### How do divisions affect team and project visibility and permissions?

Divisions do not impact team and project visibility directly. It might be useful to think of a division as a container which holds a number of teams. The division holds a paid subscription. That subscription then provides those teams with access to paid features. For example; a [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter) division’s subscription provides Starter features to the teams included in that division.

The individual [project](https://help.asana.com/s/article/project-permissions?language=en_US) and [team](https://help.asana.com/s/article/team-permissions?language=en_US) permissions will remain unchanged. The division has no impact on visibility or permissions itself.

## Reporting

### How do divisions impact reporting and analytics in Asana?

Divisions have no impact on [reporting](https://help.asana.com/s/article/universal-reporting?language=en_US). You cannot report on a division itself but can report on teams that are part of the division.

## Subscriptions

### Can I have multiple divisions with different subscription tiers?

Yes, you can have multiple divisions within your organization. Users often create divisions with different tiers in order to provide specific departments or teams with access to the features they need. For example; you can create an [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced) division for the Revenue department including the Sales and Marketing teams. You can then create a separate [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter) division for the Operations department including the IT and Support teams. Each division would have separate subscriptions and invoices and can have different billing owners.

Just because you've upgraded multiple teams to a certain tier by using a division, doesn't mean that you can't upgrade a separate single team to any tier you like. If one team is part of a different cost center for example, it may be appropriate in certain cases to upgrade that team with its own separate subscription rather than adding it to an existing division.

### Can a division exist inside an organization that already has an organization-wide paid subscription?

Yes. Enterprise and Enterprise+ organizations with an organization-wide paid subscription can create divisions to organize users into separate groups, delegate management to division admins, and control license assignment. For details on setting up and managing divisions within a paid organization, see [Divisions in paid organizations](https://help.asana.com/s/article/divisions-in-paid-orgs?language=en_US#what-are-divisions-in-paid-organizations).
