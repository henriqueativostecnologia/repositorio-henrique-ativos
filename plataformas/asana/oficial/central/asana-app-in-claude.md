# Asana app in Claude

**Available on Asana [Personal](https://asana.com/plan/personal), [Starter](https://asana.com/plan/starter), [Advanced](https://asana.com/plan/advanced), [Enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise), and [Enterprise+](https://asana.com/enterprise) plans.**

> If you are using Claude with a team account, the Claude app requires organization-wide app approval in Asana. Approving apps at the organization level requires App Management capabilities. App Management is not included in Starter. If your plan does not include App Management, your admin cannot approve the Claude app for the organization. This can limit use of the app in team and organization contexts on Starter.

The Asana app in Claude brings your work graph directly into Claude conversations, allowing you to turn AI-powered discussions into actionable work without leaving Claude.

## What is the Asana app in Claude?

The Asana app in Claude connects your Asana work with Claude's AI capabilities. From a single Claude conversation, you can check on portfolios, create and assign tasks, kick off new projects, and review work—all without switching between tools.

## Setting up the Asana app in Claude

#### To connect Asana to Claude:

1.  Open Claude and click the **+ icon**
2.  Scroll down to connectors and click **Add connectors**
3.  Search for **Asana** and click **Connect**
4.  Authenticate with your Asana account credentials
5.  Grant the necessary permissions for Claude to access your Asana data
6.  Go back to connectors and turn on the toggle for Asana

Once connected, you can start creating tasks and projects directly from your Claude conversations.

## Using the Asana app in Claude

**Create projects and tasks**

Turn ideas from Claude conversations into actionable work in Asana. Create new projects or add tasks to existing projects directly from Claude.

**Check portfolio status**

View the status of your portfolios and get real-time updates on project progress without leaving your Claude conversation.

**Assign and review work**

Assign tasks to team members and review task details, all within Claude.

### Example prompts

Take inspiration from the following prompts to get you started:

**Work status and updates**

- "What campaigns are at risk of missing their launch dates?"
- "Claude, what's the status of the Q1 Product Launch project in Asana?"
- "Claude, give me a summary of all projects waiting on executive approval"

**Project planning**

- "Create a hiring project for our new Sales Manager role with interview stages and onboarding tasks."
- "Use Asana to create a Q1 OKR planning project with goal-setting, cross-functional alignment, and progress tracking tasks"
- "Build me a content calendar project in Asana for Q2 with monthly themes, weekly blog posts, and social promotion tasks"

**Assign and delegate work**

- "Assign all copywriting tasks to the Content team based on who usually handles this work in Asana"
- "Claude, assign the 'Executive Approval' task in the Onboarding Revamp project to Sarah”
- "Distribute these compliance review tasks across the Legal and Finance teams based on their team responsibilities"
