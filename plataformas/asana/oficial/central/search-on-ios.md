# Search on iOS

With Asana for iOS, you can organize your tasks for the day and communicate with your team from anywhere. This article will focus on using the search function on iOS. Download the [Asana app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/asana-work-in-one-place/id489969512) for your iPhone and iPad from the App Store. Asana's mobile app is only compatible with iOS 16 or later.

## Using search on iOS

Use search to find tasks, projects, messages, teams, portfolios, goals, tags, or people in your workspace or organization.

#### To search:

1.  Tap the **Search bar** and type a search term
2.  Use filters to specify what you're searching for
3.  You can see your recent searches
4.  Use **Quick filters** to filter tasks
5.  Use **Shortcuts** to quicken your search
