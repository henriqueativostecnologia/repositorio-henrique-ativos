# About credits and quotas with Gemini in AppSheet Solutions

When you use [Gemini in AppSheet Solutions](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16106353#solutions), you consume *credits* based on the number and complexity of the AI tasks that are run by users of the apps owned by creators in your organization. To learn more about how to get and monitor credits for your team or organization, see [Manage credits for Gemini usage](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16274771). 

In addition, in order to ensure fairness and reduce spikes in resource use and availability, AppSheet limits how often an app can call Gemini. To that effect, your usage of AI in automations is restricted by a per-app per-min quota. You can monitor any quota violations for Gemini in AppSheet Solutions using the following:

- [Audit History](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104794)
- [Automation monitor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11447502)

If you require assistance with quotas, contact [AppSheet Support](https://www.appsheet.com/Support/Contact).
