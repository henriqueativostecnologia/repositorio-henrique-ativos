# Manage team membership

** **

**Note**: Teams are supported for Enterprise Plus plans only.

Use the **Members** page to view current team member details and usage, and manage team membership.

To access the Members page:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](http://www.appsheet.com).
2.  Select **My team** in the account profile drop-down.
3.  Click the **Members** tab.

Members page

As shown in the figure above, the Members page allows you to:

1.  View total counts for the following:
    - App creators: all users that have created an app
    - Other accounts: all users that have opened an AppSheet app

2.  View the sum total of app creators and app users (that is, other members) by month that have created an app or opened an app created by an existing member. 

    The members sum indicated in the chart represents a running total of users and does not represent “active users” in the given month/year.

3.  Click **Download membership data** to download membership data for all team apps as a CSV file. See [Monitor team membership](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11918345#team-membership).

4.  Under **App Creators**, expand a panel to view more information about an app creator. 

5.  In the expanded panel, you can:
    - View account creation date, number of apps, primary authentication source, last active date, subscription plan, and number of licenses. For AppSheet Core subscription plans, you can also view whether the [AppSheet Core security setting](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10447197?hl=en#core-security) is enabled.
    - Click **Details** to view more details for a team member. See [View team member details](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13882264).
    - Click **Transfer app ownership** or **Transfer AppSheet database ownership** to transfer app or database ownership to another team member. See [Transfer apps and databases between team members](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12007391).
    - Click **Delete** to [delete a team member account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13965216). Alternatively, you can [remove team members](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13964714) from the Google Group.

6.  Under **Other Accounts**, view the email addresses of all app users that have used an app created by an app creator on the team.
