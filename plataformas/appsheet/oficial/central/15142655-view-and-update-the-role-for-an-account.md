# View and update the role for an account

As a team or organization administrator, you can update the role for an AppSheet account. For a list of supported roles and permissions, see [Supported roles for an organization or team](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13691133#supported-roles). 

To view and update the role for an account, do the following:

1.  [Open the Accounts page](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14951131#explore-users-page) in the AppSheet Admin Console.
2.  In the **Role** column, view the current role for the account.
3.  To update the role, select the new role from the drop-down list.
