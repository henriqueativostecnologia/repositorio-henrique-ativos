# View or restore a version of an app owned by a team member

AppSheet maintains a history of changes made to an app. This is useful if you need to view or restore an earlier version of an app. See also [View or restore a version of your app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105387) using the app editor.

To view or restore a version of an app owned by a team member, do one of the following:

- [View the team member details](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13882264), and under **Apps **click **Version History** in the Info column associated with the app for which you want to view or restore a version.
- (AppSheet admins only)  Use the AppSheet Admin Console to [view details for an app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15098215) in your team or organization and select **More**  **\> Version history**.

The Edit History Enterprise dashboard opens showing the app change history. 

You can do one of the following tasks:

- Click **View** to view that version of the app in the app editor
- Click **Restore** to restore that version of the app.
- Click Delta to display more details about the specific changes made in that version of the app in JSON format
