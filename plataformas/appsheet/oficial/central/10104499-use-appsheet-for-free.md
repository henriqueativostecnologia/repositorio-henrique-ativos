# Use AppSheet for free

AppSheet is **free** for [prototype development and testing](#prototype), and for [personal use](#personal). 

By default, every user account is free until you purchase a subscription. You are encouraged to try all the features of the platform. Most app features and behavior can be tested for free until you are ready to deploy and share your app. 

See also [Get help with your free account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16276504).

A subset of features, such as [sending emails](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11447614) or [triggering bots with schedule events](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11432969#schedule) using AppSheet automation, are not fully supported until you [purchase a subscription](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15011908). That is, you can configure these features, but they won't execute as expected.
