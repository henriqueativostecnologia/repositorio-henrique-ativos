# Get started with AppSheet for admins

Get started with AppSheet administration by setting up your teams and empowering your app creators.

Organizations are only supported for customers with a **Google Workspace** administrator and users with **AppSheet Enterprise Plus** accounts. Teams are only supported with **AppSheet Enterprise Plus** accounts. See [AppSheet pricing](https://about.appsheet.com/pricing/).
