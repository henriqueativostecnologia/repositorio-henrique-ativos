# Error: OutgoingEmailAllowListViolations

If you receive an `OutgoingEmailAllowListViolations` error in Audit History, then either your team admin has [restricted the recipient list for outgoing mail](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11918568#outgoing-email-allowlist) or sending email to external recipients is not allowed by policy, as described in [Control access to AppSheet](https://support.google.com/a/answer/16018438). 

To address this issue, contact your team administrator to adjust the restricted recipient list or contact your Workspace administrator for assistance with turning off the AppSheet Core Security setting.
