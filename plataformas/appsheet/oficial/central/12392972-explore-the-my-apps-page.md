# Explore the My Apps page

To access the My Apps page:

- Sign in to [*AppSheet*](https://appsheet.com/)**. My Apps** is displayed, by default.
- Click **My Apps** in the top navigation. 
- Select **Account \> My Apps** in the app editor.

The My Apps page enables you to create new and access existing AppSheet [apps](#apps) and [databases](#databases), including those you own and those that are shared with you. 

## My Apps: Apps

Click **Apps** to manage your AppSheet apps.

My Apps page with callouts for various actions

As highlighted in the previous figure, using the My Apps page for apps you can:

- Create apps. See [Create apps: The Essentials](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11980957).
- Filter apps by most recently accessed, shared with you, and owned by you
  **Note**: Apps must be shared with **View/copy app** or **Edit app** access in order for them to display in the Shared with me list. See [Manage app access for individual users](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104983#manage-roles-app-versions).
- View the sample templates that you can explore or copy to create a new app
- View details for the apps that are displayed, such as name, status, owner, and time the app was last modified
- Identify apps with errors that need to be addressed
- Edit (in new tab), preview, copy, or delete an app by using the **More** More  menu associated with its row
  **Note**: Only app owners can delete an app.
- Search by app name, category, or owner email
- Sort apps by column
  **Note**: If you select Recent, apps are sorted by Last modified date only; you cannot change this sort order.
- Toggle between the list and grid layouts

## My Apps: Databases

Click **Databases** to manage your AppSheet databases.

My Apps page displaying databases

As highlighted in the previous figure, using the My Apps page you can:

- Create databases. See [Create and copy databases](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12635312).
- Filter database by most recently accessed, shared with you, and owned by you
- View details for the databases that are displayed, such as name, number of tables, owner, and time the database was last modified
- Edit (in new tab) or delete a database by using the **More** More  menu associated with its row
  **Note**: Only [database owners](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12637319#database-permissions) can delete an app.
- Search by database name or owner email
- Sort databases by column
  **Note**: If you select Recent, apps are sorted by Last modified date only; you cannot change this sort order.
- Toggle between the list and grid layouts

See also: [AppSheet databases](https://support.google.com/appsheet/topic/12632222)
