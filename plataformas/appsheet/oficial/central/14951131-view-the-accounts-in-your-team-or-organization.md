# View the accounts in your team or organization

View the AppSheet accounts in your team or organization (also referred to as *internal* *users*) using the Accounts page in the AppSheet Admin console.

- [Access the Accounts page](#access-accounts-page)
- [View an account summary](#view-number-accounts-owners)
- [View a list of all accounts](#view-all-accounts)
- [Download details for all accounts](#download-csv)
- [Create a team](#create-team)

Data refreshes every **24 hours**.

## Access the Accounts page

To access the Accounts page, do the following:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://appsheet.com).
2.  On the My Apps page, select **Admin Console** from your account profile drop-down.

    Select Admin Console in account profile

    Alternatively, from an Admin page, such as My account or My team, you can select **Admin \> Admin Console **in the top navigation.
3.  Select **Accounts** in the left navigation.

The Accounts page opens.

Accounts page in the AppSheet Admin Console

The Accounts page in the AppSheet Admin console allows you to do the following:

- [View an account summary](#view-number-accounts-owners)
- [View a list of all accounts](#view-all-accounts)
- [Download details for all accounts](#download-csv)
- [Create a team](#create-team)
- Click a user name in the list to [view account details](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15100835)
- [View and update the role for an account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15142655)

## View an account summary

At the top of the Accounts page, view the following summary information for the **AppSheet accounts** in your team or organization:

- Total number of AppSheet accounts in your team or organization
- Number of the accounts that have been active in the last 30 days
- Number of the accounts that own apps (not including co-authors)

## Download details for all accounts

Download details for all accounts as a CSV file by selecting **More**  **\> Export CSV** in the upper right corner of the main body of the page.

##  Create a team

Create a team by selecting **More**  **\> Create a team** in the upper right corner of the main body of the page and following the steps in [Define team membership using a Google Group](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13964712#google-group).
