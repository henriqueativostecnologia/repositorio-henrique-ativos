# GDPR and Subprocessors

Last updated: September 2024

## GDPR and AppSheet

We are committed to working with customers, partners, and other stakeholders to meet the requirements for the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) that went into effect on May 25, 2018. See:

- [Google Cloud and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)](https://cloud.google.com/privacy/gdpr)
- [AppSheet Security and Compliance FAQ](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/about.appsheet.com/en//static/pdf/appsheet-security-and-compliance-faq.pdf)
- [Data Processing and Security Terms for AppSheet Services](https://www.appsheet.com/Home/Dpst)
