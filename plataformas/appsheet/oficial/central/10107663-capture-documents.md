# Capture documents

To capture PDF documents, insert a column in your spreadsheet and include the word **File** in the [column header](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10099523). AppSheet will automatically detect this new column as a `File` type after you [regenerate the column structure](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10106675). Alternatively, you can name the column anything you like and change the **Column Type** to **File** in the **Data \> Columns** pane of the app editor.
