## Explore your app

After you [create your app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11980957), it is opened in the AppSheet app editor and you are greeted by the welcome screen. Try out your **live app** in the welcome screen, then click **Customize with AppSheet** to proceed to the AppSheet app editor, as shown below.

 

 

Explore the app editor

As shown in the previous figure, the app editor includes three main areas that enable you to:

- [Navigate your app](#navigation-bar) to select the section you want to customize.
- Based on the section selected in the navigation bar, access the learning center or [customize your app](#customize) to change the styles, control how your data is presented or navigated, configure security requirements, and more.
- Preview your **live** app to interact with and make changes. See [Explore the app preview](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13684531). 
- Quickly track down warnings or errors in your app. For example, click  Warning icon  in the tools panel to display the Errors & Warnings dialog. Click **Go to problem**, to navigate to the issue.

### Customize your app

Customize your app in the middle pane based on the feature selected in the left navigation. For example, you can customize the data in the tables on which your app is based. 

Virtual columns displayed at the bottom with a different color edit icon

Each page you see in your app is known as a *view*. You may notice that AppSheet suggests views based on the data you connected it to. You can change or customize the views in your app, as described in [Views: The Essentials](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10106688). Examples of different views are shown in the following figure.

Whenever you make edits to your app, the **save** **button** in the top right corner turns blue. Make sure you click on it so your edits are saved. Saved changes are immediately available to use in your app.

## Explore your data 

In the app editor, go to **Data   **to view and edit your data structure ([tables](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13829970) and [columns](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10106674)). 

**Tables** are comprised of **columns**, which are the attributes that describe each row, such as **Item ID**, **Name**, **Description**, and so on. **Rows** contain the data for each individual record in your data source. The rows in each table share the same set of columns.

 In the app editor, use the table and columns settings to control how your data is displayed in the app, the data types, the initial values, and more. Click **View data source** to open the data source and see your data. 

Your data is stored separately from your app

Your AppSheet app *connects* to your data source, it doesn't import your data. Your data resides separately from your app. As users interact with your app, all changes are stored directly in the data source.

Remember that when you use the [app preview](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13684531) in the app editor, you are interacting with the **live app**. Any data changes you make while interacting with the app preview are saved to your data source. 

To learn more about preparing your data, see [Data: The Essentials](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10106580).
