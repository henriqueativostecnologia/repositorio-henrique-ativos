# Create apps: The Essentials

Create an app that connects to and enables you to interact with and manage your data using a user-friendly UI from your mobile or tablet device, or desktop browser. 

Create an app using one of the following methods:

- [Create an app from the My Apps page](#create) (described below) by:
  - Starting with your data, a blank app, or a [sample template](https://www.appsheet.com/Templates)

  - Using [Gemini for App Creation](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14699210) by simply describing your idea 
- [Start with a sample app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13036033)
- Use a Google Workspace add-on, such as [Google Sheets AppSheet add-on](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/appsheet/376242260902) or  [Google Forms AppSheet add-on](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/appsheet/865237847665).

## Create an app using the My Apps page

To create an app using the My Apps page:

1.  Access **My Apps**:

    - Sign in to [*AppSheet*](https://appsheet.com/)**. My Apps** is displayed, by default.
    - Click **My Apps** in the top navigation. 
    - Select **My Apps** from the account profile drop-down.

      Select My Apps from the account profile drop-down

    For more information about the My Apps page, see [Explore the My Apps page](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12392972).

2.  Click **+ Create** to start with your own data, start by using a sample[ app template](https://www.appsheet.com/templates), start by creating a blank app, or start by using [Gemini](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14699210).

    The app is opened in the app editor. Try out your **live app** in the welcome modal, then click **Customize with AppSheet** to proceed to the AppSheet app editor.

    If you signed in with a Google account, your Workspace organization administrator must enable Google Drive for your domain for you to successfully create an app.

**App development and testing is always free on AppSheet.**

When you are developing and testing your app, feel free to use all of the features of AppSheet. You will not be restricted from using features that are covered under paid pricing plans. While you are developing and testing your app, you do not need to run a deployment check or enroll in a pricing plan. Our goal is to enable you to create an app that meets your needs and is reliable.

Once you finish development and testing, you should run a [deployment check](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104389) and [subscribe to a paid plan](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104499#subscribe-to-plan).
