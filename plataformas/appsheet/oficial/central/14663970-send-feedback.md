# Send feedback

Send feedback to AppSheet

- [Send feedback about AppSheet](#product)
- [Send feedback about the AppSheet Admin Console](#console) (AppSheet admins only)
- [Send feedback about the AppSheet Help Center](#help-center)

You can also find answers, ask questions, suggest features, and connect with other app creators on the [AppSheet Community](https://www.googlecloudcommunity.com/gc/AppSheet/ct-p/appsheet).

## Send feedback about AppSheet

Your feedback can help us improve your experience. To help us understand your feedback, include details and a screenshot. The more information you include in your feedback, the more helpful it is for us. 

To send feedback about AppSheet:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://appsheet.com).
2.  At the top right, click Help  Help & Feedback   and then ** Send Feedback.**
3.  Describe your issue or suggestion.
4.  To add a screenshot, click **Capture screenshot**. 
    - To highlight or hide parts of the screenshot, click **Highlight or Hide Info**.
    - If you don't want to include the screenshot, click Remove screenshot .
5.  Click **Send**.

## Send feedback about the AppSheet Admin Console

You must have AppSheet admin privileges to access the AppSheet Admin Console. See [Assign AppSheet admin privileges to Google Workspace admins](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14368331).

Your feedback can help us improve your experience. To help us understand your feedback, include details and a screenshot. The more information you include in your feedback, the more helpful it is for us. 

To send feedback about the AppSheet Admin Console:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://appsheet.com).
2.  On the My Apps page, select **Admin Console** from your account profile drop-down.

    Select Admin Console in account profile

    The AppSheet Admin Console opens in a new browser tab.
3.  Click **Send feedback** in the left navigation.
4.  Describe your issue or suggestion.
5.  To add a screenshot, click **Capture screenshot**. 
    - To highlight or hide parts of the screenshot, click **Highlight or Hide Info**.
    - If you don't want to include the screenshot, click Remove screenshot .
6.  Click **Send**.

## Send feedback about the AppSheet Help Center

To send feedback about the AppSheet Help Center:

1.  Click **Give feedback about this article** at the bottom of any page in the AppSheet Help Center. 
2.  Scroll to highlight the relevant content and click feedback  Feedback icon .
3.  Click the best description of the issue or Other.
4.  Share additional information about the issue. 
    The more information you include in your feedback, the more helpful it is for us. 
5.  Click **Submit**.
