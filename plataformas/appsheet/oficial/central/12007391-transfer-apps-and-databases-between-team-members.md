# Transfer apps and databases between team members

**Note**: Teams are supported for Enterprise Plus plans only.

As a team root, you can transfer [apps](#transfer-apps-between-team-members) and [databases](#transfer-databases-between-team-members) between team members. For example, you might need to do this when a user leaves an organization before deleting their account. 

See also:

- [Transfer ownership of apps databases for an account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15098213) using the AppSheet Admin Console
-  [Automate the removal of team user accounts](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11579184) using the API

If a Google Workspace account has been deleted, you can't transfer the AppSheet apps and databases associated with that account unless you temporarily restore the account, as described in [Restore a recently deleted user](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1397578). If the user restoration period has been exceeded, then it won't be possible to transfer the apps and databases.

## Transfer apps between team members

**Note**: You must have **team root** permissions to transfer apps between team members.

To transfer apps between team members:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://www.appsheet.com) using your team root account.
2.  Select **My team** in the account profile drop-down.
3.  Click the** Members** tab.
4.  Expand the team member whose apps you want to transfer.
5.  Click **Transfer app ownership**.
    The Transfer apps dialog displays. 
6.  Select the account that the apps will be transferred to in the **Transfer apps to** drop-down.
7.  Click **Transfer**.

## Transfer databases between team members

**Note**: You must have **team root** permissions to transfer apps between team members.

To transfer databases between team members:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://www.appsheet.com) using your team root account.
2.  Select **My team** in the account profile drop-down.
3.  Click the** Members** tab.
4.  Expand the team member whose databases you want to transfer.
5.  **Click Transfer AppSheet database ownership**.
    The Transfer AppSheet databases dialog displays. 
6.  Select the account that the databases will be transferred to in the **Transfer AppSheet databases to** drop-down.
7.  Click **Transfer**.
