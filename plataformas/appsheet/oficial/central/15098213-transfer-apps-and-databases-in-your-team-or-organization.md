# Transfer apps and databases in your team or organization

Transfer apps and databases in your team or organization as described in the following sections:

- [Transfer an individual app to another account](#transfer-app)
- [Transfer all apps and databases for an account](#transfer-all-apps-databases)

See also [Automate the removal of team user accounts](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11579184) using the API.

## Transfer an individual app to another account

As an admin, you can transfer an individual app to another account in your team or organization.

1.  [View the app details](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15098215) in the AppSheet Admin console.
2.  Select More  **\> Transfer app**.
3.  Enter or select the email address of the account to which you want to transfer the app.
4.  Click **Transfer**.
    The app is transferred and the Owner field is updated to reflect the new owner's account.

## Transfer all apps and databases for an account

As a team root, you can transfer apps and databases between AppSheet accounts in your team. For example, you might need to do this when a user leaves an organization before deleting their account. 

To transfer ownership of the apps and databases for an account, do the following:

1.  [Open the Accounts page](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14951131#explore-users-page) in the AppSheet Admin console.
2.  Click the user name of the account you want to transfer in the list.
    The account details page opens.
3.  Click **Transfer Ownership**.
4.  Select the assets to transfer.
5.  Enter the email of the account to which you want to transfer ownership of the selected assets.
6.  Click **Transfer**.
