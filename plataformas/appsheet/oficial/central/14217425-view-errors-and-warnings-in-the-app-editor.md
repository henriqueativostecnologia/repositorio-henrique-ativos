# View errors and warnings in the app editor

To view errors and warnings in the app editor

1.  Sign in to [*AppSheet*](https://appsheet.com/)**.**
2.  Open the app in the app editor.
    If the app has an error or warning, an icon appears in the navigation or tools panel.

    Warning icon in app editor
3.  Click  Warning icon  to open the Errors & Warnings dialog that lists all of the issues in the app.

    Errors and warning dialog
4.  Click **Go to problem**, to navigate to the issue.
