# Turn AppSheet and its features on or off

Turn AppSheet and its features on or off as described in the following sections:

- [Turn AppSheet on or off for users](#appsheet)
- [Turn Gemini for App Creation on or off](#gemini-in-appsheet)

## Turn AppSheet on or off for users

As an administrator of your organization's Google Workspace accounts, you can control who uses AppSheet. Turn the service on or off for users in your Google Admin console, as described in [*Turn AppSheet on or off for users*](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10216943).

To access the Google Admin console, click **Google Admin Console** in the bottom left corner of any page in the AppSheet Admin console.

## Turn Gemini for App Creation on or off

Gemini for App Creation lets you create an app by *describing* your idea using natural language, as described in [Create apps using Gemini for App Creation](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14699210). You can [turn Gemini for App Creation on or off](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11918568#gemini-in-appsheet) when configuring your team.
