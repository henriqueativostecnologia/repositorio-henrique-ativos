# Delete apps

Apps that you don't need anymore can be deleted in the editor. Deleting an app removes it from your account. Once deleted, it won't be accessible by you as the app creator or by your users. Data sources that are connected to your app are not deleted when you delete the app.

**Restore a deleted app: **If you have a paid account (Starter or above), you can restore a deleted app up to 32 days after it was deleted by contacting [AppSheet Support](https://www.appsheet.com/Support/Contact). After 33 days app definitions will be permanently deleted from the AppSheet database and will not be recoverable.

&nbsp;

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://www.appsheet.com).
2.  Perform one of the following steps for the app you want to delete:
    - On the **My Apps** page, locate the app and select **More**  More ** \> Delete**.
    - Open the app in the editor and select **Manage \> Collaborate & Publish \> Delete App**.
      We've made some [improvements to the app editor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12490168).
      You are **opted in** **to the new editor** by default, but you can [switch back to the legacy editor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12490168) at any time.

      ## If you are using the legacy editor

      Open the app in the editor, select **Manage \> Author**, and click **Delete App**.
3.  Confirm the delete operation.
