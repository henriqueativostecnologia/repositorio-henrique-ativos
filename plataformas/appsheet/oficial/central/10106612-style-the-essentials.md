# Style: The Essentials

Configure the theme and brand to control the appearance of your app. Your choices in this part of the editor allow you to choose a color theme, select images that are relevant to you and your users, and have an icon that represents your business. You can select these images from your cloud provider. In addition, you can change theme colors, fonts, and text size. You can also customize your [About page](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10106613) to better describe the app to your users. 

 To configure the theme and brand: 

1.   Open the app in the app editor.
2.  Go to **Settings** Settings icon ** \> Theme & Brand**.
3.  [Configure the settings](#settings).
4.  Save the app.

We've made some [improvements to the app editor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12490168).
You are **opted in** **to the new editor** by default, but you can [switch back to the legacy editor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12490168) at any time.

 

## If you are using the legacy editor

 To configure the theme and brand: 

1.  Open the app in the app editor.
2.  Select **UX \> Brand**.
3.  [Configure the settings](#settings).
4.  Save the app.

 There are several branding elements that you can customize in the editor.
