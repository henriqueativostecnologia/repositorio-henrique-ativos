# Purchase a subscription

If you have a [Google Workspace edition that includes AppSheet Core](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105400#core-editions), you and other users in your organization automatically have access to an AppSheet Core license. 

To purchase an AppSheet subscription:

1.  Choose a subscription.
    See also: [What subscription is right for me?](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105400?hl=en&ref_topic=10101310&sjid=4689496738582227265-NA#choose-subscription)
2.  Determine the number of licenses you need to purchase.
    See [How many licenses do I need to purchase for my subscription?](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105400?hl=en&ref_topic=10101310&sjid=4689496738582227265-NA#how-many-licenses)
3.  Purchase a subscription:
    - If you have a Google Workspace account, see [Add and set up AppSheet for Google Workspace](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10447197).
    - If your account is managed as part of a team or organization, contact your organization or team administrator.
    - If you have an individual account, see [Manage the subscription, licenses, and billing for an individual account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15066103).

When you [perform a deployment check](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104389) on your app, you will be notified if your account doesn't have the correct subscription and requires an upgrade. See [How to determine if a subscription upgrade is required](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105099).

We have structured our plans and pricing to encourage adoption with minimal friction and barriers to use. If none of our plans are appropriate for your scenario, contact your [AppSheet Sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact/?direct=true&pre_product=appsheet) representative to discuss options.
