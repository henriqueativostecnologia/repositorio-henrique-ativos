# Troubleshoot PDF email attachments

You can construct a bot to [send an email](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11447614) with a PDF attachment. To do so, you use a [template](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11539957) that includes variables that are replaced by the actual values in an updated data row.

Occasionally, the PDF produced is not identical in formatting to the original document. Problems include changes in the margins, changes in page break positions, and other such formatting issues.

To better understand the causes for the formatting issues and how to correct them, see [Why is the email attachment formatted incorrectly?](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11449723#why-attachment-formatted-incorrectly)
