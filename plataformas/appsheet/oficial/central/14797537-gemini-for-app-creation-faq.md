# Gemini for App Creation FAQ

Gemini for App Creation is available to all AppSheet paid accounts, including Google Workspace customers with AppSheet Core and AppSheet Enterprise Plus, and individual accounts with AppSheet Publisher Pro, AppSheet Starter, AppSheet Core, and AppSheet Enterprise Plus. 

Find answers to your questions about Gemini for App Creation.

[Expand all](#expand)** ** \|** **[Collapse all](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14797537)

#### What can I do with this feature?

Gemini for App Creation allows app creators to build an app by simply *describing* a business process or idea. AppSheet will return a list of suggested tables and columns, and app creators can choose to have the app created with that data schema, along with some pre-built views and actions.

#### Who is this feature available to?

Gemini for App Creation is included as part of your paid AppSheet license: AppSheet Publisher Pro, AppSheet Starter, AppSheet Core, or AppSheet Enterprise Plus. You may also have access to AppSheet Core through your Workspace license. For details, see [Google Workspace editions that include AppSheet Core](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10100275?sjid=14898439068259244864-NC#core-editions).

#### Can I turn off this feature?

Yes, users with administrator privileges can disable the AI-assisted app creation feature by following the steps described in [Turn Gemini for App Creation on or off](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11918568#gemini-in-appsheet). The setting is applied at a team level so if you have multiple teams, you will need to disable it for each relevant team.

#### What if I only want to make it available to some users?

Users with administrator privileges can disable the AI-assisted app creation feature by following the steps described in [Turn Gemini for App Creation on or off](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11918568#gemini-in-appsheet). The setting is applied at a team level so if you have multiple teams, you will need to disable it for each relevant team.

#### How will this setting impact Gemini in Workspace features?

[Turning Gemini for App Creation on or off](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11918568#gemini-in-appsheet) only affects the use of Gemini for app creators. It will not affect any Gemini features in Workspace.

#### Do I need Gemini Enterprise or Gemini Workspace to use this feature?

No, the AI-assisted app creation with Gemini for App Creation is available as part of paid AppSheet licenses, including those with AppSheet Core entitlements through their Workspace licenses. For more details about these entitlements, see [Google Workspace editions that include AppSheet Core](https://support.google.com/a/answer/10100275?sjid=14898439068259244864-NC#core-editions).
