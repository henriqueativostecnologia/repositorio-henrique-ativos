# Overview of AppSheet administration

The AppSheet Admin Console provides robust admin controls to view and manage accounts, app usage, apps, licenses, and governance policies in your team or organization. 

You must have AppSheet admin privileges to access the AppSheet Admin Console. See [Assign AppSheet admin privileges to Google Workspace admins](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14368331).

To access the AppSheet Admin Console, do the following:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://appsheet.com).

2.  On the My Apps page, select **Admin Console** from your account profile drop-down.

    Select Admin Console in account profile

Alternatively, from an Admin page, such as My account or My team, you can select **Admin \> Admin Console **in the top navigation.

The AppSheet Admin Console opens. 

Overview page in the AppSheet Admin Console

The **Overview** page, displayed by default, provides a high-level dashboard for your team or organization. Get information about the following data (refreshed every 24 hours): 

- Number of users, both internal and external to your team or organization, that have accessed apps
- Number of AppSheet accounts in your team or organization
- Number of AppSheet accounts that own apps in your team or organization (not including co-authors) and which own the most apps (as a chart)
- Total number of teams (displayed for organization admins only)
- Number of apps owned and which are the most popular (as a chart)
- Total number of [policies](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11948767) that have been defined by users in your team or organization
- [Subscription and license information](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14960110) (as a chart) for your team or organization, including the number of licenses purchased compared to the number used for each subscription (for more information, see [How to choose a subscription](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105400))
  **Note**: If your organization has exceeded the number of licenses purchased or has users who have accessed apps without a sufficient license, you may see warnings. For more information, see [About license warnings](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16003327).
- [Gemini usage](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16058407) in your team or organization
  - Number of credits granted to and consumed by your organization for the current month
  - Pie chart showing number of credits available versus consumed as a percentage
- Recommendations for previewing new features. This section will show more types of recommendations in the future.

From the left navigation, go to one of the following pages to view more details and manage the component in your team or organization.
