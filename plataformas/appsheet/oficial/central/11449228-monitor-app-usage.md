# Monitor app usage

To monitor app usage, do one of the following:

- Open your app in the editor, go to the **Manage \> Monitor** tab, expand **Usage Statistics**, and click **Get usage statistics** to refresh the content.
- As an AppSheet admin, use the AppSheet Admin Console to [view details for an app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15098215) in your team or organization and select **More**  **\> Usage stats**.

The Usage Statistics page opens and shows the following information:

- Total unique app users per month
- Total unique app users per day
- Unique app users emails
- Individual app users per day (Core [pricing plans](https://www.appsheet.com/Pricing) or higher)
- Per-user drilldown: app interactions per day  (Core [pricing plans](https://www.appsheet.com/Pricing) or higher)

**Note:** Use of the [app preview](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/13684531) in the editor by an app owner or co-creator doesn't count towards the total number of app users.

As an AppSheet admin, you can view app usage for all apps in a team and organization using the AppSheet Admin Console. See [View the app usage in your team or organization](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15113267).
