# Manage your apps: The Essentials

On the **Manage** page of the app editor, there's a lot you can do to customize your editing process, [who can contribute to your app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104989), and [your app's deployment](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104495). When managing your app, it can also be useful to learn [information for IT departments](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104707) so you can communicate with other people in your company about your app.

The tabs available on the Manage page are summarized in the following table.

|                                                                               | have the option to:                                                           |

|                                                                               |     either [Android](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104994) or  |
|                                                                               |     [iOS](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10105764)                |

|                                                                               | -   [Broadcast notifications to your                                          |
