# View the apps in your team or organization

View the apps in your team or organization using the Apps page in the AppSheet Admin console.

- [Access the Apps page](#explore-users-page)
- [View an app summary](#view-app-summary)
- [View all apps in your team or organization](#view-all-apps)
- [Download details for all apps](#download-csv)

Data refreshes every **24 hours**.

## Access the Apps page

To access the Apps page, do the following:

1.   Sign in to [AppSheet](https://appsheet.com).

2.  On the My Apps page, select **Admin Console** from your account profile drop-down.

    Select Admin Console in account profile

    Alternatively, from an Admin page, such as My account or My team, you can select **Admin \> Admin Console **in the top navigation.

3.  Select **Apps** in the left navigation.

The Apps page opens.

Apps page in the Admin Console

The Apps page in the AppSheet Admin console allows you to do the following:

- [View an app summary](#view-app-summary)
- [View all apps in your team or organization](#view-all-usres)
- [Download details for all apps](#download-csv)
- Click the name of an app in the list to [view details for the app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14961200#view-app-details)
- Click the user name for an account in your team or organization (internal user) to [view details for the app owner account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15100835)

## View an app summary

At the top of the Apps page, view the following information:

- Number of apps owned by your team or organization
- Number of apps owned by your team or organization that were accessed in the last 30 days

##  Download details for all apps

Download details for all apps as a CSV file by selecting **More**  **\> Export CSV** in the upper right corner of the main body of the page.
