# Delete team member accounts

**Important:**

Deleting a user account is permanent and non-recoverable. If you do not want to delete the apps or databases owned by this account, [transfer them](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12007391) before deleting the account. 

**Note**: You must have **team** **root** permissions to delete a team member.

To delete a team member:  

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](http://www.appsheet.com).
2.  Select **My team** in the account profile drop-down.
3.  Click the **Members** tab.
4.  Expand the user section.
5.  Click **Delete.**
6.  At the prompt, confirm the deletion.

To automate the removal of team members using the API, see [Automate the removal of team user accounts](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11579184).
