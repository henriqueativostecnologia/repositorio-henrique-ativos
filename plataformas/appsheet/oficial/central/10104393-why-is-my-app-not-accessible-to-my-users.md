# Why is my app not accessible to my users?

You app may not be accessible to your users for the following reasons:

## ****

## ** Reason 1:** Your app may be locked because you have more active users than purchased licenses. This video guides you how to check your active user count and purchase the necessary licenses to resolve the issue.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FnQE7bK-0N4

## ** Reason 2:** Your apps may be blocked if you have more than 10 users in any of your prototype apps

 

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L-k27EZtQQw

 

## ** Reason 3:** Your apps may be blocked if you are using any premium features that are not on your current plan.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nTUYWp0wek

## ** Reason 4:** Your app may be blocked after deployment on a free plan. This video guides you through returning your app to prototype state to resolve the issue. 

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Stwpl1UXiDg

## ** Reason 5:** Your app is paused. Resume the paused app

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dwuxrMU3t24
