# Change the owner of a database

There are time when you may need to change the owner of an AppSheet database. For example, if you create a database under one account but wish to transfer it to another account.  

**Notes**:

- To change the owner of a database, you must be the owner.
- There can only be one database owner. When you change the owner, AppSheet changes your permission to [Editor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12637319#database-permissions).
- You can also [transfer databases between team members](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12007391#transfer-databases-between-team-members).

To change the owner of a database:

1.  [Open the database in the database editor](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/12632559).
2.  Select **Settings**  Settings icon **\> Change owner**.
3.  Enter the email of the new owner.
4.  Click **OK**.
