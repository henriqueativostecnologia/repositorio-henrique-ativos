# View Gemini usage in your team or organization

View the Gemini usage in your team or organization as described in the following sections.

- [Access the Gemini usage page](#explore-usage-page)
- [View the Gemini usage summary](#summary)
- [View all Gemini usage](#view-all-usage)
- [Download app usage](#download-csv)

When you use [Gemini in AppSheet Solutions](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16106353#solutions), you consume *credits* based on the number and complexity of the AI tasks that are run by users of the apps owned by creators in your organization. To learn more about credits and how to manage them, see [Mange credits for Gemini usage](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/16274771).

## Access the Gemini usage page

 To access the Gemini usage page, do the following:

1.  Sign in to [AppSheet](https://appsheet.com).

2.  On the My Apps page, select **Admin Console** from your account profile drop-down.

    Select Admin Console in account profile

    Alternatively, from an Admin page, such as My account or My team, you can select **Admin \> Admin Console **in the top navigation.

3.  Select **Reports \> Gemini usage** in the left navigation.

The Gemini usage page opens.

Gemini usage page in the AppSheet Admin Console

The Gemini usage page in the AppSheet Admin Console allows you to do the following:

- [View summary of credits granted and consumed](#summary)
- [View](#view-all-usage) or [download](#download-csv) all Gemini usage in your team or organization
- Select the month for which you want to display Gemini usage in the upper-right corner of the page (defaults to the current month)
- Search or filter the usage list
- Click the name of an internal app in the list to [view details for the app](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/14961200#view-app-details)
- Click the user name for an account in your team or organization (internal user) to [view details for the account](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/15100835)

## View summary of credits granted and consumed

At the top of the Gemini usage page, view the following information:

- Number of credits granted to your organization for the current month
- Number of credits consumed by your organization for the current month
- Pie chart showing number of credits available versus consumed as a percentage

##  Download all Gemini usage

Download all Gemini usage as a CSV file by selecting **More**  **\> Export CSV** in the upper right corner of the main body of the page.
