# Customize your timeline view

In your timeline view, you can:

- Customize your view interval and view mode.
- Zoom in and out.
- Publish and embed on sites.

[Learn how to create your timeline view](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/12935277).

**Note**: Now, you can view the timeline view experience on your Android or iOS mobile device.

## Select your timeline view interval

Sort and access timeline view by:

#### Days (D)

Switches to a different interval.

#### Weeks (W)

Switches to a weekly interval.

#### Months (M)

Switches to a monthly interval with a Month/Day format.

#### Quarters (Q)

Switches to a quarterly interval with a total length of 4 quarters.

#### Years (Y)

Switches to a yearly interval with a total length of 12 months.

#### Multiyear

Switches to a yearly interval with a total length of 5 years.

## Select your view mode

You can choose between 3 view modes:

- **Comfortable:** The default view with more space between cards.
- **Condensed:** Makes the card details fit closer together with less space in between.
- **Show cards in collapsed view:** Merge cards into a single row for each card group.

**Tip:** When you select a view mode, only the view mode changes, not the source data.

## Learn about more formatting options

You can find formatting options above the view. Some formatting options for you timeline view are:

#### Format your timeline view

- Undo  Undo

- Text overflow  Text overflow
- Text clip  Text clip

## Zoom in and out

1.  On your computer at the top right, select the zoom percentage number.
2.  Click the Down arrow .
3.  Select the desired zoom percentage from the drop down menu.

## Return to today’s date

To return to today’s date, click Today .

## Publish your timeline view

1.  On your computer, click **File** and then ** Share ** and then  **Publish to web**.
2.  Choose to publish the entire spreadsheet or individual sheets.
    - **Tip:** You can also choose a publishing format.
3.  Click **Publish**.
4.  Copy the URL and send the file to anyone you want.

[Learn how to make Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Forms public](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/183965?hl=en).

## Print your timeline view

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/?tgif=d). 
2.  From a timeline view tab, select **File** and then  **Print**.

[Learn more about how to print from Google Sheets](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7663148?).

## Download your timeline view

You can download most common file types

- **Download to web page:** On your computer, click **File** and then  **Download** and then **Web Page**.
- **Download to PDF:** On your computer, click **File** and then **Download** and then **PDF**.
