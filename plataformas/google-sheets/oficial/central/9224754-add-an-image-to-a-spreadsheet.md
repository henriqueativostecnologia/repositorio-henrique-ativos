# Add an image to a spreadsheet

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can add images under 50 MB in .png, .jpg, or .gif format. Follow the [image usage guidelines](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/179622). SVG file format isn't supported.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc).
2.  Tap Insert Insert   and then  **Image**.
3.  Choose to place your image in the cell or over the cells. Cells with an image cannot also have text.
4.  Choose where to get your image from.
5.  Tap a photo.

### Move an image

##### Move an image from one cell to another

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc).
2.  Tap and hold the image you want to move.
3.  Tap **Cut**.
4.  Tap and hold the cell you want to move the image to.
5.  Tap **Paste**.

##### Move an image that's over the cells

Tap the image you want to move and drag it.

### Delete an image

- **To delete an image in a cell:** Tap the image  and then  **Delete**.
- **To delete an image that's over the cells:** Tap and hold the image, then tap **Clear**.

## Version: Computer

You can add images under 50 MB in .png, .jpg, or .gif format. Follow the [image usage guidelines](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/179622). SVG file format isn't supported.

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in Google [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/?tgif=d).
2.  Click the cell where you want to add an image.
3.  Click **Insert** and then  **Image**. 
4.  Select to place your image in the cell or over the the cells. Cells with an image cannot also have text.
5.  Choose an image or take a snapshot.
6.  Click **Open** or **Select**.

### Add an image from your desktop or the web

Copy an image from your desktop or the web and paste it directly into your document, presentation, or spreadsheet. It may take a moment for your image to appear.

### Use a function to add an image

Use the [IMAGE function](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/3093333). Your image will automatically resize to fit inside the cell. To choose how the image fits:

- **Size to fit**: `=IMAGE(“URL”)`, `=IMAGE(“URL”, 1)`
- **Stretch to fit**: `=IMAGE(“URL”, 2)`
- **Original size**: `=IMAGE(“URL”, 3)`
- **Custom size**: `=IMAGE(“URL”, 4, [height in pixels], [width in pixels])`

### Replace an image that's over the cells

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in Google [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/?tgif=d).
2.  Click the image you want to replace.
3.  In the top-right corner of the image, click More  More and then ** Replace image**.
4.  Choose an image.
5.  Click **Replace** or **Select**.

### Add an alternative title & description to an image

If your image is in a cell:

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in Google [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Right click the image.
3.  Click **Alt text.**

If your image is above the cells:

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in Google [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Click the image and then  More  More and then ** Alt text.**

### Move or resize an image

### Move an image

- **To move an image from one cell to another**, use cut and paste.

- **To move an image that's over the cells**, click and drag it.

- **To move an image from inside a cell to over the cells,** right click the image and click **Put image over cells.**

- **To move an image from over the cells to inside a cell:**

  1.  **​​**Click the cell you want to place the image in.

  2.  Click the image and then  More  More and then ** Put image in selected cell.**

**Tip**: To move more than one item at the same time, press **CTRL** or **Command** and click the items you want to move.

##### Resize an image

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Click the image you want to change.
3.  Drag the blue markers to resize the image.

**Tip**: To resize more than one item by the same amount, press **CTRL** or **Command** and click the items you want to resize.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can add images under 50 MB in .png, .jpg, or .gif format. Follow the [image usage guidelines](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/179622). SVG file format isn't supported.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/google-sheets/id842849113?mt%3D8).
2.  Tap Insert Insert and then **Image**.
    - Or, you can touch and hold, then drag your image to the spreadsheet.
      - If you perform this action, the image is inserted over the cells in the spreadsheet. To insert the image into a cell:
        1.  Tap the image.
        2.  Select **Insert in cell \[cell address\]**.
3.  Select to place your image in the cell or over the cells. Cells with an image cannot also have text.
4.  Choose where to get your image from.
5.  Tap a photo.

### Move an image

##### Move an image from one cell to another

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/google-sheets/id842849113?mt%3D8).
2.  Tap the cell with the image you want to move.
3.  Tap **Cut**.
4.  Tap the cell to move the image to.
5.  Tap the cell and then  **Paste**.

##### Move an image that's over the cells

Tap the image to select it, then tap and drag it.

### Delete an image

##### Delete an image in a cell

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/google-sheets/id842849113?mt%3D8).
2.  Tap the cell that has the image you want to delete.
3.  Tap the cell and then  **Clear**.

##### Delete an image that's over the cells

Tap the image and then  **Delete**.
