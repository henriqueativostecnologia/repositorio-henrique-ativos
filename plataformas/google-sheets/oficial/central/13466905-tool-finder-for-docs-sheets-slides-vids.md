# Tool finder for Docs, Sheets, Slides & Vids

In Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Vids, you can use the tool finder at the top of the screen to:

- Find menu items and tools.
- View recommended actions. These can include recent actions you took in the document.
- Get suggestions for related actions as you type.
- Activate Find & replace after you enter content from your document.

## Find tools & features

To find tools, in the toolbar at the top of the screen, select an option:

- In the toolbar, click Search or in the menu, click **Help** and then **Search the menus**.

To use a keyboard shortcut, press the following keys together:

- **PC:** The **Alt** and **/** keys
- **Mac:** The **Option** and **/** keys
- **ChromeOS:** The **Alt** and **/** keys
