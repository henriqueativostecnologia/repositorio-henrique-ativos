# Use Dark theme in Google Docs, Sheets & Slides

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can change your theme setting to make it easier to view documents, presentations, and spreadsheets on your mobile device. Collaborators can’t see the theme you choose.  

### Change your theme setting

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides app.
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  Tap Settings Settings .
4.  Tap **Choose theme**.
5.  Choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **System default.**

### Preview a document or sheet in light theme

Dark theme automatically adjusts your documents and sheets to fit the theme and improve viewing. You can preview how a document or sheet will look in light theme for collaborators.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Docs or Sheets app.
2.  Open the document or sheet you want to view.
3.  At the top right, tap More More .
4.  Tap **View in light theme**.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can change your theme setting to make it easier to view documents, presentations, and spreadsheets on your mobile device. Collaborators can’t see the theme you choose.  

### Change your theme setting

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides app.
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  Tap Settings Settings .
4.  Tap **Theme**.
5.  Choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **System default**.

### Preview a document or sheet in light theme

Dark theme automatically adjusts your documents and sheets to fit the theme and improve viewing. You can preview how a document or sheet will look in light theme for collaborators.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Docs or Sheets app.
2.  Open the document or sheet you want to view.
3.  At the top right, tap More .
4.  Tap **View in light theme**.
