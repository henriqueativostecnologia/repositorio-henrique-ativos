# Print from Google Sheets

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can customize how you print a spreadsheet with margins, page breaks, headers, footers, and more options.

### Print a spreadsheet, single sheets, or cells

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet at [sheets.google.com](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  If you want to print part of a spreadsheet, select the cells or sheet.
3.  At the top, click **File** and then **Print**.
4.  Click **Current sheet**.
    - To print the full spreadsheet, click **Workbook**.
    - To print the cells you chose in step two, click **Selected cells**.
    - To print the sheet you chose in step two, click **Current sheet**.
5.  Click **Next**.
    - If you’re using [Chrome](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/143346#print_chrome), click **Print** in the window that appears.
    - If you’re using [Firefox or Safari](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/143346#print_firefox_safari), a PDF file will automatically download. In your PDF viewer, go to **File** and then  **Print**.

### Insert page breaks

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet at [sheets.google.com](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  At the top, click **File** and then **Print**.
3.  Click **Set custom page breaks**.
4.  Drag the lines to where the page should break. Each rectangle will print on its own page.
    - To remove a page break, point to a line and click Remove Remove .
5.  When you’re done, click **Confirm breaks**.

### Format margins, headers, footers, or page settings

#### Change margins

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet at [sheets.google.com](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  At the top, click **File** and then **Print**.
3.  Under "Margins," click **Normal**. Choose a margin type.
4.  To customize your margins, click **Custom numbers**. Enter new margin numbers.

#### Customize headers or footers

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet at [sheets.google.com](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  At the top, click **File** and then **Print**.
3.  Click **Headers & footers**.
4.  Choose what info you want to include.
5.  To change the format or location of the header or footer, click **Edit custom fields**. Make your changes and click **Confirm**​.

#### Repeat rows or columns across pages

You can repeat frozen rows or columns across a printed spreadsheet’s pages.

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet at [sheets.google.com](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  If you haven’t already, [freeze a row or column](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/9060449).
3.  At the top, click **File** and then  **Print**.
4.  Click **Headers & footers**.
5.  Choose **Repeat frozen rows** or **Repeat frozen columns**.

#### Change page orientation or paper size

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet at [sheets.google.com](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  At the top, click **File** and then  **Print**.
3.  Under “Orientation,” choose **Landscape** or **Portrait**.
4.  Under “Paper size,” choose the size of paper you want to print on. To add a custom paper size, choose **Custom size** and enter a height and width.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can customize how you print a spreadsheet with margins, page breaks, headers, footers, and more options.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the [Google Sheets app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc).
2.  On the spreadsheet you want to print, tap More More .
3.  Tap **Share & export** and then Print Print .
4.  At the top, tap **Paper size**. Choose your print settings.
5.  Follow the instructions to choose the printer.
6.  Tap Print Print .

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can customize how you print a spreadsheet with margins, page breaks, headers, footers, and more options.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842849113?pt=9008&ct=sheets_hc&mt=8).
2.  On the spreadsheet you want to print, tap More .
3.  Tap **Share & export** and then Print Print .
4.  Follow the instructions to choose the printer.
5.  Choose your print settings.
6.  Tap **Print**.
