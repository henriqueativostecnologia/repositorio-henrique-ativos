# Use the BigQuery sandbox

To have access to BigQuery data in Google Sheets, you must have:

- Access to BigQuery
- A project with billing setup in BigQuery

If you don't meet these requirements, the BigQuery sandbox gives you limited access to BigQuery in Google Sheets at no charge without needing to upgrade your account. [Learn how to use the BigQuery sandbox](https://cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/sandbox#limits) or [how to upgrade your account](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/9702507#whatyouneed).

## Data limits in the BigQuery sandbox

The sandbox gives you 10 GB of active storage and 1 TB of processed query data per month. [Learn more about the limits of the BigQuery sandbox](https://cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/sandbox#limits). 
 
If you've met your data limit, [learn how to upgrade your account](#whatyouneed).
