# EFFECT

Calculates the annual effective interest rate given the nominal rate and number of compounding periods per year.

### Sample Usage

`EFFECT(0.99,12)`

`EFFECT(A2,A3)`

### Syntax

`EFFECT(nominal_rate, periods_per_year)`

- `nominal_rate` - The nominal interest rate per year.

- `periods_per_year` - The number of compounding periods per year.

### See Also

[`NOMINAL`](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/3093234): Calculates the annual nominal interest rate given the effective rate and number of compounding periods per year.

[`INTRATE`](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/3093174): Calculates the effective interest rate generated when an investment is purchased at one price and sold at another with no interest or dividends generated by the investment itself.
