# Freeze, group, hide, or merge rows & columns

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can freeze, group, hide, or merge your spreadsheet’s columns, rows, or cells.

### Freeze, group, hide, or merge rows & columns

To pin data in the same place and see it when you scroll, you can freeze rows or columns.

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](http://sheets.google.com).
2.  Select a row or column you want to freeze or unfreeze.
3.  At the top, click **View**   and then **Freeze**.
4.  Select how many rows or columns to freeze.

To unfreeze, select a row or column. Then, at the top, click **View**   and then **Freeze**   and then **No rows** or **No columns**.

##### Group or ungroup rows or columns

You can organize and summarize related data by grouping rows and columns.

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](http://sheets.google.com)
2.  Select the rows or columns you want to group or ungroup.
3.  At the top, click **View**  and then  **Group** and choose:

- **Group rows**
- **Group columns**
- **Ungroup rows**
- **Ungroup columns**

To expand or collapse grouped rows or columns, next to the rows or columns, click Plus (+) or Minus (-).

##### Hide rows or columns

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Click a row or column to highlight it.
    - To highlight multiple rows or columns, press and hold the command key on your keyboard and click the rows or columns you want to highlight.
3.  Right-click and select **Hide row** or **Hide column** from the menu that appears. An arrow will appear over the hidden row or column.

To show a row or column again, click the arrow where the row number or column letter used to be.

##### Merge rows or columns

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Select the rows, columns, or cells to merge.
3.  At the top, click **Format**   and then **Merge cells**, then select how you want your cells to be merged.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can freeze, group, hide, or merge your spreadsheet’s columns, rows, or cells.

### Freeze or unfreeze rows or columns

To pin data in the same place and see it when you scroll, you can freeze rows or columns.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id%3Dcom.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets%26hl%3Den).
2.  Touch and hold a row or column.
3.  In the menu that appears, tap **Freeze** or **Unfreeze**.

##### Hide rows or columns

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id%3Dcom.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets%26hl%3Den).
2.  Touch and hold a row or column.
3.  In the menu that appears, tap **Hide Row** or **Hide column**.

##### Group or ungroup rows or columns

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id%3Dcom.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets%26referrer%3Dutm_source%253Dsheets_hc%2526utm_medium%253Dlink%2526utm_campaign%253Dsheets_hc).
2.  Highlight the rows or columns you want to group or ungroup.
3.  Touch and hold the highlighted rows or columns.
4.  Tap More . More , and then tap **Group** or **Ungroup**.

To expand or collapse grouped rows or columns, tap Plus (+) or Minus (-).

**Note**: If there’s a group within a group, first, tap the Double arrows ».

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can freeze, group, hide, or merge your spreadsheet’s columns, rows, or cells.

### Freeze or unfreeze rows or columns

To pin data in the same place and see it when you scroll, you can freeze rows or columns.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/google-sheets/id842849113?mt%3D8).
2.  Tap a row number or column letter.
3.  In the menu that appears, tap the right arrow   and then **Freeze** or **Unfreeze**.

##### Hide rows or columns

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/google-sheets/id842849113?mt%3D8).
2.  Tap a row or column.
3.  In the menu that appears, tap the right arrow   and then **Hide row** or **Hide column**.

##### Group or ungroup rows or columns

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a spreadsheet in the [Google Sheets app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842849113?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Dsheets_hc%26mt%3D8).
2.  Highlight the rows or columns you want to group or ungroup.
3.  Tap the highlighted rows or columns.
4.  In the menu that appears, tap the right arrow   and then **Group** or **Ungroup**.

To expand or collapse grouped rows or columns, tap Plus (+) or Minus (-).

**Note**: If there’s a group within a group, first, tap the Double arrows ».
