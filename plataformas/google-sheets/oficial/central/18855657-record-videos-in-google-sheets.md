# Record videos in Google Sheets

**Feature availability:**

- This feature may only be available on certain work or school accounts
- You can only record your presentations in Google Chrome and Edge browsers. But, you can view, share, and delete recordings from any Drive supported browser.

You can record yourself in a video directly from your Google Sheets spreadsheet, showcasing the Sheets content. This capability streamlines the process of adding video explanations, presentations, or feedback, providing an easy way to communicate your ideas while referencing your content.

## Record a video in Google Slides

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://sheets.google.com/).
2.  In the top left, click **File**.
3.  Click **Record Video** .
4.  Select what you want to show in the video:
    - To record yourself in (Picture-in-picture mode) and your slides select **Camera & Screen**.
    - To record slideshow only, click **Screen**.
    - To record yourself only, click **Camera**.
    - To record voice only, click **Audio**.
5.  Optional: To apply visual effects, click .
6.  Optional: To use a different camera or microphone, select the connected devices from the drop down menus below your camera preview.
7.  Select the tab, window, or screen you want to show in the video and then click **Next**.
8.  To finish recording click **Finish**.
9.  To edit your recording in Google Vids, click Edit .
10. Optional: To share, click **Share & copy link**.

## Edit your slideshow recording with Google Vids

1.  To view your recording, open [Google Vids](https://docs.google.com/videos).
2.  Click the file of your video recording. [Learn how to customize a video with Google Vids](https://support.google.com/docs/topic/14991151).
