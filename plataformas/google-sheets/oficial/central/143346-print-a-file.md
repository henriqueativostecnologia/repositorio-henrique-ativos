# Print a file

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can print Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides using browsers like Chrome or Safari.

#### Print in Chrome

##### Print a document

1.  On your computer, open a document in [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/).
2.  Click **File** and then **Print**.
3.  In the window that opens, choose your print settings.
4.  Click **Print**.

**Important:** If you’re in a pageless document, you can still print. However, your document may appear differently in the preview window:

- Images will automatically adjust to fit the page width.
- The page will squeeze wide tables to fit. 
- If your document was previously in pages format and included headers, footers, watermarks, or footnotes, those elements will still print, even if you can't see them in your pageless doc.

If you want to control what the printed doc will look like, [switch back to pages format.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/11528737)

##### Print a spreadsheet

1.  On your computer, open a spreadsheet in [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/).
2.  Click **File** and then **Print**.
3.  Optional: [Choose your print settings, like margins or page orientation.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7663148)
4.  Click **Next**.
5.  In the window that opens, choose your print settings.
6.  Click **Print**.

#### Print a presentation

1.  On your computer, open a presentation in [Google Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/).
2.  Click **File**.
    - **Print without changes**: Click **Print**.
    - **Adjust orientation**: Click **Print settings and preview** and then in the toolbar, click **Handout** and then **Landscape.**
    - **Print with speaker notes**: Click **Print settings and preview** and then In the toolbar, click **1 slide with notes**.
3.  Click **Print**.
4.  In the window that opens, choose your print settings.
5.  Click **Print**.

#### Print in Firefox or Safari

#### Print your file as a PDF

1.  On your computer, open the document, presentation, or spreadsheet you want to print.
2.  Click **File** and then **Print**.
    - **Document or Presentation**: A PDF file will automatically download.
    - **Spreadsheet**: In the window that opens, choose your print settings. and then Click **Next**. A PDF file will automatically download.
3.  When the download completes, open the file.
4.  In your PDF viewer, go to **File** and then **Print**.
5.  In the window that opens, choose your print settings.
6.  Click **Print**.

#### Print your file in another file format

1.  On your computer, open the document, spreadsheet, or presentation you want to print.
2.  Click **File** and then **Download**.
3.  Select the file format you want.
4.  Find the downloaded file on your computer, and open it.
5.  Print the downloaded file.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

### Print a document, spreadsheet, or presentation

You can print from the Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides apps on your Android phone or tablet.

#### **Step 1: Download the app you want to use**

- [Google Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs)
- [Google Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets)
- [Google Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides)

#### **Step 2: Print from your phone**

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides app.
2.  On the file you want to print, tap More More .
3.  Tap **Share & export** and then Print Print .
4.  Follow the instructions to select the printer.
5.  Tap Print Print .

## Version: iPhone & iPad

### Print a document, spreadsheet, or presentation

You can print from the Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides apps on your iPhone or iPad.

#### **Step 1: Download the app you want to use**

- [Google Docs](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8)
- [Google Sheets](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842849113?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Dsheets_hc%26mt%3D8)
- [Google Slides](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id879478102?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Dslides_hc%26mt%3D8)

#### **Step 2: Use your computer to set up printing**

[Learn how to connect to Google Cloud Print.](https://www.google.com/cloudprint/learn/printers.html)

#### **Step 3: Print from your phone**

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides app.
2.  On the file you want to print, tap More .
3.  Tap **Share & export** and then Print Print .
4.  To select the printer, follow the instructions.
5.  Tap Print Print .

### Change the page setup of a document

First, [download the Google Docs app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8) for iPhone or iPad.

##### Change page size, color, or orientation

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the [Google Docs app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8).
2.  Open a document.
3.  In the top right, tap More .
4.  Tap **Page setup**.
5.  Select the setting you want to change:
    - **Orientation**
    - **Paper size**
    - **Page color**
6.  Make your changes.

##### See & change how your document looks when printed

You can edit your file while in print layout mode to see how it will look when it’s printed.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open a file in the [Google Docs app](https://itunes.apple.com/app/apple-store/id842842640?pt%3D9008%26ct%3Ddocs_hc%26mt%3D8).
2.  Tap More .
3.  Turn **Print layout** on.
4.  Tap Edit Edit .
