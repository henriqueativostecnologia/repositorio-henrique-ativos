# View your day, week, or month

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Check your calendar to find out when you're busy or available.

### Change your calendar view

After you choose a new view, it becomes your default view until you change it.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  In the top right, choose** **a view: **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Year**, **Schedule**, or **4 days**.

### Change view settings

Choose which day your calendar week starts, set a custom view, and use an alternate calendar.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right corner, click Settings  and then **Settings**.
3.  At the left, click **View options**.
4.  Choose your settings. Changes are automatically saved.

**Important:** The side-by-side calendars won’t work for the week or month view in Google Calendar.

### Navigate your calendar

There are two ways to move between dates:

- Use the arrows in the top left corner of the Calendar to move to recent dates in the past or future.
- Use the small calendar in the top left corner to select a date.

To return to today's date, click **Today** in the top left corner.

### Show shared calendars

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  On the left, choose which calendars you want to show:
    - **My calendars:** Calendars you own.
    - **Other calendars in side-by-side day view:** Primary calendars of shared people, rooms, or group calendars. Shared calendars must auto-accept invitations.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

Check your calendar to find out when you're busy or available.

### Go to a specific day

1.  On your Android phone, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap the month name. For example, January Down Arrow .
3.  Swipe left or right to go to other months.
4.  Tap a date to find events on that day.

**Get back to today:** At the top right corner, tap Jump to today .

### Choose how many days to view

When you open the Calendar app, you'll get a list of your upcoming events. You can switch views to find your whole day or multiple days.

1.  On your Android phone, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left corner, tap the Menu Menu .
3.  Choose a view, such as **Schedule** or **Month**. To find a list of all your events or tasks by day, choose **Schedule**.

### Change your settings

[Learn how to change your calendar settings](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/6084644), including the way your calendar looks and the day your week starts on.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

Check your calendar to find out when you're busy or available.

### Go to a specific day

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap the month name. For example, January Down Arrow .
3.  Swipe left or right to go to other months.
4.  Tap a date to find events on that day.

**Get back to today:** At the top right corner, tap Jump to today .

### Choose how many days to view

When you open the Calendar app, you'll get a list of your upcoming events. You can switch views to find your whole day or multiple days.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Choose an option:
    - **iPhone:** At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
    - **iPad:** At the top right, find the option to change the view.
3.  Choose a view, such as **Schedule** or **Month**. To find a list of all your events or tasks by day, choose **Schedule**.

### Change your settings

[Learn how to change your calendar settings](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/6084644), including the way your calendar looks and the day your week starts on.
