# Add a room to an event

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** To add meeting rooms and other resources to your event, you need to have a work or school account. If you have a personal Google Account that ends in @gmail.com, [learn how to create a calendar for a room or shared space](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/44105).

You can add meeting rooms and other resources, like projectors, to your event. If you can’t book a room, [learn why a Google Calendar meeting room declines an event](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/16107253).

Google Calendar can check for room conflicts up to 12 months in advance. If you schedule events or recurring instances more than 12 months out, Google Calendar won't detect conflicts in these.

### Add a room to your event

**Important:** If you can't add a room to an event, Google Calendar may suggest a shortcut to create a duplicate event where you can book a room. This shortcut creates a single event, without any guests.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Create a new event or open an existing one.
    - To edit an existing event, click Edit event Edit .
3.  If your account is part of an organization with Rooms, click **Add rooms** or **Add room booking**.
    - Based on who you invite, you can choose from suggested rooms or search for a new one.
    - Click the rooms or resources you want to add.
4.  Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- You can book more than one room for an event.
- If you add a room but its schedule is missing, in the scheduling grid, click Filter and view and then **Show rooms**. [Learn how to find times to meet in Google Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/6294878).
- If you're an administrator, [learn how to create rooms in your Admin Console](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/calendar/create-buildings-features-and-calendar-resources).

### Remove a room from your event

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Open an existing event.
3.  At the top right, click Edit event Edit .
4.  In the "Rooms" list, find the room or resource. 
5.  Next to the room or resource, click **Remove room** Close .
6.  Click **Save**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

**Important:** To add meeting rooms and other resources to your event, you need to have a work or school account. If you have a personal Google Account that ends in @gmail.com, [learn how to create a calendar for a room or shared space](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/44105).

You can add meeting rooms and other resources, like projectors, to your event. If you can’t book a room, [learn why a Google Calendar meeting room declines an event](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/16107253).

Google Calendar can check for room conflicts up to 12 months in advance. If you schedule events or recurring instances more than 12 months out, Google Calendar won't detect conflicts in these.

### Add a room to your event

1.  Open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Create a new event or open an existing one.
    - To edit an existing event, tap Edit event Edit .
3.  Tap **Add room**.
4.  Select a room.
5.  Tap **Save**.

**Tips:**

- You can book more than one room for an event.
- If you're an administrator, you can [add new rooms and resources in the Google Admin console](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1033925).

### Remove a room from your event

1.  Open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  To change the event, open the event and tap Edit event Edit .
3.  Tap the room and then Remove Remove .
4.  Tap **Save**.
