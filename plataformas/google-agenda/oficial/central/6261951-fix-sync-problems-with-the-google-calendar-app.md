# Fix sync problems with the Google Calendar app

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

**Important**: To find your Google Calendar events in a different calendar application, like Apple Calendar or Outlook, the instructions for syncing are different:

- [Learn how to add Calendar events to Apple Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/99358)
- [Learn how to sync Calendar with computer programs](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37648)

Events you create in Calendar appear across all devices where you’re signed in. If you can’t find your events in the Calendar app, review your settings.

### First, try these common fixes

**Important**: The Calendar app only syncs past events up to one year. To find past events beyond a year, go to [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).

Follow these steps on the device you're using the Calendar app on.

##### 1. Check your internet connection

- On your mobile device, check that data or Wi-Fi is on and you’re not in airplane mode.
- Open a browser app and try a web search.

##### 2. Update the Calendar app

- Make sure you’re using the Calendar app Calendar .
- In your device’s app store, update the Calendar app.

##### 3. Check that the calendar is visible

1.  On your phone or tablet, open the Calendar app  Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap Menu .
3.  To the left of the calendar's name, make sure the box is checked.

##### 4. Make sure new events are being added to your Google Calendar

If you add an event to a calendar that’s not associated with your Google Account, it might not show up in the Calendar app or on [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com). When you create an event, check the account to which you add events.

1.  Open the Google Calendar app  Calendar .
2.  In the bottom right, tap Create  Add .
3.  At the top of the event, check that the calendar it’s added to is associated with your Google Account.

### Next, try these troubleshooting steps

If the steps above didn’t work, read through these instructions on your computer while you try the steps on your mobile device. 

##### Check that Calendar syncs with your account

**Important**:

- Some of these steps work only on Android 9.0 and up. [Learn how to check your Android version](https://support.google.com/android/answer/7680439).
- Settings can vary by phone. If the steps here don’t match your device, [contact your device manufacturer](https://support.google.com/android/answer/3094742).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app.
2.  Tap **Passwords, passkeys & accounts**.
    - Alternatively, tap **About phone** and then **Google Account**.
3.  Select your Google Account.
4.  Tap **Account sync**.
5.  Make sure account sync is on for “Calendar.”

##### Confirm that the correct calendar syncs in the Calendar app

**Important**: It might take some time for your events to show up after you turn on sync.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap Menu   and then ** Settings**.
3.  Select the calendar that doesn’t appear.
    - If you can’t find the calendar, tap **Show more**.
4.  At the top of the page, next to “Sync,” make sure the setting is on.
    - You can only find the "Sync" setting for calendars you create, but not your primary calendar. Your primary calendar displays as "Events" (unless you changed its name).

##### Manually sync your calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app  Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu   and then  **Refresh.**

##### Make sure Calendar storage is turned on

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app.
2.  Tap **Apps** or **Apps & notifications**.
3.  To get the list of all apps, tap **See all apps**.
4.  In the top right, tap More and then **Show system and then Calendar storage**.

##### Check your device's storage

**Important**: When your device runs out of storage, the Calendar app stops syncing.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app.
2.  Find the **Storage** section.
3.  If you need to clear space, try uninstalling apps you don't use or deleting files or photos from your device.

##### Clear your Calendar app data

**Important:**

- These steps may cause you to lose some unsynced data. Any event information you can't find on Google Calendar on your computer will be lost. Only try these steps if nothing else works.
- Settings can vary by phone. If the steps here don’t match your device, [contact your device manufacturer](https://support.google.com/android/answer/3094742).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app.
2.  Tap the **Apps** and then **See all apps**.
3.  From the list of apps, tap **Calendar**.
4.  Tap **Storage & cache** and then **Clear storage** and then **Delete**.
5.  Restart your device.

### Quit the Calendar app & reopen it

Learn how to quit and reopen an app on:

- [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/quit-and-reopen-an-app-iph83bfec492/ios)
- [iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/quit-and-reopen-an-app-ipad79518d15/ipados)

### Remove your account

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Calendar app  Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your profile photo.
3.  Tap **Manage accounts on this device**  and then ** Remove from this device** and then ** Remove.**
4.  Reopen the Calendar app and add your account again.

### Uninstall & reinstall the Calendar app

1.  Find the Google Calendar app icon Calendar .
2.  [Uninstall the Calendar app](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/remove-or-delete-apps-iph248b543ca/ios).
3.  Reinstall the [Calendar app in the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-calendar-get-organized/id909319292).
