# Use a family calendar on Google

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

When you create a family on Google, a calendar called "Family" is automatically created. Anyone who joins your family or has access to your family calendar can find it when they open Google Calendar on any device where they’re signed in.

### Learn how the family calendar works

- The family manager is the default owner of the family calendar but can transfer ownership to someone else. [Learn how to transfer calendars or events in Google Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/78739).
- Anyone in the family or anyone with access to your family calendar can:
  - Make changes to events on the family calendar.
  - Manage how the family calendar is shared.
  - Edit the family calendar's name.
- On your computer, you can share the family calendar with non-family members.
- If someone leaves a family group, they lose access to the family calendar.
- If a family manager deletes the family group, the family calendar and related events must be deleted manually.
- Anyone with “Make changes and manage sharing” permission gets an email notification when a user under the age of 13:
  - Shares the family calendar
  - Makes the family calendar public
  - Changes member permissions

### Create an event on the family calendar

1.  Open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click **Click** and then **Event**.
3.  Next to Calendars , click the drop-down and select the family calendar.
4.  Add a title and event details. If you want, you can invite non-family members to join your event.
5.  Click **Save**.

### Change your family calendar notification settings

By default, you get the same notifications for upcoming events as you do for your primary calendar. However, you don't get notifications when a family member creates, edits, or deletes an event.

To change your notification settings for upcoming events:

1.  Open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  On the left side of the page, find the "My calendars" section.
3.  Hover over the name of your family calendar, then click Options More .
4.  Click **Settings**.
5.  Click **Event notifications**.
6.  To create a new notification, click **Add notification**.
7.  To remove a notification, click Remove notification .

### Share your family calendar with non-family members

**Important:** By default, non-family members can edit events in the family calendar. The calendar can be changed in the same way you change it for regular secondary calendars. [Understand permission for shared calendars](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082#Permission_settings_for_shared_calendars).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Under “Settings for my calendars,” select your family calendar and then **Calendar settings**.
4.  On the right, under “Shared with,” click **Add people and groups**.
5.  Enter the name of the person or group you want to share your calendar with.

### Delete the family calendar

**Important:** Only the calendar owner can delete the family calendar.

If you don't need the family calendar anymore, you can permanently [delete or unsubscribe from a calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37188).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

When you create a family on Google, a calendar called "Family" is automatically created. Anyone who joins your family or has access to your family calendar can find it when they open Google Calendar on any device where they’re signed in.

### Learn how the family calendar works

- The family manager is the default owner of the family calendar but can transfer ownership to someone else. [Learn how to transfer calendars or events in Google Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/78739).

  - Make changes to events on the family calendar.

  - Edit the family calendar's name.

- If someone leaves a family group, they lose access to the family calendar.
- If a family manager deletes the family group, the family calendar and related events must be deleted manually.
- Anyone with “Make changes and manage sharing” permission gets an email notification when a user under the age of 13:
  - Shares the family calendar
  - Makes the family calendar public
  - Changes member permissions

### Create an event on the family calendar

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the bottom right, tap Create Insert and then **Event**.
3.  Tap the name of your family calendar.
4.  Add a title and details for the event.
5.  In the top right, tap **Save**.

### Change your family calendar notification settings

By default, you'll get the same notifications for upcoming events as you do for your primary calendar. However, you won't get notifications when a family member creates, edits, or deletes an event.

To change your notification settings for upcoming events:

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Tap the name of your family calendar.
4.  Tap your current notifications to remove or change them.

### Share your family calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Select the family calendar you want to share.
4.  Under “Shared with,” tap **Add people or groups**.
    - If the calendar is already shared with others, you can find the names of the people or groups you shared it with.
    - To stop sharing with any users and groups, tap Remove .
5.  In the search box, enter and choose a name or group.
    - You can add multiple users and groups at the same time.
6.  Tap **Save**.

### Delete the family calendar

**Important:** Only the calendar owner can delete the family calendar.

If you don't need the family calendar anymore, you can permanently [delete or unsubscribe from a calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37188). You must delete or unsubscribe from your computer.
