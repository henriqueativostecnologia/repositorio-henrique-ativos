# Report inappropriate calendar invitations & events

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you notice a suspicious invitation or event on your calendar, report it as spam.

### Step 1: Report the event as spam

**Important:**

- To report an event as spam, it must be sent from Google Calendar. Calendar events created by another provider, app, or service can’t be reported as spam.
- When you report an event, the event is removed from your calendar. If the event recurs, all events in the series are removed.

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r).
2.  Click the event you want to report.
3.  At the top right, click **More actions** and then **Report as spam**.

### Step 2: Change event permissions

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r).
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the "General" tab, click **Event settings** and then **Add invitations to my calendar**.
4.  Select **Only if the sender is known**.
    - This adds an event to your calendar only if the sender is in your contacts, part of your organization, or someone you've interacted with.
      - This option might reveal to senders they aren't in your contacts.

[Learn how to manage new invitations](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/13159188).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

If you notice a suspicious invitation or event on your calendar, report it as spam.

### Step 1: Report the event as spam

**Important:** When you report an event, the event is removed from your calendar. If the event recurs, all events in the series are removed.

1.  On your Android device, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the event you want to report.
3.  At the top right, tap More and then **Report as spam**.

### Step 2: Change event permissions

To choose which events display on your calendar, change your settings.

1.  On your Android device, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu and then Settings .
3.  Tap **General** and then **Adding invitations** and then **Add invitations to my calendar**.
4.  Select **Only if the sender is known**.
    - This adds an event to your calendar only if the sender is in your contacts, part of your organization, or someone you've interacted with.
      - This option might reveal to senders they aren't in your contacts.

[Learn how to manage new invitations](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/13159188).

### Step 3: Review calendar access

You can remove permissions for any app that you don't want to make changes to your calendar.

**Important:** These steps might be different on your phone. For more info, visit your device's Help Center. [Learn how to change app permissions on your Android phone](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9431959).

1.  On your Android device, open Settings .
2.  Tap **Privacy** and then **Permission Manager** and then **Calendar**.
3.  To turn calendar access on or off for an app, tap it.

**Tip:** If you remove an app's permission to access your calendar data, it doesn't delete the events already on your calendar. [Learn how to delete an event](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37113).

[Learn more about how to block calendar spam on Android](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/12790228).
