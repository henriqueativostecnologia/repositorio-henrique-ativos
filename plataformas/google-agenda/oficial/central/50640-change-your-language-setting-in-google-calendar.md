# Change your language setting in Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can choose which language you want to use with Google Calendar.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  In the top right corner, click Settings menu  Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the "Language" section, choose the language you want to use from the drop-down menu.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can choose which language you want to use with Google Calendar.

**Important**: Some of these steps work only on Android 14 and up. [Learn how to check your Android version](https://support.google.com/android/answer/7680439).

To use a different language in Google Calendar, you need to change the language setting for your device.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open your Settings app.
2.  Tap **System**  and then ** Language & region ** and then  **App languages**.
3.  Select the Google Calendar app** ** Calendar .
4.  Choose a language.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can choose which language you want to use with Google Calendar.

To use a different language in Google Calendar, you need to change the language setting for your device.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, [change your device language](https://support.apple.com/109358).
2.  Open the Google Calendar app  Calendar .
