# Find times to meet in Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If someone shares their calendar with you, you can check their availability. You can also find times to meet.

**Tip**: If you have an eligible Google Workspace plan, you can use Gemini in Google Calendar to help you find a time to meet. [*Learn how to let Gemini in Google Calendar find times to meet*](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/16690875).

### Find someone else's calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  On the left, click **Search for people**.
3.  Enter the name of the person.

### Find a time when someone is available

**Important:** You can compare availability for up to 50 guests. If you add more than 50 guests to your event, only 50 schedules are shown.

If you need to check someone's availability, compare the calendars of your guests. These guests must have shared their calendars with you. This helps you find an open time slot.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top left, click **Create** and then **Event**.
3.  Add guests to your event.
4.  Click **Find a time**.
5.  **Optional:** To manage your view, click Filter and view :
    - **Show or hide rooms:** Click **Show rooms**.
    - **Filter guests:** Select **No guests**, **Required guests**, or **All guests**.
6.  Find an available time on your calendar and your guests' calendars.

**Tips:**

- To check if a room is available:
  1.  Click **Add rooms** or **Add room booking**.
  2.  Choose a room.
- If a room is selected but you do not find its schedule, click Filter and view  and then ** Show rooms**.

### Compare calendars in "Day" view

If you subscribe to someone's calendar, you can check their schedule next to yours in your main calendar grid. This is a persistent setting that allows you to check each calendar in its own dedicated column rather than have them overlap.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu** ** and then ** Settings**.
3.  On the left, click **View options**.
4.  Check the box for "View calendars side by side in Day View".
5.  In your main calendar, make sure you are in "Day" view.

### Learn what you need to find someone’s calendar

- If the person’s calendar is shared publicly or within your organization, you can find their calendar.
- If the person does not share their calendar with you, you can send them a request to share it. After they share their calendar, you get an email confirmation.
- When people share their calendar, they can choose:
  - The information others can find about their events.
  - The permissions others have.
  - [Learn how to change event visibility settings](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/34580).

### Find a time when someone is available

**Important**: You can't add other people's calendars from the Calendar app. However, after you add the calendars from your computer, you can find them in the app.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the bottom right, tap **Create ** and then **Event**.
3.  Tap **Add people**.
4.  Add guests to your event.
5.  Tap **View schedules**.
