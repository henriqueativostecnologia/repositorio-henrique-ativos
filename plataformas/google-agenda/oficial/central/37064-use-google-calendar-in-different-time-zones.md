# Use Google Calendar in different time zones

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can change your time zone and create events with certain time zones. This helps if you'll be traveling when the event happens, or if you're creating events for people in different time zones.

### How Calendar uses time zones

- When you create an event, it appears in your local time zone. It will also show up in the local time zones for anyone you invite, even if they're in a different time zone.
- When you travel to a different time zone, your calendar appears in the local time.
- Tasks adapt to the new time zone if the Calendar’s time zone is changed after a task is created. For example, a 9 AM MT task changes to an 11 AM ET task if you travel from Denver to New York.

### Change your time zone

**Important:** If you are not the owner, you can't change the time zone of a calendar.

##### Change the time zone for all your calendars

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click **Settings menu** Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, under "General," click **Time zone**.
4.  In the "Time zone" category, click **Primary time zone**.
5.  Search for a city or country.
6.  Select your preferred time zone.

**Tip:** To let Calendar confirm your primary time zone when you travel, click **Ask to update my primary time zone to current location**.

##### Change the time zone of one calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, click **My calendars**.
3.  Point to the calendar you want to change and click **More** More and then **Settings and sharing**.
4.  In your calendar settings, click **Time zone**.
5.  Search for a city or country.
6.  Select your preferred time zone.

### Use other time zones

##### Show additional time zones on your calendar grid

**Important:**

- In addition to your primary time zone, you can add a secondary and a tertiary time zone.
- Additional time zones appear on the left of your calendar grid.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click **Settings menu** Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, under "General," click **Time zone**.
4.  Check the box next to **Show additional time zones**.
5.  Under "Secondary time zone," select your preferred time zone.
6.  (Optional) Under "Tertiary time zone," select your preferred time zone.

##### Create an event with a different time zone

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/) .
2.  At the top left, click **Create** and then **Create event** and then **More options**.
3.  Next to the time for the event, click **Time zone**.
4.  Search for a city or country.
5.  Select your preferred time zone.
    - If the event starts or ends in different time zones, click **Use separate start and end time zones**.
6.  Click **OK**.
7.  Fill in the details of your event and then click **Save**.

##### Change the time zone for an event

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the event and then Edit Edit .
3.  Next to the time of the event, click **Time zone**.
4.  Search for a city or country.
5.  Select your preferred time zone.
6.  Click **OK**.

##### Turn on a world clock

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click **Settings menu** Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the "World clock" section, click **Show world clock**.
4.  Click **Add time zone**.
5.  Search for a city or country.
6.  Select your preferred time zone.

### Understand how Calendar handles daylight saving time

**Important:**

- Events in the past years or future might not reflect daylight saving time changes. It might be incorrectly displayed.
- As the date changes within a few years, the displayed time automatically adjusts.

To avoid issues with daylight saving time, Google Calendar uses Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). [Learn more about UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time).

When events are created, they're converted into UTC, but you'll always see them in your local time.

If an area switches their time zone, events created before we knew about the change might be in the wrong time zone.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can change your time zone and create events with certain time zones. This helps if you'll be traveling when the event happens, or if you're creating events for people in different time zones.

### How Calendar uses time zones

When you create an event, you'll see it in your local time zone. It will also show up in the local time zones for anyone you invite, even if they're in a different time zone.

Tasks adapt to the new time zone if the Calendar’s time zone is changed after a task is created. For example, a 9 AM MT task changes to an 11 AM ET task if you travel from Denver to New York.

### Change your time zone

When you travel to a different time zone, you can see your calendar in the local time.

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  At the bottom, tap Settings Settings .
4.  Tap **General**.
5.  Tap **Use device time zone** on or off.
    - If **Use device time zone** is on, your time zone will update automatically as you travel.
    - If **Use device time zone** is off, you can select a time zone from the drop-down menu.

- As the date changes within a few years, the displayed time automatically adjusts.

To avoid issues with daylight saving time, Google Calendar uses Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). [Learn more about UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time).

When events are created, they're converted into UTC, but you'll always see them in your local time.

If an area switches their time zone, events created before we knew about the change might be in the wrong time zone.
