# Change event color set & density in Google Calendar

In Google Calendar, you can customize the appearance of your events. You can change the color of the event text and adjust the spacing between events.

**Important:**

- These settings are only available when you use Google Calendar on your computer.
- To change the color set, set your background to “Light.” [Learn how to change the background](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/15240542).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings and then **Appearance**.
3.  Select a color set:
    - **Modern (with white text)**
    - **Classic (with black text)**
4.  Select a density setting:
    - **Responsive:** Your calendar dynamically scales and enlarges events to fill larger monitors, which makes events easier to read.
    - **Comfortable:** Your calendar keeps the standard layout with traditional spacing, regardless of your screen size.
    - **Compact:** Your calendar shows a denser view with tighter spacing, which allows you to see more events at once.
