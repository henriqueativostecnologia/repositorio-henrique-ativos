# Track responses to your event

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

When guests respond to your invitations from Google Calendar, you:

- Get an email notification
- Can track their responses

### Review your guest list

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On your calendar, click the event.
3.  At the top right, click Edit event Edit .
4.  On the right, under “Guests,” review the guest list.

### Review a guest’s proposed time

If a guest can’t attend the scheduled event, they can propose a new time. You can review the proposal and if necessary, move the event.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On your calendar, click an event with a proposal .
3.  Under the guest list, click **Review proposed time**.
4.  To change the proposed time, click **Save**.

### Turn off event response notification emails

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, under "Settings for my calendars," select the specific calendar you want to change.
4.  Scroll to the “Other notifications” section.
5.  Next to "Event responses," click Menu Dropdown .
6.  Select **None**.

### Manage guest response emails

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Create a new event or open an existing event and select Edit Edit .
3.  Check or uncheck the box next to “Get an email when guests respond.”
    - This option controls email notifications for a single event or a recurring event series. It does not change your global Calendar notification preferences, which are found in your [main Calendar settings](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/settings).
4.  Click **Save**.

When you turn off guest response emails for an event, you do not get:

- Email notifications when guests accept, decline, or mark an event as tentative.
- RSVP statuses from third-party users, such as Outlook.
- Automatic email replies to the invite, such as out of office messages.

**Tip:** Even with email notifications off, you can check when guests who use Google Calendar accept, decline, or mark an event as tentative in the guest list. However, you can’t check the RSVP status for guests who use other calendars, like Outlook.

### Allow guests to forward invitations

If you give your guests permission to invite others, they can forward invitation emails to new guests. The new guests are added to the guest list and can invite others when they respond to the invitation.

If you don’t want guests to invite others:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On your calendar, open the event.
3.  At the top right, click Edit event Edit .
4.  Under “Guests permissions,” uncheck the box next to “Invite others.”

## Version: Android phone & tablet

When guests respond to your invitations from Google Calendar, you:

- Get an email notification
- Can track their responses

### Find your guest list

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On your calendar, tap the event.
3.  In the event, scroll to your guest list.

### Review a guest’s proposed time

If a guest can’t attend your scheduled event, they can propose a new time. You can review the proposal and if necessary, move the event.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On your calendar, tap an event with a proposal .
3.  Under "Guests," tap the proposal you want to review.
4.  To change the event to the proposed time, tap Done Done .
5.  Tap **Save**.

### Allow guests to forward invitations

If you give your guests permission to invite others, they can forward invitation emails to new guests. The new guests are added to the guest list and can invite others when they respond to the invitation.

If you don’t want guests to invite others:

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On your calendar, open the event.
3.  At the top right, tap Edit event Edit and then **Guests can**.
4.  Turn off **Invite others**.
