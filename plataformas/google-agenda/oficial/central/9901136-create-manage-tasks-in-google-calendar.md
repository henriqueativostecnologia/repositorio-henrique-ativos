# Create & manage tasks in Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In Google Calendar, you can create, view, and change tasks.

### Create a task in Calendar

**Important:** Only work or school accounts can use the "Do not disturb" and "Automatically decline meetings" options.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On your calendar, click an empty slot.
    - Or, at the top left, you can click **Create**.
3.  Click **Task**.
4.  Update your task settings.
5.  Click **Save**.

**Tip:** To find a list of all tasks from the last 365 days, at the top of your calendar, click **Pending tasks**.

### Create a task from Tasks in Calendar

1.  On your computer, select an option:
    - Open [Tasks in Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/tasks).
    - At the top right, click Tasks .
2.  Click **Create**.
3.  Enter your task details.
4.  Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- To add a task to an existing list, at the top, click **Add a task**.
- Tasks with a date also appear on your calendar.

### Mark a task as complete

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.   On the left of the task name, click Complete .
3.  At the bottom right, click **Mark completed**.

### View, edit, or delete tasks

To appear on your calendar, tasks must have a date.

##### View your tasks

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar).
2.  On the left panel, go to "My calendars."
    - To find "My calendars," you may have to click Menu .
3.  Make sure the "Tasks" box is checked.

##### Edit a task

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the task you want to edit and then  Edit task Edit task .
3.  Update any task details.
4.  Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- You can also edit tasks from the side panel or from [Tasks in Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/tasks).
- If you want to change the date and time of the next repeating task in a series, click the date.
- To edit the date and time for all tasks, click the repetition info.

##### Edit a deadline

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the task you want to edit and then Edit task Edit task .
3.  Next to "Deadline ," enter a new due date for the task.
4.  At the bottom right, click **Save**.

##### Edit the task start & end time

**Important:** If you share your calendar with someone, you can change the visibility of tasks with a start and end time. [Learn how to change event and task visibility](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/34580).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the task you want to edit and then Edit task Edit task .
3.  Next to "Start date ," choose your start and end time.
4.  At the bottom right, click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- You can't add an end time for recurring tasks.
- To convert an existing task into a task with a start and end time:
  1.  Open the task.
  2.  Click **Add time**.

##### Delete a task

1.  Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the task to update and then  Delete  Delete reminder .

##### Add an attachment

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the bottom right, click Show side panel .
3.  On the right, click Tasks  Tasks .
4.  To the right of the task, click Task options  More   and then **Add attachment**.
5.  From your Google Drive, select a file.
6.  Click **Insert**.

[Learn more about attachments in Tasks](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/17017692).

### Hide tasks on your calendar

##### Hide completed tasks

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, next to "Settings menu Settings ," click the **View switcher menu** Dropdown .
3.  In the dropdown, deselect **Show completed tasks**.

##### Hide all tasks

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left panel, under "My calendars," uncheck the box next to "Tasks."
    - To find "My calendars," click Menu .

### Find & edit pending tasks

To check all uncompleted tasks from the last 365 days:

1.  Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  While viewing a day's schedule, click **Pending tasks**.
3.  In the pop-up list, choose how to update pending tasks:
    - To edit a task, hover to the right of the task. Click Edit Edit task , make your changes, and click **Save**.
    - To complete a task, hover to the far left of the task. Click Complete Mark complete .
    - To reschedule a task, hover to the right of the task. Click Reschedule this task .

### Learn about task notifications

- If you add a date and time to your task, you get notifications at the scheduled date and time.
- Tasks with a date also appear in the Calendar app.
- For tasks without a time, notifications appear at 9 AM.
- For tasks with a deadline, you get a notification to complete the task on the day of the deadline at 9 AM in your local time.

### Learn about repeating tasks

- To repeat a task, select the date and time and then Repeat .
- Tasks with subtasks can't repeat.
- In Calendar, a certain number of upcoming instances of a repeating task will appear on the calendar grid, and as time passes, new ones will be added automatically.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Calendar, you can create, view, and change tasks.

### Create a task in Calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On your calendar, tap an empty slot and then **Task**.
    - Or:
      - You can tap Add and then **Task**.
      - At the top right, you can tap Tasks and then Add task .
3.  Update your task settings.
4.  At the top right, tap **Save**.

**Tip:** To find a list of all tasks from the last 365 days, at the top of your calendar, tap **Pending tasks**.

#### Learn about task settings

|                      |                                                                                                            |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Task setting**     | **Description**                                                                                            |
| Start date and time  | Enter the start date and the time you plan to spend on the task.                                           |
| Deadline             | Enter the date you expect to complete the task. The deadline appears on the "All day" section of Calendar. |
| Description          | Enter the date when the task must be completed.                                                            |
| Task list            | Select a list for your task.                                                                               |

### View, edit, or delete tasks

##### View your tasks

Tasks must have a date to appear in Google Calendar. To turn on Tasks: 

1.  Go to the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu .
3.  Make sure the “Tasks” box is checked.
4.  Any tasks with dates will appear on your calendar.

**Tip:** A list of all uncompleted tasks from the last 365 days are shown as “Pending tasks,” which can be found in the current day.

##### Hide all tasks

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar ,
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  Uncheck the box next to “Tasks.”

**Tip:** Hiding tasks won't turn off tasks notifications. To turn off tasks notifications, go to Calendar Settings  and then **General** and then **Tasks notifications**.

##### Edit a task

1.  ​Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap a task to update and then  Edit task  Edit task .
3.  Update task details.
4.  Tap **Save**.

##### Edit a deadline

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the task you want to edit and then Edit task Edit task .
3.  Tap the Deadline , enter a new due date for the task.
4.  At the bottom right, tap **Save**.

##### Mark a task as complete

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap a task to update and then **Mark completed**.

Completed tasks are shown on your calendar by default.

##### Hide completed tasks

To hide completed tasks crossed out in your calendar, you can adjust your settings.

**In your settings**

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap Menu Menu and then Settings and then **General**.
3.  Turn off **Show completed tasks**.

##### Report an assigned task as spam

1.  On your device, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On the calendar grid, tap the task you want to report.
3.  At the top right, tap Options More .
4.  Tap **Report as spam** and then **Report as spam**.

[Learn how to report assigned tasks as spam](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/13012543)

##### Delete a task

When you delete a task from your calendar, it’s removed completely.

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the task to delete and then More More and then **Delete** and then **Delete**.

### Find & edit pending tasks

To check all uncompleted tasks from the last 365 days:

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  While viewing a day’s schedule, tap **Pending tasks**.
3.  In the pop-up list, choose how to update pending tasks:
    - To edit a task, tap the task, tap the pencil icon Edit , make your changes, and tap **Save**.
    - To delete a task, tap the task and tap the More , then tap **Delete.**
    - To complete a task, swipe right.

### Learn about task notifications

- If you add a date and time to your task, you get notifications at the scheduled date and time.
- Tasks with a date also appear in the Calendar app.
- For tasks without a time, notifications appear at 9 AM.
- For tasks with a deadline, you get a notification to complete the task on the day of the deadline at 9 AM in your local time.

### Learn about repeating tasks

- To repeat a task, select the date and time and then Repeat .
- Tasks with subtasks can't repeat.
- In Calendar, a certain number of upcoming instances of a repeating task will appear on the calendar grid, and as time passes, new ones will be added automatically.
