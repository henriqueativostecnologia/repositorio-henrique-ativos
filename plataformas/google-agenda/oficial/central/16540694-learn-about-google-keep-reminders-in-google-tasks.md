# Learn about Google Keep reminders in Google Tasks

In the second half of 2025, Google Keep reminders will migrate to Google Tasks. New reminders will be saved as tasks. You can view, edit, and complete your reminders from:

- Calendar
- Gemini
- Keep
- Tasks

## Find Keep reminders in Calendar & Tasks

You can find your Keep reminders in:

- Your calendar grid in Calendar
- Your task list

**Tips:**

- To identify a Keep reminder, in the task details, find "From Keep."
- To return to the original Keep note, select **From Keep**.

## Get reminder notifications on your mobile device

You no longer get reminder notifications from Google Keep. To receive reminder notifications on your mobile device, install one of these apps:

- Calendar
- Tasks

After you install an app on your device, make sure to allow notifications for it. [Learn how to manage Google Tasks notification settings](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/13486720).

## Edit & delete Keep reminders

You can manage Keep reminders from several Google products.

- To edit the date and time of a reminder, make changes in the:
  - Keep note
  - Task details in Tasks or Calendar
- To edit the title of a reminder, use Tasks or Calendar.
  - If you only change the title of the Keep note, the reminder’s title doesn’t update.
- You can mark reminders as complete in the:
  - Reminder notification on your mobile device
  - Keep note
  - Task details in Tasks or Calendar

## Learn what to expect when your Keep reminders move to Tasks

In general, Keep reminders move to Tasks with the same data and are visible on your Keep note and in Tasks. Here are some key points to remember when your Keep reminders move to Tasks:

- **Location-based reminders in Keep:** You can no longer create or get location-based reminders. To find your location reminder data:
  1.  On your device, open the Tasks app Tasks .
  2.  Select a Keep reminder that was moved to Tasks.
  3.  Review the description.
- **Limits on tasks:** You can have up to 100,000 tasks. If the number of Keep reminders is more than the task limit, the oldest reminders don’t migrate to Tasks.
- **Long reminders:** If your Keep reminder is too long, its title is shortened after it migrates to Tasks.
- **Pending tasks:** You can find a list of all "Pending tasks" from the last 365 days in the "All-day" section of your calendar for the current day.
- **Repeating tasks:** The maximum interval for repeating tasks in Calendar is 1000 days, weeks, months, or years. For example, if you set a task to repeat every 2,000 days, Calendar changes it to every 1,000 days.
  - Reminders that don’t repeat and are older than a year are added to an "Old Google Keep Reminders" list.

&nbsp;

- **Tasks beyond the year 3000:** Any tasks with dates beyond the year 3000 are adjusted to the year 2900.

&nbsp;

- **Work or school accounts managed by an administrator:**
  - If you’re an administrator, make sure that the "Tasks service" is turned on for your organization. [Learn how to turn on Tasks for your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/9034719).
  - If the "Tasks service" is off, your organization’s reminder data doesn’t migrate to Tasks.
  - Reminders for users with suspended Workspace licenses don’t migrate to Tasks.
  - If your organization allows Google Takeout, you can [export your reminders data before it’s deleted](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190).
