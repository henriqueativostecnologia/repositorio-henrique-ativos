# Delete or unsubscribe from a calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you don't need a calendar anymore, you can permanently delete it or unsubscribe from it. If you think you might need the calendar later, you can hide it instead.

### Learn who can delete or unsubscribe from a calendar

- As the calendar owner, you can:
  - Delete all events from your primary calendar. However, you can’t delete your primary calendar.
  - Delete any secondary calendars. If you share a calendar with other people or groups, the calendar is removed for everyone when you delete it.
- If you’re the calendar owner, you can’t unsubscribe from a secondary calendar, but you can hide it.
  - To remove it, you must transfer ownership or delete it permanently. A secondary calendar is:
    - Any calendar that you create.
    - A group calendar that’s shared with you.
- If you’re not the calendar owner, you can unsubscribe from a calendar to remove it from your list.

### Options for removing calendars

##### Hide a calendar temporarily

This is a good option for calendars that you only check occasionally.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  On the left, find the calendar you want to hide. It may be under "My calendars" or "Other calendars."
3.  Click the calendar to hide or show it.

##### Remove a calendar from your list temporarily

When you remove a calendar from your list, you won't find it under "My calendars" or "Other calendars."

##### Remove a calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  On the left, find the calendar you want to remove. It may be under "My calendars" or "Other calendars."
3.  Hover your mouse over the calendar name and click More More .
4.  Click **Hide from list**.

##### Bring back a calendar you removed

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the left column, find the calendar you want to bring back.
4.  Click **Preview** Preview .

##### Remove a calendar from your list permanently

**Important:** This action only applies to non-owners.

When you unsubscribe from a calendar, you won't be able to find it again unless the calendar owner or someone with the "Make changes and manage sharing" permission re-shares it with you.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the left column, select the calendar you want to remove.
4.  Click **Remove calendar**.
5.  Click **Unsubscribe**.
6.  Click **Remove calendar**.

##### Restore a calendar you unsubscribed from

You can ask the calendar owner or someone with the "Make changes and manage sharing" permission to share the calendar with you again.

##### **Step 1: Ask the owner to follow these steps**

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the left column, find the shared calendar you want to restore.
4.  Click the name of the calendar.
5.  Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section, and click the Secret or Public iCal address.
6.  Copy the link and send it to the requester.

##### **Step 2: Add the calendar in your account**

When you receive the calendar link, you can add it again in your settings:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, click **Add calendar** and then **From URL**.
4.  Paste the Calendar Address link you received from the other person in the URL field.
5.  Click **Add Calendar**. You should find the calendar listed on the left side of Google Calendar.

##### Delete a secondary calendar

**Important:** This action is only available to the calendar owner.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings  Settings   and then  **Settings**.
3.  In the left column, find the calendar you want to delete.
4.  Click the name of the calendar.
5.  Click **Remove calendar**  and then **Delete ** and then ** Delete permanently. **

##### Delete all events in your primary calendar

You can't delete your primary calendar, but you can delete all of its events.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  At the top of the page, click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  Click the name of your primary calendar at the top of the list. It's usually labeled as your first and last name.
4.  At the bottom of the page, click **Delete**. It may take a few minutes to fully access Google Calendar's features after clearing your primary calendar.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

If you don't need a calendar anymore, you can permanently delete it or unsubscribe from it. If you think you might need the calendar later, you can hide it instead.

- As the calendar owner, you can:
  - Delete all events from your primary calendar. However, you can’t delete your primary calendar.
  - Delete any secondary calendars. If you share a calendar with other people or groups, the calendar is removed for everyone when you delete it.
- If you’re the calendar owner, you can’t unsubscribe from a secondary calendar, but you can hide it.
  - To remove it, you must transfer ownership or delete it permanently. A secondary calendar is:
    - Any calendar that you create.
    - A group calendar that’s shared with you.
- If you’re not the calendar owner, you can unsubscribe from a calendar to remove it from your list.

### Hide a calendar temporarily

This is a good option for calendars that you only check occasionally. You can tell that your calendar is hidden if the square next to it isn't colored in.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  In your list of calendars, tap the colored box next to a calendar name.

### Delete a calendar

To delete a calendar, open Google Calendar on a computer. [Learn how to delete a calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37188#delete_calendar).

### Remove a calendar from your list permanently

To unsubscribe from a calendar:

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Select the calendar you want to unsubscribe from.
4.  Tap **Unsubscribe**.
