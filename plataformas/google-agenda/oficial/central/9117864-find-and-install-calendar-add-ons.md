# Find and install Calendar add-ons

In the Google Workspace Marketplace, you can find a wide range of add-ons to use with Google Calendar.

## Install add-ons

**Important**: For work or school accounts, if you don’t see "Get add-ons" or can’t install an add-on, [contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Get add-ons**.
3.  From the Marketplace, install the add-on. [Learn more about installing add-ons](https://support.google.com/marketplace/answer/6067029).

**Tip**: To access the Marketplace from the side panel in Calendar, on the right, click Get add-ons Plus . [Learn more about the side panel](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/106237).

[Find Calendar add-ons](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/search/?host=calendar)

## Set up conferencing add-ons

**Important**: If the add-on doesn’t work, contact the app developer through the [Marketplace](https://support.google.com/a/answer/172391).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top left corner, click Create Add and then **Event**.
3.  Next to "Add Google Meet video conferencing," click the Down arrow Dropdown .
4.  Select the add-on you want to use.
5.  Choose your account and follow the steps to sign in to the add-on.

## Uninstall add-ons

**Important**: For work or school accounts, some apps can only be uninstalled by your administrator.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Get add-ons**.
3.  From the Marketplace, uninstall the add-on. [Learn how to uninstall add-ons](https://support.google.com/marketplace/answer/13988109).
