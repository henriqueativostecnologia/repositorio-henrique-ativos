# Add or remove alternate calendars

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can add different types of calendars, including lunar or solar, Moon phase, and sports. To add or remove alternate calendars, update your calendar settings.

There are several calendars available, like:

- **Lunar:** Based on the Moon’s phases.
- **Solar:** Based on the Sun's annual cycles.
- **Moon phases:** Based on the primary moon phases: New Moon, First Quarter, Full Moon, and Last Quarter.
- **Sports:** A schedule of upcoming sports games. You can add sports calendars for baseball, basketball, cricket, football, hockey, or rugby.

### Add or remove lunar and solar calendars

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings   and then **Settings**.
3.  Click **View options**.
4.  Under “General,” click **Alternate calendars** drop-down.
5.  Select your preferred calendar.

**Tip:** To remove an alternate calendar, select **None**.

### Add or remove Moon phases

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings   and then **Settings**.
3.  Under “Add calendar,” click **Browse calendars of interest**.
4.  Check or uncheck the box next to “Phases of the Moon.”

**[Add a Moon phase calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/settings/browsecalendars)**

### Add or remove sports calendars

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings   and then **Settings**.
3.  Under “Add calendar,” click **Browse calendars of interest**.
4.  Scroll to the “Sports” section.
5.  Select a sports category.
6.  Check or uncheck the box next to a sports team to add their game schedule to your calendar.

[Add a sports calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/settings/browsecalendars)

### Add or remove lunar and solar calendars

**Important:** Some calendars, like sports or moon phases, can only be set up on your computer. When you enable these types of calendars, they appear on your mobile device.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu   and then **Settings**.
3.  Tap **General** and then **Alternate calendars**.
4.  Select your preferred calendar.

**Tip:** To remove an alternate calendar, in “Alternate calendars,” tap **No alternate calendar**.
