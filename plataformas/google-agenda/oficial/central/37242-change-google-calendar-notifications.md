# Change Google Calendar notifications

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To stay informed about your events, you can change or set up your notification settings. You get these notifications:

- On your phone
- In your web browser
- By email

You can change your notification settings for just one event or for many events.

### Learn how notifications work on your account

- Your notification settings are personal and apply only to your account, whether you use a personal, work, or school account.
- No one else can change your notification settings, including people you share your calendar with or people can make changes to events on your calendar.
- Your specific settings don’t affect the notifications of others who have access to the calendar.
- Your calendar-level settings determine your notifications for new events, unless you customize them for a specific event.

### Learn how to turn notifications on or off

You can get event notifications:

- **By email:** You get the updates in your email.
- **As desktop notifications:** When your calendar is open, these pop up outside your web browser.
- **As alerts:** They show up within your Google Calendar window.

**Tip:** If you don’t use Calendar, you get emails when someone creates, updates, or deletes an event you’re invited to.

#### Turn notifications on or off for all events

**Important:**

- To change the settings, you need to open Calendar in a web browser, like Chrome or Safari.
- If you're asked to let “calendar.google.com” show notifications, you need to allow it.

To change how notifications work for all your events:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu Settings and then **Settings**.
    1.  On the left, under “General,” click **Notification settings**.
    2.  Under “Notification settings,” you can:
        - **Turn notifications on or off:** Click the **Notifications** drop-down and select how you want to receive notifications.
        - **Adjust the timing of snoozed notifications:** Turn on “Desktop notifications,” then click **Show snoozed notifications** and customize your timing.
        - **Only get notifications if you respond “Yes” or “Maybe” to an event:** Click the box next to “Notify me only if I have responded Yes or Maybe.”

**Tip:** You can get snooze notifications only on Chrome browser. If you have many notification times for the same event, the Snooze option shows only on the last one.

#### Turn notifications on or off for one event

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the event and then Edit event .
3.  Next to Notifications :
    - **To edit your notifications:** Choose if you want to receive a notification or an email. You can change how often you want to get alerts in advance.
    - **To add another type of notification:** Click **Add notification**.
    - **To remove a notification:** Click Remove Remove .
4.  At the top, click **Save**.

#### Turn notifications on or off for specific calendars

To change how notifications work for specific calendars you own:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, under “Settings for my calendars,” click the calendar you want to change and then **Calendar settings**.
4.  Under “Event notifications” and “All-day event notifications:”
    - **To edit your notifications:** Choose if you want to receive a notification or an email. You can change how often you want to get alerts in advance.
    - **To add another type of notification:** Click **Add notification**.
    - **To remove a notification:** Click Remove notification Remove .

#### Turn email notifications on or off for specific categories

To choose whether you receive email alerts for specific event changes:

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, under "Settings for my calendars," click the name of the calendar.
4.  Click **Other notifications**.
5.  Next to each category, such as "Changed events" or "Event responses," click the dropdown menu.
6.  Select **Email** or **None**.

**Tip:** If you manage multiple calendars, or receive a high volume of invitation emails, you can create custom Gmail filters to organize, label, or archive specific types of calendar updates. For example, you can search for RSVP declines with notes and archive routine accepted invitations. [Learn how to filter and organize Calendar notifications in Gmail](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/17366804).

### Fix issues when Calendar notifications don't work

If you don’t get event notifications, update your browser settings to allow Google Calendar to send notifications. You should also make sure that:

- Notifications for your calendar are on. Follow the steps above to check.
- Your browser can show notifications. If not, turn on desktop notifications.
- Google Calendar is still open in your web browser.

#### Google Chrome

1.  On your computer, open Google Chrome Chrome .
2.  At the top right, click More More and then **Settings**.
3.  Click **Privacy and security** and then **Site settings** and then **Notifications**.
4.  Next to “Allowed to send notifications,” click **Add**.
5.  In the “Site” field, enter “calendar.google.com”.
6.  Click **Add**.

#### Other browsers

**Important**: For the exact instructions, check the support site of your browser:

- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/push-notifications-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri40734/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/manage-website-notifications-in-microsoft-edge-0c555609-5bf2-479d-a59d-fb30a0b80b2b)

1.  On your computer, open your web browser.
2.  Go to your browser’s notification preferences.
3.  Add permissions for calendar.google.com.

**Tip:** If you have a work or school account, your admin can make changes to events on your calendar. When they make changes, you don't get a notification.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To stay informed about your events, you can change or set up your notification settings. You get these notifications:

- On your phone
- In your web browser
- By email

You can change your notification settings for just one event or for many events.

### Learn how notifications work on your account

- Your notification settings are personal and apply only to your account, whether you use a personal, work, or school account.
- No one else can change your notification settings, including people you share your calendar with or people can make changes to events on your calendar.
- Your specific settings don’t affect the notifications of others who have access to the calendar.
- Your calendar-level settings determine your notifications for new events, unless you customize them for a specific event.

### Learn how to change your notification settings

**Important**: Any changes you make on your phone also sync with your computer notifications. For example, on your phone, if you set a “1 hour before” notification, you also get a pop-up notification on your computer one hour before the event.

You can choose to:

- Change your default notifications for events that happen once and events that last all day.
- Get notifications on your device, by email, or both.

##### Change your notification settings

##### For a specific calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  At the bottom, tap Settings Settings .
4.  Select one of your calendars and then a notification.
    - **To remove a notification:** Select **No notification**.
    - **To change a notification:** Select when you want to get the notification.
    - **To add a notification:** Select **Add a notification** or **Add another notification**.

**Tip:** Any changes you make to the notifications are only for you. Event guests get notifications based on their own settings.

##### For just one event

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Select an event.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  To add a notification:
    1.  Tap **Add notification**.
    2.  Set how and when you want to get notified.
    3.  Tap **Done**.
5.  To delete the notification, tap Remove Remove .
6.  Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Any changes you make to the notifications are only for you. Event guests get notifications based on their own settings.

##### Change sound & vibration settings

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  At the bottom, tap Settings Settings and then **General**.
4.  In the “Notifications” section, tap **Calendar notifications**.
5.  Select your notification, tone, and vibration settings.

**Tip:** To change your notification sound, tap **Sound**.

### Learn how to turn off notifications

##### Turn off all notifications

If you want to turn off all the notifications for Calendar, change your calendar’s main settings. You can change the settings from your phone or computer as notifications sync across your devices.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  At the bottom, tap Settings Settings .
4.  Select one of your calendars.
5.  Tap a notification and then **No notification**.
    - Do the same for each notification.

If you have more than one account or calendar, do these steps for each one.

##### ​Turn off notifications only on your phone

**Important:** Settings can differ on each device. If the steps here don’t match, [contact your device manufacturer](https://support.google.com/android/answer/3094742).

If you turn off your phone notifications, you still get them on your computer and through email.

1.  On your Android phone, open the Settings app.
2.  Tap **Notifications** and then **App notifications** and then **Calendar**.
3.  Turn off notifications for Calendar.

##### Turn off email notifications

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  At the bottom, tap Settings Settings .
4.  Select one of your calendars and then a notification and then **No notification**.

##### Turn off notifications for another calendar app

**Important**: If you get the same notifications from another calendar app, you can turn off notifications for the other app. These steps might differ for your device. If the steps here don’t match, [contact your device manufacturer](https://support.google.com/android/answer/3094742).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Settings app.
2.  Find the “Apps” or “Application manager” section.
    - You might need to scroll or go to a different page in settings.
3.  Select the calendar app you don't want notifications for.
4.  Find the “Notifications” section.
5.  Change your settings.​
