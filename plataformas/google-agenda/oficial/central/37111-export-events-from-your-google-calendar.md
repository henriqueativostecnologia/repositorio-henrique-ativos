# Export events from your Google Calendar

You can export a copy of your events from Google Calendar to your computer. You can download all of your calendars at once or export them one at a time. The exported file is an .ics file, which you can import into other calendar apps.

## Learn what you need to export your calendar

- You can’t export from the Google Calendar app. You can only export on a computer from [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
- You must have the "Make changes and manage sharing" permission for the calendar you wish to export. [Learn how to control access to a shared calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/15716974).
- If you use a Google Calendar through work, school, or another organization, your administrator may restrict export settings. If you can’t export your calendar, [contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

## Export all calendars

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings Settings   and then  **Settings**.
3.  On the left, click **Import & export**.
4.  Under "Export," click **Export**.
    - A .zip file downloads to your computer.

**Tip:** To import the files back into Google Calendar, you must open the .zip file to access the individual .ics files.

## Export a single calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under "My calendars," find the calendar you want to export.
3.  Point to the calendar name.
4.  Click More More   and then  **Settings and sharing**.
5.  Under "Calendar settings," click **Export calendar**.
    - An .ics file downloads to your computer.

## Fix export issues

- "Feed processing error" message:

  - If you get this error message when you use the "Public address in iCal format" link, use the "Secret address in iCal format" instead.
  - If you don't find the secret address, you can’t export the calendar. [Learn how to sync your calendar with computer programs](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37648).
