# Get started with Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

With Google Calendar, you can:

- Schedule meetings and events.
- Assign tasks.
- Share your schedule.
- Create multiple calendars.

### Learn about supported browsers for Calendar

**Important:** There is no standalone desktop app for Google Calendar. Instead, use a supported web browser to open Google Calendar.

To download a supported browser, select it from the list:

- [Google Chrome](http://www.google.com/chrome/)
- [Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/firefox/new/)
- [Safari](http://www.apple.com/safari/)
- [Microsoft Edge](http://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge)

**Tip:** For the best experience, update your browser to the latest version and turn on JavaScript and cookies.

### Open Calendar in your browser

1.  From a web browser, visit [calendar.google.com](http://calendar.google.com).
2.  Sign in to your Google Account.
3.  To change your settings, at the top right corner, click Settings menu Settings .

[Open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)

**Tip:** While you can’t download and install Calendar on your computer, you can use it offline. [Learn how to use Calendar offline](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/1340696).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

With Google Calendar, you can:

- Schedule meetings and events.
- Assign tasks.
- Share your schedule.
- Create multiple calendars.

### Get Google Calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Play Store .
2.  Find the Calendar app Calendar .
3.  Tap **Install**.
4.  Open the app and sign in to your Google Account.

**Tip**: To use Calendar in your mobile web browser, visit [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).
