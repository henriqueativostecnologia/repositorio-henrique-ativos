# Manage birthdays on your calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can keep track of upcoming birthdays using Google Calendar. You can create, hide, or change the color of birthdays on your calendar.

- The birthday information syncs with your calendar when you add a person and include their birthday in [Google Contacts](https://contacts.google.com).
- You can edit or remove birthday information about the person in the Contacts app for Android or [Google Contacts](https://contacts.google.com).
- You can hide all birthdays on your calendar. This hides both manually created birthdays and those imported from Google Contacts.
- If you use Calendar on an Android device, iPhone, or iPad: You can edit birthdays that are linked to Google Contacts.
  - If you edit a birthday in Calendar, the same edits are applied in Google Contacts.
- When you delete a birthday from Calendar, it doesn’t affect the information stored in Google Contacts.
- If you turn off “Sync from Contacts” in calendar settings it deletes all synced events in Calendar which includes:
  - Birthdays
  - Important dates
  - Any changes made to these events

[Add or edit birthdays in Google Contacts](https://contacts.google.com/)

### Hide all birthdays on your calendar

You can hide all birthdays on your calendar. This hides both manually created birthdays and those imported from Google Contacts.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  On the left, find "My calendars."
    - To expand it, click **My calendars**.
3.  Hover your mouse over "Birthdays."
4.  Uncheck the box.

### Change the color of birthdays on your calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  On the left, find "My calendars."
    - To expand it, click **My calendars**.
3.  Hover your mouse over "Birthdays."
4.  Click Options More .
5.  Select a new color.

### Turn off birthdays from one of your accounts

If you have multiple Google Accounts, you can hide birthdays imported from Contacts associated with one of your accounts.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar).
2.  At the top right, click Settings  and then **Settings**.
3.  Under "Settings for my Calendars," click **Birthdays**.
4.  Under "Sync from Contacts," next to the Google Accounts you want to hide, uncheck the boxes.

**Tip:** By default, sync is on for the Google Accounts on your calendar. If you uncheck any of the Google Accounts, birthdays imported from Contacts are removed from your Calendar but remain in Contacts.

[Add or edit birthdays in Google Contacts](https://contacts.google.com/)

### Create a birthday event on your calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the bottom right, tap Create Add and then **Event**.
3.  At the top, swipe to and select **Birthday**.
4.  Add a name and birth date.
    - **Tip:** You receive a reminder notification one week before, and on the day of the birthday.
      - To remove an existing notification, next to it, tap Remove .
      - To add other notifications, tap **Add notification**.
5.  At the top right, tap **Save**.

### Hide all birthdays on your calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  To the left of “Birthdays,” tap the box.

**Tip:** Calendars with an empty box are already hidden from your calendar.

### Turn off birthdays from one of your accounts

If you have multiple accounts, you can hide birthdays imported from Contacts associated with one of your accounts.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Tap **Birthdays**.
4.  To hide an account, turn off **Sync from Contacts**.

**Tip:** By default, sync is on for the Google Accounts on your calendar. If you uncheck any of the Google Accounts, birthdays imported from Contacts are removed from your Calendar but remain in Contacts.

### Change the color of birthdays on your calendar

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Tap **Birthdays**.
4.  Select a new color.
