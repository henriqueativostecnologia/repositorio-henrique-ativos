# Add a calendar to your website

You can embed your Google Calendar on your website to easily share your schedule and events with visitors.

## Embed a calendar on your website

**Important:** Your calendar is only visible to people you share it with. To make your calendar visible to everyone, make your calendar public. [Learn how to create and manage a public Google calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37083).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
    - You can only get the embed code from a computer, not the Google Calendar app.
2.  In the top right, click Settings menu Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left side of the screen, click the name of the calendar you want to embed.
4.  Click **Integrate calendar**.
5.  Under "Embed code," click **Customize**.
6.  Choose options for your calendar display.
    - Select dimensions where the width and height are each at least 500.
    - If you turn off Navigation buttons, people can only use keyboard shortcuts to navigate your calendar.
7.  At the top, next to the embed code, click Copy to clipboard .
8.  On your website, paste the embed code.

**Tip:** To subscribe to your embedded calendar, visitors can click **Add to Google Calendar** at the bottom left of the calendar.

## Let site visitors save a calendar event

**Important:** To let anyone save your calendar event, make your calendar public. [Learn how to create and manage a public Google calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37083).

To allow anyone to save events to their calendars, add a Google Calendar button to your website.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
    - You can only make changes to your calendar visibility from a computer, not the mobile app.
2.  On a public calendar, create or edit an event.
3.  At the top right, click More actions and then **Publish event**.
4.  In the "Publish event" window, copy the HTML code displayed.
5.  On your website, paste the HTML code.

## Get a public link for an event

To share a single event, get a public link for the event. Anyone with the link can get details about the event, like the time, date, and description.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
    - You can only get the event link from a computer, not the Google Calendar app.
2.  On a public calendar, open the event you want to share.
3.  At the top right, click More actions and then **Publish event**.
4.  In the "Publish event" window, copy the link to share with others.

**Tips:**

- Links from the "Publish event" feature work best on desktop web browsers.
- If you open these links in the Google Calendar app with a mobile device, the event details may not load correctly. It may also show as a blank event.
- To check the event on a mobile device:
  1.  Open the link in a web browser like Chrome.
  2.  Save it to your calendar.
