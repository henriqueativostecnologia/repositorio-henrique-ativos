# Set your working hours

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

If you have a work or school account, you can show people your availability throughout the week in Google Calendar. You can also show when you're out of office. This can help you and others schedule meetings that respect your actual working hours.

If you don't get an option to set your working hours, your administrator may not have it on for your organization. [Learn how to contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

**Tip:** If your account is eligible, you can also choose a working location. This helps collaborators find suitable meeting times and know your local time zone. [Learn how to set your working location](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/18616218).

### Learn more about working hours

- When you add your working hours, the start time must be before the end time.
- If you travel, set a working location, or change your time zone, your working hours automatically adjust to the local time in your working location.
- Working hours can only be set as a recurring weekly schedule. For temporary changes like appointments, update your regular working hours, then revert them when your schedule returns to normal.

**Important:** To set your working hours, open [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com/) from a web browser on your computer.

### Show when you’re out of office

1.  Open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the bottom right, tap Create Add and then **Out of office**.
3.  Select your out-of-office dates.
    - If you're only out of office for part of the day, deselect **All day**.
    - To schedule out-of-office events that repeat, below the day and time you select, tap **Does not repeat** and then choose a frequency.
4.  **Optional:** Change your decline settings and edit your decline message.
5.  Tap **Save**.

### Show your Calendar status in Gmail

If you use Gmail for work or school and show when you’re out of office:

- Gmail updates your calendar status.
- When someone emails you, your current status is sent to them.

The “Out of office” event must last the entire day or extend past your regular working hours.

**Important:**

- To find your current status, the sender must have access to your calendar.
- To make sure others have access to your calendar, you must share your calendar with them. [Learn how to share your calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082).

### Turn on working hours

**Important:** Google Calendar may suggest working hours based on your time zone, the work patterns in your country, and your schedule. You can accept, decline, or edit these suggestions. [Learn how to use Google Calendar in different time zones](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37064).

To let people know you're unavailable when they try to schedule a meeting with you, turn on working hours. You can only turn on working hours from a computer.

1.   On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu  and then **Settings**.
3.  On the left, under "General," click **Working hours**.
    - **Tip:** If your administrator has turned on working location, click **Working hours & location**.
4.  In the "Working hours" section, click **Enable working hours**.
5.  Select the days and hours that you work.
6.  You can split the hours you work based on when you're available:
    - To add more than one time period to your workday, like an overnight shift, click Add add .
    - To remove a time period, click Remove Remove .

### Show when you’re out of office

Your Google Calendar declines all meetings when you're not available.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top left, click **Create ** and then ** Out of office**.
3.  Select your out-of-office dates.
    - If you're only out of office for part of the day, deselect **All day**.
    - To schedule out-of-office events that repeat, below the day and time you select, click the dropdown menu Dropdown next to "Does not repeat" and then choose a frequency.
4.  **Optional:** Change your decline settings and edit your decline message.
5.  Click **Save**.
