# Add Google Calendar to your home screen

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To check your upcoming events and meetings from the home screen without opening the Google Calendar app, add a widget.

### Add the Calendar widget to your home screen

**Important:** Some of these steps may differ based on your Android phone or tablet.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, touch and hold the home screen.
2.  Tap **Widgets** and then **Calendar**.
3.  Touch and hold one of these widgets:
    - **Calendar schedule:** View your tasks and upcoming events.
    - **Calendar month view:** Show your schedule one month at a time.
4.  Drag the widget to an empty space on the home screen, then release it.

### Resize the Calendar widget

**Important:** In the Android widget, you can’t resize the button for Create Add .

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, touch and hold the widget on your home screen, then release it.
    - An outline with dots appears on the sides of the widget.
2.  To resize the widget, drag the dots.
3.  When you're done, tap outside the widget.

### Move the Calendar widget

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, touch and hold the widget.
2.  Drag the widget to an empty space on the home screen, then release it.

### Remove the Calendar widget

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, touch and hold the widget.
2.  Drag the widget up to “Remove,” then release it.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

To check your upcoming events and meetings from the home screen without opening the Google Calendar app, add a widget.

### Add the Calendar widget to your home screen

**Important:**

- You can add widgets on an iPhone or iPad running iOS 14 and up.
- To add the widget, [download the Calendar app](https://apps.apple.com/ky/app/google-calendar/id909319292).
- If you recently installed the Calendar app, you may need to open the app before the widget appears in the list of widgets.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the home screen.
2.  At the top left, tap **Add** Add .
3.  In the list of widgets, tap **Calendar**.
4.  To select the widget size, swipe right or left.
5.  Tap **Add Widget**.
6.  At the top right, tap **Done**.

### Add the Calendar widget to Today View

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, swipe right on the home screen until you find a list of widgets.
2.  Scroll to the bottom and tap **Edit**.
3.  At the top left, tap Add Add .
4.  In the list of widgets, tap **Calendar**.
5.  To select the widget size, swipe right or left.
6.  Tap **Add Widget**.
7.  At the top right, tap **Done**.

**Tip:** To hide your calendar info when the screen is locked, go to **Settings** and then **Face ID & Passcode** or **Touch ID & Passcode**, then turn off **Today View and Search**.
