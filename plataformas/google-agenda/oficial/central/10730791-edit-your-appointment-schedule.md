# Edit your appointment schedule

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To adjust your availability or make other changes, you can edit your appointment schedule.

### Make changes to your appointment schedule

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under "Booking pages," hover over the appointment schedule you want to edit.
3.  Click Options and then **Edit**.
4.  Make changes to your appointment schedule.

### Add availability to an appointment schedule

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the calendar, click any open time slot.
3.  Click **Appointment schedule**.
4.  Add a date and time.
5.  Click **Add availability to an existing schedule**.
6.  Select the schedule where you want to add your availability.
7.  Click **Add to existing schedule**.

### Show or hide your appointment schedules on your calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under "Booking pages," hover over the appointment schedule you want to show or hide.
3.  Click Options
    - **To show an appointment schedule:** Click **Show on calendar**.
    - **To hide an appointment schedule:** Click **Hide from calendar**.

**Tip:** If your appointment schedule is hidden, you can:

- Still find booked appointments on your calendar.
- Create a new schedule or add time to an available one.
- Still allow others to book appointments with you.

### Learn about schedule updates, visibility & emails

##### Learn how your appointment schedule syncs with your calendar

- Your appointment schedule and booked appointments automatically display on your calendar.
- When you select "Check calendars for availability," Google Calendar avoids schedule conflicts.
- If you RSVP “Yes” or “Maybe” to an event in Google Calendar, that block of time won’t display as open on your booking page.
- To avoid conflicts with other events, your booking page availability automatically updates.

##### Learn how appointment schedule visibility works

- Anyone with edit permission on your calendar can edit the schedule and find new bookings on your calendar.
- Anyone with your booking page link can check for your available time slots.
- If you share your booking page, but not your calendar, no one can find events on your calendar.
- On your booking pages, if you turn off “Check calendars for availability,” all appointment time blocks show as open. However, past appointment bookings and non-appointment events accepted through booking pages remain.
  - Even if the feature is off, others can book each appointment slot on a booking page only once.

##### Get confirmation emails for bookings & cancellations

##### Appointment bookings

- **For schedules on the primary calendar:** You, co-hosts, and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification.
- **For schedules on the secondary calendar:** The co-hosts and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification.

##### Appointment cancellations

- **For canceled appointments on the primary calendar:** You, co-hosts, and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification.
- **For canceled appointments on the secondary calendar:** The co-hosts and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification. If you aren’t a co-host, set up your secondary calendar to receive email notifications for cancellations. [Learn how to modify Google Calendar notifications](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37242).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To adjust your availability or make other changes, you can edit your appointment schedule.

### Show or hide your appointment schedules on your calendar

**Important:** To make changes to your appointment schedule, go to Google Calendar on your web browser.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu .
3.  Tap your **Appointment schedule**.
4.  Tap Options and then **Show on calendar** or **Hide from calendar**.
