# Create a recurring event

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

When you create an event in Google Calendar, you can make it repeat on certain days.

### Set up a new repeating event

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r).
2.  In the top left, click Create** ** Add .
3.  Add the event title and any other details.
4.  Next to "Does not repeat,"** **click the Down arrow  Down arrow .
5.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat, and when you want the event that repeats to end.
6.  At the top right, click **Save**.

**Tip:** Repeating events in Google Calendar have a limit of 730 occurrences.

### Make an existing event repeat 

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r).
2.  Click an event  and then  Edit  Edit .
3.  Next to "Does not repeat,"** **click the Down arrow  Down arrow .
4.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat, and when you want the event that repeats to end.
5.  In the top right, click **Save**.

### Make changes to events that repeat

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r).
2.  Click an event  and then  Edit  Edit .
3.  Make any changes, then at the top, click **Save**.
4.  Choose which events in the series the updates apply to.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

When you create an event in Google Calendar, you can make it repeat on certain days.

### Set up a new repeating event

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the bottom right corner, tap Create Insert and then **Event**.
3.  Add a title to your event and tap **Done**.
4.  Choose the event's date and time.
5.  Under the time, tap **Does not repeat**.
6.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat.
7.  In the top right, tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Repeating events in Google Calendar have a limit of 730 occurrences.

### Make an existing event repeat

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the event you want to update.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  Under the time, tap **Does not repeat**.
5.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat.
6.  In the top right, tap **Save**.

### Make changes to repeating events

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the event you want to update.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  Make updates to your event.
5.  In the top right, tap **Save**.
6.  Choose which events in the series you want to update:
    - **This event:** This event will be updated, but the details for all the other recurring events will stay the same.
    - **All events:** This event, and all events moving forward will be updated. Any events from the past won't be updated.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

When you create an event in Google Calendar, you can make it repeat on certain days.

### Set up a new repeating event

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the bottom right corner, tap Create  Insert and then **Event**.
3.  Add a title to your event and tap **Done**.
4.  Choose the event's date and time.
5.  Under the time, tap **More options and then Does not repeat**.
6.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat.
7.  In the top right, tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Repeating events in Google Calendar have a limit of 730 occurrences.

### Make an existing event repeat

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the event you want to update.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  Under the time, tap **More options and then Does not repeat**.
5.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat.
6.  In the top right, tap **Save**. 

### Make changes to repeating events

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the event you want to update.
3.  Tap Edit Edit . 
4.  Make updates to your event.
5.  In the top right, tap **Save**. 
6.  Choose which events in the series you want to update:
    - **This event:** This event will be updated, but the details for all the other recurring events will stay the same.
    - **All following events:** This event, and all events moving forward will be updated. Any events from the past won't be updated.
