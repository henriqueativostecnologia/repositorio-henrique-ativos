# Compare premium features for appointment schedules

**Important:** For Workspace Frontline, Essentials, or an older Google Workspace subscription that’s no longer offered, you can’t create appointment schedules.

With a personal Google Account or Workspace Business Starter plan, you can create a single booking page to let others schedule appointments on your calendar.

Some features require an eligible Google Workspace or Google One subscription. Use the chart to understand the features available for a subscription type.
