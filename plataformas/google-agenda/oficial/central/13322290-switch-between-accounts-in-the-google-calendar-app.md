# Switch between accounts in the Google Calendar app

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can find and manage events from different accounts in the Calendar app.

### Get all features with your Google Account

When you use your Google Account, you can:

- Add people to your events.
- Get invites to events.
- Use different colors for events and calendars.
- Add files or video calls to your events.
- Sync your calendar on all your devices.
- Create tasks.

#### Learn about non-Google accounts

If you use a local or third-party account with Calendar, some features might not work.

+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| **Account type**                                   | **Description**                                    | **How it works in Google Calendar**                |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| **Local account**                                  | A calendar that’s saved on your phone or created   | If you turn on this account, you can get events    |
|                                                    | by another app on your device.                     | from its calendar in the Google Calendar app.      |
|                                                    |                                                    |                                                    |
|                                                    |                                                    | You can’t switch to this account.                  |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| **Third-party account**                            | A calendar managed by a different calendar         | You can get events and switch to this account from |
|                                                    | service, such as Outlook or Apple Calendar.        | your profile picture to create events on its       |
|                                                    |                                                    | calendar.                                          |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

### Before you begin

- To switch accounts in the Calendar app, set your screen orientation to portrait mode.
- Your current account is at the top of the Menu . Other accounts you turn on are below it.
- To show or hide all calendars from accounts you turn on:
  1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
  2.  At the top left, tap Menu .
  3.  Select the calendars you want to display in the grid.

### Switch accounts

**Important:**

- You can’t switch to a local account.
- Turn on a third-party account in Calendar app settings to switch to it. [Learn how to turn on non-Google accounts](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/13322290#enable-non-google-account).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your profile picture .
3.  In the menu, you can:
    - **Select a different account:** If you have many accounts, you can pick another account that you turn on.
    - **Open your Google Account settings:** Tap **Manage your Google Account**.
    - **Switch between your work and personal profiles:** If you have a work or school account with an Android Work Profile, select:
      - **Switch to work profile**
      - **Switch to personal profile**
    - **Manage accounts on your device:** To add, remove, or update accounts, tap **Manage accounts on this device**.
    - **Add another account:** You can add another account to the Calendar app. When you add a new Google Account, it becomes the one you use.

**Tips:**

- Your profile photo shows which account you use.
  - Before you switch to a different account, save any changes you make.
  - If an account can’t do something, Calendar automatically switches to an account that can.
- To manage any personal calendars you have on your work profile, switch to the Calendar app associated with your personal profile.

### Turn on a non-Google account

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu and then **Settings**.
3.  Tap **Manage accounts**.
4.  Enable the non-Google account.

#### Learn about account switch notifications

- When you create an event in a non-Google account in Calendar, you get a notification about what that account can’t do.
- This message shows when you use an account that doesn’t support common Calendar features, such as colors, tasks, and syncing.
- To switch to your Google Account and use all Calendar features, in the notification, tap **Switch account**.
