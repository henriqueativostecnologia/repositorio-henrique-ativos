# Use labels to track calendar entries

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To review how you spend your time, you can assign labels to your calendar entries and track them in Time Insights. [Learn more about Time Insights](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/10738043).

**Important:** The labels you assign to your events are visible to you and anyone with "Make changes and manage sharing" and "Make changes to events" access to your calendar.

### What you need to view labels in Time Insights

- A computer
- Google Calendar on a work or school account
- Permission to “manage sharing” access on other people’s calendars. [Learn how to share your calendar with someone else](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082?hl=en).

**Tip:** If you don't have the Time Insights option, your admin may have turned it off for your organization. [Learn more about “Who is my administrator?](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960)”

###  Create & assign a label

#### For a specific event:

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Open an event.
3.  Click Edit Edit .
4.  Next to the calendar name, select the current color.
5.  Create a new label or add an existing label.
6.  Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- To create or assign a label, you can right-click an event invitation.
- You can create up to 200 labels.

#### From the Time Insights panel:

1.  On your computer, [open Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, click More insights .
    - **Tip:** You may have to open the Menu .
3.  On the right, next to “Time breakdown,” click Edit Edit .
    - **Tip:** You can also add or edit labels below the Time breakdown chart.
4.  In the “labels” popup, you can:
    - **Create a new label:** At the bottom left, click Add a new label .
    - **Assign a label to existing meetings:** In the field that says “Add a label,” enter your label name.
    - **Delete a label:** Next to your preferred label, click Delete .
5.  Click **Save**.

### Assign a label to an event

**Important:** Before you can assign a label to an event on your device, you must first [create the label on your computer](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/12377581?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop#create_and_assign_a_color_label).

1.  On your device, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Create a new event or tap on an existing event.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  Near the bottom, select your current label.
5.  Select an existing label.
6.  Tap **Save**.
