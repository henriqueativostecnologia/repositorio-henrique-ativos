# Use Google Calendar in a mobile browser

You can use Google Calendar in your mobile browser.

## Open Calendar in a mobile browser

**Important**: If Calendar does not open in your browser, try another browser or download the Calendar app. [Learn about supported browsers for Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/15494354).

1.  On your phone or tablet, open your browser.
2.  Go to [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).
3.  Sign in to your Google Account.

**Tips**:

- When you're offline, you can view events synced with Calendar. To create or edit events in a mobile browser, connect to the internet.
- After you sign in to Calendar on a mobile browser, data may be stored on your device to improve performance. From your browser’s settings, you might be able to access and clear this data. For example, you can [change your website content settings on Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/114662). To learn how to clear browser data, visit your browser's help center.

## Use Calendar in a mobile browser

**Important**: The mobile web version of Calendar does not support notifications. To get notifications about events, install the Calendar app. [Learn how to install the Calendar app](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/2465776).

#### Switch between day and month view

- To switch between day and month view, at the top of the page, tap **Day** or **Month**.
- To return to the current date, tap **Today**.

#### Create a new event

1.  On your phone or tablet, open a browser.
2.  Go to [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).
3.  In the top right corner, tap Create Create .
4.  Add details about your event.
5.  To invite someone to your event, under "Add a person to this meeting," enter their email address.
6.  Tap **Save**.

#### Edit & delete an event

1.  On your phone or tablet, open a browser.
2.  Go to [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).
3.  Open the event you want to edit.
    - To delete the event, tap **Delete ** and then  **Ok**.
    - To edit the event, tap **Edit**.
4.  After you've made changes, tap **Save**.

#### Hide or show a calendar

1.  On your phone or tablet, open a browser.
2.  Go to [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com).
3.  At the bottom right corner, tap **Settings**.
4.  To show or hide a calendar, tap the box next to it.
5.  Tap **Save**.

## Troubleshoot issues with Calendar in a mobile browser

If you get an error:

- On your phone or tablet, download a different browser.
- In your mobile browser, turn on cookies.
- In your device's app store, install the Calendar app.
