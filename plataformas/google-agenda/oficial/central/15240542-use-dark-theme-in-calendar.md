# Use Dark theme in Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Calendar, you can switch the default bright background to a dark background.

### Turn on Dark theme in the Calendar app

**Important:** You can use Dark theme in the Calendar app on Android 7 and up. [Learn how to check your Android version](https://support.google.com/android/answer/7680439).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open Calendar Calendar .
2.  Tap Menu and then **Settings** and then **General**.
3.  On the “General” page, tap **Theme** and then **Dark**.

**Tips:**

- To change the theme of your Calendar widget and notifications, [manage your Android screen and display settings](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9730472).
- **For Android 10 and up:** If "battery saver mode" or "Dark theme" is enabled in your system settings, widgets and the Calendar automatically switch to a Dark theme.
- **For Android 9 and lower:** Widgets are always in Light theme.

## Version: Computer

In Google Calendar, you can switch the default bright background to a dark background.

### Turn on Dark theme in Calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu and then **Appearance**.
3.  In the “Appearance” window, select **Dark**.
4.  To apply the changes, click **Done**.

**Tips:**

- If your background is set to “Light,” you can change the color set.
- If your background is set to “Dark” or “Device default,” you can’t change the color set.

### Related resources

- [Change your Calendar settings](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/6084644)

## Version: iPhone & iPad

In Google Calendar, you can switch the default bright background to a dark background.

### Turn on Dark Mode in your device settings

**Important:**

- You can use Dark Mode on iOS 13 and up.
- When you update your device settings, all apps on your iPhone or iPad change to match the background theme.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, [turn on Dark Mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT210332).
2.  Tap Calendar Calendar .
