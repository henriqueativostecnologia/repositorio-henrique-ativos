# Share your calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** If your calendar is for your work or school, your administrator decides if you can share it. If you can't share a calendar, [learn how to contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

In Google Calendar, you can share any calendar you own or one you have permission to share. When you share your calendar with someone:

- You can control what they can find and do on your calendar.
- They get an email. To find your calendar, they need to click the link in the email.
- You can stop sharing your calendar whenever you want.

### Share your calendar with people or groups

**Important:** If you want to share a calendar that someone else owns, that person must give you the “Make changes and manage sharing” permission.

#### Step 1: Find the calendar you want to share

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left side of Calendar, click **My calendars**.

[Share your calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r/settings/calendar/primary?card=sharing)

#### Step 2: Choose who to share your calendar with

1.  To the right of the calendar, click More and then **Settings and sharing**.
2.  On the left, click **Shared with**.
3.  Under “Shared with,” click **Add people and groups**.
4.  Add the email address of the person or Google group.

#### Step 3: Choose what people can do with your calendar

Decide how much access you want to give to other people:

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Permission**                                                                | **Definition**                                                                |
+===============================================================================+===============================================================================+
| **See only free/busy (hide details)**                                         | People can only find out when you're busy on your calendar. They can’t find   |
|                                                                               | the names or details about events or tasks on your calendar.                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **See event details**                                                         | People can find all details, like event names, times, places, and             |
|                                                                               | descriptions.                                                                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Make changes (see private events as free/busy)**                            | People can edit non-private events, but can't edit private events.            |
|                                                                               |                                                                               |
|                                                                               | If a private event is “Busy,” it shows up as a busy block on your calendar    |
|                                                                               | with no event details.                                                        |
|                                                                               |                                                                               |
|                                                                               | If a private event is “Free,” it does not appear on your calendar.            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Make changes and see event details**                                        | People can edit events on your calendar and find who else it’s shared with.   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Make changes and manage sharing**                                           | People have full control of events or tasks with start and end time on your   |
|                                                                               | calendar and can share your calendar with others.                             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

[Learn how to choose what others can do on your calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/15716974).

#### Step 4: Send the calendar invite

To give others access, click **Send**.

The people you share your calendar with get an email. To add your calendar, they must click the link in the email.

If they can’t find the email:

- Make sure you have the correct email address.
- Ask the person to check their spam or trash mail folder.
- Ask the person to search for your email.
- Take them off the sharing list, then add them again.

### Let everyone find your calendar

You can share your calendar with:

- Anyone or the public

- People in your work, school, or other organization

##### Share your calendar with anyone

When you make your calendar public, people can:

- Find your calendar on the internet or in search results.
- Use your calendar with other apps.
- Subscribe to your calendar and check it in Google Calendar.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, find the “My calendars” section.
    - If you can’t find all your calendars, click **My calendars**.
3.  To the right of the calendar, click More and then **Settings and sharing**.
4.  On the left, click **Access permissions for events**.
5.  Under “Access permissions for events,” select **Make available to public**.
6.  Next to a setting, in the drop-down, select the access level.

##### Share your calendar with your work or school

**Important:** Your administrator can have special permissions that allow them to find your calendar and all event details, even if you don't share your calendar. To learn more, [check with your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

If you use Calendar for your work or school, you can let everyone in your organization find your calendar. When you do this:

- People in your organization can find and subscribe to your calendar.
- People outside your organization can’t find your calendar.
- If you invite someone outside your organization to a meeting, they can still find info about that meeting.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, find the “My calendars” section.
    - If you can’t find all your calendars, click **My calendars**.
3.  To the right of the calendar, click More and then **Settings and sharing**.
4.  On the left, click **Access permissions for events**.
5.  Under “Access permissions for events,” select **Make available for {company name}**.
6.  Next to a setting, in the drop-down, select the access level.

### Stop sharing your calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, find the “My calendars” section.
    - If you can’t find all your calendars, click **My calendars**.
3.  To the right of the calendar, click More and then **Settings and sharing**.
4.  Turn off share settings for the calendar.
    - **For public sharing:** Under “Access permissions for events,” turn off **Make available to public**.
    - **For work or school sharing:** Under “Access permissions for events,” turn off **Make available for {company name}**.
    - **For specific people or groups:** Under “Shared with,” next to the name of the person or group you want to stop sharing access with, click Remove Remove .
