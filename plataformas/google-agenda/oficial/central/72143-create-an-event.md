# Create an event

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can create Google Calendar events from your computer or device.

### Ways to create an event

##### Click an empty time in your calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  **Optional:** If you want to add guests to your event, on the left click **Search for people** and start typing the names of your guests.
3.  Click any time on the calendar that doesn't already have an event scheduled.
4.  Add a title to the event, and any event details.
5.  Click **Save**.

**Tip:** To create a longer event, drag your mouse down the page while you're selecting the time.

##### Click the Create button

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  **Optional:** If you want to add guests to your event, on the left click **Search for people** and start typing the names of your guests.
3.  In the top left corner, click Create Add .
4.  Add a title to the event, and any event details.
5.  At the top of the page, click **Save**.

##### Quickly create an event

You can quickly create an event for a specific time if you see "Add title and time" in the text box.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  Click the space next to date you want to add an event to.
3.  Add a title and time for your event.
4.  Click **Save**. Calendar will automatically create an event at the time you set.

**Examples:**

- Tennis practice at 5pm
- Dinner at 7pm

**Tip**: You can use the keyboard shortcut **Shift + C** to quickly create an event in any view.

### Create an event in a shared calendar

**Important:** To create events in a shared calendar, you can:

- Create a new calendar and share it. [Learn how to create a new calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37095?hl=en).
- Share an existing calendar. [Learn how to share your calendar with someone](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/37082).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top left, click Create Add .
3.  Add a title and any event details.
4.  At the bottom, click on the calendar name next to Calendar Event .
5.  Next to the calendar name, click the Down arrow Down arrow . A list of all the calendars you can create events for appears.
6.  Select your preferred calendar.
7.  Click **Save.** The event appears with both the calendar name and the name of the person who created the event.

**Tip:** You can't create events for Birthday or Holiday calendars.

### Event options

##### Create an all-day event

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.   In the top left corner, click Create Add .
3.  Add a description for your event.
4.  Under the date and time, check the box next to **All day**.
5.  Click **Save**.

You'll see all-day events at the top of the page for that day.

##### Create a repeating event

You can create a repeating event. Learn how to [create and manage repeating events](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37115).

##### Add video meetings

If you use Calendar for work or school, a [Meet video meeting](https://support.google.com/meet/) is added when you add invitees and click **Save**.

To manually add a video meeting, select **Add Google Meet video conferencing**. Click **Save**.

**Tip**: If you use Calendar for work or school, you can add third-party conferencing apps from the G Suite Marketplace. [Get extra Calendar tools](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/9117864).

##### Add live streaming

If you use Calendar and Google Meet for work or school, you can add view-only live streaming to an event for up to 100,000 people to watch. [Learn how to live stream a video meeting](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308630).

##### Pick a color for your event

When you're creating an event, you can choose the color that event will show as in your calendars. People won't be able to see the color you set for events unless you've given them permission to make changes on your calendar. [Learn how to manage your calendar permissions](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  Right click an existing event. Then, pick a color.

### Edit an event you've created

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  Click on the event you want to edit. If you see Edit event , click it.
3.  Make changes to your event.
4.  At the top of the page, click **Save**.

### Let guests edit your event

You can let your guests edit event details, such as adding rooms, adjusting the time, or adding attachments.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click an event and then Edit event .
3.  At the right, under “Guest permissions,” check “Modify event.”
4.  Click **Save**.

**Tip:** When you check “Guests can modify event,” they can manage the event as you can.

### Troubleshoot issues

##### Can't save events

Try these steps in order:

1.  Refresh the page.
2.  Clear your browser's [cache and cookies](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050).
3.  Make sure [Javascript is turned on](https://support.google.com/answer/23852).
4.  Sign out of Google Calendar.
5.  Close your browser.
6.  Open your browser and sign in to [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) again. Make sure to use "https" in the web address instead of "http."

If you're using a third-party app to sync your calendar, try turning off sync before signing back in again.

##### When I try to add a guest, my contact does not show up as a suggestion

Only contacts with a valid email address show up as suggestions.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can create Google Calendar events from your computer or device.

### Create an event

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap Create Insert and then Event Event .
3.  Optional:
    - **If you invite guests,** add them to the event.
    - **If you want to change which calendar you add an event to,** go to the top and tap the calendar name.
    - **If you want to let guests edit your event, invite others, or view the full guest list:**
      - Under "Guests can,"turn on the access levels you want to give guests.
4.  Swipe up to edit event details like:
    - Title
    - Location
    - Who's invited
    - Event visibility

      **Important:** If you don't share your calendar, you won't find visibility settings for your event.
5.  Tap **Save.**

### Create an event in a shared calendar

**Important:** To create events in a shared calendar, you must first:

- On a computer, create a new calendar and share it. [Learn how to create a new calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37095?hl=en).
- On a computer, share an existing calendar. [Learn how to share your calendar with someone](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/37082).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On the bottom right, tap Create Add and then Event Event .
3.  Add a titleand any event details.
4.  Tap on the calendar name next to Calendar event . A list of all the calendars you can create events for appears.
5.  Tap your preferred calendar.
6.  At the top right, tap **Save.** The event appears with both the calendar name and the name of the person who created the event.

**Tip:** You can’t create events for Birthday or Holiday calendars.

#### Create an event by tapping an empty time

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu and then **Day, 3 Day,** or **Week** and then an empty time.
3.  To change the event duration, tap and drag the top or bottom circles of the event.
4.  Add an event title, invite guests, or add rooms.
5.  To find a time guests are available, swipe down or tap **View schedules.**
6.  To edit event details like the location, notifications, and descriptions, at the top of the event next to "Save," swipe up.
7.  Tap **Save.**

**Tips:**

- To switch categories from “Event” to “Task,” tap **Task**.
- When you switch categories in the middle of the creation process, the Calendar app retains as much of the data as possible.

### Event options

##### Create an all-day event

1.  On your Android phone, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap Create Add and then **Event**.
3.  Add a description for your event.
4.  Tap the box next to All day.
5.  Tap **Save**.

You'll see all-day events at the top of the page for that day.

##### Create a repeating event

You can create a repeating event. Learn how to [create and manage repeating events](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37115).

##### Add video meetings

If you use Calendar for work or school, a [Meet video meeting](https://support.google.com/meet/) is added when you add invitees and tap **Save**.

To manually add a video meeting, tap **Add video conferencing** and then **Save**.

**Tip**: If you use Calendar for work or school, you can add third-party conferencing apps from the G Suite Marketplace. [Get extra Calendar tools](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/9117864).

##### Pick a color for your event

When you're creating an event, you can choose the color that event will show as in your calendars. People won't be able to see the color you set for events unless you've given them permission to make changes on your calendar. [Learn how to manage your calendar permissions](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082).

1.  On your Android phone, open the Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap on an existing event.
3.  Tap edit Edit .
4.  Scroll, select a color.
5.  Tap **Save**.

### Edit or cancel events

##### Edit an event

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Open the event you want to edit.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  Make changes to your event, then tap **Save**.

**Tip**: To move an event, drag and drop it to a new time and date on your calendar.

##### Delete an event

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Open the event you want to delete.
3.  In the top right, tap More More .
4.  Tap **Delete** and then **Delete**.

##### Let guests edit your event

You can let guests edit your event details. For example, they can invite others or view the full guest list.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Open the event you want to let guests edit.
3.  Tap Edit Edit .
4.  In the "Guests can" section, turn on the access levels you want to give guests.
5.  Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** The Android guest access levels follow any access levels you've applied on your computer. For example, if you turned on "Modify event" using your computer, that access level will be turned on in the Android app.
