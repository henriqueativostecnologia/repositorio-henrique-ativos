# Block someone in Google Calendar

To prevent unwanted invitations, you can block an event organizer. When you block someone, it:

- Prevents future invitations from them on your calendar.
- Blocks them in other Google services like Chat, Drive, and Photos.

## Before you begin

- You can only block someone from Google Calendar on a computer.
- You can only block other Google Accounts. To block non-Google accounts, [block their email address in Gmail](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/8151).
- After you block someone, any existing invitations from the blocked user remain on your calendar. If you don’t want the invitations on your calendar, you can manually delete them.

**Report an event as spam**

- In Google Calendar, [you can report inappropriate invitations and events as spam](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/13155911).
- If you receive unsolicited events from an unknown sender, report the event as spam.
- If the same sender repeatedly invites you to unwanted events, you should block them.

## Block an event organizer in Calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the event from the person you want to block.
3.  At the top right of the event, click More options More .
4.  Click **Block** **\[name\]** and then ** Block**.

**Tip:** After you block someone, Google Calendar automatically removes the event.

## Unblock an event organizer

1.  On your computer, go to [myaccount.google.com/blocklist](https://myaccount.google.com/blocklist).
2.  Next to the account, click **Remove** Delete .

**Tip:** After you unblock someone, it does not restore any previously blocked events. They must manually send new invitations for events you wish to attend.

## Learn what to expect when you block someone

Blocking someone does not block all possible interactions from this person. In particular:

- **Gmail notifications:**
  - If previous events from the blocked person are updated, they can still trigger notifications in Gmail.
  - To stop the notifications, [block the sender's email address](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/8151).

&nbsp;

- **Access to your calendars:**
  - Blocking someone does not remove their access to your calendar if you previously shared it with them.
  - To remove their access, [update your share settings for your calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37082).
- **Secondary calendars:**
  - You can’t block someone from a secondary calendar you have subscribed to. To avoid seeing invitations to a secondary calendar, [unsubscribe from that calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37188).
  - However, if you block someone, the block applies even if the person invites you from a secondary calendar.
- **Delegates:**
  - If you are a delegate for another user, you can’t block someone on their behalf.
  - If a user you blocked has a delegate that sends an invite, that invitation will still be blocked.
  - If a blocked user acts as a delegate for an unblocked user, invitations sent on behalf of that unblocked user are blocked.
- **Guest modification access:**
  - If you edit an event with "Guest can modify" permissions and you blocked the organizer, the event is removed from your calendar.
