# Use focus time in Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To reduce distractions while you work, you can schedule a focus time event in Google Calendar.

When you schedule a focus time event, you can:

- Mute chat notifications.
- Automatically decline meetings.

### Learn who can use focus time

To schedule a focus time event, you need to have an eligible work or school account:

- [Google Workspace for Business Standard and Plus](https://workspace.google.com/business/)
- [Google Workspace for Enterprise Standard and Plus](https://workspace.google.com/enterprise/)
- [Google Workspace for Education](https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_us/workspace-for-education/editions/overview/)
- [Google Workspace for Nonprofits](https://www.google.com/nonprofits/offerings/workspace/)

### Schedule focus time

**Important:** You can only create focus time events from Day and Week views. The events must have a specific start and end time.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the time you want to schedule focus time.
3.  At the top of the event, click **Focus time**.
4.  Select when you want your event to begin and end.
5.  Set your focus time preferences:
    - To receive notifications for Chat messages, next to "Do not disturb," deselect the box.
    - To automatically decline meetings during focus time, next to “Automatically decline meetings,” check the box.
6.  Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- When you deselect **Do not disturb**, it becomes the default setting for new focus time events.
- By default, focus time declines new and existing meetings. However, you can update your focus time settings to “Only decline new meetings.”

### Edit or delete focus time

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click the focus time event.
    - Focus time events are marked with headphones Headphones .
3.  Select an option:
    - To edit the event:
      1.  Click Edit Edit .
      2.  Make your edits.
      3.  Click **Save**.
    - To delete the event, click Delete event Delete reminder .

### Change how meetings are declined during focus time

1.  Click the focus time event.
2.  Click Edit  Edit .
3.  Under “Automatically decline meetings,” select:
    - **Only new meeting invitations**
    - **New and existing meetings**
4.  Click **Save**.

### Repeat a focus time event

1.  Click the focus time event.
2.  Click Edit Edit .
3.  Next to “Does not repeat,” click the Down arrow Down arrow .
4.  Select how often you want to repeat your focus time event.
5.  Click **Save**.

### Change privacy settings on a focus time event

1.  Click the focus time event.
2.  Click Edit Edit .
3.  Under the calendar name, click **Default visibility**.
4.  Next to “Default visibility,” click the Down arrow Down arrow .
5.  Select **Public** or **Private**.

### Change the color of a focus time event

1.  Click the focus time event.
2.  Click Edit Edit .
3.  Click Color Event color .
4.  Select a color.
5.  Click **Save**.

**Tip:** Your color selection is saved for future focus time events.

### Learn about focus time & Chat notifications

- You can only choose to mute Chat notifications if:
  - Your organization turns on Chat.
  - The focus time block is less than 24 hours.
- The first time you use focus time, "Do not disturb" for Chat notifications is turned on by default.
- When you create or edit focus time, you can turn off Chat notifications.

- [Set your working hours & location](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/7638168)
- [Modify Google Calendar notifications](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37242)

## Version: Android phone & tablet

To reduce distractions while you work, you can schedule a focus time event in Google Calendar.

When you schedule a focus time event, you can:

- Mute chat notifications.
- Automatically decline meetings.

### Learn who can use focus time

To schedule a focus time event, you need to have an eligible work or school account:

- [Google Workspace for Business Standard and Plus](https://workspace.google.com/business/)
- [Google Workspace for Enterprise Standard and Plus](https://workspace.google.com/enterprise/)
- [Google Workspace for Education](https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_us/workspace-for-education/editions/overview/)
- [Google Workspace for Nonprofits](https://www.google.com/nonprofits/offerings/workspace/)

### Schedule focus time

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the bottom right, tap **Create** and then **Focus time**.
3.  Select when you want your event to begin and end.
4.  Set your focus time preferences:
    - **To mute Google Chat notifications:** Turn on **Do not disturb**.
    - **To automatically decline meetings:**
      1.  Turn on **Automatically decline meetings**
      2.  Decline **New and existing meetings** or **Only new meeting invitations**.
5.  **Optional:** For your focus time event, you can also:
    - Set a custom color Event color .
    - Add a description.
    - Change the event visibility settings .
6.  At the top right, tap **Save**.

**Tips:**

- When you turn off **Do not disturb**, it becomes the default setting for new focus time events.
- By default, focus time declines new and existing meetings. However, you can update your focus time settings to **Only new meeting invitations**.

### Edit or delete focus time

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap a focus time event.
    - **Tip**: Focus time events are marked with headphones  Headphones .
3.  Select an option:
    - **To edit the event:**
      1.  At the top right, tap Edit Edit .
      2.  Make your edits.
      3.  Tap **Save**.
    - **To delete the event:** At the top right, tap More options and then **Delete** and then **Delete**.

### Learn about focus time & Chat notifications

- You can only choose to mute Chat notifications if:
  - Your organization turns on Chat.
  - The focus time block is less than 24 hours.
- The first time you use focus time, "Do not disturb" for Chat notifications is turned on by default.
- When you create or edit focus time, you can turn off Chat notifications.

### Related resources

- [Set your working hours & location](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/7638168)
- [Modify Google Calendar notifications](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37242)
