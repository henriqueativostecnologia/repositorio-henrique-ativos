# Subscribe to someone else’s calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

In Google Calendar, you can subscribe to someone else's calendar if they share it with you. If someone hasn’t shared their calendar with you, you can ask for access to their primary calendar.

- To subscribe to a new calendar, you must use a computer web browser. You can't subscribe to a calendar in the Google Calendar app for Android, iPhone, or iPad.
- After the calendar owner approves your request, their calendar automatically appears in the Google Calendar app. 

### Ask to subscribe to a calendar

**Important:**

- You can’t subscribe to calendars that aren’t from Google.
- If you subscribe to more than 400 calendars, you can encounter performance issues when you browse calendars.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, next to “Other calendars,” click Add other calendars Add other calendars and then **Subscribe to calendar**.
3.  In the “Add calendar” box:
    - Enter the person's email address.
    - Select a calendar from the list
4.  After you request access:
    - **If the calendar isn't shared with you:** The owner of the calendar gets an email that requests to give you access.
    - **If the calendar is already shared with you:** In your primary calendar, on the left, it shows under "Other calendars."

**Tip:** When someone shares their calendar with you, you get an email with a link to add their calendar.

[Add a calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/settings/addcalendar)

### Approve a request to subscribe to your calendar

**Important:** If someone asks to subscribe to your calendar, you need to approve the request before they can get access to it.

1.  On a computer, open the email that requests access to your calendar.
2.  In the email, click the link.
3.  In the “Settings” page that opens, select:
    - The user
    - What the user can do to your calendar:
      - **See only free/busy (hide details)**
      - **See event details**
      - **Make changes to events**
      - **Make changes and manage sharing**
4.  Click **Send**.

### Use a link to add a public calendar

**Important:** You can only add a calendar with a link if the other person's calendar is public. [Learn more about public calendars](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37083).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, next to "Other calendars," click Add other calendars Add other calendars and then **From URL.**
3.  Enter the URL of the published calendar.
4.  Click **Add calendar**.
    - The calendar displays on the left, under "Other calendars."

[Add a calendar from a URL](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/settings/addbyurl%20)

### Hide someone else's calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under “Other calendars,” uncheck the calendar you want to hide.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Calendar, you can subscribe to someone else's calendar if they share it with you. If someone hasn’t shared their calendar with you, you can ask for access to their primary calendar.

- To subscribe to a new calendar, you must use a computer web browser. You can't subscribe to a calendar in the Google Calendar app for Android, iPhone, or iPad.
- After the calendar owner approves your request, their calendar automatically appears in the Google Calendar app. 

### Show or hide a calendar that you subscribe to

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu .
3.  To the left of a calendar, check or uncheck the box.

**Tip:** If you can’t find a calendar, check your sync settings. [Learn how to fix sync problems with the Google Calendar app](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/6261951).

### Add a calendar that someone shares with you

When someone shares their calendar with you, you get an email with a link to add their calendar.

1.  In your email, tap **Open Calendar**.
    - The shared calendar opens.
2.  To find the calendar, tap Menu .
