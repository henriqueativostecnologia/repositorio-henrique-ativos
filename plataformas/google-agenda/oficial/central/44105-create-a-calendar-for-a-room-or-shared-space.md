# Create a calendar for a room or shared space

If you manage a shared space, like a conference room or basketball court, you can create a calendar for the space and let people book time in it.

**Note:** If you're using Google Calendar through your work, school, or organization, learn how to [add resources to a work or school event](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/143753) instead.

## Create a shared space calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com). You can't create shared calendars from the Google Calendar app.
2.  On the left, next to "Other calendars,"click Add other calendars Plus **and then Create new calendar**.
3.  Add a name for your calendar, and any other information you want to include.
4.  At the bottom of the page, click **Create calendar**.
5.  On the top left, click Back Back .
6.  Under "My calendars,"next to your new calendar's name, click More More **and then Settings and sharing**.
7.  Under "Auto-accept invitations," select an option:
    - **Auto-accept invitations that do not conflict:** All invitations show on the calendar. If there are no conflicts, people with permission to view all event details automatically accept an invite to any new event.
    - **Automatically add all invitations to this calendar:** All invitations show on the calendar. People can RSVP to the invitation.
    - **Do not show invitations:** Invitations don’t show on the calendar. People subscribed to the calendar must turn on notifications to receive invitations for new events in their email. After they RSVP in the email notification, the event shows up on the calendar.
8.  Make your calendar public, or share it with certain email addresses.
9.  On the top left, click Back Back .

## Add a shared space calendar

You can add calendars created by other people in your organization, such as room schedules, to your list.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com). You can't add shared calendars from the Google Calendar app.
2.  On the left, next to "Other calendars," click Add Plus **and then**  **Browse resources**.
3.  Next to the calendar you want to add, check the box.
4.  On the top left, click Back Back .
5.  You'll see the new calendar under "Other calendars."

## Set Calendar hours

To set hours for your space, block off the time when it isn't available.

1.  Open the calendar you created for your space.
2.  For the hours that aren't available, create an event during that time with a name like "Closed." If the space is always unavailable at that time, you can make the event repeat.
3.  Click **Save**.

#### Create an event that shows up on a shared calendar

## Step 1: Get the ID of the calendar you want to share

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the left, under "My calendars," point to the calendar you want to share.
3.  Click More More   and then  **Settings and sharing**.
4.  On the left, click **Integrate calendar**.
5.  Copy the Calendar ID.

## Step 2: Create an event

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  In the top left, click Create Add .
3.  Enter the event information.
4.  Under "Add guests," add the calendar ID.
5.  Click **Save**.
