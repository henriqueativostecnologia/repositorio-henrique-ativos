# Add another account to Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

In Google Calendar, you can add more than one account.

### Add an account

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your **Profile photo** and then **Add another account**.
3.  To finish the sign-in process, follow the instructions on the screen.

### Turn off auto-sync for an account

**Important:**

- To save data and battery life, you can limit your sync to happen only while you use the app. For example, when you open your calendar or create and change events.
- If you close the Calendar app while a sync’s in progress, it might take some time before the sync stops.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your **Profile photo** and then **Manage accounts on this device**.
3.  Select an account.
4.  Tap **Account sync**.
5.  Turn off **Calendar**.

### Remove an account

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your **Profile photo** and then **Manage accounts on this device**.
3.  Select an account.
4.  Tap **Remove account**.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

In Google Calendar, you can add more than one account.

### Add an account

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your **Profile photo** and then **Add another account**.
3.  To finish the sign-in process, follow the instructions on the screen.

### Turn off auto-sync for an account

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your **Profile photo** and then **Manage accounts on this device**.
3.  Turn off your account.

### Remove an account

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top right, tap your **Profile photo**.
3.  Tap **Manage accounts on this device** and then **Remove from this device** and then **Remove**.
