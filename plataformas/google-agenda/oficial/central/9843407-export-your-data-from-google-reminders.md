# Export your data from Google Reminders

You can export and download your data from Reminders. You can download data that hasn't been deleted. You can create an archive to keep for your records or use the data in another service. [Learn how to download your data.](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190)

If you're using a work or school account, some data might not be available for download. If you're a super administrator of your Google domain, you can download or migrate your organization’s data. Learn how to [export your organization's Google Workspace data](https://support.google.com/a/answer/100458).

Exported data from Google Reminders might include:

- Title
- Created time
- Archived, upcoming, and active reminders
- Expiration date
- Recurrence information
- The application that created the reminder, like Keep
- Location
