# Print your calendar

In Google Calendar, you can print your calendar for a specific date range.

## Customize & print your calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  To select a date range, at the top right, click:
    - **Day**
    - **Week**
    - **Month**
    - **Schedule**
    - **4 days**
3.  At the top right, click Settings menu Settings **and then** **Print**.
4.  On the "Print preview" page, change details such as font size or orientation.
5.  Click **Print**.

**Tip:** When space is available, the locations or rooms of events are also displayed.

## Set up advanced print options

#### Hide your calendars

On the left, under "My calendars" or "Other calendars," click the name of the calendars you want to hide. If a calendar does not have a check mark next to it, it's hidden.

**Tip:** To show a calendar, click the box.

#### Fit your calendar on a page

### Change the orientation

1.  At the top right, click Settings menu  Settings **and then** **Print**.
2.  On the "Print preview" page, in the "Orientation" dropdown, click the Down arrow  Drop down arrow .
3.  Click **Landscape** or **Portrait**.
4.  Click **Print**.

### Hide weekends

1.  At the top right, click Settings menu  Settings **and then** **Print**.
2.  On the "Print preview" page, deselect **Show weekends**.
3.  Click **Print**.

### Change the font size

1.  At the top right, click Settings menu  Settings **and then** **Print**.
2.  On the "Print preview" page, in the "Font size" dropdown,  click the Down arrow  Drop down arrow .
3.  Choose a font size.
4.  Click **Print**.

**Tip:** If you change the font size, it won't change the number of days on a page. It may make your calendar easier to read.

#### Save your calendar as a PDF

1.  Follow the steps to print your calendar.
2.  In the final window with print options, under "Destination," click **Change**.
3.  Under "Print destinations," click **Save as PDF**.
4.  Click **Save**.

**Tip:** To find your calendar PDF check your computer’s downloads.

#### Print your calendar in full color

1.  At the top right, click Settings menu  Settings and then **Print**.
2.  On the "Print preview" page, in the "Color & style" dropdown, click the Down arrow  Drop down arrow .
3.  Click **Full color**.
4.  Click **Print**.

**Tip:** If “Full color” is selected, event responses appear as a:

- **Did not respond:** Transparent box
- **No:** Transparent box with crossed-out circle
- **Yes:** Full color box
- **Maybe:** Full color box with a question mark

#### Include calendar event details

1.  At the top right, click the "View switcher" dropdown menu and then  **Schedule**.
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu  Settings and then  **Print**.
3.  On the "Print preview" page, select:
    - **Print descriptions**
    - **Print your response**
4.  Click **Print**.
