# Manage Google Calendar invites in other calendar services

**Important:** The instructions in this article only apply to third-party email providers like Microsoft Outlook. They do not apply to Gmail.

If you don’t use Google Calendar and a Calendar user invites you to an event, you can get one or more invitation emails. These emails make sure your calendar shows the correct information for the event.

## Filter notifications

While required for event sync, the emails can clutter your inbox. To make it easier to manage your inbox:

1.  In your email provider, create a filter or rule for emails that include "noreply-calendar-sync@google.com" in the message header.
2.  Choose where you want the emails to go, like your archive or trash.
3.  Save the filter or rule.
    - Only one email for each invitation or update should show in your inbox.

**Tips:**

- If you use Microsoft Outlook:
  - [Learn how to create inbox rule](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/use-inbox-rules-in-outlook-com-or-outlook-on-the-web-4b094371-a5d7-49bd-8b1b-4e4896a7cc5d)
- If your calendar service is managed by an administrator, they can set up filters for you. [Learn how organizations can filter Calendar email invitations](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14957193).
- Invitation emails show the correct information for recurring events for up to 30 days in the past.
