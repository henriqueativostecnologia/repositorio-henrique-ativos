# Set your working location

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can set your working location in Google Calendar so collaborators know where you are and can easily find time to meet. Calendar automatically detects the relevant time zone and suggests meeting times that match where you work.

If you don't get an option to set your working location, your administrator may not have this feature enabled for your organization. Some working location features, such as specific addresses and automatic time zone updates, may not be available due to your organization's data compliance and location restrictions. [Learn how to contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

### Check eligibility for working location

To set a working location, you need to have an eligible Google Workspace edition:

- Business Standard
- Business Plus
- Enterprise Starter
- Enterprise Standard
- Enterprise Plus
- Education Fundamentals
- Education Standard
- Education Plus
- Nonprofits

### Set your default working location

**Important:** To set a working location, turn on working hours. [Learn how to set your working hours](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/7638168).

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Settings menu and then ** Settings**.
3.  On the left, under "General," click **Working hours & location**.
4.  Select the days you work.
5.  Next to each day, enter an existing working location or create a new one.

**Tip:** To change your working location, go to a specific day on your calendar and click the location chip. You can apply the change to that specific day or set it to repeat weekly.

### Add your working location

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click **Create** and then ** Working location**.
3.  **Optional:** Adjust the dates that you want to add a working location to.
4.  Select a working location:
    - Home
    - Office
5.  **Optional:** To add a different location, click **Other locations** to search for a specific building, city, or street address.
6.  Click **Save**.

### Edit your working location for one day

**Important**: When your calendar is shared with someone with free or busy visibility, they can get information about your availability and working location.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top of your calendar, click the location chip. For example, **Home**.
3.  At the top right, click Edit working location Edit task .
4.  Select a working location:
    - Home
    - Office
5.  **Optional:** To add a different location, click **Other locations** to search for a specific building, city, or street address.
6.  Click **Save**.
7.  Choose a frequency, and then click **OK**.

### Edit your working location for a range of days

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top of your calendar, click the location chip. For example, **Home**.
3.  At the top right, click Edit working location Edit task .
4.  To adjust the date range, click on each date.
5.  Select a working location:
    - Home
    - Office
6.  **Optional:** To add a different location, click **Other locations** to search for a specific building, city, or street address.
7.  Click Save.
8.  Choose a frequency, and then click **OK**.

### Add your working location for part of a day

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Click an empty space on your calendar.
3.  At the top of the event, click **Working location**.
4.  Choose the time and date you want to add a working location.
5.  **Optional:** To make your working location repeat:
    1.  Click the day and time.
    2.  Next to "Does not repeat," click the Down arrow Dropdown .
    3.  Choose how often you want the event to repeat and end.
6.  Choose a location or add a new one.
7.  Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- After you create a working location for part of your day, you can drag it to another date or time.
- You can set your working location to repeat. [Learn how to make an existing event repeat](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37115).

### Delete a working location

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  At the top of your calendar, click the location chip.
3.  At the top right, click Delete working location .
4.  Choose the date range you want to delete for the week or your entire schedule, and then click **OK**.

### Learn how your working location is shared with others

- When you share your calendar with someone, they can find your working location.
- To help coordinate in-office time with others, your administrator may ask you to set your working location. If so, a yellow banner at the top of your calendar shows the request.
- If you have "Make changes and manage sharing" access to a calendar, you can edit the default working location. [Learn how to choose what others can do with your calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/15716974).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can set your working location in Google Calendar so collaborators know where you are and can easily find time to meet. Calendar automatically detects the relevant time zone and suggests meeting times that match where you work.

If you don't get an option to set your working location, your administrator may not have this feature enabled for your organization. Some working location features, such as specific addresses and automatic time zone updates, may not be available due to your organization's data compliance and location restrictions. [Learn how to contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

### Check eligibility for working location

To set a working location, you need to have an eligible Google Workspace edition:

- Business Standard
- Business Plus
- Enterprise Starter
- Enterprise Standard
- Enterprise Plus
- Education Fundamentals
- Education Standard
- Education Plus
- Nonprofits

### Add your working location

**Important:** To set a working location, turn on working hours. [Learn how to set your working hours](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/7638168).

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the bottom right, tap Create Add and then **Working location**.
3.  To adjust the date range, tap on each date.
4.  Select a working location:
    - Home
    - Office
5.  **Optional:** To add a different location, tap Add location to search for a specific building, city, or street address.
6.  Tap **Save**.

### Edit your working location

**Important:** When your calendar is shared with someone with free or busy visibility, they can find your availability and working location.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  Tap the location you want to change and then Edit Edit task .
    - To adjust the date range, tap each date.
3.  Tap your current working location, and choose:
    - Home
    - Office
4.  **Optional:** To add a different location, tap Add location to search for a specific building, city, or street address.
5.  Tap **Save**.

### Add your working location for part of a day

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the bottom right, tap Create Add and then **Working location**.
3.  Turn off **All-day**.
4.  To choose the start and end time, tap each date.
5.  **Optional:** To make your working location repeat:
    - Tap **Does not repeat**.
    - Choose how often you want the event to repeat and end.
6.  Select a working location:
    - Home
    - Office
7.  **Optional:** To add a different location, tap Add location to search for a specific building, city, or street address.
8.  Tap **Save**.

**Tips:**

- You can touch and hold, then drag a working location for part of your day to another date or time.
- You can set your working location to repeat. [Learn how to make an existing event repeat](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37115).

### Delete a working location

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  At the top of your calendar, tap the location chip.
3.  At the top right, click More options and then **Delete**.
4.  Choose the date range you want to delete for the week or your entire schedule, and then click **Delete**.

### Learn how your working location is shared with others

- When you share your calendar with someone, they can find your working location.
- To help coordinate in-office time with others, your administrator may ask you to set your working location. If so, a yellow banner at the top of your calendar shows the request.
- If you have "Make changes and manage sharing" access to a calendar, you can edit the default working location. [Learn how to choose what others can do with your calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/15716974).
