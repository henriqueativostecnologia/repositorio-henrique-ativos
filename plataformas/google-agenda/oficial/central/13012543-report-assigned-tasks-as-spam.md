# Report assigned tasks as spam

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can report an assigned task as spam from a shared surface, like a space or document.

When you report an assigned task as spam, it’s:

- Removed from your personal list.
- No longer assigned to you in the shared space.

### Report an assigned task as spam in Google Tasks

1.  Go to [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/), [Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/), [Chat](https://chat.google.com), [Drive](https://drive.google.com/), or a file in Google [Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0), [Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/), or [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/u/0/).
2.  In the side panel, on the right, click Tasks Tasks .
3.  Click the task you want to report.
4.  To the right of the task, click Open menu More .
5.  Click **Report as spam** and then **Report as spam**.

### Report an assigned task as spam in Google Calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  On the calendar grid, click the task you want to report.
3.  At the top right of the task, click Options More .
4.  Click **Report as spam** and then **Report as spam**.

**Tip:** To report an assigned task as spam, enter the keyboard shortcut: **!**

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can report an assigned task as spam from a shared surface, like a space or document.

When you report an assigned task as spam, it’s:

- Removed from your personal list.
- No longer assigned to you in the shared space.

### Report an assigned task as spam in Google Tasks

1.  On your device, open the Google Tasks app Tasks .
2.  Tap the task you want to report.
3.  At the top right, tap Options More .
4.  Tap **Report as spam** and then **Report as spam**.

### Report an assigned task as spam in Google Calendar

1.  On your device, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .
2.  On the calendar grid, tap the task you want to report.
3.  At the top right, tap Options More .
4.  Tap **Report as spam** and then **Report as spam**.
