# Verify emails for appointments

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

To prevent unwanted bookings, you can ask guests without a Google Account to verify their email before they book an appointment.

### Learn how email verification works

- To use email verification for appointment schedules, you need to have an eligible Google Workspace subscription. [Learn more about premium features for appointment schedules](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/1608416#upgrade-plan).
- By default, email verification is off.
- When you sign in to a Google Account and make an appointment:
  - Your booking details are pre-filled.
  - You aren't asked to submit a PIN.

### Turn email verification on or off

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Create or edit an appointment schedule.
3.  Under “Booking form,” check or uncheck the “Require email verification” box.
4.  Click **Save**.

### Verify your email to you book an appointment

1.  Fill out the booking form with your email address.
2.  Check your email for a PIN.
3.  On the booking page, enter the PIN.
4.  Click **Submit**.
