# Transfer calendars or events in Google Calendar

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can transfer calendars or events you own to new owners. You can also move events to other calendars.

### Before you transfer calendars or events in Google Calendar

- You can’t transfer your primary calendar, which is automatically assigned to you when you first sign in to Calendar.
- You can only transfer ownership of secondary calendars. A secondary calendar is:
  - Any calendar that you create.
  - A group calendar that’s shared with you.
- If you delete your Google Account, this also deletes all calendars you own. To save the calendar, transfer ownership to someone else.
  - If you move to a new Google Account, [import events to your new Google Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37118).
- You can’t transfer events with more than 200 guests to another owner or calendar.
- After you transfer an event or calendar to another owner, they get an email with a link. The new owner has 60 days to accept ownership, and you remain the owner until then.
- When you transfer events with a Google Meet link, the video conferencing policies applicable to that event are also affected. [Learn how Google Meet settings apply to events created on or transferred to other calendars](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/10003792).

### Transfer a calendar you own

**Important:**

- Only the current calendar owner can do a transfer.
- For work or school accounts, the new calendar owner must be in the same organization.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com).
2.  On the left side of the page, click **My calendars**.
3.  Next to the calendar, click Options More and then **Settings and sharing**.
4.  Under "Shared with," next to the person that you want to transfer the calendar to, click the drop-down menu Dropdown .
5.  Select **Transfer ownership**.
6.  To confirm, click **Transfer ownership**.

**Tip:** As soon as the new owner accepts the transfer, your permission level for the calendar automatically changes to “Make changes and manage sharing.”

### Transfer an event to a calendar you don't own

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Select an event you own.
3.  At the top right of the event, click Options More and then  **Change owner**.
4.  Enter a new owner and a message to send them.
5.  Click **Change owner**.

### Transfer an event to a calendar you own

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Select an event.
3.  At the top right, click Edit event Edit .
4.  Next to Calendar Calendar , click the Down arrow Down arrow .
5.  Choose the calendar you want to move the event to.
6.  Click **Save**.

### Copy an event to another calendar

1.  On your computer, open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/).
2.  Select an event.
3.  At the top right, click Options .
4.  Choose a calendar to copy the event to.
5.  Click **Save**.

### Move events between calendars

**Important:**

- To move an event from one calendar to another, you must:
  - Be the organizer of a Google Calendar event.
  - Have edit access to the calendar you move the event to.

- When you move an event that recurs to another calendar, you do the same to future instances. You can’t move only one instance of an event that recurs.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app Calendar .

2.  Tap the event and then Edit event Edit .

3.  Under the name of the event, tap the calendar you want to move the event to.

4.  At the top right, tap **Save**.
