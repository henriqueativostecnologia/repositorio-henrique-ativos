# View & reorder your files and folders

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can change layouts and sort your files and folders to find them easily.

On My Drive, there are 4 ways you can sort a list of your files:

**Tip:** If you're on the "Home" or "Recent" page, you can’t sort your files.

[Learn how to find files in Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375114).

### Change between list & grid layout

There are 2 ways to view files and folders in Google Drive: list and grid layout. By default, Drive shows the list layout.

- To change to the grid layout, go to your toolbar and click the grid layout icon .
- To change back to the list layout, go to your toolbar and click the list layout icon Menu .

### Reorder files & folders

##### If you're viewing your files in a list

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  On the side panel, click **My Drive**.
3.  Click the column that you want to use to sort.
4.  To reverse the order, click the up arrow Up arrow or down arrow down arrow .

##### If you're viewing your files in a grid

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  On the side panel, click **My Drive**.
3.  In the top right, click the title of the current sort, like "Name" or "Modified."
4.  Click on the type of sorting you want.
5.  To reverse the order, click the up arrow Up arrow or down arrow down arrow .

##### Find recently edited files

See the files that were most recently edited.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  In the top right, click Information Info .
3.  Click Activity.

### Change how you sort your files & folders

In “My Drive," "Shared with me," and "Starred" views, you can choose to show files and folders together or show folders on top with files below.

1.  On your computer, [go to Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  On the far right, immediately above your list of files, click Sort Sort .
3.  Select an option. To show:
    - Folders on top, click **On top**.
    - Files and folders intermingled, click **Mixed with files**.

**Tip:** Your preferences for sorting files and folders will be saved; you can update your sorting options by making a new selection following the steps above.

### Turn off preview card

Preview cards appear when you hover on a file or folder icon in Drive. These thumbnails can help you save time and gain context on your files and folders before you open them.

Preview cards contain file and folder info and updates, such as who last edited the file.

To turn off the feature:

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](http://drive.google.com).
2.  Click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  On the side menu, select **General**.
4.  Scroll to “Preview card.”
5.  Uncheck the box next to “Show details card when hovering on a file or folder icon.”

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can change layouts and sort your files and folders to find them easily.

### Change between list & grid layout

There are 2 ways to view files and folders in Google Drive: list and grid layout.

- To change from list to grid layout, go to your toolbar and click the grid layout icon .
- To change back to the list layout, go to your toolbar and click the list layout icon .

### Reorder files & folders

#### Change your "Sort by" method in Google Drive

1.  [Open the app for Google Drive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs)  Drive .
2.  At the bottom right, tap Files  Files .
3.  At the top, under "My Drive," tap your "Sort by" method.

#### Change your "Sort by" method in Google Docs, Sheets, Slides

1.  Open the app for Google [Docs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.docs&referrer=utm_source%3Ddocs_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Ddocs_hc)  Docs , [Sheets](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.sheets&referrer=utm_source%3Dsheets_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dsheets_hc)  Sheets , or [Slides](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs.editors.slides&referrer=utm_source%3Dslides_hc%26utm_medium%3Dlink%26utm_campaign%3Dslidess_hc) .
2.  At the top right, tap More  More   and then  **Sort by**.
3.  Select a "Sort by" method.

**Tip:** In Google Drive, you can reverse the direction of your sort. At the top right, next to your "Sort by" method, tap the arrow  Reverse sort direction (Down) .
