# Apply labels to files in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you use Drive for work or school, your organization might set up labels to organize files.  

**Tips: **

- You can apply up to 150 labels to each file.
- Only administrators can create labels.
- To apply labels to a file, the administrator must give you access and allow you to edit the file.
- To find labels applied to a file or search for files by their labels, you need: 
  - Access to the file
  - View permission for the label

### Apply labels to an unopened file

1.  On your computer, [go to Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right click the file and then click **Labels** and then **Apply labels** .
3.  Select the label you want to apply.
4.  If the label has one or more fields, you can choose or enter values.

**Tips:**

- To remove a label from a file, find the label you want to remove and click Remove Remove .
- If you don't find the Labels section, your administrator may not have set up labels or allowed you access. Contact your administrator.

### Apply labels in bulk

1.  On your computer, [go to Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Choose up to 100 files to which you want to apply the label.
3.  Right click the mouse, then click **Labels** and then **Apply a label**.
4.  Select a label and choose any field values to apply to the file.

### Apply labels to files in Docs, Sheets, or Slides

1.  On your computer, go to Docs, Sheets, or Slides.
2.  Click **File** and then **Labels** .
3.  In the side panel, review labels and apply new ones.
4.  To remove a label from a file, select a label and click Remove Remove .

### Apply labels to non-Google files in Drive Preview

1.  On your computer, [go to Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  To view a file in Drive Preview, click More More and then **Labels** .
3.  On the side panel, review labels and apply new ones.
4.  To remove a label from a file, select a label and click Remove Remove .

### Modify a label

1.  On your computer, [go to Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right-click the file, then click Labels .
3.  Locate the applied label you want to modify, then click Edit .
4.  In the dialog, update the label's field values.
5.  Click **Save changes**.

### Search for files with labels

Use Drive search options to find content with specific labels or fields.

1.  On your computer, [go to Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  At the top, click **Search in Drive** and then **Show search options**.
3.  Next to “Labels,” click **Search labels**.
4.  Click the label. You can select a field and specify a value.
5.  Click **Search**.

**Tip:** Search returns only files you have permission to access.

  - Access to the file

### View labels on your Android device

**Important:** You can't apply or change labels in the mobile app.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, [open the Drive app](https://www.google.com/drive/download/).
2.  Find the file for which you want to view labels.
3.  Without opening the file, click More More and then **Details and activity** and then **Labels**.
4.  Open files and click More More and then  **Details** and then **Labels**.
