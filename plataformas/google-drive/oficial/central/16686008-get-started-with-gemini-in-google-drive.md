# Get started with Gemini in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

**Important**: To use Gemini in Drive, you must have an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn more about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/13952129?hl=en&co=DASHER._Family%3DBusiness-Enterprise).

To enhance your productivity and save you time, Gemini in Drive helps you learn from and interact with your files in new ways. You can:

- [Perform complex research & discuss topics synthesized from multiple files](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16963068)
- [Share your research sources with others using Drive projects](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684520)
- [Quickly search & retrieve files using AI Overviews](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16685111)
- [Manage your calendar & draft messages with Gemini in Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/18545992)
- [Create Content, Images & Audio Overviews with Gemini in Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16685527)
- [Summarize files and folders](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16686465)
- [Use Gems with Gemini in Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684485)
- [Organize files](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16671865)
- [Manage your files with Gemini in Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/18745288)

To understand how your data is used and what to expect from Gemini suggestions, [learn more about disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684790).

### Learn to navigate Gemini in Google Drive

1.  Open the Google Drive app Drive .
2.  At the top right, tap **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Select a suggested prompt or type your own question in the prompt box at the bottom.

To understand how your data is used and what to expect from Gemini suggestions, [learn more about disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684790).

### Give feedback on generated output

Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request.

If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can submit feedback.

1.  On your computer, at the bottom right of the generated text, click Good suggestion or Bad suggestion .
2.  If you click Bad suggestion , you can select the issue you found and enter additional feedback.
3.  Click Submit.

To provide general feedback on this feature, at the top, go to Help and then Send feedback to Google.

To report a legal issue, [create a request](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420).

### Learn to navigate Gemini in Google Drive

When using Drive on the web, Gemini opens in a full-screen, immersive workspace.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Use the full screen interface to conduct your research:
    1.  **Prompt box:** At the bottom, type your request.
    2.  **Menu:** At the top left, access your Conversation history, Gems, and saved Projects.
    3.  **Sources Panel:** On the left side, manage explicit file sources and toggle broad search settings for other Google Workspace apps.

To understand how your data is used and what to expect from Gemini suggestions, [learn more about disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684790).
