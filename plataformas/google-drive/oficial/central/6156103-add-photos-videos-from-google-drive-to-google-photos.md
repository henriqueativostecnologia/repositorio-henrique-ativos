# Add photos & videos from Google Drive to Google Photos

To organize your photos and videos across services, you can add photos from Google Drive to Google Photos. 

## Before you get started

- The photo must be larger than 256 pixels.
- The [file type](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6193313#rawfiles) must be .jpg, .heic, .png, .webp, .gif, and most RAW files.
- If you use a Google Account through work or school, you’ll have to download from Google Drive and re-upload to Google Photos.

**Tip: **Learn how to [download all your photos and videos](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190).

## Add photos & videos from Google Drive to Google Photos 

1.  On your computer, go to [photos.google.com](https://photos.google.com/).
2.  At the top, click Add Add and then **Google Drive**.
3.  Find and select your photos.
4.  Click **Upload**.

## What happens when you change or delete a photo

Any changes you make in Drive will only apply to Drive. Any changes you make in Photos will only apply to Photos. 

## Check your storage

Your photos and videos are stored using your [Google Account's storage space](https://www.google.com/settings/storage).

- Items copied from Google Drive to Google Photos are backed up based on your backup quality. 
- If you copy an item from Google Drive to Google Photos, the new copy in Google Photos will also count against your storage. 
- [Learn how to change your backup quality](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6220791).
