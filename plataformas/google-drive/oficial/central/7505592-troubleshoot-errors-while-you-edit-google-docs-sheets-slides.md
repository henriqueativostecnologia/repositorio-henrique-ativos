# Troubleshoot errors while you edit Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Vids, or Pics

If you get “An error occurred” which prevents you from making edits on your Docs, Slides, Sheets, Vids, or Pics, you can troubleshoot the issue. If you urgently need to access a file and resume your progress, you can follow these steps to resume your workflow.

If your document doesn’t open, you may find these messages :

- "Encountered an error. Please try reloading this page, or coming back to it in a few minutes."
- "A server error occurred. Please press **Reload** in your browser."

If you get these messages, wait for 5 minutes then reload the page.

## Troubleshooting steps

#### Turn off extensions

Some extensions in your browser may interfere with Docs, Slides, Sheets, Vids, or Pics. To log in your Google Account and open your file, [use a private browser](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464). To identify what interferes with the editor, one-by-one you can [turn off extensions](https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/2664769).

**Tips:**

- If you use a different browser, you can also turn off extensions on [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT203051), [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/disable-or-remove-add-ons), and [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/add-turn-off-or-remove-extensions-in-microsoft-edge-9c0ec68c-2fbc-2f2c-9ff0-bdc76f46b026).
- Ad blocker extensions can interfere with Google Vids. [Learn how to uninstall an extension](https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/2664769#uninstall-extension).

#### Clear browsing data

You can clear your browsing data, cookies, and cached files. Learn how to [clear your cache and cookies in Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647). After you clear browsing data, you may need to sign in to your account again.

**Tip:** If you use a different browser, you can also clear cache and cookies on [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204098), [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/how-clear-firefox-cache), and [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/view-and-delete-browser-history-in-microsoft-edge-00cf7943-a9e1-975a-a33d-ac10ce454ca4).

#### Wait five minutes & reload

Sometimes you might see an error message and the error will resolve itself. Wait for five minutes and reload the page.

#### Improve your internet connection

If you have a poor internet connection, you can try moving to a location where the internet is more reliable. Alternatively, you can also try using a cable connection when possible.

#### Update your outdated software or device

If you use an older computer or browser, it might not meet the system requirements for Docs, Slides, Sheets, Vids, or Pics. You can also try to open the same file in another device. If you can open the same document on a different computer, the issue may be specific to your device. You can update your browser to the latest version.

The browser saves your opened tabs and windows and reopens them automatically when it restarts. Your Incognito windows won't reopen when Chrome restarts.

**Tip:** To prevent copy and paste image errors on your phone, update your apps to the latest version.

[Learn about system requirements and browsers](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2375082).

#### Try a different browser

If you frequently see error messages when using your regular browser, try a different browser.

**Important:** Vids doesn't currently support editing or commenting in Safari. Playback is supported once the video is processed.

[Learn about system requirements and browsers](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2375082).

#### Share edit access with other accounts

You can share edit access of your file with another account and edit the file using a different account. You can share the file in Google Drive without opening the file.

[Learn how to share files from Google Drive](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822).

## Resume your work

#### Create a copy of your file in Drive

**Important:** You’ll lose some features, like version history, in your copied file.

1.  On your computer, open [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right-click the file.
3.  Click **Make a copy**.

**Tip:** You may need to wait a few seconds for your copied file to appear in the same location as your original file.

#### Download & upload a file

**Important:** You’ll lose some features, like version history, in your downloaded file.

Download a file

1.  On your computer, open [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right-click the file.
3.  Click **Download**.
    - If you have supported software installed, you can also download the file and work locally.

Upload a file

1.  On your computer, open [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Click **New**.
3.  Click **File Upload**.
4.  Upload the file.

#### Import your file's data from a new spreadsheet (Google Sheets only)

**Important:** Any formulas from the original Sheet won’t be imported to your new Sheets after you use IMPORTRANGE().

To import your data to a new spreadsheet and preserve your data, use IMPORTRANGE if:

- The issue appears on Google Sheets.
- After you create a copy of your file in Drive, it still doesn't work.

To use IMPORTRANGE(), you need to know your original file’s URL and tab name.

#### Import read-only Sheet (Google Sheets only)

**Important:** If a Google Sheets file is too large to edit, it becomes read-only.

To continue to edit this document:

- Copy individual tabs to a new spreadsheet. To create a new spreadsheet, click **Copy to** and then **New spreadsheet**.
- Download the spreadsheet or copy and paste the information from the current spreadsheet into a new document.

## Help us improve & find help in the community

If you still see reload errors, you can ask for help or report the problem.

- If you still have any questions, you can visit the [Google Docs Editors Help Community](https://support.google.com/docs/thread/new) to ask for help.
- To report an issue, open your Docs, Sheets, Slides file and at the top, click **Help** and then **Help us improve**.
