# Contact Google Drive support

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Get help with common problems below. If you need help with a different Google Drive problem, contact us.

### Find solutions to common problems

**Important:** This article details the current retention policy. This is also in the latest version of Google Photos app.

- **Find or recover a file:** If you can't find a file, [search for it in Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/1716222). If you think you deleted the file, you can recover it from:
  - **Your Google Drive trash:** To restore a file from Drive trash, you must restore it within 30 days before it’s permanently deleted.
  - **A** **local device:** To recover uploaded files you delete directly from your computer or phone storage, check your device’s Recycle Bin or Trash.
  - **Google Photos:**
    - Photos and videos that you delete will stay in your trash for 30 days before they are permanently deleted.
    - Google Drive support can't recover photos.
    - Get help from the [Google Photos Help Center](https://support.google.com/photos/topic/6128818).
- **Share files:** [Learn how to share files and folders](https://support.google.com/drive/topic/2375188).
- **Use Drive for desktop:** [Get help with Drive for desktop](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2565956).

#### Contact us

If you still have a problem, you can contact us via our forum, feedback, or support service.

You’re not signed into a Google Account. To request file recovery, log into your Google Account and reload this article.

[Sign into your Google Account](https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/drive/answer/1716222)

#### Ask your question in the forum

Visit the [help forum](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/drive) to ask a question or search for an answer to your question, available in [English](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/drive), [Spanish](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/docs-es), and [Portuguese](https://productforums.google.com/forum/#!forum/docs-pt).

#### Submit user feedback

If you've discovered a new bug or technical issue, report the issue right from Google Drive.

1.  Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Click Help Help .
3.  Click **Send feedbac**k.
4.  Enter your feedback, then click **Send**.
