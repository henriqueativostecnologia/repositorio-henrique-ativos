# Comment on Microsoft Office files, PDFs, images, and other files

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can leave and read comments on Microsoft Office files, PDFs, images, and other files in Google Drive.

**Note:** When you review a video file, you can add comments to the entire file or add them to specific timestamps. [Learn how to comment on a video in Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2423694#comment_on_video).

### Comment on Microsoft Office files

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Drive](https://drive.google.com).
2.  To open a Microsoft Office file, double click.
3.  At the top right, click Add comment Add comment .
4.  Click in the area you want to comment on.
5.  Enter your comment.
6.  Click **Comment**.

**Tip:** When you leave a comment on a Microsoft Office file in Drive, the comment also displays in Microsoft Office.

##### Comment on PDFs

Comments you make on PDFs can show up in some PDF-viewing apps.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Double-click the PDF you want to comment on.
3.  At the top right, click Add comment Add comment .
4.  Select the area you want to comment on.
5.  Enter your comment and click **Comment**.

##### Comment on images

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Double-click the image you want to comment on.
3.  At the top right, click Add comment Add comment .
4.  Click and drag over the area you want to comment on.
5.  Enter your comment and click **Comment**.

##### Comment on video, audio, and other files

When you make a comment on video, audio, and other files, the comments will only be available in Google Drive.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Double-click the file you want to comment on.
3.  At the top right, click Add comment Add comment .
4.  Click the text, cell, or section you want to comment on.
5.  Enter your comment and click **Comment**.

#### Resolve conflicts between versions of the same file 

If someone uploads a new version of the file, it will replace the existing file in Google Drive. Any comments added outside of Google Drive will replace comments made in Drive. Any edits made outside of Google Drive will also remove all resolved or inactive comments.

### How sharing affects files

When you share Microsoft Office files, PDFs, images, or other file types with someone, you can choose what they can do with it.

- **View**: People can view comments, but can’t make new comments or share the file with others.
- **Comment**: People can make comments and suggestions, but can’t change or share the file with others.
- **Edit**: People can make comments and changes to the file, accept or reject suggestions, and share the file with others.

[Learn how sharing affects Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Forms](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822#view_comment_edit)

### Comment on Microsoft Office files

When you leave a comment on a Microsoft Office file, the comments will still appear when you open the file in Microsoft Office.

If you don’t have Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides installed on your Android phone or tablet:

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the [Google Drive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs) app.
2.  Tap the file you want to comment on.
3.  At the top, tap Comment Comment and then **New**.
4.  Select the text, cell, or area you want to comment on.
5.  Enter your comment and tap **Comment**.

If you have Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides installed, you can read and make comments there. [Learn more about commenting in Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/65129?co%3DGENIE.Platform%253DAndroid%26oco%3D1)

3.  At the top right, tap Add comment Comment and then **New**.
4.  Tap an area to comment and tap **Add comment**.

You can comment on specific sections of a PDF, image, or Powerpoint presentation.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the [Google Drive](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/google-drive/id507874739) app.
2.  Tap the PDF, Powerpoint, or image you want to comment on.
3.  At the top right, tap Add comment Comment and then **New**.
4.  Tap where you want to add the comment. You can move your comment box by tapping and dragging.
    - To comment on specific text, tap and hold the text  and then  **Add comment**​.
5.  Tap **Add comment here**.
6.  Enter your comment and tap Done Done .

To see comments, at the top right, tap Add comment Comment .
