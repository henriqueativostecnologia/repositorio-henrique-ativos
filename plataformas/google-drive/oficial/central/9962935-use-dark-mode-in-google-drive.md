# Use Dark Mode in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, Computer, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can change your theme setting to make it easier to view files on your device.

You can use the Dark Mode setting to help save battery life.

1.  On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Drive app Google Drive icon .
2.  At the top left, tap Menu Menu .
3.  Tap Settings Settings .
4.  Tap **Choose theme**.
5.  Choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **System default**.

## Version: Computer

You can change your theme setting to make it easier to view files on your device.

To turn Dark Mode for web on or off in Google Drive:

1.  On your computer, [go to Google Drive](http://drive.google.com).
2.  At the upper right, click Settings Settings .
3.  Under “Appearance,” choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Device default**.
    - **Light:** Light background with dark text
    - **Dark:** Dark background with light text
    - **Device default:** Automatically match the color scheme of your device

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can change your theme setting to make it easier to view files on your device.

You can use the Dark Mode setting to help save battery life.

**Important:** Dark Mode is only available on iOS 13 and up.

### Turn on Dark Mode

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, [turn on Dark Mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT210332).
2.  Open the Google Drive app Google Drive icon .
