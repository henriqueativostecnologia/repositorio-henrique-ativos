# Organize, save, and share your Gemini sources using projects in Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important**: To use Gemini in Drive, you must have an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn more about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/13952129?hl=en&co=DASHER._Family%3DBusiness-Enterprise).

**Note:**

- You can only create new Drive projects on the web (drive.google.com).
- On the Drive mobile app (Android and iOS), you can:
  - Open and view existing projects,
  - View project summaries and conversation history,
  - Ask Gemini questions about the project sources. Project sources are view-only on mobile. You cannot add or remove sources when using the mobile app.

While a [*standard Gemini conversation in Drive*](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16963068#ask_gemini_in_drive) saves your conversation to history, creating a project locks in a specific set of source files, folders, or emails. This allows you to have multiple unique conversations using the exact same foundation of documents or emails, and makes it possible to share the project with your team while maintaining the original location of your source files and folders.

In this article, you’ll learn how to:

#### Save your current conversation as a project

1.  In your full-screen **Ask Gemini** view, click **Save as a project** at the top right.
2.  Gemini will automatically suggest a project name based on your sources, which you can click to edit.

**Tip**: Creating a project does not move or change the location or permissions of your original Drive files.

#### Create a project from the New menu

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/)

2.  At the top left, click **New** and then **New Project**.
    1.  **Tip**: Alternatively, you can go to [project.new](https://project.new/) to create a new project.

3.  Enter a name for your project (e.g., "Q3 Financial Analysis").

4.  Select files, folders, or emails from Gemini’s recommendations, or explicitly search for your own files, folders, or emails by clicking **Add More sources**.

5.  Click **Create Project**.

#### Resume and refine your project research

Gemini automatically saves your conversation history so you can pick up your work later without losing context.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  At the top left, click **Projects**.
3.  Select an existing project.
4.  In the left panel, click **History**. Project specific conversations will appear under **Suggested**.
5.  Continue the conversation where you left off.
    - **Tip**: If you allow Gemini to search beyond your pinned sources and pull from broader Workspace context, Gemini will cite relevant documents in its response. On any citation card, click Add as source to permanently pin that document to your project sources for all future prompts.

#### Use your projects across Google Workspace

**Important**: Using Drive projects across Google Workspace apps (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Chat, Meet and Gmail) requires access to Gemini Beta, which is currently limited to work and school accounts. If you cannot find this feature, your administrator may need to [enable Beta features for your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14170809).

You can reference your Drive projects as a unified context source across Google Workspace apps, including Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Gmail.

##### @mention a project in Gemini side panels and bottom bars

1.  In any Gemini side panel or bottom bar across Google Workspace, click the prompt box.
2.  Type @ followed by the project name.
3.  From the suggestions menu, select your project. A project smart chip will appear in your prompt.
4.  Enter your question or prompt, then press Enter. Gemini references all sources bundled in the project to answer.

**Tip:** Hover over a project smart chip to view a preview card showing the project title, owner, and last modified date.

##### Select a project from the Drive file picker

1.  In the Gemini side panel or bottom bar, click **Add sources** or the icon, then choose **Add from Drive**.
2.  In the Drive file picker window, click the **Projects** tab.
3.  Select the project you want to use as your context source, then click **Insert**.

#### Share a project and manage permissions

You can share a project with your team to work together, streamline onboarding, or collaborate on unified research.

##### How sharing permissions work

- **Your conversation history remains private:**

  - When you share a project, you only share the core list of source files. Collaborators will start their own blank conversations with Gemini.

  - If you want to share specific Gemini outputs, copy the text or export it to a Google Doc before sharing with your team.

- **If collaborators can't access the source, they can't have a Gemini conversation about it:**
  - Collaborators must already possess Drive permissions to view the underlying files grouped in the project.
  - If a collaborator does not have permission to view a source file in their regular Drive, they will not be able to view or reference its content in the Gemini conversation.

- **Gmail sources you add are private to you:**
  - You can add specific Gmail emails as sources. These emails are private to you.
  - If you share a project that includes your emails, project editors (but not viewers) can see the number of emails you attached. However, they cannot see the subject lines or read the email bodies, and none of your email content will be used for their Gemini prompts.

#### Manage Collaborator Roles

- **Editor**: Can have a conversation with Gemini, add or remove source files from the master list (web only), and share the project with others.
- **Viewer**: Can have a conversation with Gemini and temporarily turn existing sources on or off for their own analysis. They cannot add or remove files from the project list.
- **Set an expiration**: To automatically end a user's access on a specific date, select Add expiration in the sharing menu.

  - Open and view existing projects,
  - View project summaries and conversation history,
  - Ask Gemini questions about the project sources. Project sources are view-only on mobile. You cannot add or remove sources when using the mobile app.

While a [*standard Gemini conversation in Drive*](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16963068#ask_gemini_in_drive) saves your conversation to history, creating a project locks in a specific set of source files, folders, or emails. This allows you to have multiple unique conversations using the exact same foundation of documents or emails, and makes it possible to share the project with your team while maintaining the original location of your source files and folders.

### Resume a project

1.  Open the Google Drive app.
2.  At the top left, tap the left navigation menu , or tap **Ask Gemini** and then At the top left, tap More options
3.  Select Projects to view all your projects or select a project from the Recent Projects list to view the project summary, sources, and conversation history.

&nbsp;

4.  After selecting a project, to resume an existing conversation, at the top left, tap the left navigation menu to see your conversation history for this project
5.  Select an existing conversation

**Tip**: sources are view-only. You must use the web version of Drive ([*drive.google.com*](https://drive.google.com)) to add or remove files from the project or to @mention projects across Google Workspace.
