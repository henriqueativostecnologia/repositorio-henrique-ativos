# Add & customize your Google Drive widget

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can quickly access files, scan, search, or upload to Google Drive with Drive widgets on your Android and iOS devices. 

### Add the Google Drive widget to your home screen

1.  On your Android device, tap and hold the home screen.
2.  At the bottom, tap **Widgets**.
3.  Tap Drive Google Drive icon .
4.  To select a widget, tap **Drive quick actions**, **Drive suggested files**, **Drive scan**, or **Drive shortcut**.
5.  Tap Add Add .
    - To add a Drive shortcut, tap the Drive folder you want to add.

**Tip:** You can move or resize a widget once it’s added.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can quickly access files, scan, search, or upload to Google Drive with Drive widgets on your Android and iOS devices. 

### Add the Google Drive widget to your home screen

**Important:** This feature is only available on iOS 14 and up and iPadOS 14 and up.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, tap and hold the home screen.
2.  In the top left, tap Add Add .
3.  Search for the Google Drive app and tap it.
4.  To select widget size, swipe right or left.
5.  Tap **Add widget**.
6.  Place the widget on your home screen and, in the top right, tap **Done**.

### Turn off suggested files

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, go to the home screen.
2.  Tap and hold the Google Drive widget.
3.  Tap **Edit widget**.
4.  Turn "Show suggested files" off Off .
