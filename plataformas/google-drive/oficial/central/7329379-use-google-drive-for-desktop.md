# Use Google Drive for desktop

**Looking for more advanced controls?** Dive into topics like customizing settings, using offline access, and managing photos photos backups in our [advanced Drive for desktop guide](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16631477). 

**Important:** Your computer space and Google storage are not the same: 

- The space tracker in Drive for desktop shows the room left on your computer's hard drive, not your Google Account storage.

- Streaming a file uses almost no computer space, but mirroring downloads a full copy directly to your computer.

- Shared files can fill up your hard drive if you sync them, but they never count against your Google space. [Understand your computer and Google storage when using Drive for Desktop](17196458).

## Learn about Google Drive for desktop benefits

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96qNcgGKmvk

You can easily work with your files with Google Drive for desktop. You can also:

- **Keep local files safe:** Your computer files are securely stored in the cloud.
- **Keep files up-to-date across surfaces:** Any edits you make to files sync automatically to your computer and to Google Drive.
- **Have a familiar desktop experience:** Access all your Google Drive files and folders like any other drive on your computer.
- **Interact with Microsoft Office files:** When you use Google Drive for desktop with Microsoft Office, you can:
  - [Work in Microsoft Office & sync files to Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/9406611#work-in-office-and-sync)
  - [Work in Microsoft Office files with others in real time](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/9406611#Work_Office_Files_Real_Time)
  - [Send & save files with Microsoft Outlook](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/9406611#send-and-save-files)
- **Use files offline:** [Work on files and folders even without the internet](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375012#files_offline_drive_web). This includes files from shared drives.

## Install Google Drive for desktop

#### Install on Windows

**Important:** Before you start, check that your [operating system is compatible with Google Drive for desktop](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375082#D4desktop).

1.  Download Google Drive for desktop:

    [DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS](https://dl.google.com/drive-file-stream/GoogleDriveSetup.exe)

2.  Open "GoogleDriveSetup.exe."

3.  Follow the on-screen instructions.

**Tip:** If you use a work or school account, you might not be able to use Google Drive for desktop or your organization might have to install it for you. If you have questions, [ask your administrator](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6208960).

On Google Drive for desktop, at the bottom right, in the system tray, you can find the Google Drive for desktop menu Drive File Stream .

**Tip:** To ”Show hidden icons,” click the arrow.

To make it easier to find Google Drive for desktop when it's closed, you can pin it.

- **To add Google Drive to the Start menu:** In your Start menu, right click **Google Drive** and then **Pin to Start**.
- **To add Google Drive to the taskbar:** In your Start menu, right click **Google Drive** and then **Pin to Taskbar**.

#### Install on macOS

**Important:** Before you start, check that your [operating system is compatible with Google Drive for desktop](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375082#D4desktop).

1.  Download Google Drive for desktop:

    [DOWNLOAD FOR MAC](https://dl.google.com/drive-file-stream/GoogleDrive.dmg)

2.  Open “GoogleDrive.dmg.”

3.  Follow the on-screen instructions.

**Tip:** If you use a work or school account, you might not be able to use Google Drive for desktop. Your organization must install it for you. If you have questions, [ask your administrator](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6208960).

On Google Drive for desktop, at the top right, in the menu bar, you can find the Google Drive for desktop menu Drive File Stream .

To make it easier to find Google Drive for desktop when it's closed, you can pin it.

- **To add Google Drive to your Dock:** In the “Applications” folder, drag the Google Drive app to the left side of the recently used apps separator line.

## Sign in to Google Drive for desktop

### Sign in for the first time

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CC0QDx8Hn8I

For this same video with audio descriptions, go to [Get started with Google Drive for Desktop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v2VVG3F0h1A).

When you open Google Drive for desktop for the first time, or after your account has been disconnected, to log in:

1.  On your computer, open Google Drive for desktop Drive File Stream .
2.  Click **Get started** and then **Sign in**.
3.  Sign in to the Google Account you want to use with Google Drive for desktop.

**Tip:** You can use up to 4 accounts at one time with Google Drive for desktop. [Learn how to use multiple accounts at the same time](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/13470231#zippy=%2Cuse-multiple-accounts-at-the-same-time).

## Use multiple accounts at the same time

You can use up to 4 accounts at one time with Google Drive for desktop.

### Add an account

1.  On your computer, click the Google Drive for desktop menu Drive File Stream .
2.  In the top right-hand corner, click your profile picture and then + Add account.
3.  Sign in through your browser.
4.  Restart Google Drive for desktop.

### Disconnect an account

**Important:** If a streaming account is disconnected, any offline files are removed.

1.  On your computer, click the Google Drive for desktop menu Drive File Stream .
2.  Click your profile picture and then **Disconnect account**.
3.  In the pop-up window, click **OK**.

## Navigate key features in Google Drive for desktop

1.  **Check sync status:** On your homepage, the new "Sync status" tile displays your recently synced files and current sync activity. It replaces the previous "Activity" view.
2.  **Find detailed sync activity:** A comprehensive list of all recent sync activity.
3.  **Get notifications:** Important updates, like time-sensitive sync errors, show in your notifications. 
4.  **Use enhanced search:** Search directly within Google Drive for desktop to find all files from your streamed Google Drive location, unlike Windows Search or macOS Spotlight. 
    - **Tip:** For a quick search, use a search hotkey combination. The default values for the search hotkey are:
      - **Windows:** **Ctrl + Alt + g**.
      - **macOS:** \[Command icon\] **+** \[Accent icon\] **+** **g**. You can set up your hotkey in advanced settings.
5.  **Have simple sync control:** You can pause or resume the sync process of Google Drive for desktop at any time.
6.  **Add new folders to sync:** Sync more folders on your computer with Google Drive.
7.  **Access your synced files:** Once your files are synced, you can access them from two main places:
    - **Through the Drive for desktop app:** Click the folder icon to browse all your Drive files. You can also use the search bar in the app to find a specific file. 
    - **Directly on your computer:** Open File Explorer (on Windows) or Finder (on macOS) and navigate to the Google Drive folder. Here you will find "My Drive," "Shared drives," and other synced folders.

To open a file, double-click it. Files in Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides open in your web browser, while other files open in their default desktop applications.

**Tip:** If your Google Drive and "My Drive" folder are empty, you won’t find the "Shared drives" or "Other computers" views.
