# Use Google Drive with Yahoo Mail

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: iPhone & iPad, Android phone & tablet, Computer. All versions are copied below.

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can send photos, documents, and other files saved in Google Drive using Yahoo Mail.

Before you can attach files, you’ll have to [connect Google Drive to the Yahoo Mail app](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/sln27288.html).

### Share files with Google Drive

- When you share a file with someone, they will only be able to view it. [You can change the way files are shared from Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822).
- You can always [stop sharing a file or change who can view it](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494893).

### Remove Yahoo Mail from Google Drive

You can remove Yahoo Mail from Google Drive at any time.

1.  On a computer, sign in to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Click Settings Settings and then  **Settings**.
3.  Click **Manage Apps**.
4.  Next to "Yahoo Mail," click **Options**.
5.  Click **Disconnect from Drive**.
