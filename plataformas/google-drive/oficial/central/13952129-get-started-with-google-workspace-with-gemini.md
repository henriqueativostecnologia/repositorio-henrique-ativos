# Get started with Google Workspace with Gemini

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Business / Enterprise, Education. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Business / Enterprise

With Google Workspace with Gemini, you can use AI-assisted features to draft emails, revise documents, and more.

In this article:

- [What you need to use Google Workspace with Gemini](#requirements)
- [Learn about Google Workspace with Gemini features](#features)
- [Compare Gemini features in Google Workspace](#workspace-compare)
- [Compare Gemini features in Google AI plans](#ai-plan-compare)
- [Learn about Gemini Apps & Gemini Notebook](#apps-notebook)
- [Frequently asked questions](#faq)

### What you need to use Google Workspace with Gemini

- Be 18 or over.
- An eligible Google Account:
  - If you have a work or school Google Account, you can get access to AI-powered features with an eligible Google Workspace plan.
  - If you have a personal Google Account, you can get access to AI-powered features with:
    - [Google AI Plus](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16882689)
    - [Google AI Pro](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/14534406)
    - [Google AI Ultra](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16286513)

##### Additional requirements

- Some AI features may not be available in your language. If you can’t use a feature in your language, you can switch to a supported language in your Google Account. [Learn more about supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14925782).
- If you have a personal Google Account, you can use these Gemini in Gmail features at no charge in the US:
  - Help me write
  - AI Overviews for email threads
  - Suggested Replies

- To check if you have access to Google Workspace with Gemini, look for the Gemini icon in a Workspace app. For example, at the top right of Gmail or Docs, click Ask Gemini .
- You can also [contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).

### Frequently asked questions

##### How does Google protect my data?

The addition of these AI features includes the same robust data protection and security standards that come with all Google Workspace services:

- Users get enterprise-grade data protections when they use the Gemini app. Submissions aren't reviewed by humans or otherwise used to train models.
- Your interactions with Gemini in Google Workspace apps stay within your organization. Prompts and content you generate with these features is stored alongside your Workspace content and is never shared outside your organization.
- Your existing Google Workspace protections are automatically applied. These AI features bring the same enterprise-grade security as the rest of Google Workspace, automatically applying your organization’s existing controls and data handling practices, such as data-regions policies and Data Loss Prevention.
- Your content is not used for any other customers. None of your content is used for model training outside of your domain without permission.

Learn how Google Workspace [keeps your data secure and prioritizes privacy](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub).

If you have a Google AI plan, [learn how Gemini protects your data](14615114).

##### Can I control access to these AI features?

You have the option to turn off some AI features. [Learn how to turn Google Workspace smart features on or off](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/15604322).

In addition, if you have a work or school account, your administrator can also:

- [Turn off access to the Gemini App](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/gemini-app/turn-the-gemini-app-on-or-off).
- Turn off Gemini Notebook as an [additional service](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/users/advanced/turn-on-or-off-additional-google-services).
- Stop users from [letting Google AI take notes in meetings](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/meet/let-google-meet-ai-take-notes-for-my-users).

Your existing Google Workspace data protections are automatically applied to [AI features](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub).

If you have a Google AI plan, [learn how Gemini protects your data](14615114).

##### Are there tips for using Gemini effectively?

You can help improve your success with Gemini with these [basic best practices](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14151066):

- [Learn how to write good prompts for Gemini](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14200040).
- Use this [cheat sheet for an overview of Gemini features](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14143478).

### Learn about Gemini Apps & Gemini Notebook

##### Gemini Apps

- You can chat with the Gemini web app at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/) or the [Gemini mobile app](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14571493) on Android and iOS. You can use the Gemini app to:
  - Brainstorm ideas, develop a plan, or find different ways to get things done.
  - Get a quick summary of more complex topics.
  - Create first drafts of outlines, emails, blog posts, and more.
- [Learn about Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745).

##### Gemini Notebook

- Gemini Notebook is an AI-powered research assistant that lets you upload sources and then ask questions, summarize information, generate new ideas, and listen to Audio Overviews based on those sources. [Learn more about Gemini Notebook](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16164461).

### Frequently asked questions

##### How does Google protect my data?

The addition of these AI features includes the same robust data protection and security standards that come with all Google Workspace services:

- Users get enterprise-grade data protections when they use the Gemini app. Submissions aren't reviewed by humans or otherwise used to train models.
- Your interactions with Gemini in Google Workspace apps stay within your organization. Prompts and content you generate with these features is stored alongside your Workspace content and is never shared outside your organization.
- Your existing Google Workspace protections are automatically applied. These AI features bring the same enterprise-grade security as the rest of Google Workspace, automatically applying your organization’s existing controls and data handling practices, such as data-regions policies and Data Loss Prevention.
- Your content is not used for any other customers. None of your content is used for model training outside of your domain without permission.

Learn how Google Workspace [keeps your data secure and prioritizes privacy](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub).

If you have a Google AI plan, [learn how Gemini protects your data](14615114).

##### Can I control access to these AI features?

You have the option to turn off some AI features. [Learn how to turn Google Workspace smart features on or off](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/15604322).

In addition, if you have a work or school account, your administrator can also:

- [Turn off access to the Gemini App](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/gemini-app/turn-the-gemini-app-on-or-off).
- Turn off Gemini Notebook as an [additional service](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/users/advanced/turn-on-or-off-additional-google-services).
- Stop users from [letting Google AI take notes in meetings](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/meet/let-google-meet-ai-take-notes-for-my-users).

Your existing Google Workspace data protections are automatically applied to [AI features](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub).

If you have a Google AI plan, [learn how Gemini protects your data](14615114).

##### Are there tips for using Gemini effectively?

You can help improve your success with Gemini with these [basic best practices](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14151066):

- [Learn how to write good prompts for Gemini](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14200040).
- Use this [cheat sheet for an overview of Gemini features](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/14143478).
