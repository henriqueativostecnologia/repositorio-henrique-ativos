# Let Gemini organize your files

**Important:** This feature is only available for Drive web ([drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)) in English.

To save time and reduce clutter, Gemini can help organize loose files in your Drive. Based on file content, your organizing patterns, and general organizing best practices, Gemini will suggest moving files into existing folders or creating new folders for you.

To understand how your data is used and what to expect from Gemini suggestions, [learn more about disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961#pn_what_data).

## Let Gemini help you organize your files

**Important:**

- Eligible files include PDFs, Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Microsoft Office files, images, and videos with transcripts. Gemini can also organize shortcuts.
- You must have Editor access to the files.

To organize your files with Gemini:

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  Open My Drive, or a folder.
    - If you have files that can be organized, the "Suggest file moves" button will appear.
3.  Click **Suggest file moves**.
    - Gemini will analyze your loose files and suggest moving them into existing or new folders. Gemini will not move any files without your permission.
4.  Review Gemini's suggestions. You can:
    - **Refine suggestions:** If you want to adjust how your files are grouped, at the top of the list, click Refine Filter icon . You can:
      - **Refine with a prompt:** Enter specific instructions to customize the organization, such as “Group these by year.”
      - **Organize by project:** Automatically group files based on project context.
      - **Organize meeting notes:** Specific grouping for notes and agendas.
      - **Adjust folder creation:** To change the granularity, select **More new folders** or **Fewer new folders**.
      - **Shorten folder name:** Make suggested folder names more concise.
      - **Try again:** Generate new suggestions.
    - **Rename a suggested new folder:** Hover over the suggested folder name and click Rename , you will be prompted to type a new name.
      - If the folder appears in the list of recommendations for more than one file, you’re prompted to rename those folders as well. To rename the folder, click Rename folders and then **Accept**.
    - **Change folder destination:**
      1.  Under "Suggested location," hover over the suggested folder and click Change location .
      2.  Next to the folder where you want to move the item, click **Move** and then **Move**.
          - The item you moved is temporarily grayed out.
      3.  To dismiss the notification, click Close Close .
    - **Accept move & suggestions:**
      1.  Check the box next to each file you want to move.
      2.  At the top of the list, click **Move files**.
      3.  A dialog box will appear to confirm. Click **Create and move**.
    - **Reject suggestion:** To remove a suggestion, click Remove Close .
      - If you reject a suggestion, Gemini won’t suggest that file or folder pair again for 30 days.
5.  A confirmation message will appear at the bottom left after your files are moved.

**Tips:**

- If you move files by mistake, you can click **Undo** in the confirmation message that appears at the bottom of your screen after the files are moved.
  - Windows keyboard shortcut: ctrl + z
  - MacOS keyboard shortcut: command + z
- New folders will not be deleted when you undo a move to prevent potential data loss.
