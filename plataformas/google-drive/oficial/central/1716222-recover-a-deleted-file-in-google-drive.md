# Recover a deleted file in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important:** You can use this article to recover files you delete from your Google Drive account.

You’re not signed into a Google Account. To request file recovery, log into your Google Account and reload this article.

[Sign into your Google Account](https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/drive/answer/1716222)

### Get help with a WhatsApp backup

Your WhatsApp backup is managed by WhatsApp, even when stored on your Google Account. This includes the end-to-end encryption that protects your backup.

Because of this encryption, Google cannot view, access, or restore your messages, photos, or other media from your WhatsApp backup file.

For any issues with your backup, such as restoring it to a new phone or recovering media, visit the [WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/)

###  Restore a file from Google Drive Trash

1.  Go to [drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com).
2.  On the left, click **Trash**.
    - All your deleted files are listed in “Trash.”
    - To find out how long ago files were added to the “Trash,” you can sort files by their “Trashed date” date.
3.  To restore a file:
    1.  Right-click the file.
    2.  Click **Restore**.

**Tip:** Files remain in Trash for 30 days, then they’re deleted forever.

###  Restore a file from Google Drive Trash 

If you deleted something recently using Google Drive or the Google Drive desktop app, you might be able to restore the file yourself.

#### Restore from your Trash

1.  Open your Google Drive App.
2.   Next to the file you want to recover, tap Menu  More
3.  Tap **Restore**.
