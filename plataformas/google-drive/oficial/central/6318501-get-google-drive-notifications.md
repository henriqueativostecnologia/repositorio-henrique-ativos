# Get Google Drive notifications

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can choose whether to get mobile, web, or email notifications from Google Drive for activity and comments in Google Docs, Drawings, Sheets, or Slides.

### Notification types

You can choose to be notified if:

- Someone shares a new file or folder with you.
- You’re mentioned in a comment or action item.
- Someone requests access to a file you own.

###

### Change notification settings

If you use Google Drive on more than one computer, you‘ll need to change these settings on each computer.

1.  Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  In the top right, click Settings Settings and then **Settings**.
3.  In the left panel, click **Notifications**.
4.  Next to the settings you want to change, check or uncheck the box.

###

Learn more about how to manage [notifications for specific Google Docs, Drawings, Sheets and Slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/91588).

## Version: Android phone & tablet

You can choose whether to get mobile, web, or email notifications from Google Drive for activity and comments in Google Docs, Drawings, Sheets, or Slides.

### Notification types

You can choose to be notified if:

- Someone shares a new file or folder with you.
- You’re mentioned in a comment or action item.
- Someone requests access to a file you own.

###

### Access Drive notifications

1.  Open the [Google Drive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs) app.
2.  Tap **Priority** or **Home**.
3.  At the top, tap **Notifications**.

### Change notification settings

1.  Open the [Google Drive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs) app.
2.  In the top left, tap Menu  Menu   and then  **Settings**.
3.  Tap **Notification settings**.
4.  Choose which settings you'd like to change.

**Tip:** If you change these settings in the Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides apps, they will also change for Google Drive.

###

Learn more about how to manage [notifications for specific Google Docs, Drawings, Sheets and Slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/91588).

## Version: iPhone & iPad

You can choose whether to get mobile, web, or email notifications from Google Drive for activity and comments in Google Docs, Drawings, Sheets, or Slides.

### Notification types

You can choose to be notified if:

- Someone shares a new file or folder with you.
- You’re mentioned in a comment or action item.
- Someone requests access to a file you own.

###

### Change notification settings

1.  Open the [Google Drive](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/google-drive-free-online-storage/id507874739) app.
2.  In the top left, tap Menu Menu and then Settings Settings .
3.  Tap **Notifications**.
4.  Turn Notifications Enabled on or off.
    - If you enable notifications in your device settings, you can turn on the following:
      - Shared items
      - Request for access
      - Comments
      - Approvals
5.  Choose which settings you'd like to change.

###

Learn more about how to manage [notifications for specific Google Docs, Drawings, Sheets and Slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/91588).
