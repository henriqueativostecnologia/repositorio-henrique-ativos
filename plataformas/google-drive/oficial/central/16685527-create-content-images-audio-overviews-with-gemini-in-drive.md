# Create Content, Images & Audio Overviews with Gemini in Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

**Important**: To use Gemini in Drive, you must have an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn more about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/13952129?hl=en&co=DASHER._Family%3DBusiness-Enterprise).

### Generate a Google Docs document

You can use Ask Gemini to create a Google Docs document.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Enter a prompt, for example:
    1.  *“Create a meeting notes agenda”*
    2.  *“Generate a draft with a response to this email”*
    3.  *“Create a quick project coordination doc with a 30/60/90 plan section”.*
4.  Click **Submit**.

### Generate a Google Slides presentation

You can use Ask Gemini to create a fully editable presentation in Google Slides with a single prompt.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Enter a prompt, for example:
    1.  *"Create a slide on quantum computing for a general audience with a modern design."*
    2.  **Optional**:
        1.  To give your presentation the same style as an existing slide deck, you can use *@\<presentation name\>* to reference it. For example: *“Create a presentation based on @old_project”.*
        2.  To refine your presentation plan, you can answer Gemini’s follow-up questions or clarifications. These questions will differ based on your topic or references you provided. You can skip these questions.
4.  Click **Submit**. Gemini generates a presentation plan.
5.  Once satisfied with the plan, click **Approve**.
6.  Keep the tab open until Gemini generates your presentation. You can switch to other tabs or windows but do not close the existing tab.
7.  Once the presentation is generated, you can open it in Google Slides to view and edit. [Learn more about how to Generate presentations with Gemini in Google Slides](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/17111393).

### Generate a Google Sheets spreadsheet

You can use Ask Gemini to create a Google Sheets spreadsheet.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Enter a prompt, for example:
    1.  *"Create a sheet to track my monthly expenses with columns for date, category, amount, and notes."*
    2.  *"Create a table for a project tracker in Google Sheets with columns for task, assignee, deadline, and status."*
    3.  *"Create a 10-day itinerary for a first-time visitor covering Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka."*
    4.  *"Create a visual dashboard in Sheets of this sales data."*
4.  Click **Submit**.

### Export Gemini's output to Docs or Sheets

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Ask for a table, proposal, or formatted list.
4.  Once generated, click the **Export to Docs** or **Export to Sheets** icon.
    1.  Note: Any file created via Ask Gemini's immersive workspace automatically saves to your My Drive root folder.

### Create Audio Overviews with Gemini

**Important:** Audio Overview generation only supports a single file at a time. If you select multiple files, a folder, or a Drive project, the generation will fail.

You can use natural language to generate an Audio Overview from your files.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Type a prompt to create an audio overview, such as "*Generate an audio overview for the @Monthly Report."*
4.  You can also generate an overview directly from a file. To generate an overview:
    1.  Select the file from the list in your Google Drive.
    2.  Right click the file and select **Create audio overview** **.**
5.  Follow the prompts to customize your audio file, such as its length and output format.
6.  To play your audio overview, wait for it to finish generating, and then click click Play .
7.  Once the audio overview is generated, you will receive an email with the file link.
    - All audio overviews are saved in the "Audio overviews" folder in My Drive.
    - Gemini will generate a name for the Audio overview based on the content.

If you refresh the page or close the side panel or the tab before the generation is done, the file doesn’t generate.

You can generate an overview directly from the following file types:

- PDF files.
- Google Docs
- Google Slides
- Microsoft Office Word Document
- Microsoft Office Powerpoint Presentation
- Plain Text files
- Videos

**Tips**:

- You can only generate audio overviews on the web, but you can access them on the web, iOS, and Android.
- You can only generate 20 audio overviews per day.

### Create Images with Gemini

To create images:

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** .
3.  Click **Tools** and then **Create Images**
4.  Type a prompt.

**Important:** The features below are available for

- Gemini Alpha, which is available for work and school accounts only. Your administrator controls access to Gemini Alpha work and school accounts. If you can't find this feature, your administrator may not have [Alpha features enabled for your organization](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14170809).
- [Google AI Ultra and Google AI Pro personal accounts.](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)
- Google Workspace Experiments, which is a trusted tester program for personal accounts to try new AI features. [Learn more about Workspace Experiments.](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/13447104)
- Desktop and in English only.

### Use a skill with Gemini

**Important:** Before you can use a skill in Drive, you must create and turn it on in [Workspace Studio](https://studio.workspace.google.com).

To analyze and extract insights from your files, use skills in Google Drive.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  At the top right, click Ask Gemini .
3.  In the message field, enter `@` followed by the skill name. For example, “@Project Summary.”
4.  Enter your prompt.
5.  Click Submit .

**Tip:** To combine information across multiple documents within a folder and generate a summary report, use the Project Summary skill.
