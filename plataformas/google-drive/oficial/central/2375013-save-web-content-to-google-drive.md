# Save web content to Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can save images, videos, audio, PDFs, and other files you find on the web on Google Drive with the "Save to Google" Chrome extension.

Keep [Google program policies](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/148505) and [copyright laws](https://support.google.com/legal/topic/4558877) in mind whenever you save something to your Google Drive from the web.

### Get the "Save to Google Drive" Chrome extension

You can download the "Save to Google Drive" extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/save-to-google-drive/gmbmikajjgmnabiglmofipeabaddhgne).

### Save items or webpages

1.  On your computer, open Google Chrome.
2.  Open a webpage with content you want to save.
    - **Save a link, image, HTML5 audio, or video:** Right-click on the item to save and click **Save \[item\] to Google Drive**.
    - **Save the webpage:** In the top right, click Google Drive Google Drive .

#### Cancel a download

In the download window, click **Cancel**.

#### Change how you save web content

In the upper right corner, right-click Save to Google Drive Google Drive and then  **Options**.
