# Change your card & fix payment issues

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

If you have issues with Google One payments, try these solutions. You can also update your Google One payment methods.

If you're ever locked out of your Google Account, recovery options can help you get back in. [Learn more about account recovery](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/15165466).

### Change the card you use

You can edit the credit card you use for your Google One membership. If you want to change how much you pay, [change your storage plan](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9003633).

1.  On your computer, go to [Google One](https://one.google.com/).
2.  Click Settings Settings .
3.  Click **Change payment method**.
4.  In Google Play, on the left, click **My subscriptions**.
5.  Next to "Google One," click **Manage.**
6.  Next to "Primary payment method," click **Update**.
7.  Select an option and follow the steps on the screen.

### Get help buying a Google One subscription

If you had difficulty signing up for Google One for the first time, you can [ask a Google Expert](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9269729) to help you.

### Fix issues with payments

If you find errors when you make transactions, try these troubleshooting steps.

##### Fix a declined payment

1.  Sign in to [Google Pay](https://payments.google.com/).
2.  Click **Update payment method information**.
3.  Choose a payment method.
4.  Click **Continue**.
5.  Fill out new payment method.

**Tip:** Declined payments are sometimes caused by expired cards. If your card has expired, you can update the expiration date or add a new payment method.

##### Change, update, or add payment method

If you recently added a new credit card, you may have to update the information on your account.

1.  Go to [Google Pay](https://payments.google.com/).
2.  Click **Payment methods**.
3.  Next to the card you want to edit, click **Edit**.
4.  Enter the expiration date and CVC found on the card.
5.  Make sure your billing address is correct.
6.  Click **Save**.

##### Add another payment method

1.  Sign in to [Google Pay](https://payments.google.com/).
2.  Click **Payment methods**.
3.  Click **Add payment method**.
4.  Choose a payment method:
    - **Add new credit or debit card**
    - **Add a bank account**
5.  Follow the steps to add your payment method.

If your Google One membership is billed through a third-party partner, [learn how to make changes to your Google One plan](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/15801606#G1_plan_changes).

##### Make sure your payment method is supported

- [Credit card](https://support.google.com/pay/answer/7625139#cards)
- [Bank account (US only)](https://support.google.com/googlepay/answer/11494673)
- [PayPal](https://support.google.com/pay/answer/7625139#paypal)
- Google Play balance (Selected countries/regions only)
  - Check the [list of countries](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6080850) that supports Play balance as a payment method for subscriptions.

##### Contact your credit card provider

If your credit card information is correct but your payment won't go through, contact your credit card provider.

They may have more information on why your credit card can't be charged.

##### Unauthorized charge

[Learn how to report unauthorized charges](https://support.google.com/payments/answer/6224188).

If someone in your family made an unauthorized charge, you can also [limit what they can buy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7039872).

##### Can't get into your account

Sometimes, to finish transactions, you need to [verify your identity](https://support.google.com/payments/answer/6220286?hl%3Den).

If you can't sign in to your Google Account, [get help](https://support.google.com/googleone/troubleshooter/2402620).

##### Regional restrictions

If you can’t pay for Google One because of regional restrictions or other banking issues in your country/region, [learn what to do](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/12002337).

### Find purchases by family members

The family manager can find all the purchases made with the family payment method. [Learn how to find your family's purchases.](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/6294544#see_purchases)

The Google One plan manager can be a different person than your family manager. If so, the Google One plan manager can't find purchases made with the family payment method.

2.  At the top, tap **Menu** and then **Settings**.
3.  Tap **Change payment method**.
4.  Tap **Google One** and then **Update**.
5.  Select an option.
6.  Follow the steps on the screen.

#### If you sign up through the App Store

**If you sign up for Google One through the App Store on an iPhone or iPad and later switch to an Android device:** You can't adjust your membership or change your payment method in the app. To update your account and other info, [use the App Store on another device](https://www.apple.com/itunes/).
