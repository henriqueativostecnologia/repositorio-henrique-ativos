# Buy more Google storage

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

Your Google Account comes with up to 15 GB of cloud storage at no charge. Storage is shared across:

- Google Drive
- Gmail
- Google Photos

Get more cloud storage with:

- Google One, which starts with 100 GB of storage. [Check if Google One is available for your account](https://one.google.com/about#upgrade).
- Google Workspace for work and school. Storage sizes vary. For more information, [contact your administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960).
- Google Workspace Individual, which comes with 1 TB of storage. [Learn about Workspace Individual](https://support.google.com/google-workspace-individual/answer/10758004?hl=en).

If you have a work or school account, you can't buy more storage for yourself. If you need more storage, contact the person who gave you your account.

### Storage subscriptions

- At any time, you can change your storage plan to either a monthly or annual fee.
- A Google One subscription doesn't expire — it'll renew automatically unless you cancel.
- When you switch to a different payment schedule, it can take up to 24 hours for these changes to take effect.
- In addition to the listed price, you may be charged local taxes or fees. These rates may be changed by local entities without notice from Google. Google doesn't charge extra fees beyond the listed price. If you live in the European Economic Area or Morocco, you'll be charged Value-Added Tax (VAT) on Google Play purchases. For these purchases, you can [request a VAT invoice or receipt](https://support.google.com/googlepay/answer/7644144).
- Buying Google storage is only available in some countries. [Find countries where you can buy storage.](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/table/3541286)

You can get extra storage for your Google products.

### Upgrade your Google storage

**Important:** Make sure your payment method is up to date before you upgrade your storage. [Learn how to update your payment details](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/6362993).

#### Buy more storage with Google One

After you upgrade, your Google One membership replaces your current Drive storage plan. Google One members get even more storage space, plus exclusive benefits and family plan sharing. If you want more storage space as a Google One member:

1.  Make sure you’re signed in to your Google Account.
2.  On your computer, go to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com/).
3.  On the left, click **Upgrade**.
4.  Choose your new storage limit.
5.  Review the new plan prices and payment date. Then, click **Next**.
6.  To confirm your Google One plan, click **Subscribe**.

[Change your storage plan](https://one.google.com/changestorageplan)

##### Learn more

- [How your existing storage works with Google One](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9004014)
- [How much Google One costs in your location.](https://one.google.com/about/plans)
- [How to switch to a monthly or yearly storage plan.](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9314361)

### About Google One plans offered by another company

**Important:** If your Google One plan is offered through another company, you'll need to contact that company directly to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan.

#### Manage a Google One plan offered by another company

##### Switch to a Google One plan in the Google One site & app

If you bought your current plan from another company, and instead want a plan that’s only available for purchase on the Google One site or app, you need to:

1.  Cancel your current Google One plan by contacting the company that sold you the plan.
2.  Subscribe to a [Google One plan](https://one.google.com/storage) available on the Google One site and app.

If canceling puts you out of storage, you will need to subscribe to a new plan right away to avoid interruptions to Google services. [Learn what happens when you exceed your storage limit](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9312312#overquota).

##### Fix payment issues

To fix payment issues, contact the company offering you the plan and make sure your payment method hasn't expired and the details are correct. You can find a link to contact the company in Google One settings.

Keep in mind, you won’t be able to update your payment method on the Google One site or app.

##### Payment issues

This can happen if your payment method has expired or the details are incorrect or outdated. To fix payment issues, contact the company offering you the Google One plan.

##### Benefit access

If there’s a payment issue, you will continue to have access to your Google One benefits for a limited time. If you don’t resolve the issue promptly, your Google One plan will end and your storage limit will be reduced to the default amount of storage. If you exceed this amount, you will experience interruptions to Gmail, Google Photos, Drive, and other Google services. [Learn more about what happens when you exceed your storage limit](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9312312#overquota).

### Update your Google One membership through Pixel Pass

#### Change your plan

If you have a 200 GB Google One plan through Pixel Pass, you can change your plan to have more storage through the [Google One website](http://one.google.com). Your monthly price will change based on the plan you choose, and you'll get the new price at checkout.

1.  On your computer, go to [one.google.com](http://one.google.com).
2.  Sign in to your Google Account.
3.  Click Settings  Settings   and then **Change membership plan**.
4.  Select an upgrade plan.

If you purchased your Pixel Pass subscription through the Google Store, you can also update your Google One plan on the [Google Store](https://store.google.com/).

### Learn about pricing for Google One

Google One members get increased storage space and exclusive benefits. [Learn how much Google One costs in your location](https://one.google.com/about/plans).

#### Google One price updates

- Effective July 27, 2026, the price of Google One plans will increase in Algeria, Egypt, Nigeria, Pakistan and Turkey. [Learn more about the 2026 price updates](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/17251824).
- Effective February 18, 2025, the price of Google One plans will increase in Australia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Egypt, Ghana, Indonesia, Japan, Kazakhstan, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Turkey and the United Republic of Tanzania. [Learn more about the 2025 price updates](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/17231329).

##### Use Google One with Workspace Individual

Your Workspace Individual plan comes with 1 TB of storage. You can buy more storage with Google One. [Learn about Workspace Individual](https://support.google.com/google-workspace-individual/answer/10758004).

If you're a Google One plan member:

- Your Google One plan storage is in addition to your Workspace Individual storage.
- There's no change to your Google One membership or [Family Plan and Membership Sharing](https://one.google.com/faq/plan-sharing).
- You stay enrolled unless you choose to cancel. 

### Storage space doesn’t increase after upgrade

It may take up to 24 hours for your new storage to become available after you purchase a new storage plan.

If your storage is still incorrect after 24 hours, sign into your Google Account to [check if your payment went through](https://payments.google.com/gp/w/u/0/home/activity?sctid=5194439335198343). 

If your payment went through, it’s been 24 hours since you upgraded, and your storage is still incorrect, [try these troubleshooting steps](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9776477#storageincrease).

If a charge is applied to your payment card, but you don’t find a payment reflected on your Google Account, check if you’re signed into multiple Google Accounts. If so, Google One may be purchased on a different account. You can cancel the purchase on that account and repurchase Google One on the desired account. [Learn how to cancel purchases on Google One](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9056360).

### Switch to a plan with less storage

If you don’t use all the space you have right now, or you want to pay less for Google storage, [downgrade or cancel your storage plan](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/6374282).

##### Change Google One membership with Pixel Pass

Pixel Pass subscribers can change Google One membership plans at [one.google.com](http://one.google.com). If you want less than 200 GB of Google storage, cancel your Pixel Pass subscription and sign up for a separate Google One membership. [Learn how to Cancel your Pixel Pass plan](https://support.google.com/pixelpass/answer/11202782).

### If you need more help

Google One members can ask for help with Android backups and other topics like family sharing, Google Photos management, and storage plan info. [Learn how to ask a Google expert](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9269729?hl=en&ref_topic=9003736).

##### Manage subscriptions that were not purchased on the Play or App Store

If you purchased your subscription through the Google One website, you can manage your plan [*on the web*](https://one.google.com/).

1.  At the top right, select Settings Settings and then **Change membership plan**.
2.  Choose your new storage limit. You can choose a monthly, quarterly, or annual subscription.
    - Currently, quarterly subscription is only available in India for Android and web.

If you have Google One on an Android device, [you can change your membership plan in Settings](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9003633?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).

**Tip:** You can also [manage your payment method](https://play.google.com/store/paymentmethods) or [manage your subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) with Google Play.

##### You can link your Apple account to only one Google Account

To make changes to your Google One membership plan on your iPhone or iPad, you must make those changes in the Google app used to purchase the plan.

- Google One
- Gmail
- Google Drive
- Google Photos

[Learn how to manage your membership in other apps](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9003707).

If a charge is applied to your payment card, but you don’t find a payment reflected on your Google Account, check if you’re signed into multiple Google Accounts. If so, Google One may be purchased on a different account. You can cancel the purchase on that account and repurchase Google One on the desired account. [Learn how to cancel purchases on Google One](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9056360).

### Switch to a plan with less storage

If you don’t use all the space you have right now, or you want to pay less for Google storage, [downgrade or cancel your storage plan](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/6374282).

##### Change Google One membership with Pixel Pass

Pixel Pass subscribers can change Google One membership plans at [one.google.com](http://one.google.com). If you want less than 200 GB of Google storage, cancel your Pixel Pass subscription and sign up for a separate Google One membership. [Learn how to Cancel your Pixel Pass plan](https://support.google.com/pixelpass/answer/11202782).

### If you need more help

Google One members can ask for help with Android backups and other topics like family sharing, Google Photos management, and storage plan info. [Learn how to ask a Google expert](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9269729?hl=en&ref_topic=9003736).

##### Manage subscriptions that were not purchased on the Play or App Store

If you purchased your subscription through the Google One website, you can manage your plan [*on the web*](https://one.google.com/).

1.  At the top right, select Settings Settings and then **Change membership plan**.
2.  Choose your new storage limit. You can choose a monthly, quarterly, or annual subscription.
    - Currently, quarterly subscription is only available in India for Android and web.

If you have Google One on an Android device, [you can change your membership plan in Settings](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9003633?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).

**Tip:** You can also [manage your payment method](https://play.google.com/store/paymentmethods) or [manage your subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) with Google Play.

##### You can link your Apple account to only one Google Account

To make changes to your Google One membership plan on your iPhone or iPad, you must make those changes in the Google app used to purchase the plan.

- Google One
- Gmail
- Google Drive
- Google Photos

[Learn how to manage your membership in other apps](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/9003707).
