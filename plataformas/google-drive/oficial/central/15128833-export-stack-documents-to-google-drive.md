# Export Stack Documents to Google Drive

Starting the week of September 23, 2024, we are removing support for [Stack: PDF Scanner by Google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.area120.paperwork&hl=en&gl=US), a legacy PDF scanner and document organizer whose functionality has been incorporated into the Google Drive app.

Going forward, we recommend [using Google Drive to scan](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/3145835?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid), save, and organize your important documents.

**Tip:** As of September 23, 2024, the “Export all Stack Documents to Google Drive” feature is no longer available. You can still export individual documents from Stack with these steps.

## Export an individual document from Stack:

To export an individual document from Stack:

1.  Open Stack app.
2.  Tap to open the document you want to export.
3.  Tap **Share**.
4.  Select the app to export to (e.g. Google Drive).
