# Get approvals on files in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Computer

You can get approvals on your content in Google Drive. When your material is ready for review, you can lock the file and ask the recipient to approve the already drafted content. 

**Note**: This feature is only available for Enterprise and Google Workspace for Education Plus accounts. It is not available for personal (@gmail.com) or standard Workspace accounts.

### Request an approval

Requesting an approval is only available for eligible work or school accounts, and you're currently not signed in.

[Sign in to your work or school account](https://www.google.com/accounts/Logout?continue=https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822)

### Edit & reset approvals

By default, files stay unlocked during an approval. Editors can change, comment, or make suggestions on the file. When someone edits the file, the approval process changes based on your settings:

- **If you checked "Require all approvers to review the same content"**: Edits reset all approvals. All reviewers must review and approve the new changes.
- **If you unchecked "Require all approvers to review the same content"**: Edits do not reset approvals. Reviewers can change their decisions at any time until the approval is complete.

### Lock a document

You can lock a document to make sure reviewers can’t change a file while it undergoes approval. Edits, comments and suggestions can't be added to locked documents.

- To lock a document, click **File** And then **Lock**.
- To unlock a document, click **File** And then **Unlock**.

### Unlock a file to edit

After a file is approved and locked, anyone with edit access can unlock the file and make new edits.

**Important:**

- **If edits reset approvals**: If you unlock a file during an active review, you reset all approvals. Previous approvers must re-approve the file. After the approval process is complete, you can unlock the file without losing the final approvals.
- **If edits do not reset approvals**: The file doesn't lock when the approval is complete. Editors can change the file without losing the final approvals.

### Unlock a Google Docs, Sheets or Slides file

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Double click on the file.
3.  At the top of the document, click Locked And then **Unlock file.**

### Unlock other file types in Google Drive

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right-click on the file And then **Unlock.**

### Approve or reject a file

In Google Docs, Sheets, & Slides, when an approval is in process, a banner will show at the top of the file.

- To approve the file, click **Approve**. You can also add an optional note.
- To reject the file, click **Reject**. You can also add an optional note.
- To open the sidebar to review approval details, click **View details**.

### Open approval sidebar

You can review the approval details of a document in the approval process in the sidebar.

### From Google Drive file list

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right-click on the file And then **Approvals**.
3.  In the sidebar on the right, on the approval card that appears, click **Open**.

### From Google Drive preview

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Double click on the file to open Drive preview. This will open for files that aren't Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, or Microsoft Office files.
3.  To open the sidebar, in the top right, click Menu 3 dot menu icon And then **Approvals.**
4.  On the approval card that appears, click **Open**.

### From Google Docs, Sheets, & Slides

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Double click on the file to open the file in Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides.
3.  To open the sidebar:
    - On the top, click **File** And then **Approvals.**
    - On the banner, click **View details.**

### Review edits made during approval

For Google Docs, Sheets and Slides files, you can review content changes during an approval.

1.  Open the [approval sidebar](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/9387535#sidebar).

2.  On the sidebar on the right, select an option from the dropdown:

    - Since approval started
    - Since you last approved
    - Since you last viewed

    **Tips**:

    - You must view changes from the approval sidebar or you won’t find the option “Since last time I viewed” in the dropdown.
    - The file must be approved or be unchanged or you won’t find the option “Since I last approved” in the dropdown.

3.  Click **View.**

### Edit your request

After you file your approval request, you can change or cancel it.

1.  In the file, open the [approval sidebar](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/9387535#sidebar). 
2.  From the sidebar, you can:
    - **Change approvers:** Next to the current approver, click More  More   and then  **Change approver**.
    - **Add approvers:** At the top right, click** **Add approvers Add approver .
    - **Change the due date:** At the top, click the due date  and enter a new date. 

### Cancel approval request

If you no longer want someone to approve your request, you can cancel it.

1.  In the file, open the [approval sidebar](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/9387535#sidebar). 
2.  At the top right, click More More   and then  **Cancel** **request**.

### Find files that need approval

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  At the top, in the search box, click the Down arrow  Down arrow .
3.  Next to Approvals, select **Awaiting my approval** or **requested by me**.
4.  Click **Search**.

### View previous approvals for a file

**Important:** You can only view previous approvals for Docs, Sheets, and Slides files.

1.  On your computer, go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Open the file you to want to view approvals for.
3.  If approved versions are available, at the top, click **View approved versions**.
4.  In version history, on the right, click **View approval detail**.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

Requesting an approval is only available for eligible work or school accounts, and you're currently not signed in.

[Sign in to your work or school account](https://www.google.com/accounts/Logout?continue=https://accounts.google.com/ServiceLogin?continue=https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822)

### Edit & reset approvals

**Important:** Once an edit is made, all approvers must approve the new changes. 

By default, files with requested approvals stay unlocked. Editors can edit, comment, or make suggestions on the file. 

### Lock a document

**Important:** To lock a document on mobile, turn on **Lock file before sending approval request** before you send the request.

Lock a document to make sure approvers can’t change it until it’s approved. No one can add edits, comments, or suggestions to locked documents.

### Unlock a file to edit

**Important:** When a file is unlocked or edited as part of an approval, recorded approvals reset and previous approvers must re-approve the document with the new changes. 

After a file is approved and locked, anyone with edit access can unlock the file and make new edits. When you attempt to edit a locked file on mobile, you’ll be asked if you want to unlock the file. If you agree, the file will be unlocked and you’ll be able to edit the file. 

When you make edits after approvals are complete, those approvals are associated with the previous version of the file. [Learn how to check approvals on previous versions of a file](#Previous_Android). 

**Tip:** Unlock files after approvals are complete so they don’t get reset.

##### From Google Drive file list

1.  On your Android device, open Google Drive Drive .
2.  Next to the file you want to approve or reject, tap More   And then ** **Approvals** **.
    - **To approve the file:** Tap **Approve**. You can also add an optional note. 
    - **To reject the file:** Tap **Reject**. You can also add an optional note.

##### From Google Drive preview

1.  On your Android device, open Google Drive Drive .
2.  Open the file you want to approve or reject.
3.  Tap More And then  Approvals  .
    - **To approve the file:** Tap **Approve**. You can also add an optional note. 
    - **To reject the file:** Tap **Reject**. You can also add an optional note.

    - Approval activity

3.  Tap More  And then  Approvals to find:
    - Approval status
    - Due date
    - Approval activity

### Edit your request

##### From Google Drive file list

3.  From the approval details view:

    - **Add approvers:** At the top, tap Add approver  Add approver .
    - **Change the due date:** At the top, tap Calendar  and enter a new date.
