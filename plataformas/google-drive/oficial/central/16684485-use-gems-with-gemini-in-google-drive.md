# Use Gems with Gemini in Google Drive

**Important**:

- To use Gemini in Drive, you must have an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. [Learn more about Gemini features and plans](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/13952129?hl=en&co=DASHER._Family%3DBusiness-Enterprise).
- Gems in Google Drive are only available on Drive web ([drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)).

To save time with repetitive tasks, you can create custom prompts with Gems in Gemini. You can have a conversation with a Gem in Ask Gemini.

To understand how your data is used and what to expect from Gemini suggestions, [learn more about disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684790).

To use Gems with Gemini in Google Drive:

1.  Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2.  At the top right, click **Ask Gemini** **.**
3.  In the new full-screen view, at the top left, click **menu **.
4.  Select Gems and choose the pre-made or custom Gem you want to use.
5.  At the bottom, enter a prompt.

**Tip**: To create a Gem, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/). You can then use your Gem in the Gemini app and Gemini side panel. [Learn how to use Gems in Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780).
