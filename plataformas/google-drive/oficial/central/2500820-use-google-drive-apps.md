# Use Google Drive apps

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Computer, Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

### Use and manage apps

##### Create files

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  On the left, click **New**.
3.  Choose an app.
4.  After you save your file, you can find it in "My Drive."

**Note:** Not all Google Drive apps will be able to create files in Drive.

##### Open files

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Right-click on a file.
3.  Move your cursor over "Open with"
4.  Select an app.

You can make an app the default for opening certain types of files.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Click Settings Settings and then ** Settings**.
3.  Click **Manage apps**.
4.  Find the app you want to use.
5.  Next to the app, to the left of "Use by default," check the box.

##### Save files

Your Google Drive app files are automatically saved after you:

- Edit a file.
- Choose "Export to Google Drive."
- Choose "Save a copy."
- Choose "Send to Drive."

### Help with Google Drive apps

##### Get help for Google Drive apps

Many Google Drive apps are made by companies that have their own terms of use and privacy policies. To contact the company that made an app:

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Click Settings Settings and then  **Settings.**
3.  Click **Manage apps**.
4.  Next to the app, click **Options**.
5.  Click **View product page**. You'll find contact info on the [Google Workspace](https://gsuite.google.com/marketplace/category/works-with-drive) Marketplace page.

##### Remove Google Drive apps

When you open files with Google Drive apps, some apps may ask permission to view some or all files in your Google Drive.

You can revoke permission at any time.

1.  On your computer, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2.  Click Settings Settings and then  **Settings**.
3.  Click **Manage apps**.
4.  Next to the app, click **Options**.
5.  Click **Disconnect from Drive**.
