# Supported languages for Google Workspace with Gemini

Some features in Google Workspace with Gemini are available in multiple languages.

## Supported languages

You can get an AI Overview at the top of an email thread in:

For other Google Workspace with Gemini features, English is the only supported language. To use Google Workspace with Gemini features that are supported in English only, [set your Google Account language to English](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32047).
