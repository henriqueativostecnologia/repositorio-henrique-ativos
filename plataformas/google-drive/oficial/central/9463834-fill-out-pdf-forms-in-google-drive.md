# Fill out PDF forms in Google Drive

This article has different instructions for each platform/version: Android phone & tablet, iPhone & iPad. All versions are copied below.

## Version: Android phone & tablet

Want advanced Google Workspace features for your business?

[Try Google Workspace today!](https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome?utm_source=gwslearningcenter&utm_medium=support&utm_campaign=9463834)

You can fill out PDF forms in Google Drive on your Android device.

1.  On your Android device, open the [Google Drive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs) app.
2.  Tap the PDF that you want to fill out.
3.  At the bottom right, tap Edit   and then  Form Filling .
    - If you don’t see the option, you may not be able to fill out this PDF.
4.  Enter your information in the PDF form.
5.  At the top right, tap **Save**.
    - To save as a copy, click More More   and then **Save as**.

### Fill out a PDF form

You can fill out PDF forms in Google Drive on your iPhone or iPad.

1.  On your iPhone or iPad, open the [Google Drive](https://apps.apple.com/app/google-drive/id507874739) app.
2.  Tap the PDF you want to fill out.
3.  At the bottom, tap **Fill out form**.
    - If you don’t see the option, you may not be able to fill out this PDF.
4.  Enter your information in the PDF form.
5.  At the top right, tap **Save**.
    - To save as a copy, click More  More   and then  **Save as**.
